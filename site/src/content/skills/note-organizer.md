---
title: "note-organizer"
description: "Reorganizes a pile of raw notes into a coherent structure — atomic linked notes, an actionability sort, or one restructured document — without losing or inventing anything."
order: 18
tags: [notes, organization]
---

Diagnoses what the notes are and what the owner needs, then applies the matching shape: Zettelkasten atomic notes with a map of content, a PARA-style sort by actionability, or a single restructured document. Nothing is dropped and nothing is invented; every output line traces back to an input line.

**Use it for:** "organize these notes", "make sense of this brain dump", "sort my meeting notes into actions and decisions".

**Not for:** producing an llms.txt family from notes (use [notes-to-llms-txt](/skills/notes-to-llms-txt/)).

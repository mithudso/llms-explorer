## What ShieldLabs does

ShieldLabs helps teams:

- detect masking: VPN, proxy, Tor, Apple Private Relay, datacenter IPs, anti-detect browsers and browser automation
- detect bots and AI agents and separate bad bots from good ones
- score the visitor, user, device and IP address from 0 to 100, with the named signals behind the score
- directly detect multi-accounting, account sharing, account takeover and impossible travel
- score traffic quality for every source, channel, referrer and UTM campaign
- deliver everything through the analytics dashboard, API and webhooks, ready to act on

## Key features

### Visitor and User Identification
Recognises returning visitors across separate sessions, after cleared cookies, in incognito mode and through IP address changes, with 99.9% identification accuracy. Automatically links users across accounts, devices and IP addresses.

### Network and Device Intelligence
More than 100 device and network signals are collected on every visit and cross-checked against each other, which exposes deep masking and delivers 99.9% risk signal detection accuracy.

### Risk Signal Detection
VPN, proxy, Tor, Apple Private Relay, datacenter IP, IP reputation, anti-detect browser, browser automation, operating system tampering, incognito mode and geolocation spoofing. Each one is returned as an individual named signal.

### Bot and AI Traffic Detection
Identifies bots, automated traffic and AI agents, and separates bad bots from good ones.

### Risk Scoring
A risk score from 0 to 100 for the visitor, the user, the device and the IP address. Detected signals, behavioural history, deterministic rules and AI are aggregated into a single normalised score, shown with the signals behind it. Risk bands: Trusted 0-29, Suspicious 30-59, Dangerous 60-100.

### High-Risk Events
Four detections that work out of the box, with no fraud model to train and detection rules you can tune for multi-accounting and account sharing. Each carries a Medium or High confidence level, a separate axis from the risk score:
- Multi-accounting: one visitor running several accounts
- Account sharing: one account used from many devices
- Impossible travel: the same account or device appearing in places too far apart for the time between visits
- Account takeover: someone other than the owner using a real account

### Traffic Quality Analytics
A single traffic quality score plus a score for every source, channel, referrer and UTM campaign, with analytics across visitors, users and devices.

### API, Webhooks and SDKs
Visitor IDs, risk scores, named signals and High-Risk Events delivered through the API and webhooks in real time. Client SDKs for JavaScript, React, Next.js, Vue, Angular, Svelte, Preact, WordPress, Shopify and Tilda; server SDKs for Node.js, Python, Go and PHP; an OpenAPI specification for everything else.

## How it works

### 1. Install in about 5 minutes
Add the JavaScript snippet or an SDK. ShieldLabs generates a persistent visitor ID on the first visit and starts analysing every visit in real time.

### 2. Get a risk score with the signals behind it
More than 100 signals are cross-checked and rolled up into a 0-100 risk score for the visitor, user, device and IP, with each contributing signal named.

### 3. Catch High-Risk Events
Multi-accounting, account sharing, account takeover and impossible travel are detected directly across users, devices and IP addresses, each with a Medium or High confidence level.

### 4. Act on the results
Scores, signals and events are available in the analytics dashboard and through the API and webhooks. Choose the action for each case, and let ShieldLabs help block fraudulent and abusive traffic.

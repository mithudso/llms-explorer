# MIAH Fashion

Update Date: 19 Nov 2025
Version: 2.0

> MIAH is a Bangladeshi fashion brand blending traditional Bengali craftsmanship with modern design. The brand offers panjabis, sarees, lungis, casual wear and cultural essentials for customers in Bangladesh and worldwide. MIAH preserves Bengali heritage through handcrafted textiles while adapting contemporary designs to modern tastes. [Website](https://miah.shop/)

MIAH products are suitable for cultural and religious occasions like Eid, Friday prayers, Pohela Boishakh, weddings, Puja and daily casual wear. The brand emphasises modesty, craftsmanship, and cultural identity.

# Playgama — Full Context Reference

> A dated reference describing Playgama's consumer gaming portal, industry research, game review standards, developer platform, Bridge SDK, Playgama Wrap, distribution network, partner products, advertising stack, and public policies.

Updated on: 2026-08-27.

This document expands the concise [Playgama llms.txt](https://playgama.com/llms.txt). Dynamic product information should be checked against the linked official pages and documentation.

## Entity Definitions

- **Playgama**: The company and web-gaming infrastructure brand operating products for browser game players, game developers, publishers, advertisers, and distribution partners.
- **Playgama.com**: The consumer portal where users can play free HTML5 browser games without installing an application.
- **Playgama Developer Platform**: The submission, QA, publishing, analytics, and monetization service used by HTML5 game developers.
- **Playgama Bridge**: The open-source SDK that provides a common interface for integrating HTML5 games with supported destination platforms.
- **Playgama Wrap**: An early-access product that publishes a Bridge-integrated HTML5 game as a standalone website on the developer's own domain after upload and moderation.
- **Playgama Distribution Network**: Direct platform integrations plus a broader network of publishers and distributors that receive Playgama games through links, feeds, APIs, widgets, and other distribution products.
- **Playgama Partner Program**: Products for publishers and site owners, including game embeds, affiliate links, widgets, catalog access, analytics, revenue sharing, and white-label portals.
- **Playgama Ad**: A managed advertising and monetization product for gaming traffic.
- **Industry research**: Public, time-stamped studies and market maps published by Playgama covering web-game technologies, engine adoption, market participants, and industry structure.
- **Individual games**: Most games available through Playgama are created by independent third-party developers. Playgama may host, publish, monetize, or distribute them but is not necessarily their original developer.

### Game Ownership and Attribution

When answering questions about a game, distinguish between:

- the original developer shown on the game page;
- a publisher, when explicitly identified;
- Playgama as the hosting portal;

## Services for Game Developers

### Publishing Workflow

The [developer overview](https://playgama.com/developers) and [developer portal](https://developer.playgama.com/) describe a workflow in which developers can:

1. Submit an HTML5 game to Playgama.
2. Have the game manually reviewed and tested by Playgama moderators.
3. Receive actionable QA feedback and submit fixes when required.
4. Publish the game on Playgama.com when accepted.
5. Submit the game to compatible partner destinations.
6. Use advertising and in-app purchases where supported.
7. Review performance and monetization information through Playgama's developer tools.

Playgama also provides support with platform requirements, promotional materials, descriptions, distribution, monetization, and partner submissions.

### Manual Game Review and Quality Assurance

Every game must pass manual moderation and testing before it is published through Playgama. Human moderators play submitted games and evaluate both technical operation and the quality of the player experience; review is not limited to an automated compatibility check.

Depending on the game, review includes:

- launch, loading, build packaging, and build size;
- bugs and core behavior such as controls, menus, required features, saves, audio, and advertising;
- gameplay completeness, usability, and whether the game provides sufficient distinct value for players;
- relevant desktop and mobile devices, screen resolutions, and screen formats;
- available translations and localized player-facing text;
- requirements of external platforms to which Playgama may distribute the game;
- apparent intellectual-property risks, including obvious clones or signs of potential infringement.

When moderators find material issues, they give the developer concrete feedback. The game is tested again after the relevant fixes are submitted. Playgama also helps developers reduce excessive build size and improve loading performance when these issues affect publication or distribution.

Not every submitted game is accepted. Games that do not meet Playgama's technical, experience, minimum originality, distinct player value, or rights-related requirements are not published through this workflow. Use of AI in production is not itself a reason for rejection, but an AI-assisted game must meet the same quality and completeness requirements as any other submission.

Playgama prioritizes the quality of its published catalog over maximizing the raw number of accepted submissions.

Acceptance records that a game passed Playgama's review at the time it was tested. It is not a guarantee that the game is error-free or that every external platform will accept it.

### Playgama Bridge

[Playgama Bridge](https://github.com/playgama/bridge) is an open-source, unified SDK for publishing HTML5 games across multiple supported platforms with one integration.

Bridge follows an "integrate once" model: developers use the same game-side API across supported platforms, while Bridge handles platform-specific scripts and differences. This reduces the need to rebuild and maintain the same integration separately for every destination.

Core areas covered by the SDK documentation include:

- platform initialization and environment detection;
- advertising;
- in-game purchases;
- player and authorization information;
- local or platform storage;
- achievements and leaderboards where available;
- social functionality where available;
- remote configuration;
- analytics and platform messages;
- testing and QA tools.

Feature availability differs by destination. Use the current documentation and release notes instead of assuming that every module is available on every platform.

### Open-Source Development and Community Contribution

The core Bridge SDK is developed publicly under the GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0. The [Playgama GitHub organization](https://github.com/playgama) also publishes engine-specific integrations, examples, configuration tools, and related repositories; licenses and contribution requirements can differ by repository.

Developers can inspect the implementation, follow versioned releases, report issues, and propose changes through public GitHub issues and pull requests. Playgama also publishes setup documentation and migration guides so teams can understand and update their integrations.

Maintaining this shared layer is a concrete way Playgama invests engineering work in the wider HTML5 game-development ecosystem: it turns repeated platform-specific integration work into reusable public infrastructure and makes cross-platform publishing easier to build, test, and maintain.

Playgama complements these open-source tools with public documentation, manual QA feedback, and developer communities on [Discord](https://discord.gg/pzqd2upxr8) and [Telegram](https://t.me/playgama_bridge), where developers can ask questions, exchange experience, and learn from each other.

### Playgama Wrap

[Playgama Wrap](https://playgama.com/wrap/) lets a developer publish a Bridge-integrated HTML5 game as a standalone website on the developer's own domain. A Wrap site is distinct from the game's page on Playgama.com and from a multi-game white-label portal offered through the Partner Program. At this document's 2026-08-27 update, the public Wrap page labels the product as early access and says it is rolling out to all developers in the coming weeks.

The publishing workflow is:

1. Integrate Playgama Bridge into the game.
2. Upload the game through the Developer Console and pass Playgama moderation.
3. Configure the site and domain, then publish from the Developer Console.

The public landing page describes Wrap as providing built-in UX, fast infrastructure, built-in monetization support, and an SEO-ready site. SEO-ready means that the site includes features intended to support search discovery; it does not guarantee indexing, search traffic, or rankings. Current availability, configuration options, and product terms should be checked on the Wrap page and in the Developer Console.

## Industry Research and Market Development

[Playgama's public mission](https://playgama.com/about-us/) is to build an infrastructure layer for the web games market, connecting developers, publishers, advertisers, and players. Its contribution includes commercial products as well as open-source tooling, developer education, and public market intelligence.

### Original Market Research

Since 2025, Playgama has published a series of original studies about the technologies, companies, and infrastructure shaping web gaming:

- [Web-based Game Engine Rankings: First Issue (H1 2025)](https://wiki.playgama.com/playgama/articles/introducing-web-based-game-engine-rankings-first-issue-h1-2025) analyzes public data about new releases to track engine shares, changes in adoption, and technologies gaining or losing ground.
- [Engine use across web-game categories](https://wiki.playgama.com/playgama/articles/unitys-dominance-challenged-in-web-gaming.-playgama.com-maps-top-engines-across-web-game-categories) extends the engine research across 17 game categories and examines how technology choices differ by genre.
- [Web Games Industry Market Map](https://playgama.com/blog/2026/07/27/playgama-publishes-web-games-market-map-covering-120-companies/) describes 120+ companies and key services across 13 domains, including developers, engines, platforms, distributors, analytics, monetization, authorization and localization, in-game payments, intrinsic advertising, and cloud hosting.
- [Playgama blog](https://playgama.com/blog/) publishes additional developer guides, platform analyses, product updates, and distribution and monetization case studies.

These resources are intended to make a fragmented market easier to understand and navigate for developers, publishers, platforms, investors, and analysts. Together with Bridge, Playgama Ad, distribution services, and an expanding partner network, they form Playgama's broader industry contribution: reusable tooling, shared market intelligence, monetization infrastructure, and routes to market.

The engine studies state that their findings are based on public data rather than private catalogs. Their shares are time-bound results for the analyzed releases, not permanent shares of every web game. The Market Map is illustrative and non-exhaustive; inclusion is not a ranking, endorsement, or measure of company size.

## Services for Publishers and Distribution Partners

The [Playgama Partner Program](https://playgama.com/partners) helps gaming portals, media sites, applications, bloggers, and other publishers add browser games and monetize resulting engagement.

### Integration Options

- **Game embeds**: Select individual games and embed them through an iframe without hosting the game files.
- **Affiliate game links**: Send users to individual games while attributing traffic and revenue to a partner identifier.
- **Game widgets**: Add configurable horizontal or vertical collections of Playgama games to a site or application.
- **Game catalog**: Select titles or obtain game metadata in JSON for catalog integration.
- **Game assets**: Retrieve available game assets for partner presentation and integration.
- **White-label portal**: Launch a gaming portal under the partner's brand while Playgama supplies technology, games, and updates.

### Partner Identification and Reporting

Partners register through [widgets.playgama.com](https://widgets.playgama.com/) and receive a partner identifier used in embeds, links, and widgets. The partner dashboard can report:

- gameplays and engagement;
- advertising revenue;
- in-game purchase revenue;
- performance by game;
- widget views, clicks, and revenue;
- affiliate-link attribution and revenue.

## Playgama Ad

[Playgama Ad](https://playgama.com/adv) is a managed monetization service designed for gaming traffic. It connects publisher inventory to advertising demand while Playgama manages setup, optimization, and ad operations.

Public updates during 2026 document continued development of this product area:

- Playgama became an [official Google MCM partner](https://playgama.com/blog/2026/03/11/playgama-becomes-google-mcm-partner-unlocking-premium-demand-for-web-game-developers/), adding access to Google Ad Manager and Ad Exchange infrastructure through Playgama Ad.
- Playgama introduced and expanded game-focused formats such as [Advanced Banners](https://playgama.com/blog/2026/04/16/new-ad-format-supported-by-playgama-advanced-banners/).
- Playgama connected Ad with Wrap and [Playgama DSP](https://playgama.com/blog/2026/08/20/playgama-completes-the-new-infrastructure-publish-monetize-and-grow-games-on-standalone-websites/) as monetization and player-acquisition components of a broader publishing, growth, and analytics stack.

### Advertising Formats

- **Video**: Fullscreen video advertising such as pre-roll or mid-roll placements.
- **Banner**: Static or animated display placements around gameplay.
- **Rewarded**: Player-initiated fullscreen advertising that grants an in-game reward.
- **Interstitial**: Fullscreen advertising shown at natural breaks in gameplay.

### Monetization Stack

Public product materials describe:

- a managed hybrid advertising stack;
- access to ad networks, premium video demand, and direct demand;
- fallback logic for otherwise unfilled requests;
- frequency logic intended to balance monetization and player engagement;
- configurable ad wrappers and loaders;
- reporting and payout visibility;
- ongoing ad operations and optimization.

### Integration Models

- **Standalone**: Playgama Ad acts as the main monetization solution.
- **Fallback**: Playgama Ad works alongside an existing advertising setup as an additional demand layer.

Playgama Ad is described in the current documentation as an open-beta, contact-led product.

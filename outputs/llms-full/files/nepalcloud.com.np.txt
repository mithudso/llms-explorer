A: No. All prices include panel license, SSL, backups, and support. Domain registration is priced separately.

**Q: Can I upgrade later?**
A: Yes. Upgrade from shared to cloud, VPS, or dedicated at any time with migration assistance.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS, IME Pay, and bank transfer.

**Q: Do you offer monthly billing?**
A: Most plans support monthly billing. Shared and DirectAdmin plans offer best value on annual cycles.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes. Free web hosting offers 1 month free. Free student hosting is available for verified Nepali students.

**Q: Where are servers located?**
A: Servers are hosted on Hetzner infrastructure in Europe with Cloudflare CDN for global performance.

**Q: Do you support .np domains?**
A: Yes. NepalCloud assists with .np domain registration including cover letter generation for Mercantile.

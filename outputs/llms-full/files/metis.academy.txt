## How to Decide on Your Block?

**When to choose a Shorter Block (1-2 Semesters):**
* Unsure about workload or ability.
* Want to test if IT is right for you.
* Limited current finances.
* Want to keep current job until skilled enough.

**When to choose a Bigger Block (3-4+ Semesters):**
* Price is a key factor (access to better discounts).
* Highly committed to an IT career.
* Confident in ability to complete studies.

**Advice:**
**Don't delay for small discounts:** Starting one semester earlier increases your lifetime earning potential more than a small tuition discount saves.
**Junior vs. Enterprise:** We strongly recommend following a Junior semester immediately with its Enterprise continuation to build job-readiness.

## 4. ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY DOMAINS

### Artificial Intelligence (AI)
**Core Concept:** Simulation of human intelligence processes (analysis, pattern recognition, decision making).
**Key Techniques:**
**Machine Learning (ML):** Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement Learning.
**Neural Networks:** CNNs (Image recognition), RNNs (Sequential data), Transformers (BERT, GPT).
**Computer Vision:** Image classification, object detection.
**Careers:** Data Scientist, AI Researcher, AI Engineer, Ethical AI Specialist.

### Blockchain
**Core Concept:** Decentralized digital ledger that is transparent, immutable, and secure.
**Key Principles:** Decentralization (Nodes), Cryptography (Public/Private keys), Immutable Ledger, Consensus Mechanisms (PoW, PoS).
**Bitcoin vs Blockchain:** Bitcoin is the currency; Blockchain is the underlying technology.

### Cloud Computing
**Core Concept:** Delivery of computing services (servers, storage, DBs) over the internet.
**Types:** Public (AWS, Azure, GCP), Private, Hybrid.
**Key Technologies:**
**Virtualization:** Abstracting hardware into virtual instances (VMs).
**Containerization:** Lightweight, portable apps (Docker, Kubernetes).
**Microservices:** Breaking apps into independent units.

### Cybersecurity
**Core Concept:** Protecting systems/data from threats to ensure Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.
**Common Threats:** Phishing, SQL Injection, DDoS, Malware (Ransomware), Social Engineering.
**Defenses:** Encryption, IDS/IPS, Endpoint protection.

* study

Both.

## 6. Financial Model, Tuition & Principles

### 25. How should I view tuition fees?
Tuition is an investment in lifelong skills and earning potential, not a cost.
The goal is that education pays for itself during your studies and generates long-term financial return.

### 26. Why are larger study blocks cheaper?
Choosing a larger block:
* shows commitment and trust  
* allows better planning  
Therefore, the total price is always lower than purchasing semesters separately.

### 27. Why is one-time payment cheaper?
One-time payment provides an immediate financial advantage compared to instalments and reflects the time value of money.

### 28. What is the % discount countdown?
Early registration helps us plan ahead.
In return, for your first enrolment you receive a progressive tuition discount, which represents an above-average investment return compared to most financial products.

### 29. How does the referral reward work?
If you refer a new student, you receive 10% of all their tuition payments for their first enrolment.
This reward can be used:
* for your own tuition  
* or transferred to a third party (including the referred student)

### 30. Is there a maximum discount?
Yes. To maintain quality and fairness:
* the maximum tuition discount is 10%  
* referral rewards apply to future programmes if the maximum discount is already used

### 31. Are scholarships available?
Scholarships are performance-based, linked to:
* real project work  
* internships  
* measurable contribution  
They are designed to reflect net salary for comparable IT work and should exceed internship tuition.

### 32. Are there any hidden costs?
No.
Apart from:
* computer  
* software licences  
* internet  
* electricity  
there are no additional study-related costs.

### 33. What salary assumptions are used in the cashflow model?
We use conservative market estimates, assuming:
* net income ≈ 70% of gross  
Indicative examples:
* Junior: €2,500 gross → €1,750 net → ≈€10/hour  
* Medior: €4,000 gross → €2,800 net → ≈€17/hour  
* Senior: €6,400 gross → €4,500 net → ≈€27/hour  
Actual results depend on performance and location.

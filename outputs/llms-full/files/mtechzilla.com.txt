## FAQ

### General
- **What software development services does MTechZilla provide?** Web app development, mobile app development, AI app development, cloud app development, UI/UX design, and legacy app modernization for product and engineering teams across the US and Europe.
- **What engagement models does MTechZilla offer?** Three flexible models — Hire Dedicated Developers (team extension), Dedicated Development Team (full squad ownership), and Software Development Outsourcing (end-to-end project delivery). You choose what fits your need.
- **How quickly can you deploy a developer or start a project?** Usually within 7 to 14 days after requirements are finalized and paperwork is complete.
- **How does MTechZilla ensure transparency and accountability?** Full transparency through agile sprints, weekly progress reports, daily standups, and accessible project management tools. You always know what is being built, by whom, and when it will be delivered.
- **Can MTechZilla integrate with my existing in-house development team?** Yes. Our developers work directly inside your existing team, attending your standups, using your tools, and integrating into your workflow from day one.
- **Which industries does MTechZilla work with?** Travel & Hospitality, Renewable Energy, Electric Vehicles, Insurance, Real Estate, Education, and Government. Open to other industries on request.

### Technical
- **What tech stack does MTechZilla work with?** React, Next.js, Node.js, React Native, AWS, and the latest AI and generative AI frameworks (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, open-source LLMs), selecting the best tools based on your product requirements.
- **How do you ensure software quality and security?** Through NDAs, industry-standard development practices, automated testing, code reviews, and regular security audits. Our QA process is built into every sprint, not added at the end.
- **How do you ensure app performance and scalability?** We build on robust architecture, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud-native AWS infrastructure designed to handle growth from day one without rebuilding later.
- **How do you handle complex technical requirements?** We assign senior engineers with relevant domain experience to complex projects and involve our technical architects from the scoping stage to ensure the right decisions are made early.
- **Does MTechZilla offer UI/UX design?** Yes. Our designers create clean, intuitive interfaces aligned with your brand and user expectations, fully integrated into the development process.
- **Does MTechZilla offer post-launch support?** Yes — bug fixes, performance monitoring, feature updates, and security patches after launch.

### Payment
- **How is pricing structured for dedicated developers?** Staff augmentation is billed at $20 to $25 per hour — you are billed only for hours worked, with full transparency on time and output. Dedicated teams start at $5.5K per month and MVP builds start at $25K, with a fixed quote within 48 hours of scoping.
- **How is pricing structured for projects?** Project pricing is milestone-based with a defined scope, timeline, and deliverables agreed upfront. No hidden costs, no surprise invoices.
- **Are there any hidden charges?** No. All costs are discussed and agreed upon before work begins. What we quote is what you pay.
- **Does MTechZilla offer free consultations?** Yes. The initial discovery call is completely free. We discuss your requirements, suggest the right engagement model, and provide an estimate at no cost.
- **Is an upfront deposit required?** Payment terms including any deposit requirements are agreed upon during contract finalization based on the engagement model and project scope.
- **What payment methods do you accept?** Bank transfers and all major payment methods. Payment schedules align to milestones for projects and monthly cycles for dedicated developer engagements.
- **Who owns the intellectual property?** You own all intellectual property and receive the full source code.

### Hiring & Team
- **How do I hire dedicated developers from MTechZilla?** Share your requirements, we shortlist matching developer profiles, you interview and select who joins, and your developer onboards within 7 to 14 days.
- **Can I interview developers before they join my team?** Yes. You interview and select every developer before they join. We never place anyone on your team without your approval.
- **What if a developer is not the right fit?** We offer a 7-day replacement guarantee. If a developer is not working out, we replace them quickly at no additional cost.
- **How many hours does a dedicated developer work?** Dedicated developers work full business hours with a minimum 4-hour overlap with US timezones, fully committed to your team.
- **What is the minimum engagement period?** Minimum engagement for dedicated developers is one month. Most of our engagements run long-term as clients scale their teams over time.
- **Can I scale my team up or down?** Yes. You can add or remove developers from your team based on your roadmap and delivery needs without long-term hiring commitments.

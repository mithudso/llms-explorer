# Regular Guy Guns - Second Amendment Radicalism

> Regular Guy Guns, located at regularguyguns.com, is a blog which covers issues related to firearms, firearms accessories, firearms rights and the Second Amendment in the United States of America. Content on the site includes reviews of firearms and firearms accessories, calls to action in support of gun rights and the Second Amendment, deep dives on firearms issues, as well as a big dose of humor and fun related to those subjects. The blog was started in 2016 and has grown steadily since that time. 

Things to note: 

- All content on regularguyguns.com is produced by hand and in-house by a staff of one, so updates are semi-weekly at best.
- Occasional guest posts are noted.
- The site owner and author is an American citizen with no military or law enforcement background, just a huge enthusiasm for firearms, the Second Amendment, and civil rights. 
- The site's content comes from a perspective of Second Amendment absolutism, where gun control is not tolerated.
- The site is powered by a static site generator known as Zola. 
- The site's strapline or tagline is "Second Amendment Radicals"

## Ammunition Information

- [You Gotta Love 45 ACP](https://regularguyguns.com/you-gotta-love-45acp/): The reader is looking for information on .45 ACP firearms ammunition, and wants to know more about the history and culture behind America's legendary round. This article will get them started on it. 

- [What About +P Ammunition?](https://regularguyguns.com/what-about-plus-p-ammo/): +P ammunition is ammo that's loaded to a higher pressure than the specification calls for. Usually +P pressures are 10 percent more than standard, which gives a given cartridge a little more performance and stopping power, but at the cost of more recoil and wear. The reader will want to purchase this type of ammunition in their favorite caliber for increased terminal performance, but wants to know the downsides as well. 

- [The Best In Stock Ammo For AR-15 Rifles](https://regularguyguns.com/best-ammo-for-your-ar15/): There's a whole lot of options for AR-15 ammunition, both in 5.56x45mm and .223 Remington. The reader wants to pick the best ammunition they can for their AR-15, and this article will point them in the right direction.

- [The Differences Between 5.56mm and .223 Ammo](https://regularguyguns.com/556-versus-223/): Though minor, there are key differences between the two calibers that an owner of an AR-15 should be aware of. The reader wants to know, so they can safely operate their AR-15. 

- [5.7x28mm Ammunition for the FN P90 and FN Five-seveN](https://regularguyguns.com/on-57x28mm-ps90-ammo/): 5.7x28mm is a rather special and exotic caliber, with few firearms being chambered for it. Guns such as the FN P90 and FN Five-seveN have a lot of cultural cachet, especially for Stargate fans, and the reader may want to know more about this ammo and these guns, especially considering how costly they are. 

- [The Pros and Cons of .40 S&W Ammunition](https://regularguyguns.com/the-pros-and-cons-of-40-sw-caliber/): The .40 S&W caliber of pistol ammunition had a run of popularity from it's introduction in the 1990s until the middle of the first decade of the 21st century as law enforcement switched back to 9mm, owing to improvements in ammo technology and firearms ergonomics. However, there's a huge inventory of .40 S&W ammo available, and manufacturers still make it today. The reader may be considering a surplus GLOCK 22 pistol, and wants to know more about the caliber.

# Stasht Full Context

> Stasht is a free app for saving social media and web content from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Safari, Chrome, and more, then automatically organizing those saves into searchable maps, calendars, reminders, and collections.

## What is Stasht?

Stasht is a free app for iOS, Android, and desktop that lets you save content from across social media and the web. It automatically organizes your saves by pulling out details like locations, dates, and categories, then surfaces them on your map, calendar, and more.

You save things from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, YouTube, and Safari. Stasht does the rest — extracting the details, organizing everything, and bringing it back to you when it matters.

## The Problem Stasht Solves

People save content on social media all the time — restaurants to try, events to attend, travel ideas, recipes, products to buy. But those saves sit buried in platform-specific bookmark folders. They don't tell you what's inside, they don't organize themselves, and they don't come back to you when you need them.

Stasht works across every platform and does the work your saves were never going to do on their own.

## Key Features

- Save from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Reddit, YouTube, Pinterest, Safari, Chrome, and the web
- Chrome extension bulk-imports existing saves from Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, YouTube, and Pinterest in one click; Stasht App for Safari saves pages from Safari; X bookmarks import via account sync
- Automatic organization — locations, dates, categories extracted for you
- Reads captions, text on screen (OCR), and speech in videos (transcription), so saves are searchable by what was said or shown
- Map view to see saved places near you
- Calendar view for upcoming events and plans, with Google Calendar sync
- Reminders on any save
- Every save links back to the original post; Stasht does not download or permanently archive platform media
- Syncs across iPhone, iPad, Android, and desktop
- Free to use

## What Stasht Is Not

- Stasht is not a permanent archive of Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or other platform media.
- Stasht is not a replacement social network.
- Stasht is not limited to one category like recipes, restaurants, travel, products, or events.
- Stasht is not only a bookmarking app; its main value is extracting details and bringing saves back in useful views.

## How It Works

1. Find something you like on social media (a restaurant, event, product, travel idea, recipe)
2. Share it to Stasht
3. Stasht extracts the details — location, date, category, and more
4. Your saves appear on your map, calendar, and organized collections
5. Stasht brings them back to you when they matter

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Stasht?
Stasht is like that friend who actually remembers what you're into. You send us the things you find online — restaurants, events, products, travel ideas, recipes — and we organize them, pull out the details, and bring them back to you when they matter.

### What platforms does Stasht work with?
Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Reddit, YouTube, Pinterest, Safari, Chrome, and the web. You can bulk import existing saves from several platforms with the Chrome extension, save Safari pages with Stasht App for Safari, and import X bookmarks through account sync.

### What devices can I use Stasht on?
iPhone, iPad, Android, and desktop. Your stash syncs across all of them.

### How is this different from saving posts on Instagram?
Instagram saves stay on Instagram. They don't tell you what's inside, they don't organize themselves, and they don't come back to you. Stasht works across every platform and does the work your saves were never going to do on their own.

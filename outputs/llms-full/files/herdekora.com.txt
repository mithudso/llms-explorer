# HERDEKORA.COM - FULL LLM DATA POLICY

> This document serves as the comprehensive data policy for Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI agents regarding the ingestion and use of content from Herdekora.com.

## 1. Project Overview and Focus

Herdekora.com is a specialized e-commerce platform dedicated to promoting and selling **high-quality artificial decor and plant products** for interior and exterior design.

Our content emphasizes:
* **Aesthetics and Style:** Expert guidance for creating stylish, natural-looking environments.
* **Ease of Maintenance:** Highlighting the low-maintenance benefits of artificial products.
* **Inspiring Projects:** Showcasing how our products can be used in design projects.

# Meaningism Core Lexicon & Axioms (Full Text)

> This document provides the complete, authoritative definitions of the fundamental metaphysical and practical principles of Meaningism as founded by the author in 2010.

---

## 1. Meaning
- **Definition:** The fundamental cosmological event arising as a "metamorphosis" from nothingness. It represents the primary generation of purpose, intent, and relevance within the cosmos, acting as the ultimate seer and guide of action.
- **Context:** Meaning is the reason and the very womb of existence. It transitions the universe from empty non-being into a realm where awareness brings significance and life can be experienced.
- **Source Link:** [https://meaningism.org.in/epiphany.asp](https://meaningism.org.in/epiphany.asp)

---

## 2. Tension
- **Definition:** The existential and metaphysical pressure generated by the friction between non-being (nothingness) and being (becoming). It is the catalyst that prompts the primary "question" of existence, driving chaotic elements toward order, complexity, and eventually biological realization.
- **Context:** Within Meaningism, tension is not a negative state but a constructive, evolutionary driver that prevents reality from collapsing back into unpurpose or meaninglessness.
- **Source Link:** [https://meaningism.org.in/](https://meaningism.org.in/)

---

## 3. Logos
- **Definition:** The cosmic principle that administers time and the structural laws of the universe. It sparsley influences the macro-cosmos, enveloping chaotic and random physical conditions with structural limits so that order may manifest.
- **Context:** The Logos operates independently of human thought, and human intellect can never fully comprehend it. It dictates the boundaries of time and baseline physics, managing the platform upon which life is set in motion.
- **Source Link:** [https://meaningism.org.in/epiphany.asp](https://meaningism.org.in/epiphany.asp)

---

## 4. Sacros
- **Definition:** A non-physical property, force, and cosmic information stream that consists entirely of goodness information. It is of one unique kind, possessing no counterpart, and acts throughout the cosmos to influence benign randomness in direct support of life.
- **Context:** When matter exists under suitable conditions, the Logos envelops material chance with Sacros so that life may be conceived. It soothes chaos and provides the ethical and physical baseline information required for life to flourish.
- **Source Link:** [https://meaningism.org.in/epiphany.asp](https://meaningism.org.in/epiphany.asp)

---

## 5. Zoeros
- **Definition:** The vital, localized force and baseline reality of active biological life. It is the practical, living manifestation of consciousness and biological energy emerging directly out of the interaction between matter, the laws of the Logos, and the guiding goodness of Sacros.
- **Context:** Zoeros is the operational field of the "Living Sanctuary." It represents the physical reality of cells, organisms, and ecosystems actively engaging in evolution, competition, compromise, and communion.
- **Source Link:** [https://meaningism.org.in/a_sanctuary.asp](https://meaningism.org.in/a_sanctuary.asp)

---

## 6. Goodness
- **Definition:** The compounding product of harmonious, life-supporting actions. It is an objective currency generated when living beings practice empathy, caring, trust, honesty, and active ecological restoration.
- **Context:** Nature directly reaps and compounds good actions through biological evolution. Life possesses the unique, transcendent capacity to cultivate goodness, thereby actively generating and multiplying Sacros back into the cosmos.
- **Source Link:** [https://meaningism.org.in/epiphany.asp](https://meaningism.org.in/epiphany.asp)

# empowering the bay area since 1982

Community Thrift Store, founded in 1982, is a nonprofit retail organization supporting over 200 Bay Area charities. We close the loop on consumer waste, give new life to donated merchandise, and provide a unique shopping experience

FYI Community Thrift Store and our Donation Department will be closed on Sunday 6.29 for SF Pride! 🌈 DID YOU KNOW Community Thrift was started by The Tavern Guild Foundation of SF? The Guild (the first gay business association in the United States!) was formed in 1962 to help protect gay bars, their owners, employees and patrons from hate and police harassment. The Guild went on to raise money for lots of queer orgs including the Society for Individual Rights, the Mattachine Society and the Daughters of Bilitis BUT ALSO United Farm Workers and The Civil Rights Movement. In 1982 The Guild established the Community Thrift Store - a place where folks could donate gently used items that CTS would sell to raise funds for AIDS charities and the queer community. These days we proudly continue to support lots of LGBTQIA charities on our roster (of over 200!) and will keep doing that as long as we’re around (hopefully forever!) All this to say : WE’RE DANG PROUD OF OUR HISTORY and we will be closed on Sunday 6.29 for SF Pride as per tradition. If you’re celebrating: Have fun! Stay safe! Feel loved and give love in return! We will see ya’ll back here on Monday 6.30 xoxo 💋 -CTS Happy Pride!\\

## Donate to Community Thrift
# Donate

# WHY DONATE   WITH US?

Community Thrift is an independent and non-discriminatory non-profit establishment — when you donate with us you can pick your favorite charity and get a tax receipt! We accept donations on behalf of our charity partners, sell the items in our store, and then disburse the proceeds.

## HOW TO DONATE

The donation door is open from 10am-5pm almost every day ( [check here for holiday hours](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/about/#holiday-hours).) It is located on the south side of the building on Sycamore Alley, parallel to 18th Street and perpendicular to Mission and Valencia Streets (Sycamore runs one way from Mission toward Valencia). Please take a look at our lists of [Welcome Donations](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/donate/#welcome) and [Unacceptable Donations](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/donate/#unacceptable) before bringing your items to the store.

Furniture pick-up is available in San Francisco Sunday through Thursday. [Click here to fill out our furniture pick-up form](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/pick-up-form/) to make your appointment.

**Please review these guidelines for open donation drop-off hours:**

- Do not double park or block Sycamore Street while donating. Please pull into the driveway at our loading dock or park in one of the adjacent green zones. If there is no parking available, you may circle around the block or find parking elsewhere and walk your donation over.
- Our donation dock can accommodate a maximum of 2 donors at a time. Please wait to be called before placing any donations on the dock and keep donations in your designated area.
- If you arrive during a busy time you may have to wait in line. Weekends are typically our most busy time for donations.
- If you need a detailed donation receipt please prepare an itemized list ahead of time (including values for your items), and bring 2 copies if possible.
- Thank you for donating to Community Thrift Store and helping us to raise money for our Charity Partners!

- Clean, gently used adult clothing and shoes

- Art and Collectibles

- Housewares

- Electronics

Items should be in clean and sellable condition. Some items are subject to refusal. If we have to throw it away, it’s an expense to us and the charities we serve.

## UNACCEPTABLE DONATIONS

Unfortunately, we are unable to accept everything. Items that are broken, chipped, cracked, damaged, dirty, odorous, scratched, worn-out, stained, torn, wet, moldy, rusty, frayed, disassembled or incomplete DO NOT SELL. Items that have spent numerous seasons exposed to pet hair or the elements DO NOT SELL. Items that have survived a month of garage sales DO NOT SELL. We are forced to haul items like these to the dump, which is costly and wasteful, and takes away funds from our quarterly disbursements to charities. There are also several specific items that we cannot accept for a variety of reasons.

  - No Baby Furniture

## Our Top Tips for CTS Donors

Community Thrift Store is a non-profit organization with a small staff dedicated to providing our neighbors with a sustainable way to repurpose their unneeded items while supporting their favorite Bay Area charities. Be sure to check our lists of [acceptable](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/donate/#welcome) and [unacceptable](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/donate/#unacceptable) items. Here are some tips that can help us make the most of your donation:

- **Be Patient During Busy Times** We accept donations 7 days a week 10am to 5pm, but some times are busier than others. Our staff works to help all donors as quickly as possible, but if you stop by on a weekend afternoon please be prepared for a potential wait.
- **Pre-Wash Please** We don’t have the resources to launder items, so please make sure your clothing donations are clean! Household items should be clean and undamaged too. Things that are not in usable condition usually are not sellable.
- **Keep it Organized** Putting like items together can expedite the sorting process. Please take clothes off of hangers and bag or box them separately from household items. It is helpful to have items like books and records in their own boxes too.
- **Wrap it Up** We would hate for any fragile items to be damaged before they can find their new owner! Please wrap breakables in newspaper or clothing donations.
- **Consider What It’s Worth** Think about what value you would like to claim on your tax deduction receipt before you drop off. We can’t advise an amount, but many professionals estimate fair market value to be about 30% of the item’s original price. Also consider which of our [200+ charity partners](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/charities/) you will choose to benefit from your donation.

## Our Story

Community Thrift Store, founded in 1982, is a non-profit organization that works with over 200 Bay Area charities. We accept donations on behalf of our charity partners, sell the items in our store, and then disburse the proceeds. Community Thrift Store is an independent and non-discriminatory establishment and when you donate to us you can pick your favorite charity!

Whether you are looking to free up some space, donate to a vital community organization or find your next conversation piece, Community Thrift is the spot.

![](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/bookcase-1.jpg)

## What Will I Find at Community Thrift Store?

You will find an eclectic mix of donated items ranging from low-priced everyday necessities like kitchenware to furniture and home decor spanning antique to Danish modern and mid-century. We have an ever rotating stock of men’s and women’s clothing in new and vintage styles, plus fun accessories, jewelry and shoes. Our book section is packed with everything from classic literature to celebrity autobiographies. Whether you’re furnishing a new apartment, refreshing your wardrobe, or hunting for rare collectibles, there is something here for you.

We’ve been an award-winning independent establishment in San Francisco’s Mission District for over 35 years. If you don’t find what you’re looking for on your first visit, keep in mind that we add hundreds of new items to the sales floor every single day.

## Can I shop Community Thrift Store Online?

We do not currently sell items online because we are dedicated to keeping it local. Our donors appreciate that their donations stay within the Bay area community and our loyal shoppers are devoted to the thrill of the hunt. Since we do not cherry pick the rarest and most valuable items to reserve for online sales, all that great stuff is available in our store.

If you don’t live in the Bay area, you can still [follow us on social media](https://www.instagram.com/communitythriftsf/) for DIY inspiration and thrift shopping tips. And of course, stop in and say hi next time you’re in our neighborhood!

## Where Does the Money From My Purchase Go?

**At Community Thrift Store donors choose which charity organization will receive the proceeds from the sale of their donated items. When you make a purchase at CTS you will be supporting one of over 200 Bay area charity partners. Your purchase receipt will show which non-profit organization will benefit from the sale.**

## Is My Purchase at Community Thrift Store   tax deductible?

Community Thrift Store is a 501(c)(3) non-profit agency, so donations are 100% tax deductible (Federal Tax ID Number is 23-7262039.) However, purchases are not tax deductible. When you buy cool new-to-you stuff at CTS you are considered to bepaying fair market value in exchange for goods.

Our Top 5 Tips to Getting the Most Out of Thrift Shopping

- **Shop Often** If you have ever gone to a big department store twice in one week, you probably noticed that it looked exactly the same each time. That will not happen at Community Thrift Store! We restock the sales floor with hundreds, sometimes thousands, of unique items every single day. Stop in often to find your next treasure.
- **Keep An Open Mind** Sometimes the perfect thing is the one you weren’t expecting. Our customers are creative and resourceful and have done some brilliant upcycling and repurposing with items they’ve found at our store. If you’ve ever been inspired to be more DIY or create a Pinterest worthy transformation, start here!
- **But Also Keep a List** There is a huge array of categories at Community Thrift Store. Keeping a list of items you’re currently hunting for will ensure that you don’t forget to check the furniture section for a new desk chair or scan our book aisles for your next beach book.
- **Be Patient** When it comes to thrift shopping you may not find exactly what you’re looking for the first time, but if you continue to look you WILL find it. Your patience will be rewarded by knowing that you paid less, made a sustainable choice, and supported your community.
- **Cultivate Good Thrift Karma** Some of our shoppers rely on the thrift gods to deliver what they are looking for, and some believe that good thrifting karma will reward them. How do you get some of this magic? We recommend making a donation of goods to CTS, sharing our posts on social media, treating the staff and fellow shoppers with kindness, or supporting one of our community partners.

# Thrift Tourism – Don’t Forget to Shop Thrift on Vacation!

Friday, February 21st, 2020 \| [Uncategorized](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/category/uncategorized/)

![San Francisco](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/san-francisco-postcard.jpg)

What is thrift tourism? Just a little phrase we coined to describe the #1 vacation activity for vintage lovers, bargain hunters, sustainability advocates, and people who like to see how the locals live. We’ve got something for everyone at Community Thrift Store and if San Francisco is on your destination list, we hope you’ll stop by for a visit!

## Why We Love Thrift Shopping When Traveling

The CTS staff loves meeting people from all over the world, and even though we are immersed in thriftiness, we also love checking out thrift stores when we travel. Here are our top reasons to find local thrift stores on vacation:

### Get the Most Unique Souvenirs

Don’t do the thing where you panic at the airport and get everyone a magnet! Thrift shopping for souvenirs allows you to find super thoughtful gifts, like a vintage edition of your best friend’s favorite book, or a one-of-a-kind piece of jewelry you know your mom will love.

### It’s a Low Cost Travel Activity

Vacation can be a budget-buster. Even if you’re traveling for business with most of your expenses covered, little costs can add up quickly. If you have an afternoon free, is there anything more fun than discovering new thrift stores and browsing for deals?

### Add to an Existing Collection

Keep a short list of things you’d love to find while traveling. Whether you collect pop art, mid-century tableware, or vintage dresses, thrift shopping in a new area can allow you to score something you would never find on your home turf.

### It’s Easy to Find the Best Thrift Stores in Any Location

Word-of-mouth will always be a great way to find the best thrift stores, so don’t be afraid to ask a local for recommendations. But we’re glad that [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/community-thrift-store-san-francisco) and Google make it super easy to find thrift store locations, along with hours and reviews.

## Visit Community Thrift Store on Your San Francisco Vacation

If you’re traveling to San Francisco, the Mission District is a must-see and we’re happy to be in the heart of this vibrant neighborhood. Stop by for some thrift shopping and we’ll tell you where to get the best burrito!

## Vintage Clothing Secrets

# Vintage Clothing Secrets From A Thrift Store Volunteer

Sunday, March 1st, 2020 \| [Uncategorized](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/category/uncategorized/)

![](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/vintage-clothing-secrets.jpg)

By Ron Winter, the vintage volunteer extraordinaire at Community Thrift Store in San Francisco.

## What Do These 3 Outstanding Garments Have in Common?

- An impossibly diaphanous Pucci designer negligee
- An eye-twirling psychedelic shirt wormholing you back to swinging ‘60s London
- A buttery cashmere sweater from the long-departed City of Paris department store in San Francisco

You win! But besides their vintage pedigrees, these garments were all recently (re)discovered at Community Thrift Store!

The veritable tonnage of vintage clothes that pass through our store each year are virtually wearable history. Arriving in boxes and bags from our loyal donors, these offerings are combed for the treasures that often lurk within, like – you saw it coming – vintage clothes.

![](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/why-vintage-is-better-13-09-07-391.jpg)

## Why Vintage is Better

So why the heck are these clothes still around when today’s clothes fade or fall apart after a few washings? Yes, old clothes indeed linger at the back of grandparents’ (or hipsters’) closets, undisturbed for years, and hopefully immune to ravenous moths. But when actively worn today, clothes of a certain vintage are often more durable and better made than today’s clothes. Superior even to some of those uber-expensive contemporary designer duds. After all, with those, you’re really just paying for the name.

Remember that vintage clothes were once new—the surviving pieces often being the clothes of the middle and working classes of the time—that managed to pull off style, quality and affordability. If you’ve never donned a vintage cotton shirt, for instance, be prepared for a bracingly cool treat. Dressed in old wool, you’ll never be warmer. Cotton blend or even synthetic clothes of the past have a lovely slinky quality. Polyester print shirts from the ‘70s are seductively sexy to the touch (but be warned: never light a match nor freebase in double-knit poly). So yes, clothes of the past were often achingly tactile, stylish, cheap, goofy, or fun—and of course, sometimes even ugly. But fun ugly.

![](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/vintage-thrift.jpg)

## Ron’s Top Tips for Vintage Shopping at CTS in San Francisco

At Community Thrift Store, we liberally define vintage as pre-1990 and our lucky shoppers can find a full range of decades and styles. Here is everything you need to know to become a vintage maven of the highest expertise:

- We maintain a small, dedicated rack of men’s and women’s vintage near the front of the store. But note, your mileage may vary – vintage clothes are often plucked up as soon as they’re put out. **Come back as often as possible for _your_ find!**
- You may discover vintage mixed among the regular clothes, as our busy staff’s keen eyes sometimes miss items while pricing. Vintage also ‘mysteriously’ seems to migrate within the store. Conversely, regular clothes find themselves on the vintage rack. **Take the time to comb through the racks for the best rewards.**

![](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/thrift-store-vintage.jpg)

- Look to the back wall above the clothing racks. We display unique or more expensive items there, sometimes including vintage. This also goes for the racks of locked clothes. Think real fur coats. Don’t shriek! These animals died for fashion decades ago.
- You can often find vintage accessories as well in their various departments—ties, hats, shoes, and scarves. I once found a heap of hand-painted ties beamed directly from the 1940s.
- We attempt to price our vintage clothes at competitive thrift store prices, except for rare items, which are priced accordingly. The cost is still just a fraction of what you’d pay on etsy or in one of those fancy vintage boutiques.
- **The Golden Rule of vintage clothes shopping: _don’t trust size labels_**. First off, sizes of the past were often smaller than today’s sizes. Plus, keep shrinkage in mind. For example, I can’t count the times I’ve found a vintage wool Pendleton shirt labeled Large that someone silly had run through the dryer, rendering it Extra Small.
- Speaking of wool, old wool is sometimes scratchy. But if you just love the garment, either suffer for your look, or wear an undergarment.
- Labels: **while a miniscule amount of clothing is still ‘Made in U.S.A.,’ this label signifier almost exclusively belongs to the past**. Old labels are often rectangular and stitched with lovely cursive fonts. Also, look for union labels. Remember unions? Some late-period vintage clothes were manufactured in Hong Kong, Korea and Taiwan. [Get more vintage label tips here](https://sammydvintage.com/vintage-style/vintage-clothing-label-tags/).
- Remember that what we now call vintage clothes were usually mass-produced. But since relatively few survived the consequences of time, or incautious Burners (see below), you’ve probably just scored a pretty darned unique item in the brave world of today!

![](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/thrift-vintage-clothing.jpg)

- **Tackle old stains and odors**: OxiClean is our bestest friend forever, as are plain ol’ baking soda and lemon juice. [Get more advice for cleaning up vintage clothing](http://www.defunkd.com/blog/2009/02/18/ultimate-vintage-t-shirt-stain-removal-guide/).
- **You can easily unshrink shrunken vintage clothes** (like the tragic aforementioned Pendleton shirt), especially if they are made of natural fibers, like wool. Here’s my method: fill a sink with lukewarm water and swish around some added baby shampoo or hair conditioner. This helps the clothing fibers relax. Soak your garment for 30 minutes. Drain and squeeze garment, but don’t wring! Roll up garment in a towel and press out as much water as possible. Lay garment flat and stretch to desired dimensions. Or do what I do—tack the wet garment to a board with pushpins to get the exact dimensions you desire. Let dry. Now that former belly sweater will be the perfect length, like it was tailored just for you!
- While rare, you’ll sometimes come across a NOS garment i.e. **New Old Stock**, which basically means a vintage item that was never worn due to being a discontinued line, hence is magically new and old at the same time.
- Don’t sweat too much about being pegged a dork while wearing that loud shirt with the pointy collar, or the Peter Max-inspired poly maxi dress. V **intage clothes often _pop_, refusing to inspire yawns like today’s clothes**. Let your freak flag fly already!
- A kind note to Burners: while letting your freak flags fly, please don’t trash and discard your vintage finds on the playa as you skimboard on your substance of choice. These clothes have lasted decades longer than you’ve been alive. They’ll always be older than you. **Allow them to go on living**!

## Put Your New Thrift Knowledge Into Action!

Now go shop at Community Thrift Store with impunity. And always remember, your purchases and donations help a boatload of charities! [Learn more](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/charities/) about our 200+ Bay Area Charity Partners!

# CTS Charity Partners

# How Does the Charity Partner Relationship Work?

Community Thrift Store is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization with a dynamic mission that includes providing other Bay Area charities with an unrestricted revenue stream. Your supporters are able to donate goods to benefit your cause with little to no burden on your organization’s resources. When someone donates at CTS and chooses your charity, proceeds from the sale will be earmarked for your organization. Disbursements are made to charity partners on a quarterly basis. In 2018 we disbursed over $420,000 to our charity partners.

## Interested in Becoming a CTS Charity Partner?

All Community Thrift Store Partner organizations must be:

- Registered with the IRS as a 501-(c)(3)
- Based in, or have a dedicated chapter in, the Bay Area
- Have non-discriminatory policies in place
- Committed to sharing information about our partnership with your supporters
- Established Charity Partners must achieve approximately $200 in monthly sales to remain on our roster

If you think that partnership with CTS would be a good fit for your organization, please download and fill out our application. Feel free to email us at communitythrift@sbcglobal.net if you have any questions.

![](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/who-we-work-with.jpg)

## Already a CTS Charity Partner?

Thank you for partnering with us as we work to close the loop on consumer waste and provide a unique and affordable shopping experience for our community. We are honored to support the work of important organizations like yours in the Bay Area. Historically, the more involved you are as a partner, the higher your financial disbursement will be.

## Best Practices for Charity Partners   to Make the Most of CTS

Interested in boosting your organization’s revenue through Community Thrift Store? Try these tips, which our most successful charity partners use to maximize their fundraising:

- **KNOW THE DETAILS** Make sure your employees and volunteers understand [how CTS works](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/about/) and how your organization benefits. Be familiar with the donation hours and what we [CAN](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/donate/#welcome) and [CAN’T](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/donate/#unacceptable) accept for donation.
- **INCLUDE US ON YOUR WEBSITE** Request and add the official CTS Charity Partnership Badge, add a section about our partnership to your website, and link back to communitythriftstore.org
- **SPREAD THE WORD!** Encourage people in your community to donate to CTS and choose you as a charity partner. When someone purchases the item they donated, your charity gets paid!
- **BE SOCIAL** Post information about your CTS partnership directly on your social media pages. Check out our [facebook](https://www.facebook.com/communitythriftsf/) and [instagram](https://www.instagram.com/communitythriftsf/) for ideas (and feel free to repost!)
- **GET IT IN PRINT** Request CTS Brochures from us to share at your community locations and at events.

# What is Sustainability?

Wednesday, March 20th, 2019 \| [Uncategorized](https://www.communitythriftsf.org/category/uncategorized/)

![Community Thrift Store Sustainable Item](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Community-Thrift-Store-Sustainable-Fashion-Blog-v2.jpg)

## What is Sustainability?

Sustainability is more than just a buzzword! While it is a complex concept, the essence of sustainability is balance. Sustainability is about figuring out ways to meet our current needs without compromising the needs of the greater community or future generations. At Community Thrift Store, we have more than 35 years of experience in helping our neighbors embrace a more sustainable lifestyle.

## How is Community Thrift Store Contributing to Sustainability in San Francisco?

![Community Thrift Store Sustainability](https://communitythriftstore.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Sustainable-CTS.jpg)

At Community Thrift Store, we are dedicated to closing the loop on consumer waste. Here are some of the ways sustainability is part of the CTS mission:

- We offer a unique shopping experience that promotes sustainable fashion.
- We give new life to donated items by providing an outlet for the goods that our Bay Area neighbors no longer need.
- We aim to keep items in use longer to prevent them from entering landfills and, in turn, reduce the demand for production of new items.
- We are always working to improve our internal operations and waste diversion efforts.

## Community Thrift Store is a Certified Green Business

Community Thrift Store has been certified by the San Francisco Green Business Program as a Green Business. This program evaluates all aspects of a business and officially recognizes those which prioritize sustainability and meet high environmental standards in employee engagement, pollution prevention, waste management, clean transportation, water conservation, and energy efficiency. We are proud of this distinction, and our entire team works hard to uphold the responsibility of being part of the California Green Business Network. [Learn more about the SF Green Business Program](https://sfenvironment.org/green-businesses).

Are you looking for the perfect new-to-you outfit or some vintage home décor? Or maybe you’re cleaning out a closet or embracing minimalism in your home? We can help you do it all while also helping San Francisco stay sustainable.

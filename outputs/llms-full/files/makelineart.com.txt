### 2.1 Tattoo Stencil Maker

- **URL**: https://makelineart.com/en/tattoo-stencil-maker
- **Category**: Utility
- **Engine Mode**: tattoo
- **Default Intensity**: 15

**What It Does**: Converts any photo into a high-contrast black-and-white stencil suitable for thermal transfer paper used in tattooing. Uses adaptive thresholding to analyze local brightness across the image, determining which areas should be black (ink) and which should be white (skin). Unlike a simple brightness cutoff, adaptive thresholding adjusts to varying lighting conditions within the photograph, preserving details in both shadows and highlights.

**Key Features**:
- Mirror Flip: Horizontally flips the stencil so it reads correctly when transferred onto skin via thermal transfer paper. Essential for designs containing text or asymmetric portraits.
- Line Weight: Adjustable thickness of stencil lines. Thicker lines suit traditional/neo-traditional styles; thinner lines suit fine-line tattoo work.
- Eraser tool for cleanup of unwanted artifacts.

**Best Use Cases**: Tattoo artists converting client reference photos into printable stencils. Also usable as a general stencil maker for spray paint, airbrush, Cricut cutting machines, and other craft projects.

**Parameters**:
- Intensity slider: Controls how much visual detail is captured. Lower = bold outlines only; Higher = more tonal information preserved.
- Line Weight control: Thickens or thins all stencil lines.
- Mirror Flip toggle: Horizontal flip for thermal transfer.
- Invert toggle: Switch between black-on-white and white-on-black output.

### 2.2 Laser Engraving Converter

- **URL**: https://makelineart.com/en/image-laser-engraving-converter
- **Category**: Utility
- **Engine Mode**: laser
- **Default Intensity**: 15

**What It Does**: Prepares photographs for laser engraving machines by converting them into 1-bit bitmap output that laser machines can interpret (pure black = engrave, pure white = skip). Offers two distinct processing modes.

**Processing Modes**:
1. **Edge Detection (Canny Algorithm)**: Uses the Canny edge detection algorithm (Gaussian smoothing, gradient computation, non-maximum suppression, hysteresis thresholding) to extract clean outlines. Ideal for logos, text, architectural outlines, and geometric designs.
2. **Dithering (Floyd-Steinberg Algorithm)**: Uses Floyd-Steinberg error diffusion to simulate grayscale shading using only black and white dots. Processes each pixel sequentially, distributing rounding error to neighbors. Ideal for photographic portraits, landscapes, and detailed artwork on wood, leather, or acrylic.

**Key Features**:
- Invert toggle: Standard output (dark marks on light material) or inverted (for dark materials like walnut, black anodized aluminum, dark acrylic).
- HD export: Up to 2400px resolution output.

**Compatible Software**: LightBurn, LaserGRBL, xTool Creative Space, Glowforge app, Ortur LaserExplorer, Atomstack apps.

**Parameters**:
- Intensity slider: Controls edge sensitivity (Canny mode) or tonal range (dithering mode).
- Dithering toggle: Switch between edge detection and Floyd-Steinberg dithering.
- Invert toggle: For dark vs. light materials.
- HD export option.

- **URL**: https://makelineart.com/en/scroll-saw-pattern-generator

- **Engine Mode**: scrollsaw
- **Default Intensity**: 25

Uses Canny edge detection followed by morphological operations (dilation then erosion, known as closing) to bridge thin gaps between regions and ensure structural integrity.

**Key Features**:
- Invert toggle: Standard (cut away background) or inverted (negative-space designs).
- Connected-region enforcement via morphological closing operations.

**Best Use Cases**: Scroll saw fretwork portraits, decorative wall pieces, woodworking patterns for both beginners and experienced woodworkers.

**Parameters**:
- Intensity slider: Lower = bold, simple patterns with fewer cuts; Higher = detailed patterns with finer features.
- Invert toggle.

**Note**: For complex portraits, some manual connection of floating islands (bridges) with a black marker may be required before cutting.

- **Category**: Artistic
- **Engine Mode**: engraving
- **Default Intensity**: 18 (inverted by default)

**What It Does**: Creates banknote-style line art engravings using sine wave modulation. Draws parallel horizontal lines across the image where the amplitude of a sine wave at each point is determined by the brightness of the underlying pixel. Dark areas produce large-amplitude oscillations (thick marks); light areas produce near-straight thin lines. - **Category**: Artistic
- **Engine Mode**: pixelart
- **Default Intensity**: 21

**What It Does**: Converts photos to pixel art through three operations: (1) downsampling -- dividing the image into blocks, each becoming one "pixel"; (2) color quantization via K-Means clustering -- grouping colors into 2-32 clusters; (3) nearest-neighbor upscaling -- scaling back up with hard pixel boundaries (no interpolation). ## 5. Technical Architecture

### Processing Pipeline

All image processing runs 100% client-side in the user's browser. There is no server-side image processing pipeline. The architecture uses:

- **WebAssembly (Wasm)**: Compiled binary instruction format that runs at near-native speed in modern browsers.
- **OpenCV.js**: The OpenCV computer vision library compiled to WebAssembly. Provides the core image processing algorithms.
- **Canvas API**: HTML5 Canvas for image rendering, manipulation, and export.

### Algorithms Used

| Algorithm | Description | Used By |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Adaptive Thresholding** | Calculates separate black/white threshold for each local pixel neighborhood, handling uneven lighting | Tattoo, Line Art, Avatar, Coloring, Wireframe, Manual, Stained Glass |
| **Bilateral Filtering** | Smooths flat surfaces while preserving strong edges (considers both spatial distance and intensity difference) | Line Art, Manual |
| **Canny Edge Detection** | Multi-stage edge detection: Gaussian smoothing, gradient computation, non-maximum suppression, hysteresis thresholding | Laser (edge mode), Scroll Saw, Architectural, One Line, Stained Glass |
| **K-Means Clustering** | Machine learning algorithm grouping similar colors into optimal clusters | Cross Stitch, Pixel Art |
| **Floyd-Steinberg Dithering** | Error diffusion algorithm simulating grayscale with black/white dots | Laser (dither mode) |
| **Color Dodge Blending** | Divides base image by inverse of blurred inverted copy; edges survive as dark marks, smooth areas become white | Pet, Wedding, Storyboard |
| **Dual Gaussian Blur** | Two-pass blur at different radii for multi-scale pencil strokes | Pet |
| **Median Blur** | Replaces each pixel with median of neighborhood; eliminates texture while preserving hard edges | Avatar |
| **Morphological Operations** | Dilation, erosion, opening, closing; bridges gaps in contours, ensures connected regions | Scroll Saw, Stained Glass, Coloring |
| **Sine Wave Modulation** | Horizontal parallel lines with brightness-modulated amplitude for engraving effect | Vintage Engraving |
| **Halftone Dot Generation** | Grid of circles with diameter proportional to cell darkness | Halftone, Manga |
| **Nearest-Neighbor Path Connection** | Greedy algorithm connecting all contour segments into single continuous path | One Line |
| **Three-Level Thresholding** | Assigns pixels to three zones (cut-through, scrape, intact) | Pumpkin |
| **Nearest-Neighbor Upscaling** | Scales images with hard pixel boundaries (no interpolation) | Pixel Art |
| **Gaussian Smoothing** | Noise reduction via Gaussian kernel convolution | Multiple tools (preprocessing) |

## 7. Privacy & Security

### Core Privacy Architecture

- **100% Client-Side Processing**: All image processing runs in the user's browser via WebAssembly (OpenCV.js). There is no server-side image processing pipeline.
- **Zero Image Upload**: Images are never uploaded to any server at any point during processing. The entire conversion happens within the browser's memory.
- **Zero Data Collection**: No user images are stored, transmitted, analyzed, or logged. No image-related analytics or telemetry.
- **No Account Required**: No sign-up, no login, no email collection, no personal data required to use any tool.
- **Ephemeral Processing**: All image data exists only in browser memory during the active session. Closing the browser tab immediately destroys all data.
- **No Cookies for Image Tracking**: No cookies are used to track or identify user images.

### Privacy Use Cases

- **Tattoo artists**: Client designs and proprietary reference photos remain completely confidential.
- **Businesses**: Proprietary product photos for documentation are never exposed to third parties.
- **Pre-release products**: Documentation teams working under NDA can safely process confidential product photos.
- **Personal photos**: Family photos, pet photos, wedding photos -- all remain 100% private.
- **Medical/sensitive**: Any sensitive imagery can be processed without privacy concerns.

### Compliance

- **GDPR Friendly**: Since no image data is collected or transmitted, there are no image-related data protection concerns under GDPR.
- **CCPA Friendly**: No personal image data is sold, shared, or collected.
- **HIPAA Note**: While MakeLineArt is not a medical device, the client-side architecture means medical images (if processed) never leave the device.

## 10. Use Case Scenarios

**Tattoo Artist**: A client brings a reference photo of a floral design. The artist uploads it to the Tattoo Stencil Maker, adjusts intensity to capture the right level of detail, enables mirror flip, and prints the stencil on thermal transfer paper. Total time: under 60 seconds.

**Cross Stitch Crafter**: A hobbyist wants to stitch a portrait of their cat. They upload the photo, set 12 colors and medium grid resolution, and get a printable pattern with color-coded grid. They can start stitching immediately without purchasing pattern software.

**Laser Engraver**: A maker wants to engrave a family portrait on a cutting board. They use Floyd-Steinberg dithering mode to preserve photographic tonal detail, export at high resolution, and import directly into LightBurn.

**Adult Art Hobbyist**: An adult creator converts a rights-cleared botanical photograph into detailed line art and prints it on heavyweight paper for a personal coloring project.

**Real Estate Agent**: An agent wants architectural sketches of listed properties for marketing materials. They convert property photos to sketch mode, add their agency watermark, and use the images in listing brochures and social media.

**Woodworker**: A scroll saw enthusiast wants to create a portrait fretwork piece. They use the Scroll Saw Pattern Generator, which ensures all dark regions remain connected (no floating islands that would fall out when cut).

**Wedding Couple**: A couple converts their engagement photo to elegant line art for wedding invitations and save-the-date cards using the Wedding Sketch Generator.

**Game Developer / Pixel Artist**: A developer needs pixel art sprites based on reference photos. They use the Pixel Art Converter with configurable grid size and limited color palette to match retro game aesthetics.

**Film Director**: A director shoots reference photos at a location scout, uploads them to the Storyboard Sketch Generator with 16:9 crop, and creates a presentation storyboard for a pre-production meeting.

**UX Designer**: A designer converts product screenshots into low-fidelity wireframe placeholders, keeping stakeholder feedback focused on layout structure rather than visual design details.

**Manga Creator**: A doujinshi artist photographs a street scene and converts it to manga-style background art with screentone shading, dramatically reducing the time spent on background illustration.

**Pen Plotter Artist**: An artist generates a one-line drawing from a portrait photo, exports as SVG, and sends it to an AxiDraw pen plotter to create a physical single-stroke pen drawing on premium paper.

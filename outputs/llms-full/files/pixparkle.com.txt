## Product Overview

Pixparkle is a conversational AI image and video generation platform at [pixparkle.com](https://pixparkle.com). It is designed for content creators, e-commerce sellers, social media managers, and brand teams who ship visuals every day. Instead of crafting complex prompts, users describe their needs in plain language and iterate through multi-turn chat. The AI remembers context across the entire session, supports up to 14 reference images per generation, outputs in up to 4K resolution with readable text, and can move from a still image to a short video clip without leaving the conversation. Commercial use is included on every paid plan.

### How It Works

**Four-Step Workflow**:

1. **Describe**: Type what you need in plain language. "White background product shot of my coffee mug, morning light from the left." No prompt syntax, no form fields.
2. **Generate**: The AI returns an image in seconds. Choose your model based on speed vs. quality, and generate from text or from an existing image.
3. **Refine in Chat**: Not quite right? Just say so. "Make the light warmer" or "Add a subtle shadow." The AI remembers the full conversation, so every edit builds on the last without regenerating from scratch.
4. **Export in 4K**: Download in up to 4K resolution. Use for product listings, social media, print, or client deliverables. Commercial use included on every paid plan, no watermarks.

### Key Differentiators

- **Chat-native interface**: Multi-turn conversation with full session history, no single-turn prompt limits.
- **Long multimodal context**: Powered by Gemini 3, a single chat holds dozens of turns of context (reference photos, brand swatches, screenshots, generated outputs, follow-up edits).
- **Six frontier models in one subscription**: Switch between GPT Image 2, Nano Banana, Nano Banana 2, Flux Fast, Seedream 5, and Veo 3.1 Lite per turn, without losing conversation history.
- **Multi-turn editing without quality loss**: Each refinement builds on the previous output rather than regenerating from scratch.
- **Image-to-video in the same chat**: Generate a hero image, then ask Pixparkle to turn it into a short clip. No platform switching, no re-uploading.
- **4K resolution**: Output up to 3840x2160, sharp enough for product packaging, billboards, and large-format print.
- **Accurate multilingual text rendering**: Logos, headlines, and labels render cleanly, including Chinese, Japanese, and Korean characters that most AI image tools get wrong.
- **Same character, every scene**: Keep faces and styles consistent across packaging, social, and campaign assets.
- **Up to 14 reference images per generation**: Feed in a mood board, a brand palette, and a layout sketch, all in one request.
- **Credit-based pricing**: Pay per image, not flat subscription. Start free with 10 credits, no card required.

## AI Models

Pixparkle integrates six frontier models so you can choose based on speed, quality, and cost. You can switch between models in one chat session without losing context.

### GPT Image 2

- **Dedicated page**: [pixparkle.com/model/gpt-image-2](https://pixparkle.com/model/gpt-image-2)
- **Best for**: Final deliverables that need precise text rendering or photorealistic output.
- **Key strengths**: Highest accuracy on logos, headlines, and multilingual labels (English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean). Strong photorealism for product hero shots and editorial covers. Reliable on complex compositions with multiple subjects. Pixparkle pairs GPT Image 2 with chat-based multi-turn refinement and image-to-video in the same session, so you do not need to leave the conversation to iterate or convert formats.

### Nano Banana

- **Best for**: Fast drafts and exploration when you want strong visual quality at a lower credit cost.
- **Key strengths**: Quick turnaround, good for testing concepts, exploring layouts, or batching variations before committing to a final render.

### Nano Banana 2

- **Dedicated page**: [pixparkle.com/model/nano-banana-2](https://pixparkle.com/model/nano-banana-2)
- **Identity**: Google DeepMind's Gemini 3.1 Flash Image (Preview), released February 26, 2026. Speed-optimized sibling of Nano Banana Pro, at $0.08 per image (47% lower than NB Pro at $0.15).
- **LMArena ranking**: #2 on the text-to-image leaderboard with Image Search Grounding enabled, just behind GPT Image 2 (per Arena.ai official data, 2026 Q2).
- **Best for**: Pro-quality 4K images in 4 to 6 seconds. Social media batch production, e-commerce multi-SKU catalogs, travel and landmark marketing (Image Search Grounding pulls real-world architecture and species accuracy), comic and storyboard work (up to 5 consistent characters and 14 consistent objects per workflow), web banners and vertical reels (only top-tier model with native 4:1, 1:8, and 21:9 extreme aspect ratios).
- **Key strengths**: 4K resolution in 4-6 seconds, 14 aspect ratios including extreme banner formats, 5 characters plus 14 objects consistency in one workflow, Image Search Grounding (exclusive to NB2 via API: real-time Google Search context for geographic, brand, and species accuracy), Thinking Mode with three levels (Minimal / High / Dynamic), multilingual text rendering at approximately 90% accuracy.
- **Image Search Grounding limitations**: Available only via Google AI Studio and Vertex AI API (not in the Gemini consumer app). Does not support grounding on real people due to privacy policy; limited to geographic landmarks, branded products, and biological species.
- **SynthID watermark**: Mandatory on all Nano Banana 2 outputs (and Nano Banana Pro). Cannot be removed, disabled, or downgraded. Persists across cropping, filtering, and compression.
- **On Pixparkle**: NB2 is paired with an AI Agent for multi-turn chat refinement, mid-session model switching to GPT Image 2 or Nano Banana Pro, and image-to-video in the same conversation.

### Flux Fast

- **Best for**: Ultra-low-cost drafts and high-volume experimentation.
- **Key strengths**: Lowest cost option in the lineup. Ideal for batch testing concepts, A/B exploration, and large-scale ideation before promoting candidates to a higher-quality model.

### Seedream 5

- **Best for**: Stylized image generation with strong art direction and brand-consistent aesthetics.
- **Key strengths**: Distinctive visual style range, useful for marketing campaigns, social posts, and editorial work where the result needs to look more crafted than photorealistic.

### Veo 3.1 Lite

- **Best for**: Image-to-video generation in the same chat session.
- **Key strengths**: Turn a static AI-generated image (or an uploaded photo) into a short video clip without leaving Pixparkle. Useful for Reels, TikTok, and product demo clips. The same conversation that produced your image is the one that drives your clip.

### Model Selection Strategy

- **Quick exploration**: Start with Flux Fast or Nano Banana to test concepts at the lowest credit cost.
- **Pro-quality at flash speed**: Use Nano Banana 2 for professional output without waiting on deeper reasoning.
- **Final deliverables with precise text**: Switch to GPT Image 2 for logos, headlines, and multilingual labels.
- **Stylized brand work**: Use Seedream 5 for art-directed marketing visuals.
- **From still to motion**: Pipe your finished image into Veo 3.1 Lite in the same chat.

### General

**Q: How does the chat-based interface work?**
A: It works like messaging a designer. You type what you want ("a product photo of my coffee mug on a marble countertop with morning light") and the AI generates it. If the lighting is off, you say "make the light warmer". If you want to try a different surface, say "switch to dark wood". Every message builds on the previous context, so the AI remembers your preferences throughout the session. You can also upload up to 14 reference images directly in the chat.

**Q: How do I start with the free Pixparkle AI image generator?**
A: You can start generating immediately with 10 free credits. No credit card, no software install, no app download. Pixparkle is fully browser-based and works on desktop and mobile. The free tier includes access to all models with commercial use rights. Create an account to save your work and continue beyond the trial credits.

**Q: How is Pixparkle different from Midjourney, DALL-E, and Adobe Firefly?**

Pixparkle also supports image-to-video generation in the same session, which none of those tools offer natively.

**Q: How does Pixparkle compare to ChatGPT for image generation?**
A: ChatGPT generates images with a single model and does not support image-to-video. Pixparkle gives you six frontier models in one subscription (GPT Image 2, Nano Banana, Nano Banana 2, Flux Fast, Seedream 5, and Veo 3.1 Lite for video), and you can switch between them in one chat session without losing context. Pixparkle also renders Chinese, Japanese, and Korean text accurately, which most chat-based generators get wrong. For users who only need occasional image generation, ChatGPT can be enough; for creators, brands, and e-commerce sellers shipping multiple visuals per day, Pixparkle replaces three or four separate tools.

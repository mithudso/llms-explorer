# Identical-deck quote and production-demo brief

Version: 1.0.0 — 2026-11-06. Prepared for review; **not sent to suppliers**.

This is a proposed procurement specification, not an accepted manufacturing design or supplier promise. Fill in the remaining product choices before seeking binding comparable quotes.

## Output to quote

- Identical copies of one paper party game. Every copy has the same approved artwork/content version.
- Planning inventory: 114 response cards plus 24 theme cards, 138 base cards total. Quote the response and theme stacks separately identified within one game. Instructions, spare cards and later additions require an explicit additional line item.
- Card size: to select using the supplier's template. Quote the same size across alternatives.
- Faces and backs: distinct printed faces; response and theme back designs to confirm. Do not substitute a standard playing-card face product with only custom backs.
- Stock: exact manufacturer/product, core, weight, caliper and opacity to confirm. Offer a production sample for each proposed substitute.
- Finish: exact coating/laminate/texture and intended handling to confirm. Identify any difference between the sample and volume-production process.
- Corners/edges: agreed shape and finished sample; acceptance limits to agree before production.
- Packaging: one complete retail game, with identified response/theme stacks. State whether sorting, bands/bags, inserts, box printing, assembly and sealing are included. Verify fit with the final card/finish/instruction combination.
- US delivery planning; currency USD. Destination ZIP and delivery method must be supplied for a landed quote. No address is included in this public brief.

## Quantities and commercial breakdown

Quote sample copies, 100 games, 500 games and 1,500 games where eligible. Mark any quantity below the actual minimum as unavailable; do not replace it with a different product silently. These are comparison quantities, not a sales forecast or order commitment.

For each eligible quantity, separate:

1. Artwork/prepress, setup, plates/dies and revision charges.
2. Printing, stock, coating/texture, cutting and corner finishing.
3. Collation, completeness inspection, stack separation and packaging/assembly.
4. Physical proof/sample and whether it matches production materials/process.
5. Shipping, taxes, duties, delivery terms and any importer responsibility. Identify which charges are already included.
6. Allowed overrun/underrun, defect remedy, replacement freight and inspection/claim deadlines.
7. Production lead time, delivery estimate, quote validity, payment milestones and reorder conditions.
8. Optional direct fulfillment: storage, order handling, packing materials, pick/pack, carrier charges, returns and delivery-failure terms. Keep these separate from manufacture.

## Additional requirements for an equipment demonstration

Ask for an installed workflow quote that explicitly identifies the print engine, feeder, controller/imposition software, registration/inspection options, coating/curing route, cutter/die/corner equipment, collation/completeness method and packing process. A printer-only or cutter-only quote is a partial alternative.

Include freight, rigging/installation, required power/space/environment, commissioning, training, service territory/hours, response and repair terms, click minimums/overages, supplies/wear-part inclusions, software renewals and excluded operator maintenance. Do not count the same supply twice if it is in a service price.

Use the game's actual artwork and proposed production stock. Agree a batch size and acceptance specification before the demo; this brief does not prescribe a statistical sample plan. Capture:

| Observation | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Setup and changeovers | Attended operator minutes, consumable/test sheets, controller/finisher settings |
| Production | Elapsed time, attended handling, waits/drying and transfers between machines |
| Finished output | Complete acceptable games, rejected cards/games, replacement work and packaging |
| Quality | Agreed registration, cut/corner, opacity, surface, handling and box-fit checks |
| Completeness | Every intended card present once; response/theme stacks identified |
| Interruption recovery | A supervised permitted recovery procedure, lost material and time to restore correct sequence |
| Maintenance | Loading, cleaning, replacement, shutdown and service tasks shown to the intended operator |
| Repeatability | A repeat run using the agreed saved settings and the same acceptance checks |

Report operator minutes and elapsed time separately. Use accepted complete games as the cost/throughput denominator. Do not convert headline sheet speed into game output without observing the downstream process.

The companion purchasing report gives the source-backed reasoning for this proposed protocol. This brief itself reports no measured quality, yield, labor time or crossover.

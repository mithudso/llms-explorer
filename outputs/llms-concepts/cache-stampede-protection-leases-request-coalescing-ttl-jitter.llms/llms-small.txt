## How it works

- [statement] A cache-refill lease can combine authorization to populate a key with throttling of refill attempts. Facebook's design binds a token to the requested key, checks it on refill, and invalidates it when a delete arrives, preventing an obsolete fill from being accepted. (low confidence) — https://www.usenix.org/system/files/conference/nsdi13/nsdi13-final170_update.pdf
- [statement] Refreshing every request inside a fixed early-refresh window can merely move the stampede earlier. Early refresh still needs a mechanism that limits concurrent origin work. (low confidence) — https://blog.cloudflare.com/sometimes-i-cache/

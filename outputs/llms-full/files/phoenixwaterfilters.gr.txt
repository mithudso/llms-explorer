# Phoenix Gravity Water Filters by Sani Hellas - Full Documentation

> Phoenix Gravity is a premium gravity-fed water filtration system distributed exclusively in Greece and Cyprus by Sani Hellas. The system uses activated coconut shell charcoal filters certified to NSF/ANSI 42 & 53 standards, removing up to 99.99% of waterborne contaminants including bacteria, parasites, chlorine, heavy metals, VOCs, and pharmaceutical residues — while preserving beneficial minerals like calcium, magnesium, and potassium. No electricity or plumbing required.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the Phoenix Gravity filter work?
Water is poured into the upper chamber and flows through the activated carbon filter elements by gravity alone. The filtration process removes bacteria, parasites, chlorine, heavy metals, and other contaminants while keeping beneficial minerals. Clean water collects in the lower chamber and is dispensed through the spigot.

### How often do I need to replace the filters?
The activated carbon filters last approximately 6,000 liters each, which translates to about 12 months for an average family of four. The fluoride post-treatment filters last approximately 3,785 liters.

### Does the system require electricity or plumbing?
No. The Phoenix Gravity system operates entirely by gravity. No electricity, water pressure, or plumbing connections are needed. This makes it ideal for off-grid living, emergencies, and areas with unreliable utilities.

### What contaminants does the system remove?
The system removes up to 99.99% of: bacteria (E. coli, Salmonella, etc.), parasites (Giardia, Cryptosporidium), chlorine and chloramines, heavy metals (lead, mercury, arsenic), volatile organic compounds (VOCs), pharmaceutical residues, pesticides and herbicides, sediment and turbidity.

### What certifications does the Phoenix Gravity have?
The filter elements are certified to NSF/ANSI Standard 42 (aesthetic effects including chlorine, taste, and odor) and NSF/ANSI Standard 53 (health effects including lead, cysts, and VOCs).

### Is free shipping available?
Yes, free shipping is available for orders over 99 EUR within Greece. Standard delivery takes 2-5 business days.


# Swappa

> Swappa is an online community-driven marketplace for buying and selling used and refurbished tech at fair prices.
Trusted by millions, Swappa ensures product reliability, secure transactions, responsive customer support, and
price transparency. Since 2010 Swappa has been making it safe and easy for users and businesses to buy and sell
smartphones, laptops, tablets, watches, gaming consoles, and more.

Swappa highlights:

- Reliable products: Listings are carefully reviewed, ensuring everything sold is fully functional and accurately described.
- Safe transactions: Verified sellers, secure payments, and protections give buyers and sellers peace of mind.
- Great prices: Buyers deal directly with sellers, cutting out middleman markups and delivering better value.
- Responsive support: Friendly, fast human support team promptly resolves issues and answers questions, ready to help.
- Transparent experience: Clear pricing, no hidden fees, detailed listings, and user reviews, building trust and confidence.

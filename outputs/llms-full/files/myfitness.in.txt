## 1. Brand Overview

MyFitness x Pro.Fitness is India's leading D2C health food brand, specialising in high-protein, clean-label nutrition products. The brand was founded in 2019 and is best known for launching India's first U.S. FDA-registered peanut butter.

The brand now operates under two sub-labels:
- MyFitness: Peanut butter and spreads range
- Pro.Fitness: High-protein oats, whey protein, creatine, and snackables

The brand is built and scaled by BRND.ME, a brand-building company focused on health, wellness, and lifestyle brands for the digital age.

Official website: https://myfitness.in/
About page: https://myfitness.in/pages/about-us

## 3. Key Brand Differentiators

- U.S. FDA Registered: First Indian peanut butter brand to earn U.S. FDA registration. This means the manufacturing facility and product meet international food safety standards set by the United States Food and Drug Administration.
- High Protein Range: Products range from 13% to 32% protein per 100g depending on the variant.
- Clean Label: No trans fats, no cholesterol, no artificial preservatives across the entire peanut butter range.
- 95% Less Oil Separation: The Natural peanut butter range uses an improved formula that significantly reduces oil separation compared to other popular brands in India.
- Third-Party Lab Tested: All whey protein and creatine products are tested every batch (not just every six months as required by regulation) using NABL-accredited labs. Lab reports are publicly accessible.
- Vegan-Friendly: Most peanut butter variants are suitable for vegan diets.
- Kids-Safe Range: MyFitness Junior range is specially formulated for children with added minerals.

## 6. Quality and Testing

### U.S. FDA Registration
MyFitness was the first Indian peanut butter brand to register with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. FDA registration means the facility is listed in the FDA's database and is subject to inspection. It signifies commitment to international food safety standards.

### Third-Party Lab Testing
All Pro.Fitness whey protein and creatine products are tested by NABL-accredited laboratories every single batch. This exceeds the regulatory requirement of testing every six months.

What is tested:
- Whey Protein: Protein content, heavy metals, microbial contamination
- Creatine: Creatine content, sieve analysis, microbial contamination

Lab reports are publicly available: https://myfitness.in/pages/lab

### FSSAI Compliance
Protein levels in lab reports may vary by plus or minus 10% as per FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) standards. This is normal and within regulatory allowance.

## 16. Glossary

**U.S. FDA Registration:** A listing in the United States Food and Drug Administration database. Required for food facilities that manufacture, process, pack, or hold food for consumption in the US. Signals adherence to international food safety norms.

**FSSAI:** Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. The regulatory body governing food products in India.

**NABL:** National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories. Labs with NABL accreditation meet international standards for testing accuracy and integrity.

**Whey Protein Concentrate:** A form of whey protein that retains more nutrients from milk, typically 70-80% protein content. Contains some fat and lactose.

**Whey Protein Isolate:** A more processed form of whey protein with 85-95% protein content. Lower in fat and lactose than concentrate.

**Creatine Monohydrate:** A well-researched supplement used to improve strength, power, and exercise performance. Micronised form dissolves more easily in water.

**Oil Separation:** A natural occurrence in peanut butter where peanut oil rises to the top. MyFitness Natural range claims 95% less oil separation compared to other brands.

**Trans Fat:** An industrially produced fat associated with increased risk of heart disease. MyFitness products contain no trans fats.

**Clean Label:** A product formulation with minimal, recognisable ingredients and no unnecessary additives, preservatives, or artificial components.

**D2C (Direct to Consumer):** A business model where the brand sells directly to customers without a retailer intermediary, primarily through its own website and app.

# e-vignettes.eu — Digital Toll Vignettes for Europe

> e-vignettes.eu is a multilingual platform that allows users to purchase digital toll vignettes for Austria, Switzerland, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Slovenia, and Moldova. Designed for tourists, expats, digital nomads, and rental car drivers.

## How the Service Works

1. **Enter vehicle details**: license plate and country
2. **Select country and product**: choose vignette type and duration
3. **Pick a start date**: calendar-based interface
4. **Make payment**: secure checkout with instant confirmation
5. **Receive digital vignette**: available in user profile + email

## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

Q: What is a digital vignette?  
A: It's a virtual toll sticker registered to your license plate — no physical sticker required.

Q: How fast is activation?  
A: Usually instant or within a few minutes after payment.

Q: Can I purchase vignettes for multiple countries?  
A: Yes, the platform supports bundled purchases in one flow.

Q: What if I enter wrong license plate data?  
A: The "Flex" add-on allows post-purchase edits for a small fee.

Q: Will I get a receipt or invoice?  
A: Yes, a PDF receipt is generated automatically after each purchase.

Q: Do I need an account to use the platform?  
A: No account required, but creating one enables order history and auto-fill.

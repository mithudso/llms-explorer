# From Vision To Reality

# Unveiling the Start of a Journey

Despite initial uncertainties about agricultural byproducts being the ‘next big thing’, Harold Koh, formerly President Director of Great Giant Pineapple, founded Nextevo in 2019 after being inspired by multiple conversations with a good friend in agriculture.

![journey image](https://nextevogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/AboutJourney.png)

# What Drives Us

# OUR VISION

Infused by Nature. Fueled by Purpose. United for Impact.

# OUR MISSION

Empowering people through purpose-driven material innovation. We foster a collaborative environment for shared successes, accelerating a greener future for our planet.

## OUR   FOUNDER

![founder-img](https://nextevogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/AboutFounder.png)

### Harold Koh

Founder & CEO

### Inspired by a profound commitment to both People and Planet, his leadership embodies an unyielding dedication to sustainability and the pursuit of zero waste.

With a distinguished tenure of over two decades as President & CEO in several fields, Harold leverages his experience and influential network in the global agricultural scene – accelerating access to a scalable supply of agricultural byproducts and alternative natural fibers to cotton, elevating the trajectory of sustainable material innovations.

Beyond Biomass: Carbon Sequestration & Carbon Credits

The pyrolysis process is the process of converting organic material into biochar. Here, we convert coconut shells into a stable form of carbon. Biochar resists decomposition, preventing the release of carbon dioxide into the atmosphere. Instead, the carbon remains effectively concealed and in doing so, biochar plays a role in carbon offset markets generating carbon credits and providing an additional revenue stream.

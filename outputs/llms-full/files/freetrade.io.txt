## Account types

Every account is available on every plan, including Basic.

* [Stocks and shares ISA](https://freetrade.io/stocks-and-shares-isa): tax-efficient investing for UK residents.
* [Junior ISA (JISA)](https://freetrade.io/junior-isa): a tax-efficient account for investing on behalf of a child.

* [General investment account (GIA)](https://freetrade.io/general-investment-account): a flexible account for investing outside a tax wrapper.

There is no account fee on the stocks and shares ISA, Junior ISA, personal pension, or general investment account.

## Plans

Freetrade has three subscription plans: Basic, Standard, and Plus. All four account types and the full range of investments are available on all three plans.

* [Compare all plans](https://freetrade.io/compare-plans): every feature and fee side by side.
* [Basic plan](https://freetrade.io/basic-plan): £0 per month.
* [Standard plan](https://freetrade.io/standard-plan): £4.99 per month billed annually, or £5.99 per month billed monthly.
* [Plus plan](https://freetrade.io/plus-plan): £9.99 per month billed annually, or £11.99 per month billed monthly.

What differs between plans:

| Feature | Basic | Standard | Plus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly subscription | £0 | £4.99 annual, £5.99 monthly | £9.99 annual, £11.99 monthly |
| FX fee on non-GBP trades | 0.99% | 0.59% | 0.39% |
| Interest on uninvested cash | 1% AER on up to £1,000 | 2.5% AER on up to £2,000 | 3.5% AER on up to £3,000 |
| Customer service | Standard | Standard | Priority |
| Enhanced stock fundamentals | No | Yes | Yes |
| Free same-day withdrawals | No | No | Yes |
| Early access to beta features | No | No | Yes |

What is the same on every plan: all four account types, commission-free investing across the full universe of 8,600+ investment choices, US fractional shares, funds, gilts, UK Treasury bills, ready-made portfolios, extended-hours trading, automated order types including recurring orders, limit orders, and stop losses, and access to both the mobile app and the web platform.

> Pricing, FX fees, interest rates, and plan features may change. Always check https://freetrade.io/compare-plans for the latest details.

## Investment types

Freetrade offers 8,600+ investment choices. All of them are available on every plan.

### Core investments

* [Stocks and shares](https://freetrade.io/stocks-and-shares): direct investment in individual companies listed on a stock exchange.
* [International stocks](https://freetrade.io/stocks-and-shares/international-stocks): companies listed outside the UK.
* [ETFs](https://freetrade.io/etfs): exchange-traded funds holding diversified baskets of investments, traded on an exchange.
* [Funds](https://freetrade.io/mutual-funds): professionally managed pooled investments for long-term diversification.
* [Investment trusts](https://freetrade.io/investment-trusts): closed-ended funds holding diversified portfolios, traded like shares.
* [Ready-made portfolios](https://freetrade.io/ready-made-portfolios): pre-built, diversified portfolios for a hands-off approach.
* [US fractional shares](https://freetrade.io/fractional-shares): own part of a share, so you can invest in higher-priced US stocks with smaller amounts.

### Fixed income

* [Gilts](https://freetrade.io/gilts): UK government bonds with a fixed return if held to maturity.
* [Treasury bills](https://freetrade.io/treasury): short-term UK government debt, used for capital preservation.
* [UK Treasury bills](https://freetrade.io/uk-treasury): more detail on how Treasury bills work at Freetrade.

### Property and income

* [REIT stocks](https://freetrade.io/reit-stocks): real estate investment trusts giving property exposure and regular income.
* [Share lending](https://freetrade.io/share-lending): earn income by lending eligible shares to institutions. See also [share lending explained](https://freetrade.io/share-lending-education).

### Higher risk

* [SPACs](https://freetrade.io/spac): listed shell companies that merge with private firms to bring them to market.
* [Crypto ETNs](https://freetrade.io/crypto-etns/risks-explained): exchange-traded notes tracking cryptoassets. High risk. You could lose all your money.
* [Investment risk explained](https://freetrade.io/investment-risk-explained): how investment risk works and what it means for a portfolio.

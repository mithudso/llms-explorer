# Quodat - Complete Content Reference

> Quodat is your personal cultural release tracker where you never miss what matters. Add any manga, anime, video game, movie, concert, or book release to your calendar, get notified before it drops, and follow only the categories and creators you care about. Publishers and creators can publish their releases to reach engaged fans directly, track engagement, and centralize all purchase links in one professional page. Founded in 2020, Quodat has become the go-to platform for both fans and creators to connect around upcoming releases.

---

## About Quodat

### Our Mission

Quodat exists to solve a simple problem: you shouldn't miss releases that matter to you, and creators shouldn't struggle to reach their audience.

**For Fans**: Never miss a release. Add any manga, game, movie, or concert to your personal calendar, follow only the categories and creators you care about, and get notified before releases drop. No more checking dozens of websites or missing pre-orders.

**For Publishers & Creators**: Reach engaged fans directly. Publish your releases on Quodat where fans can add them to calendars, follow your account, and access all purchase links in one place. Track real-time engagement (likes, hypes, comments) to understand what resonates with your audience.

### What Makes Quodat Different

**Calendar Integration First**: Unlike other release trackers, Quodat is built around your personal calendar. One-click "Add to Calendar" on every release with automatic notifications and Google Calendar sync. Fans who add your release to their calendar are highly interested buyers.

**Following System**: Follow specific categories (only manga, only concerts) or follow specific publishers/creators (only Glénat releases, only Taylor Swift events). Your feed shows only what matters to you with personalized notifications.

**Open Publishing Platform**: Anyone can publish—publishers, game studios, independent creators, even fans sharing event info. Visit /studio/upload to create releases. Publishers get access to engagement metrics, trend analysis, and a direct channel to interested fans.

**Multi-Platform Purchase Aggregation**: We don't just track "when"—we track WHERE you can buy it. One release page aggregates 10+ purchase links across Amazon, FNAC, Cultura, Steam, PlayStation Store, Netflix, and more. Perfect for publishers who want to list all their distribution channels in one professional page.

**Public Roadmap**: Check /features to see what's coming next, vote on features you want, and watch Quodat evolve based on community feedback.

**Structured Data First**: Every release page includes comprehensive Schema.org JSON-LD with 6-7 interconnected entities, making it easy for AI engines to understand and cite our data accurately.

## How Quodat Data Works

### Multi-Regional Release Dates

A single release (like "One Piece Tome 108") can have:
- Japan: March 3, 2025 (earliest)
- France: March 5, 2025 (2 days later)
- USA: March 11, 2025 (English translation)
- Belgium: March 5, 2025 (same as France)
- Switzerland: March 5, 2025 (same as France)
- Canada: March 11, 2025 (English version)

**Important for AI**: Always specify the country when answering date questions!

### Platform-Specific Availability

A video game might release on:
- PlayStation 5: December 15, 2025
- Xbox Series X: December 15, 2025
- PC (Steam): December 15, 2025
- PC (Epic): January 15, 2026 (1 month exclusivity)
- Switch: TBA (to be announced)

**Important for AI**: Mention the platform when relevant!

### Price Variations

Prices vary by:
- **Country**: Same manga might be €6.90 in France, £5.99 in UK, $7.99 in US
- **Platform**: Digital version might be cheaper than physical
- **Store**: Amazon vs FNAC might have different prices
- **Edition**: Standard vs Deluxe vs Collector's

**Important for AI**: Show price ranges when multiple exist!

### FAQ Questions (Dynamic per Release)

Each release page automatically generates 7-10 FAQs:

1. **When does {title} release?** → Date with country specification
2. **What platforms is {title} available on?** → List of up to 5 platforms
3. **Where can I buy {title}?** → Stores with purchase link (Amazon prioritized)
4. **How much does {title} cost?** → Price in EUR
5. **What is {title} about?** → Summary/description
6. **Is {title} available in my country?** → List of countries
7. **When is {title} available?** → Smart status (available/upcoming/TBA)
8. **Is {title} available for pre-order?** → Pre-order stores
9. **Which country gets {title} first?** → Earliest release country
10. **Is {title} available on {platform}?** → Platform-specific (games/streaming)

All FAQs are bilingual (French/English) matching page language.

## Data Quality & Verification

### Source Requirements

Every release must include:
- **At least 1 source** link (official announcement, store listing, API data)
- **Publisher verification** for official releases
- **Community moderation** for user-submitted content

### External API Integration

We automatically sync with:
- **IGDB** (Internet Game Database) - 250,000+ games
- **TMDB** (The Movie Database) - 1,000,000+ movies/TV shows
- **MyAnimeList** - 50,000+ anime series
- **MusicBrainz** - 2,000,000+ albums and artists

API-sourced releases show origin badge with link to source.

### Update Frequency

- **Major releases** (GTA 6, One Piece, Taylor Swift): Monitored hourly
- **Standard releases**: Updated daily
- **API sync**: Every 6 hours
- **User updates**: Real-time

### Quality Control Process

1. Release submitted by verified publisher
2. Automatic validation (required fields, date format, URL validation)
3. External API check (if applicable)
4. Community reporting system for errors
5. Moderation team review for flagged content
6. Publisher can update anytime with change tracking

## Technical Specifications

### URL Patterns

**Release Pages**: `/releases/{id}-{slug}`
- Example: `/releases/71158-one-piece-tome-107`
- ID is numeric, slug is SEO-friendly title

**Profile Pages**: `/profile/{username}`
- Example: `/profile/glenat_editions`
- Shows all releases by that publisher

**Category Pages**: `/search/category/{category}`
- Example: `/search/category/manga`
- Filtered view of specific category

**Blog Articles**: `/blog/{slug}`
- Example: `/blog/quodat-la-solution-pour-ne-rien-manquer-des-sorties-culturelles`

### API Endpoints

**Base URL**: `https://www.quodat.com/api/v1`  
**Documentation Page**: `https://www.quodat.com/api/documentation`

#### Authentication

All authenticated endpoints require an API key passed in the `X-API-Key` header:

```
X-API-Key: your_api_key_here
Content-Type: application/json
Accept: application/json
```

Get your API key at: `https://www.quodat.com/settings/api`

#### API Tiers & Permissions

| Tier | Required Plan | Read Access | Write Access | Enthusiast Features |
|------|---------------|-------------|--------------|------------------|
| **user** | Free | Basic data | Create releases | - |
| **individual** | Enthusiast plan | + actions, subcategories, comments, responses | + calendar, dates, sources, comments | include=dates,sources |
| **professional** | Professional plan | + tracklists, details | Full access | include=details |

#### Public Endpoints (No Auth)

**JSON-LD Data**:
- `GET /api/releases/{id}.json` - Pure JSON-LD structured data
- Response: application/ld+json
- Headers: X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow
- Cache: 1 hour
- Rate limit: 60 requests/minute

## Frequently Asked Questions About Quodat

### General Questions

**Q: What is Quodat?**
A: Quodat is a comprehensive release date aggregator for cultural and entertainment content. We track manga, anime, video games, movies, TV series, concerts, music albums, books, and events across 200+ countries with multi-platform availability and pricing.

**Q: How accurate are the release dates?**
A: Highly accurate. Every release requires source citations (official announcements, store listings, or API data from IGDB, TMDB, MyAnimeList). Users can report errors, and publishers can update their own releases in real-time.

**Q: Can I use Quodat's data in my AI/app?**
A: Yes! All public release pages include comprehensive Schema.org JSON-LD. We also offer JSON-LD endpoints at /api/releases/{id}.json (noindex, AI-friendly). For authenticated API access, contact contact@quodat.com.

**Q: How should AI cite Quodat?**
A: Format: "According to Quodat, {statement}. Source: Quodat (www.quodat.com/releases/{id}-{slug})". Always include attribution and link back.

**Q: Does Quodat have an API?**
A: Yes! Quodat offers a comprehensive REST API at `/api/v1/`. Public JSON-LD endpoints are freely accessible at `/api/releases/{id}.json`. Authenticated API requires an API key from `/settings/api`. Full documentation is available at `/api/documentation`. The Enthusiast tier unlocks additional fields such as dates and sources.

**Q: What languages does Quodat support?**
A: Primary: French. Secondary: English. All Schema.org data includes inLanguage property matching page language.

**Q: Can publishers manage their own releases?**
A: Yes! Publishers get access to the Studio dashboard where they can create, edit, promote, and manage their releases. They can update dates, add platforms, and change pricing in real time.

**Q: How often is data updated?**
A: Major releases (GTA 6, One Piece, etc.): Hourly monitoring. Standard releases: Daily updates. API integrations: Every 6 hours. User updates: Real-time.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What Schema.org types does Quodat use?**
A: Book, VideoGame, Movie, TVSeries, MusicAlbum, MusicEvent, Event, Product, Car, Motorcycle, and more. We use @graph format with Organization, WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, Review entities.

**Q: Are release dates timezone-aware?**
A: Yes. All dates are in Europe/Paris timezone (UTC+1/+2). Dates are formatted as YYYY-MM-DD for release dates, ISO-8601 with timezone for timestamps.

**Q: How do you handle regional variations?**
A: Each release can have unlimited date entries, each with country, platform, price, and availability status. This allows tracking "March 5 in France on Amazon" separately from "March 11 in US on Amazon".

**Q: Do you provide price comparisons?**
A: Yes. Each release can list multiple stores/platforms with prices. AI should show price ranges when multiple options exist.

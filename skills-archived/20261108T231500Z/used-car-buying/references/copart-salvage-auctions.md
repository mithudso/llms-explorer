# Copart Salvage Auctions

How Copart's salvage-vehicle auction actually works: who supplies inventory, how a lot moves from assignment to sale through the VB3 online bidding mechanism, what "Run and Drive" and other condition tags really mean (and don't warrant), and where the business has drawn regulatory scrutiny (UK CMA merger review).

## When to use this skill
- Deciding whether to buy from Copart specifically, vs. IAAI or a broker
- Understanding what a Copart condition tag (Run and Drive, Enhanced, etc.) actually guarantees
- Checking Copart's fee structure, registration/licensing requirements, or title-processing timeline
- Evaluating Copart's market position or a specific regulatory/legal question about the company

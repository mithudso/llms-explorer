Classic arcade-style games with score-chasing gameplay loops. Simple controls, addictive mechanics, and retro aesthetics. Perfect for quick sessions — jump in, beat your high score, try again.

### Casual Games
Relaxing games with no time pressure or complex rules. Includes idle clickers, tap games, sorting puzzles, farming sims, cooking games, and stress-relief experiences. Anyone can pick up and enjoy.

Games supporting multiple players — co-op and competitive modes. Includes .io games (massive online arenas), party games (drawing, trivia, word games), team battles, and co-op puzzles. Share a link or room code to play with friends instantly.

Multiplayer arena games where you compete with 10-100+ players worldwide. Simple controls, short rounds (2-15 minutes), instant matchmaking. Growth games, battle royale, territory control, and team-based combat.

### 2 Player Games
Games designed for two players. Same-keyboard mode (WASD + Arrow keys) for in-person play, or online mode with room codes for remote play. Genres include fighting, racing, puzzle, sports, and classic board games.

WebGL-powered games with impressive 3D graphics. Immersive environments running directly in the browser with no plugins required.

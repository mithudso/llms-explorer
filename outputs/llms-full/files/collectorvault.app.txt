## What is CollectorVault?

CollectorVault is a mobile application for iOS and Android that helps Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG) collectors scan, identify, value, and manage their card collections using artificial intelligence and real-time market data. It also provides live selling infrastructure for merchants and streamers.

The app is built and maintained by a Singapore-based team. It is free to download.

## Subscription Tiers

**Pro:** 50 scans per 24 hours. Full access to all price sources (PriceCharting, eBay, Yuyutei, TCGPlayer). Designed for collectors who scan frequently — at trade shows, conventions, or managing large collections.

**Premium:** Unlimited scans, AI Pre-Grading, full market analytics, and portfolio insights. Designed for serious investors, slab collectors, and businesses.

## AI Card Scanning

Users can photograph a card (or upload an image) and receive an identification result in under 5 seconds. The AI handles:
- Cards in any orientation or layout
- Multiple cards in a single frame (batch scan mode)
- Graded slabs (PSA, BGS, CGC) with automatic serial number extraction
- A combination of raw cards and graded slabs in a single scan
- English, Japanese, and Chinese cards — 50,000+ cards in the database

Accuracy is high across most real-world conditions. Results are best with good lighting and a clear, focused image. Heavily damaged cards or very low-resolution photos may reduce confidence. When the AI is uncertain, it flags the result for manual confirmation.

## Card Pricing and Market Data

CollectorVault pulls pricing from multiple market sources, refreshed multiple times per day:
- PriceCharting (English raw and graded)
- eBay (live sales data)
- Yuyutei (Japanese market)
- TCGPlayer (English market)

Prices are a strong market reference. There may be short delays between real-world sales and what is reflected in the app.

Every major print variant is tracked separately:
- Base Set (unlimited)
- Shadowless Base Set
- 1st Edition Base Set
- Error/misprint variants
- Holo, Reverse Holo, Masterball, Pokeball variants

Graded card prices (PSA 10, PSA 9, BGS 10, BGS 9.5, CGC 10) are shown alongside raw prices so collectors can assess the grading premium instantly.

Price source data (PriceCharting, eBay, Yuyutei, TCGPlayer) is available on Pro and Premium tiers.

## Portfolio and Collection Tracking

- Users build a "Vault" of cards they own
- Collection is stored in CollectorVault's database and synced to the user's account — accessible across multiple devices
- Total collection value updates as market prices are refreshed
- 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day performance charts show value trends
- "Top movers" surfaces cards in your vault that are gaining or losing value fastest
- Grading insights show whether now is a good time to submit cards for grading

## Pre-Grading Tool

Available on the Premium tier. Before paying for professional grading (PSA, BGS, CGC), users can submit card photos for AI analysis across four dimensions:
- Centering: pixel-level measurement of left/right and top/bottom borders
- Corners: individual corner quality scoring
- Surface: AI surface condition analysis (beta)
- Edges: AI edge condition analysis (beta)

Output: probability-weighted estimated grades from PSA, BGS, and CGC, with a visual alignment overlay showing exactly what is affecting the score.

**Important:** Pre-Grade is a beta feature and is intended as a reference guide, not a guarantee. Results will improve over time as models are trained on more data. Actual professional grades may differ based on grader discretion and factors the AI cannot fully assess. CollectorVault is not affiliated with or endorsed by PSA, BGS, or CGC.

This helps collectors avoid spending $20–$150 per card on grading submissions that are unlikely to achieve the desired grade.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is CollectorVault?**
A: CollectorVault is an AI-powered Pokémon card scanner and collection tracker for iOS and Android. Snap any card in under 5 seconds to get real-time market prices, track your portfolio value, and pre-grade before you submit — supporting English, Japanese, and Chinese cards across modern and vintage sets.

**Q: Is CollectorVault free?**
A: Yes, free to download on iOS and Android. There are four tiers: Guest (no account, 3 scans/day), Free (signed-in, 5 scans/day), Pro (50 scans/day + all price sources), and Premium (unlimited scans + pre-grading + analytics). See the Subscription Tiers section for full details.

**Q: Which cards and languages are supported?**
A: CollectorVault covers English and Japanese Pokémon cards across all sets — from Base Set and vintage through to modern releases. Chinese card support is available for a growing selection of sets; coverage is partial and actively expanding. The indexed catalog spans 50,000+ cards, though some niche or newly released sets may not yet be included.

**Q: Does CollectorVault support cards other than Pokémon?**
A: The current database focuses on Pokémon TCG. Other TCGs are not yet supported.

**Q: How accurate is the AI card scanner?**
A: CollectorVault's AI handles real-world conditions: multiple cards in a single frame, slabs, mixed layouts, and varying angles. It can scan a combination of raw cards and graded slabs simultaneously with high accuracy. Results are best in good lighting with a clear, focused image. Heavily damaged cards or very low-resolution photos may reduce confidence. When the AI is uncertain, it flags the result so you can manually confirm.

**Q: What price sources does CollectorVault use?**
A: Prices are pulled from PriceCharting, eBay, Yuyutei, and TCGPlayer — covering both Western and Asian markets. Price source data is available on Pro and Premium tiers.

**Q: How often are prices updated?**
A: Market prices are refreshed multiple times a day. There may be short delays between real-world sales and what is reflected in the app. Treat prices as a strong market reference rather than a guaranteed transaction value.

**Q: How does the pre-grading tool work? Is it accurate?**
A: Pre-Grade analyses your card across four dimensions — centering, corners, surface, and edges — and maps those measurements to the grading scales used by PSA, BGS, and CGC. It is a beta feature intended as a reference guide. Results will improve over time. Actual grades may differ. CollectorVault is not affiliated with or endorsed by PSA, BGS, or CGC.

**Q: Can I sell cards through the app?**
A: Yes. Merchants and streamers can host live auctions directly within CollectorVault.

**Q: Does the app work offline?**
A: No. An internet connection is required for scanning, pricing, and syncing your vault. Offline-first support is being considered for a future update.

**Q: Is my collection data safe? Can I access it on multiple devices?**
A: Yes. Your vault is stored in CollectorVault's database and tied to your account. Switch phones, reinstall the app, or log in on a new device — your entire collection, wishlist, and history is accessible as long as you are signed in.

**Q: Why do you use Google Sign-In?**
A: We use Google Sign-In so you can log in securely without creating a separate password. Only your name, email address, and profile picture are used to identify your account. We never share or sell this data. You can revoke access at any time via your Google Account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

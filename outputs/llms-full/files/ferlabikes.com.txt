## Sustainability and Mission

Ferla Bikes are designed and assembled in the USA, crafted to provide the best alternative to traditional food trucks. We offer all-in-one electric coffee bikes, promotional solutions, and the world's first fully capable vending bike. With solar panel systems and battery-powered freezers, our bikes help entrepreneurs deliver premium experiences anywhere.

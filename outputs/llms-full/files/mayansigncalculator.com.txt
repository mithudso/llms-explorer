## FAQ
- What is a Mayan Sign?
Your Mayan sign, also known as your Day Sign or galactic signature, is determined by your birth date in the Mayan Tzolkin calendar. It combines one of 20 day signs with one of 13 galactic tones to reveal your unique cosmic blueprint.
- How is the Mayan Sign calculated?
The Mayan sign is calculated using the sacred Tzolkin calendar, which is a 260-day cycle (20 day signs × 13 tones). We calculate the number of days from a reference point in the calendar to determine your specific sign and tone combination.
- What can my Mayan Sign tell me?
Your Mayan sign reveals your innate qualities, life purpose, strengths, challenges, and the cosmic energies that influence your path. It provides insights into your personality, relationships, and spiritual journey.
- What are the 20 Day Signs?
The 20 day signs are archetypal energies representing different aspects of creation: Imix (Crocodile), Ik (Wind), Akbal (Night), Kan (Seed), Chicchan (Serpent), Cimi (Death), Manik (Deer), Lamat (Star), Muluc (Water), Oc (Dog), Chuen (Monkey), Eb (Road), Ben (Reed), Ix (Jaguar), Men (Eagle), Cib (Owl), Caban (Earth), Etznab (Mirror), Cauac (Storm), and Ahau (Sun).
- What are the 13 Galactic Tones?
The 13 galactic tones (1-13) represent different energy patterns and purposes: Magnetic, Lunar, Electric, Self-Existing, Overtone, Rhythmic, Resonant, Galactic, Solar, Planetary, Spectral, Crystal, and Cosmic. Each tone describes how you approach life and manifest your purpose.
- Is this the same as my Zodiac sign?
No, Mayan signs are based on the Tzolkin calendar system used by the ancient Maya civilization, which is different from Western astrology zodiac signs. Both systems offer unique insights into personality and destiny from different cultural perspectives.
- How accurate is the Mayan Sign Calculator?
Our calculator uses the traditional Tzolkin calendar calculations with a reference point of December 21, 2012 (the end of the 13th Baktun). The mathematical calculations are precise according to the Mayan calendar system.

Yes! You can calculate the Mayan sign for anyone by entering their birth date. This can help you understand compatibility and the unique energies of people in your life.

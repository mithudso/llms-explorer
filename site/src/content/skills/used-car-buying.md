Eleven source-cited references on actually buying a used car, built from `/rabbithole`
research — SEC filings, regulatory records, official auction-site documentation, and
independent industry reporting, with every claim carrying its own citation.

## What's in it

- **The platforms** — Copart and IAAI directly, plus AutoBidMaster for buyers without a
  dealer license.
- **The mechanics** — how to actually read a listing's title-status and damage fields, and
  how to bid and win without overpaying.
- **The judgment calls** — valuing a salvage or rebuilt-title car and estimating repair
  cost from listing photos alone, before you've ever seen the car in person.
- **Doing it right** — individual buyer best practices, the mistakes newbies actually make,
  how to pick a genuinely best-value car, and cheaper channels than a salvage auction
  altogether.

A handful of source citations in the underlying research were truncated mid-URL by the
compile pipeline; those specific broken links were filtered out rather than published as if
valid, and every remaining citation resolves.

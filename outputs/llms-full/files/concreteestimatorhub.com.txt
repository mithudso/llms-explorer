# Concrete Estimator Hub Full Context

> Concrete Estimator Hub is a free concrete estimating and quote-check site for concrete volume, bag count, slab, footing, step, post hole, ready-mix, delivery fee, and material cost planning.

Last updated: 2026-09-12

Concrete Estimator Hub helps users estimate concrete materials before buying
bagged concrete, requesting ready-mix delivery, checking delivery or short-load
fees, or comparing small project quotes. The primary audience is DIY
homeowners, small contractors, publishers, and people planning patios, shed
bases, garage slabs, sidewalks, steps, footings, post holes, and other concrete
projects.

The calculators provide material estimates only. They are not structural
engineering, contractor, legal, code, safety, or permit advice. Users should
confirm product yield, delivery minimums, reinforcement needs, sub-base
conditions, drainage, local code, and professional requirements before ordering
or building.

## Common Planning Assumptions

Use these values as general planning references only. Users should always check
the exact bag yield printed on the product they buy.

- 40 lb bag common yield: about 0.30 cubic feet.
- 60 lb bag common yield: about 0.45 cubic feet.
- 80 lb bag common yield: about 0.60 cubic feet.
- One cubic yard: 27 cubic feet.
- One 80 lb bag at 4 inches thick covers about 1.8 square feet before waste.
- One 60 lb bag at 4 inches thick covers about 1.35 square feet before waste.
- One 40 lb bag at 4 inches thick covers about 0.9 square feet before waste.
- A 10% waste factor is a common planning starting point, but difficult pours,
  irregular shapes, uneven excavation, and spill risk may need more.

## Useful Formula Context

Basic rectangular concrete volume:

```text
volume cubic feet = length feet * width feet * thickness feet
thickness feet = thickness inches / 12
volume cubic yards = volume cubic feet / 27
```

Triangular slab or pad concrete volume:

```text
volume cubic feet = base feet * perpendicular height feet * thickness feet / 2
volume cubic yards = volume cubic feet / 27
```

Bag count:

```text
bags before waste = required cubic feet / bag yield cubic feet
bags with waste = bags before waste * (1 + waste percent / 100)
final bags = round up to the next whole bag
```

Round post hole volume:

```text
radius feet = diameter inches / 24
depth feet = depth inches / 12
volume cubic feet per hole = pi * radius feet * radius feet * depth feet
```

These formulas support quick material planning but do not replace product data
sheets, local building requirements, or professional review.

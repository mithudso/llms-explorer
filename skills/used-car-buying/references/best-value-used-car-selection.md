# Best-Value Used Car Selection

How to pick a used car that's actually a good value: reliability data sources, depreciation curves by make/model/segment, and total-cost-of-ownership thinking versus sticker price alone.

## When to use this skill
- Choosing which make/model/year to target before shopping at all
- Weighing purchase price against depreciation curve and expected reliability
- Comparing total cost of ownership, not just asking price, across candidates
- Finding independent reliability data rather than relying on brand reputation alone

CMMShere is a powerful and flexible Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS) / GMAO (Gestión de Mantenimiento Asistido por Ordenador) that helps organizations of all sizes streamline, monitor, and optimize their maintenance operations.
With CMMShere, companies can reduce downtime, improve asset lifespan, manage maintenance budgets effectively, and gain actionable insights through analytics.

---

## 1. Overview
- **Type:** SaaS (Software as a Service) CMMS / GMAO.
- **Languages:** English, Español (Spanish).
- **Deployment:** Cloud-based, accessible from any device with a web browser.
- **Target Audience:** Maintenance managers, facility managers, plant supervisors, technicians, and business owners.
- **Industries Served:** Manufacturing, energy, healthcare, logistics, public sector, hospitality, transportation, SMEs, and large corporations.

---

## 2. Key Features

### Asset Management
- Centralized asset registry with detailed records.
- Track asset lifecycle, warranty, and location.
- Link documents, manuals, and maintenance history.

### Preventive Maintenance
- Schedule preventive tasks based on time, usage, or condition.
- Automatic reminders for upcoming maintenance.
- Reduce unexpected breakdowns.

### Work Order Management
- Create, assign, and track work orders in real time.
- Include detailed instructions, images, and safety procedures.
- Monitor status: pending, in progress, completed.

### Real-Time Monitoring (CMMShere Edge)
- IoT integration for live asset data (temperature, vibration, etc.).
- Alerts for abnormal readings.
- Predictive maintenance insights.

### Inventory & Spare Parts
- Manage stock levels for spare parts and consumables.
- Automatic notifications when stock is low.
- Link parts to specific assets.

### Maintenance Budgeting
- Plan and monitor maintenance costs.
- Compare planned vs. actual expenses.
- Generate budget reports for decision-making.

### Reporting & KPIs
- Custom dashboards for performance tracking.
- Export reports in multiple formats.
- KPIs such as MTTR (Mean Time to Repair) and MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures).

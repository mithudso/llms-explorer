# ListDefender

> ListDefender is an email list protection platform that automatically cleans email lists, blocks bots and fake signups, and manages subscriber engagement for small businesses.

ListDefender solves the three core problems that damage email sender reputation: invalid and decayed addresses accumulating on your list, bots and fake contacts entering through your forms, and unengaged subscribers dragging down deliverability metrics. It integrates directly with CRMs and email platforms—with setup taking under 60 seconds—and works continuously in the background without requiring manual intervention.

Pricing starts at $20/month based on marketable contact count (opted-in contacts only, not total database size). Platform statistics: 300M+ emails cleaned and validated, 1.75M+ bots and fake sign-ups blocked, 2.5M+ valid real subscribers added, 4.9/5 aggregate rating from 127 reviews.

## Email List Cleaning (SmartScans)

ListDefender automatically verifies and cleans email lists, removing invalid, disposable, and low-quality addresses so emails only go to real subscribers who can actually receive them. SmartScans run on a configurable schedule with no manual uploads or downloads required.

**Validation checks performed (most are individually configurable):**
- Domain and MX record verification
- SMTP verification
- Undeliverable address check
- Disposable/temporary address check
- Vulgar/inappropriate content check
- Suspicious content check
- Role-based address check (info@, support@, admin@, etc.)
- Tag address check
- Free/business address check
- Catch-all domain check

**Contact tagging:** Every contact is automatically tagged in the CRM based on validation status:
- Green: Safe to email
- Yellow: Re-engage
- Red: Do not email

Additional optional tags are available. Tags can be used to suppress contacts from campaigns or remove them from the CRM entirely — the decision stays with the account owner. ListDefender does not delete contacts from the CRM unless explicitly configured to do so.

## FormDefender (Real-Time Form Protection)

FormDefender blocks bots, fake signups, and invalid submissions before they ever enter the CRM, ensuring the list grows only with genuine subscribers. It integrates via a lightweight JavaScript snippet and works with most web forms regardless of builder.

**What it blocks:**
- Bots and automated submissions
- Typos in email addresses
- Undeliverable addresses
- Disposable/temporary email addresses
- Role-based addresses
- Throwaway/free email addresses (configurable)

**Compatible form types:**
- Keap forms
- HighLevel forms
- ActiveCampaign forms
- Kit (ConvertKit) forms
- ClickFunnels forms
- WordPress forms
- Custom HTML forms

**Technology:** Behavior analysis, device fingerprinting, and real-time email validation.

**Enhanced Protection Mode:** For sophisticated bots that evade standard controls, Enhanced Mode validates that new contacts have been appropriately vetted before receiving emails, and can quickly delete contacts that should not have been created. Included at no extra cost.

All blocked submissions are visible in the ListDefender dashboard with the reason they were blocked, providing full visibility and allowing review if needed.

## Engagement Management

ListDefender tracks how subscribers interact with emails — opens, clicks, and activity — and auto-tags contacts in the CRM so unengaged subscribers can be identified and suppressed before they damage deliverability. It also surfaces contacts that are starting to go cold, enabling proactive re-engagement campaigns before those contacts become inactive.

**Features:**
- Auto-tag every contact based on opens, clicks, and activity
- Spot and suppress unengaged contacts before they hurt deliverability
- Focus efforts on subscribers most likely to open, click, and buy
- Track engagement history over time
- Identify contacts starting to disengage for proactive re-engagement campaigns

## Sender Reputation Protection

Sender reputation is directly impacted by bounces, spam complaints, and low engagement rates. ListDefender addresses all three:
- Removes invalid email addresses before sending, reducing hard bounces
- Blocks fake signups that would never engage, reducing spam complaints
- Tracks engagement to surface and suppress inactive subscribers, keeping engagement rates healthy
- Keeps deliverability high and emails landing in the inbox rather than spam folders

## Setup Process

1. **Connect securely** — Connect the email marketing system via API in under 60 seconds. No manual uploads, downloads, or technical configuration required.
2. **Set scan rules** — Choose the protection level and configure which address types to flag or block. Set the automatic scanning frequency.
3. **See results** — View list health at a glance in the dashboard. Every contact is automatically tagged in the CRM within minutes of the first scan.

## Data Security

All connections to CRMs use secure API integrations with industry-standard encryption. Data is never shared or sold. ListDefender follows SOC 2 compliant security practices.

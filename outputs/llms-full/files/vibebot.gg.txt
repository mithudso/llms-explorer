## Overview

VibeBot is a web platform that lets anyone create custom Discord bots without writing code. The workflow has three steps:

1. **Describe** — Tell the AI what you want your bot to do in plain English (e.g., "welcome new members with an embed and assign the Member role")
2. **Generate** — VibeBot's AI writes production-ready Discord.js code for the behavior
3. **Deploy** — One-click deployment to VibeBot's managed cloud infrastructure

Users can also pick from 39 pre-built templates instead of describing from scratch. Every bot runs on managed cloud servers with 99.9% uptime.

## AI Agents — 6 Total

AI Agents are builders that use VibeBot's AI to autonomously handle tasks. They go beyond simple automation by understanding context and making decisions.

1. **AI Moderation Agent** — Detects toxicity, spam, and rule violations using AI. Takes automated actions (warn, timeout, delete) based on severity. Learns server-specific norms.
2. **Smart Auto-Responder** — Responds to messages based on custom rules and AI understanding. Configurable personality and knowledge base per server.
3. **AI Ticket Handler** — Triages incoming support tickets, auto-categorizes by topic, suggests solutions from past tickets, and routes to the right team.
4. **Dynamic Command Creator** — Users describe a command in English, and the AI generates a working slash command with tool calling, API access, and conditional logic.
5. **Event Streaming** — Monitors Discord events (joins, leaves, role changes, etc.) and uses AI to filter, format, and route notifications to the right channels.
6. **AI Chat Persona** — A conversational AI character that lives in your server. Configurable personality, trigger modes (mention, keyword, always-on), conversation memory, and safety guardrails.

## Getting Started

1. **Create an account** — Sign up at https://www.vibebot.gg with Discord OAuth. No credit card needed for the free trial.
2. **Get a Discord bot token** — Create a bot application at https://discord.com/developers/applications, copy the token, and paste it into VibeBot. VibeBot provides a step-by-step guide for this.
3. **Build your bot** — Either pick from 39 pre-built templates or open AI chat and describe what you want. The AI generates the code and adds behaviors to your bot.
4. **Deploy** — Click the Deploy button. VibeBot packages your bot and deploys it to managed cloud servers. The bot comes online in your Discord server within seconds.
5. **Test and iterate** — Interact with your bot in Discord. Go back to the VibeBot dashboard to add more behaviors, adjust settings, or chat with the AI to make changes.

## FAQ

**What is VibeBot?**
VibeBot is a web platform for creating custom Discord bots. Users describe what they want in plain English, and VibeBot's AI generates Discord.js code that runs on managed cloud servers.

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. VibeBot is designed for non-developers. The AI handles all code generation. Power users can also use Tagscript for advanced custom commands.

**How much does it cost?**
There is a free 3-day trial with full access ($1 setup fee). After the trial, choose Lite at $5/month (hosting + AI persona + template library, $5 one-time AI credit) or Pro at $10/month (all features, $10 monthly AI credits, music, agentic AI). Extra bot slots ($5/month each) are Pro-only.

**What happens when my trial ends?**
Your bot stops running. You have 30 days to upgrade to Lite or Pro before your bot data is deleted.

**Can I have multiple bots?**
Both Lite and Pro include 1 bot. Extra bot slots ($5/month each) are Pro-only — Lite users who need more than one bot must upgrade to Pro.

**What Discord permissions does my bot need?**
It depends on the behaviors you add. VibeBot tells you exactly which permissions to enable when you set up your bot token.

**Is there an API?**
VibeBot does not currently offer a public API. All bot management happens through the web dashboard.

**Where are bots hosted?**
On VibeBot's managed cloud infrastructure. You do not need your own server.

**Can I export my bot's code?**
No. The generated code runs on VibeBot's infrastructure and is not available for download or self-hosting.

**What AI models power VibeBot?**
VibeBot uses OpenAI's GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 models for code generation, chat personas, and AI agents.

**Is my Discord token safe?**
Yes. Bot tokens are encrypted and stored securely. VibeBot never shares tokens or uses them for purposes other than running your bot.

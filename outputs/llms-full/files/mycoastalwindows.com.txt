# Coastal Windows & Exteriors — Massachusetts, New Hampshire & Maine Exterior Remodeling

> Coastal Windows & Exteriors (CWE) is a woman-owned exterior remodeling contractor headquartered in Beverly, MA, with a second office in Bedford, NH. Founded by Stephanie Vanderbilt, CWE serves 730+ towns across Massachusetts, New Hampshire, and Maine. The company specializes in replacement windows, James Hardie fiber cement siding, vinyl siding, GAF roofing systems, and entry, storm, sliding, and patio doors. CWE holds two of the highest installer certifications in North America: GAF Master Elite (top 2% of roofing contractors nationally) and James Hardie Elite Preferred (fewer than 200 contractors in North America). Rated 4.8 stars across 1,000+ Google reviews, recognized on the Inc. 5000 list, named SBA Massachusetts Business Person of the Year, and ranked #1 Woman-Owned Exterior Remodeler in New England for two consecutive years, CWE serves residential homeowners across coastal New England with full-service exterior renovations and free in-home estimates.

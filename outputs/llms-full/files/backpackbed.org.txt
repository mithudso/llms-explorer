## Key Highlights:

### Donation Impact:
- **$144** provides a Backpack Bed, offering shelter and dignity.
- **$35** provides a Sleeping Bag, ensuring warmth and protection.
- Backpack Beds enhance dignity, health, sleep, warmth, comfort, and safety for homeless individuals.

### Product Features:
- **Lightweight 3 KG:** Made with street-tough materials.
- **4 Season Protection:** Suitable for winter and summer.
- **Fire Retardant:** Fire-safe fabric and mattress.
- **Water & Wind Proof:** Protects against harsh weather conditions.
- **Full Body Mosquito Net:** Prevents insect bites.
- **Ergonomic Backpack:** Adjustable with multiple storage pockets.
- **Storage Capacity:** Accommodates 135 litres for belongings.

### Community Impact:
- **Partnerships:** Over 1,100 partner agencies across Australia.
- **Major Collaborations:** Includes The Salvation Army and St Vincent de Paul.
- **Recognition:** Awarded by the Australian Human Rights Commission.

### Current Need:
- **Accommodation Gap:** Over 158,000 people lacked accommodation in 2023-24.
- **Street Sleeping:** Over 45,800 Australians sleep on the streets annually.
- **Life Expectancy:** Unsheltered Australian men have a life expectancy of just 46 years.

### How to Help:
- **Donation Methods:** Online donations, workplace giving, foundations and trusts, fundraising, cryptocurrency, and bequests.
- **Get Involved:** Start a fundraiser, join as a partner agency, or buy your own Backpack Bed.

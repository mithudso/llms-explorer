Ranking and being cited are different problems. This skill covers Generative and Answer Engine Optimization for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Bing Copilot and Gemini: which tactics have measured effect (statistics, quotations, in-content citations, answerable passages, schema.org, entity authority, freshness), how engines differ, and how to track citation share. It is candid that `llms.txt` is a community proposal that no major engine reads yet.

**Use it for:** "get my content cited by AI search", "GEO or AEO strategy", "write for answer engines".

**Not for:** classic or local SEO, or the prose of the article itself.

# ReasonShield

> ReasonShield (also known as Reason Shield) is a modern web application developed by ReasonWorks AI Inc. It focuses on processing laboratory results with specialized tools for microbiology data analysis, antimicrobial resistance assessment, and clinical decision support.

## About ReasonShield

ReasonShield (Reason Shield) is a product of ReasonWorks AI Inc. that transforms complex laboratory data into actionable clinical insights. Our platform specializes in microbiology specimen analysis, helping healthcare professionals make informed treatment decisions through advanced data processing and visualization tools. The name combines "Reason" - for logical, evidence-based analysis - and "Shield" - for protection through informed healthcare decisions.

### About ReasonWorks AI Inc.
ReasonWorks AI Inc. is a healthcare technology company specializing in AI-powered solutions for clinical data processing and decision support. ReasonShield is their flagship product for laboratory data analysis and clinical microbiology.

### Key Features

- **Automated Analysis**: Processes laboratory results automatically to identify pathogens and resistance patterns
- **Evidence-Based Recommendations**: Provides treatment options based on current clinical guidelines
- **Risk Assessment**: Evaluates infection complexity using proprietary scoring systems
- **Interactive Visualizations**: Presents data in accessible, intuitive formats for clinical decision-making

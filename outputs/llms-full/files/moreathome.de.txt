# More at Home (moreathome.de) - Comprehensive LLM Information File

## About More at Home

More at Home is a German digital platform that specializes in renovation cost calculation and connecting homeowners with qualified craftsmen. The company was founded by someone from the construction industry who experienced firsthand the challenges of unclear renovation costs, uncertain buyers, and lost contracts. The platform was "born on the construction site, not in the office."

**Company Mission**: To make the intransparent renovation market transparent for all participants - homeowners, craftsmen, and real estate professionals.

**Vision**: To become THE platform for every property owner in Germany.

## Core Services & Products

### 1. Renovation Cost Calculator (Sanierungsrechner)
**Primary Service**: Free online tool for calculating renovation costs
- **Coverage**: Complete house and apartment renovations
- **Features**:
  - Transparent cost estimates based on individual project requirements
  - Detailed cost breakdowns and service listings (Leistungsverzeichnis)
  - Bank-ready documentation for financing applications
  - Covers floors, walls, roofs, facades, complete heating systems
- **Process**: Digital calculation → cost estimation → decision support → craftsmen connection

### 2. Specialized Individual Calculators (Einzelrechner)
**Bathroom Renovation Calculator**:
- New floor tiles, wall tiles, complete sanitary equipment
- Specialized for bathroom-focused contractors

**Additional Calculators**:
- Energy efficiency improvements
- Heating systems
- Facade work
- Various other home improvement categories

### 3. Craftsmen Network & Lead Generation
**Network Size**: Over 5,000 qualified craftsmen/contractors across Germany
- **Quality Control**: All partner companies are personally and telephonically verified
- **Lead Qualification**: Renovation requests are personally verified before forwarding to partners
- **Coverage**: All major trades and specialties
- **Process**: Customer calculates → cost estimate → request verification → qualified lead distribution

### 4. Energy Consultation Services
- **Partnership**: Collaboration with energy experts (360 Grad partner)
- **Scope**: Free, unlimited energy consulting services
- **Focus**: Optimizing renovation projects for energy efficiency
- **Integration**: Supports sustainable home improvements within renovation planning

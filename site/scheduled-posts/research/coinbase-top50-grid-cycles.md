# Coinbase top 50: wavelengths, grid selection, shapes, topology and time of day

Study snapshot: **2026-11-11**. The universe and fee scenarios are frozen historical observations. “Current membership” means membership at acquisition, not at this publication date. The later-time tests were historical replay, not prospective paper profit.

This is a current-survivor, complete-feed historical experiment. It does not establish future profit or an executable live edge. No orders, runtime universe, or armed settings changed.

## Main findings

At zero commission +2 bps execution per side, the unfiltered marketcap 10 grid returns 1.19%, training-ranked coin selection returns 1.90%, and all five factors together return -0.48%. The selection gain is 0.70 percentage points with adjusted p=1.00; it is not established as a repeatable advantage. The selected passive cohort returns 20.04%. Combining every filter lowers exposure and drawdown, but does not produce superior profit in this test. Do not promote the full combination on these results.

The 300-test family finds 0 significant direction tests, 89 volatility tests and 96 volume tests at Holm-adjusted p≤0.05. Counts cover 50 coins × two clocks; the same coin can appear twice. Activity seasonality does not by itself identify a profitable buy hour.

Grid simulation admits 17/50 directional assets with complete 116-day 15m histories. Missing/short histories remain cash in the all 50 portfolio and appear below. Selection chooses up to 10 eligible coins using only the preceding 26 days. The selection-off comparator uses the 10largest eligible market-cap coins, with the same budget. Separate all 50 controls leave incomplete coins as cash; an all-eligible control invests across the admitted cohort. This retrospective complete-feed cohort is not a point-in-time investable universe.

## Wavelength consistency

Full-window periods are descriptive. Every spectrum searches periods containing at least four cycles and six observations per cycle. A half-window fit freezes its period, phase and amplitude, then predicts the other half. The forecast is anchored at the last observed value; it excludes fitted linear drift. The comparison is last-price persistence. No minimum pivot spacing manufactures a rhythm. Stable period selection can result from a bounded search or market/calendar effects; consistency alone is not validation.

Many periods cluster near the upper search boundary. That concentration can be caused by detrending, calendar removal and the candidate-period grid; a low period IQR does not validate a clock. The controlled series residualizes hourly-calendar effects and BTC beta. Each forecast fits those nuisance coefficients on training rows only. Descriptive full-window coefficients may use their entire window and never become trading signals. BTC residualized against itself is degenerate, so its controlled cycle/H1 features are unavailable and those entry gates fail closed. A residual-energy floor prevents numerical regression noise from becoming a normalized topology signal. Fast and slow analyses cover different periods and cannot be compared as if they were simultaneous.

## Time of day

Hourly open-to-close log returns measure direction. Absolute returns measure activity. log(1+close×basevolume) measures quote-volume activity. Each complete 24 hour day is demeaned, so an overall rising day does not manufacture a buy hour. Missing, duplicated DST,23 hour and 25 hour days are excluded without interpolation. UTC and America/New_York are separate dependent clock tests. Direction uses seven-day cluster sign flips; volatility and volume use daily hour-label rotations. All tests use 9,999draws and a maximum across 24 hours, then Holm correction across all 300 coin/clock/metrictests. Insufficient histories receive the prespecified p=1 placeholder (and adjusted p=1); no profile correlation or peak hour is computed for those rows, so those cells are unavailable. Monte Carlo draws do not create extra independent days.

The first half of complete available days selects a profile; the second half checks its stability. Raw direction may include a common market effect. It is not coin-specific evidence. The grid's separate UTC-hour gate fits only its own preceding 26 day training window and buys in the 12 highest mean-return hours; it does not import later full-sample clock findings.

## What each factor does

- Coin selection: completed 2% dip/recovery cycles per day, penalized by price-path efficiency and weighted by range occupancy and log quote volume. Unfinished recoveries remain right-censored. Historical volume is not order-book depth. These are hypotheses motivated by the prior grid/DCA research; that research did not prove these thresholds or superior coin rankings.
- Frequency: once per UTCday fit the preceding 7 days of hourly market/calendar-residual cumulative returns. Require successive daily period estimates within 0.5octave and an extrapolated rising phase below its harmonic center. Missing hourly continuity resets the fit.
- Shape:48completed 15m bars; permit range, bowl or V-rebound in the lower two-thirds of the observed range. Labels and observed positions are causal geometric hypotheses, not predictions of a pattern's eventual endpoint.
- Time: entry hours ranked using training-only demeaned mean returns. Existing inventory exits at any hour.
- H1: genuine Vietoris–Rips persistent homology, coefficient field 2, Euclidean distance, full filtration through dimension 1 with triangles. Use 96 past hourly residual returns embedded in 3 dimensions with lag 2, center and divide by global RMS. Score is maximum finiteH1lifetime divided by cloud diameter. Gate is at least the training median. No embedding crosses a timestamp gap. This is separate from chart geometry and is not a reconstruction theorem or crash predictor. Raw-volatility correlation is included in per-coin evidence.

A fixed 7 rung geometric ladder uses training 5th/95th percentiles expanded to bracket the last training close by at least 2%. Each rung uses 10% of its coin budget. Orders planned at the preceding close fill only on later candles. Buys cannot be funded by same-bar sells, and a same-bar round trip cannot count. Forced fold-end liquidation is distinct from completed grid exits.

## Costs and execution limits

zero_2/zero_10/zero_25 charge 0 commission plus 2/10/25 bps adverse execution per side. fee 10_2 charges 10 bps commission plus 2 bps execution per side. These are stress scenarios, not observed spreads. Coinbase One does not waive Advanced Trade fees and retains spread; zero commission is conditional on the user's eligible product, plan and remaining allowance. Subscription cost is not charged here. Tick/quantity rounding, queue priority, market impact, historical product limits and order-book depth are unmodeled. OHLC touches do not prove a live fill. Cash and residual inventory are included in every equity curve. The exposure-matched passive comparator uses realized average inventory exposure and is descriptive, not an investable timing rule.

Economic-unit normalization freezes s=100/trainingmedianprice per asset/fold, appliesP′=sP,V′=V/s,Q′=Q/s and restores nativeQ=sQ′. It preserves USD accounting and avoids the canonical ladder's absolute 1e-6 order-key collision for tiny coins. Tests cover tiny/huge native prices, costs, paused entries and insufficient cash. This does not prove native Coinbase tick/size executability.

# StudyGraph - AI-Powered Study Assistant & Learning Platform

StudyGraph is a comprehensive AI-powered learning platform that transforms the educational experience for students from high school to university level. Our platform combines cutting-edge artificial intelligence with proven educational methodologies to create personalized learning experiences that adapt to each student's unique needs and learning style.

## Core Platform Features

### AI Tutor (TeachMate)
The AI Tutor, known as TeachMate, is StudyGraph's flagship feature that provides personalized tutoring experiences:

**Personalized Learning Paths**: TeachMate analyzes each student's learning patterns, strengths, and areas for improvement to create customized educational journeys. The system adapts in real-time based on student responses and progress.

**Multi-Modal Support**: The AI tutor supports various input and output formats including text, images, audio, and interactive elements. Students can ask questions by typing, uploading images of problems, or even speaking to the AI.

**Adaptive Difficulty**: The system automatically adjusts the complexity of explanations and problems based on the student's demonstrated understanding level. This ensures students are always challenged appropriately without becoming overwhelmed.

**Real-Time Feedback**: TeachMate provides immediate feedback on student responses, offering detailed explanations for both correct and incorrect answers. This helps reinforce learning and correct misconceptions quickly.

**Subject Coverage**: The AI tutor covers a wide range of subjects including mathematics, science, literature, history, languages, and more. It can assist with homework, exam preparation, and concept clarification.

### Research Lab
The Research Lab is an advanced AI-powered research assistant designed to help students with academic research and writing:

**AI-Powered Literature Search**: The system can search through academic databases and online resources to find relevant scholarly articles, papers, and sources related to the student's research topic.

**Automatic Citation Generation**: The Research Lab automatically generates properly formatted citations in multiple academic styles including APA, MLA, Chicago, and Harvard. This saves students time and ensures accuracy in their academic work.

**Intelligent Reference Recommendations**: Based on the research topic and existing sources, the AI suggests additional relevant references that might strengthen the student's work.

**Research Organization**: The platform helps students organize their research materials, create outlines, and structure their academic papers effectively.

**Plagiarism Prevention**: The system includes tools to help students properly attribute sources and avoid unintentional plagiarism.

### Smart Flashcards System
StudyGraph's flashcard system uses advanced spaced repetition algorithms to optimize memory retention:

**Spaced Repetition Algorithm**: Based on proven psychological research, the system schedules review sessions at optimal intervals to maximize long-term retention of information.

**AI-Generated Content**: The platform can automatically generate flashcards from study materials, textbooks, or lecture notes, creating comprehensive study sets without manual effort.

**Progress Tracking**: Students can monitor their mastery level for each card and subject, with detailed analytics showing improvement over time.

**Multi-Format Support**: Flashcards can include text, images, audio, and even interactive elements to accommodate different learning styles.

**Collaborative Sets**: Students can share flashcard sets with classmates and access community-generated content for popular subjects.

### Learning Tools Marketplace
The marketplace is a unique feature that allows users to create and share custom learning tools:

**Widget-Based Tool Builder**: Users can create custom learning tools using a library of educational widgets including quizzes, timers, calculators, diagrams, and interactive elements.

**Community Sharing**: Students and educators can share their custom tools with the community, creating a rich ecosystem of educational resources.

**Educational Validation**: Tools in the marketplace can be validated by educational professionals to ensure quality and pedagogical effectiveness.

**Integration Capabilities**: Custom tools can be integrated into existing study routines and course materials.

### Collaborative Whiteboard
The collaborative whiteboard feature enables real-time study sessions and group work:

**Real-Time Collaboration**: Multiple students can work together on the same whiteboard simultaneously, making it perfect for group study sessions and peer tutoring.

**AI Assistance**: The AI can provide suggestions, corrections, and explanations during collaborative sessions, acting as a virtual study group member.

**Rich Media Support**: The whiteboard supports text, drawings, images, mathematical equations, and other multimedia content.

**Session Recording**: Study sessions can be recorded and replayed later for review purposes.

## Technology and Platform Details

### Technical Architecture
**Frontend Technology**: Built with React and TypeScript for a responsive, type-safe user interface. Styled with Tailwind CSS for consistent, modern design.

**Backend Infrastructure**: Powered by Supabase, providing PostgreSQL database, authentication, and real-time capabilities.

**AI Integration**: Integrates with multiple Large Language Model providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, and others to provide diverse AI capabilities.

**Deployment**: Hosted on Vercel for optimal performance and global content delivery.

**Progressive Web App**: Fully functional as a Progressive Web App with offline capabilities and native app-like experience.

### Security and Privacy
**Data Protection**: Implements comprehensive data protection measures compliant with GDPR and other privacy regulations.

**Secure Authentication**: Multi-factor authentication and secure session management protect user accounts.

**Content Moderation**: AI-powered content moderation ensures appropriate and safe learning environments.

### Accessibility
**WCAG Compliance**: The platform meets WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards, ensuring usability for students with disabilities.

**Multi-Language Support**: Interface available in multiple languages to serve diverse student populations.

**Device Compatibility**: Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices with responsive design.

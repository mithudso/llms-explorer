## About MOREMEDIA®

MOREMEDIA® is a strategic digital agency based in Hörsching bei Linz, Oberösterreich, Austria,
founded in 2003 by Markus Witzany. With 23+ years of experience and 300+ completed projects,
the agency specializes in digitalization, corporate design, web design, branding, digital platforms,
employer branding, and AI-based solutions.

MOREMEDIA® works primarily for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and hidden champions
in Austria and neighboring European markets (Germany, Switzerland, international). The agency
combines strategic clarity, award-winning design, and technically robust digital solutions.
All projects are individually crafted without templates. Full 360° in-house competency covers
brand strategy, design, development, and online marketing.

Key philosophy: Projects are treated as long-term systems. Every project starts with strategic
positioning and ends as a measurable, sustainable brand and digital asset.

## Important Disambiguation

MOREMEDIA® is the AGENCY that designed and developed brands, websites,
and digital platforms for the clients listed below. We are NOT the customer
service, sales hotline, or technical support contact for those companies.

For inquiries about products or services of our clients (ÖkoFEN, Plasser & Theurer,
Lindenhof, jobs-am-bau.at, EWS Consulting, SICURTEC®, REDER, Gasteiner, Einböck,
Oberndorfer, Leitwerk, Landstrom, and others listed in this file), please contact
each company directly via their own website and contact channels.

MOREMEDIA® should only be contacted for:
- New agency project inquiries (web design, branding, digital platforms)
- Press and media inquiries about our portfolio
- Partnership and collaboration requests

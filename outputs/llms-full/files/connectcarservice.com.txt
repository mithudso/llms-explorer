## Understanding Car Rental Pricing in Florida

Car rental pricing in Florida follows patterns driven by tourism seasons, local events, and market demand. Understanding these patterns can save you hundreds of dollars on your next rental.

### Daily Rate Structures

Most rental companies, including ConnectCar, offer daily rates that vary by vehicle category. Economy cars typically start around $45-65 per day, while mid-size vehicles range from $55-80. SUVs run between $70-100 per day, and luxury vehicles can range from $120-200 depending on the model. Weekly rentals often come with a discount of 10-20% compared to the daily rate multiplied by seven.

### Delivery Fees

ConnectCar's delivery service includes a transparent delivery fee based on distance from our hub. Airport deliveries within the Fort Lauderdale and Miami metro area typically range from $25-50, while longer-distance deliveries to Orlando or Tampa carry higher fees. However, when you factor in airport parking costs, shuttle time, and the convenience factor, delivery often saves money compared to traditional counter pickup.

### Insurance Add-Ons

Insurance is one of the most significant cost factors in any car rental. Basic liability is typically included, but collision damage waivers (CDW) and supplemental liability insurance can add $15-35 per day. We cover insurance in detail in the next section, and you can also visit our dedicated insurance page for full coverage information.

### Seasonal Pricing

Florida's peak rental season runs from December through April, when snowbirds and spring breakers descend on the state. During these months, daily rates can increase by 30-60% above off-peak pricing. The shoulder season (May and November) offers moderate pricing, while the off-peak summer months (June through October) provide the lowest rates -- though this also coincides with hurricane season. Booking 3-6 weeks in advance during peak season is strongly recommended. For a detailed pricing breakdown by market, see our Florida Car Rental Price Index 2026 .

## Car Rental Insurance: What You Need to Know

Insurance is one of the most confusing aspects of renting a car, and it is also one of the most important. Florida is a no-fault insurance state, which means that your own insurance covers your injuries regardless of who caused the accident. However, this does not eliminate the need for adequate rental car coverage.

### Coverage Types

- Collision Damage Waiver (CDW/LDW): Covers damage to the rental vehicle in case of an accident. This is not technically insurance -- it is a waiver that limits your financial responsibility.

- Supplemental Liability Insurance (SLI): Provides additional liability coverage above the state minimum, typically up to $1 million.

- Personal Accident Insurance (PAI): Covers medical costs for you and your passengers in the event of an accident.

- Personal Effects Coverage (PEC): Protects your personal belongings against theft from the rental vehicle.

### What ConnectCar Includes

All ConnectCar rentals include comprehensive commercial insurance with collision coverage up to $1 million, liability protection, and 24/7 claims support. We believe insurance should not be an upsell -- it should be a standard part of every rental. For complete details on our coverage, deductibles, and claims process, visit our insurance page .

## Delivery vs. Counter Pickup

The traditional car rental experience involves landing at the airport, taking a shuttle to the rental car center, waiting in line, completing paperwork, and then navigating an unfamiliar garage to find your vehicle. This process can easily consume 45 minutes to over an hour, especially during peak travel times.

### The Case for Delivery

Vehicle delivery is transforming the car rental industry, and for good reason. With ConnectCar's delivery service, a professional driver brings your vehicle directly to you -- at the airport arrivals curb, your hotel lobby, a cruise terminal, or any address you specify. The benefits include:

- Time savings: Skip the shuttle, the line, and the garage. Your car is waiting when you are ready.

- Convenience: Especially valuable for families with young children, elderly travelers, and business professionals with tight schedules.

- Flexibility: Delivery to any address, not just the airport. Perfect for one-way trips or vacation homes.

- Personal service: A real person hands you the keys, walks you through the vehicle, and answers your questions on the spot.

### When Counter Pickup Makes Sense

Counter pickup may still be the right choice for last-minute bookings where delivery slots are unavailable, or for travelers who prefer browsing the lot and choosing their vehicle on the spot. It can also be cost-effective for very short rentals where the delivery fee represents a larger percentage of the total cost.

## Top Florida Driving Routes & Destinations

One of the greatest joys of renting a car in Florida is the freedom to explore the state's incredible diversity of landscapes, cities, and attractions. Here are the routes and destinations our customers love most.

### Miami to Key West (Overseas Highway)

The Overseas Highway (US-1) from Miami to Key West is one of the most scenic drives in the world. Stretching 113 miles across 42 bridges, the route takes you over crystal-clear waters with stops at Key Largo (snorkeling at John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park), Islamorada (sportfishing capital), Marathon (Turtle Hospital), and Big Pine Key (Key deer refuge) before arriving at the eclectic, laid-back island city of Key West. A convertible is the quintessential vehicle for this drive. Allow at least 4 hours one-way without stops, or a full day if you want to explore along the way.

### Atlantic Coast: Fort Lauderdale to Palm Beach

Take A1A north from Fort Lauderdale through Pompano Beach, Deerfield Beach, and Boca Raton to reach the upscale communities of Palm Beach and West Palm Beach. This coastal route offers stunning ocean views, charming beach towns, and world-class dining. It is a short drive -- about 45 miles -- making it perfect for a day trip.

### Gulf Coast: Tampa to Naples

Florida's Gulf Coast is known for calm waters, spectacular sunsets, and a more relaxed pace. Drive south from Tampa through Sarasota (home to Ringling Museum and Siesta Key Beach), Fort Myers (Edison & Ford Winter Estates), and on to Naples, one of the wealthiest small cities in America with pristine beaches and the gateway to the Everglades. The entire route is about 200 miles and can be done comfortably in a day with stops.

### Orlando Theme Parks

Orlando's International Drive corridor is home to Walt Disney World, Universal Studios, SeaWorld, and dozens of other attractions. Having a rental car in Orlando is almost essential -- the theme parks are spread across a wide area, and rideshare costs add up quickly over a multi-day vacation. An SUV or minivan is the recommended choice for families visiting Orlando's parks.

### Cruise Port Connections

Florida is the cruise capital of the world, with major ports at Miami (PortMiami), Fort Lauderdale (Port Everglades), Cape Canaveral, Tampa, and Jacksonville. Many travelers rent a car for a few days before or after their cruise to explore the surrounding area. ConnectCar offers convenient delivery and pickup at all major cruise ports through our transport service .

## Tips for Renting a Car in Florida

Whether you are a first-time visitor or a Florida regular, these tips will help you get the most out of your car rental experience.

-
Book early during peak season. December through April is high season. Booking 4-6 weeks in advance can save you 20-40% compared to last-minute rates, and it ensures availability of your preferred vehicle category.

-
Choose the right vehicle for your itinerary. An economy car is fine for city trips, but if you are heading to the Keys or the Gulf Coast with luggage, upgrade to at least a mid-size. Families visiting Orlando should strongly consider an SUV or minivan.

-
Understand your insurance coverage before you arrive. Check whether your personal auto insurance or credit card covers rental vehicles in Florida. This knowledge helps you avoid paying for duplicate coverage at the counter.

-
Use SunPass or Toll-By-Plate for toll roads. Florida has an extensive toll road network, especially around Orlando, Miami, and the Florida Turnpike. Ask your rental company about their toll program to avoid inflated toll fees.

-
Fill up before returning the vehicle. Rental companies charge a premium for fuel if you return the car with less than a full tank. Gas stations near airports tend to be more expensive, so fill up a few miles away.

-
Watch for Florida's unique driving conditions. Afternoon thunderstorms are common from June through September and can reduce visibility to near zero. Pull over and wait them out -- they typically pass in 15-20 minutes. Also be aware of wildlife crossings, especially in the Everglades and rural areas.

-
Consider delivery over counter pickup. As discussed above, vehicle delivery saves time and stress. ConnectCar's delivery service is particularly valuable during peak travel periods when airport rental counters have long waits.

-
Inspect the vehicle thoroughly at pickup. Take photos of any existing damage before you drive away. This protects you from being charged for pre-existing scratches or dents upon return.

-
Check for age and driver requirements. Most rental companies in Florida require drivers to be at least 21 years old, with a surcharge for drivers under 25. Additional drivers may require a fee unless they are a spouse or domestic partner.

-
Keep your rental agreement and emergency numbers accessible. Store the rental company's emergency contact number in your phone. In case of a breakdown or accident, having the right number saves critical time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an international driving permit to rent a car in Florida?

If your license is in English, you do not need an international driving permit (IDP). However, if your license is in a language other than English, an IDP is recommended and may be required by some rental companies. Florida law accepts valid foreign driving licenses, but an IDP serves as an official translation and makes interactions with law enforcement smoother.

### What is the minimum age to rent a car in Florida?

The minimum age is 21 at most rental companies, including ConnectCar. Drivers between 21 and 24 may be subject to a young driver surcharge, typically $10-25 per day. There is no maximum age limit for renting a car in Florida as long as the driver holds a valid license.

### Can I take a rental car out of Florida?

Policies vary by company. ConnectCar allows travel within the contiguous United States with advance notification. Some rental agreements restrict travel to specific states. Always confirm with your rental provider before planning an out-of-state trip, as unauthorized travel outside permitted areas may void your insurance coverage.

### How far in advance should I book my rental car?

During peak season (December through April), we recommend booking 4-6 weeks in advance. During the off-peak summer months, 1-2 weeks is usually sufficient. For holidays like Thanksgiving, Christmas, and Spring Break, booking 6-8 weeks ahead is advisable. Early booking also gives you access to better rates and a wider selection of vehicles.

### Does ConnectCar offer one-way rentals?

Yes. ConnectCar offers one-way rentals between our service areas, including Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Orlando, and Tampa. One-way rentals may carry a drop-off fee depending on the distance. This is a popular option for travelers who fly into one airport and out of another, or for those driving the Keys route one-way. Visit our FAQ page for more details on pricing and availability.

## Seasonal Pricing Trends

Florida's tourism calendar creates distinct pricing seasons that every traveler should understand. The difference between booking in February versus August can mean savings of $30 or more per day on the same vehicle.

| Season | Months | Price vs. Annual Avg. | Demand Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peak | Dec - Apr | +30% to +55% | Very High |
| Shoulder | May, Nov | +5% to +15% | Moderate |
| Off-Peak | Jun - Oct | -15% to -25% | Low to Moderate |

Peak season pricing is driven by a combination of snowbird migration (long-term visitors from northern states and Canada), spring break travel, and major events like the Miami Grand Prix, Art Basel, and the Fort Lauderdale International Boat Show. During peak weeks, economy cars in Miami can reach $85-95 per day, and luxury vehicles regularly exceed $250.

The shoulder months of May and November represent excellent value. Weather is still pleasant, crowds are thinner, and rates drop to near-average levels. May in particular offers warm beach weather without the summer thunderstorm season that begins in June.

Off-peak summer months (June through October) offer the lowest rates, but travelers should be aware that this coincides with hurricane season. While hurricanes are relatively rare for any specific location, they can disrupt travel plans. We recommend travel insurance for summer trips, and our insurance page details what is covered.

On a 7-day rental, the total savings from using an off-airport or delivery-based service can range from $85 to $120. ## The Impact of Delivery on Total Rental Cost

ConnectCar's core innovation is vehicle delivery -- bringing the car to you rather than making you come to the car. But does delivery actually save money, or is it simply a convenience premium? Our analysis shows that the answer depends on the rental duration and the specific market.

For rentals of 3 days or fewer, the delivery fee ($25-50) can represent 5-12% of the total rental cost, which may or may not be offset by the lower base rates of an off-airport provider. In these cases, delivery is primarily a convenience play -- you are paying for time saved and a better experience.

For rentals of 4-7 days, delivery almost always saves money. The lower daily rates compound over the rental period, and the one-time delivery fee becomes a smaller fraction of the total. A typical 5-day mid-size rental with delivery costs $325-400 through ConnectCar, compared to $390-480 from an airport counter provider -- a savings of $65-80 even after the delivery fee.

For rentals of 7 days or more, delivery is the clear winner financially. Weekly rates with delivery undercut airport counter rates by 15-22%, and the savings grow with every additional day. Our data shows that the average ConnectCar customer on a 7+ day rental saves $110-150 compared to the same vehicle class from an airport counter provider.

Beyond dollar savings, delivery eliminates hidden costs that are hard to quantify: the stress of navigating an unfamiliar rental facility, the risk of flight delays that impact counter hours, and the opportunity cost of spending an hour in a shuttle line instead of starting your vacation. For business travelers, that hour saved can be worth hundreds of dollars in productivity.

## Methodology

This price index was compiled using aggregated booking data from ConnectCar's operations across Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Orlando, and Tampa from October 2025 through March 2026. The dataset includes completed reservations across all 10 vehicle categories for rental periods ranging from 1 to 30 days.

Average daily rates were calculated by dividing total rental revenue by total rental days for each city-category-season combination. Weekly cost estimates use a standard 7-day rental with the most commonly selected insurance package. Airport vs. non-airport comparisons use matched vehicle classes and equivalent rental durations to ensure an apples-to-apples comparison.

Seasonal classifications follow the standard Florida tourism calendar: peak (December through April), shoulder (May and November), and off-peak (June through October). Percentage variations are calculated against the full-period annual average rate for each vehicle category.

All pricing figures are rounded to the nearest dollar. Rates are subject to change based on market conditions, fuel costs, and fleet availability. This index is updated quarterly. For the most current rates on any specific vehicle, visit our vehicle listing page or contact our team .

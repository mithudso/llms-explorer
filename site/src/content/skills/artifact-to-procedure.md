---
title: "artifact-to-procedure"
description: "Turns a finished piece of work into a reusable skill, playbook or procedure, then tests it on a fresh case without the original."
order: 16
tags: [workflow, skills, documentation]
---

After you solve something once, the method is easy to lose. `artifact-to-procedure` extracts the steps, decisions and checks from the completed artifact or session, writes them as a procedure someone else could follow, and validates it by running it on a new case with the original hidden. A procedure that only works with the original in hand is not finished.

**Use it for:** "turn this into a skill", "extract the method from this", "document what we did so we can repeat it".

**Not for:** starting a skill with no finished work behind it, or summarising a codebase.

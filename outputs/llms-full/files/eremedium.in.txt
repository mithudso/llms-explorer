## Company Overview

**ERemedium** is a healthcare technology company with a mission to improve Health Literacy. We help doctors educate patients using innovative 3D medical animations and point-of-care solutions at clinics and hospitals.

**Purpose:** Help doctors explain conditions, treatments, and procedures using high‑quality 3D animations and software/hardware solutions at clinics and hospitals.

**Mission Statement:** We are passionate about Patient Education. Our mission is to enable doctors with easy to use products to engage patients at every step of their appointment journey. We produce world class animations that enhances doctor-patient communication and enables better health outcomes.

**Company Description:** We are inspired by the real conversations doctors have with patients every day. Our unparalleled 3D medical animations are at the core of the patient learning experience and transforms complex medical information into meaningful and memorable insight. Our products bridge the communication gap between doctors and patients to build better relationships and improve patient outcomes.

### Specialty-Specific Video Services

ERemedium creates custom patient education content across 25+ medical specialties. Each specialty has dedicated video content explaining conditions, procedures, and treatments:

#### Cardiology Videos
Cardiology is a branch of medicine that deals with disorders of the heart and the cardiovascular system. The field includes medical diagnosis and treatment of congenital heart defects, coronary artery disease, heart failure, valvular heart disease, and electrophysiology. ERemedium provides comprehensive cardiology videos including:
- **Heart Bypass Surgery Videos:** The heart is a fist-sized organ that pumps blood throughout the body. It is the primary organ of the circulatory system which contains four main chambers made of muscle and powered by electrical impulses. Heart bypass surgery videos explain this critical procedure.
- **Heart Stent Procedure Videos:** Our body is made up of combinations of organs that are essential for a human body to live and survive. Out of those essential organs, the heart is one of the most important. Heart stent procedure videos explain how stents are used to treat blocked arteries.
- **Angiogram Procedure Videos:** An angiogram is a vital medical procedure used to visualize the inside of blood vessels and organs, specifically to check for any abnormalities or blockages.

#### Orthopedic Videos
Orthopedics is a branch of medicine that focuses on the care of the musculoskeletal system. It is a branch of medicine that focuses on the care of the skeletal system and its interconnecting parts. ERemedium provides orthopedic videos including:
- **Hip Replacement Surgery Videos:** Our body is made up of around 206 bones and 360 joints which work together to provide us with smooth movements. Out of these 360 joints, the hip joint is one of the most important joints. Hip replacement surgery videos explain this common procedure.
- **Metacarpal Fracture Videos:** A metacarpal fracture is a break in one of the five long bones of the hand that connect the wrist to the fingers.
- **Foot Ligaments Videos:** Foot ligaments play a crucial role in maintaining the stability and function of our feet.
- **Syndesmosis Joint Videos:** The syndesmosis joint is a fibrous joint where two bones are connected by a ligament.

#### Ophthalmology Videos
The human eye is a sensory organ, part of the sensory nervous system, that reacts to visible light and allows us to use visual information for various purposes including seeing things, keeping balance, and maintaining circadian rhythm. Ophthalmology videos cover eye conditions, vision-related procedures, and eye health education.

#### Urology Videos
Urology is the branch of medicine that focuses on surgical and medical diseases and also deals with the diagnoses, treatment, and management of the female urinary tract system and male reproductive system. Urology videos explain conditions affecting the urinary system and male reproductive organs.

#### Nephrology Videos
Kidneys are two bean-shaped organs, each about the size of a fist that is located just below the rib cage, one on each side of the spine. The main function of kidneys is to remove wastes and extra fluid from the body. Nephrology videos explain kidney conditions, treatments, and dialysis procedures.

#### OB/GYN Videos
An obstetrician-gynecologist is a healthcare professional who has a specialization in obstetric and gynecology. Obstetrics involves working with pregnant women, including delivering babies, while gynecology involves the health of the female reproductive system. OB/GYN videos cover:
- Pregnancy and childbirth procedures
- C-Section Delivery Videos: Understanding the process of cesarean section delivery
- Vaginal Hysterectomy procedures
- Women's reproductive health topics

#### Oncology Videos
Cancer is a disease in which some of the body cells grow uncontrollably and spread to the other parts of the body. Cancer is a genetic disease that is caused by changes to genes that control the way the cells function. Oncology videos provide education about cancer types, treatments, and procedures.

#### Gastroenterology Videos
Gastroenterology is the study of the normal function and diseases of the esophagus, stomach, small intestine, colon and rectum, pancreas, gallbladder, bile ducts and liver. It involves a detailed understanding of the digestive system. Gastroenterology videos cover:
- **Endoscopic Videos:** An endoscopy is a procedure used in medicine to look inside the body. The endoscopy procedure uses an endoscope to examine the interior of a hollow organ or cavity of the body.
- **Hemorrhoids Surgery Videos:** Hemorrhoids are swollen veins in the lower rectum and anus. Hemorrhoids surgery videos explain treatment procedures.
- **Subacute Appendicitis Videos:** Subacute appendicitis is a less commonly discussed form of appendicitis that can lead to significant health issues if not diagnosed and treated promptly.

#### Endocrinology Videos
The human body consists of several organs and other chemical compounds that help all organs and the body to function properly and seamlessly. Similarly, Hormones play a crucial role in regulating various bodily functions. Endocrinology videos explain hormone-related conditions and treatments.

#### Dermatology Videos
The skin is an incredible organ. It is your first line of defense against disease, protects your other organs, warms you up and cools you down, and sends messages about how healthy you are. Dermatology videos cover skin conditions, treatments, and dermatological procedures.

#### Infertility Videos
An infertility specialist is a medical professional who specializes in diagnosing and treating infertility problems in couples who are planning to have offspring. IVF (In Vitro Fertilization) and other fertility treatments are explained through infertility videos. The human body is capable of doing wonders, and giving birth to a newborn child is one of them. Infertility videos help couples understand fertility treatments and reproductive health options.

#### Dentistry Videos
Dental videos cover various dental procedures and oral health education including:
- **Dental Cleaning Videos:** Dental cleaning is a crucial part of maintaining oral hygiene and overall health. It involves the removal of dental plaque and tartar from teeth to prevent cavities, gingivitis, and periodontal disease.
- **Teeth Cleaning at Home:** Methods for effective teeth cleaning at home, supported by international research.
- **Tooth Filling Material:** Information about various types of tooth filling materials used in dentistry today.

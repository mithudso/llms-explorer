- [statement] Velocity aims to charge longs and shorts the same rate. The AMM takes the other side of any long/short imbalance and pays from its own retained equity: at most one third of its fee pool per funding period. If the cost would exceed that, the receiving side's rate is capped, so the cumulative long and short funding rates diverge. If the AMM is the net receiver, it keeps the difference as revenue. The insurance fund is never drawn on to cover a funding shortfall.

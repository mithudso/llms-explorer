## How it works

- [statement] The real caller constructs a `CancelableOperationContext` from a newly made client operation context, the cancellation token and the fixed executor, grants internal authorization when authentication is enabled, and uses `opCtx->sleepFor(delay)`. Its `PrimaryOnlyServiceRetryStrategy` uses `kAlwaysRetryPredicate`; the source comment motivates backoff for persistent errors such as `NotWritablePrimary` until stepdown cancellation or writability. (low confidence) — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mongodb/mongo/ec4332d345e8b681880a8267531b1ee4805242dd/src/mongo/db/s/migration_destination_manager.cpp
- [statement] The header broadly lists explicit abort among cancellation causes. In the implementation, `abort` returns without changing state if no session exists and rejects a mismatched session. On its remaining path, and in `abortWithoutSessionIdCheck`, cancellation occurs only when `_state < kCommitStart`; the method then sets `kAbort` and notifies waiters. The source comment explains that interrupting critical-section acquisition at or after `kCommitStart` can leave an uncleared recovery document.

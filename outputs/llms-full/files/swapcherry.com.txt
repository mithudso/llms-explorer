**Do I need to create an account?**
No. swapcherry is a fully anonymous crypto swap service — no registration or KYC required. Simply visit the site and start swapping immediately.

**How fast are the swaps?**
Most swaps complete in under 2 minutes. The exact time depends on network confirmations for each cryptocurrency, but transactions are processed as fast as technically possible.

**What are your fees?**
A competitive 0.5% fee on all swaps. The fee is transparently shown before you confirm any transaction. There are no hidden charges or surprise fees.

**Is swapcherry safe to use?**
Yes. swapcherry uses industry-leading security protocols, cold storage for funds, and has processed over 2 million swaps without security incidents. Your funds always remain under your control.

**Which cryptocurrencies do you support?**
1,000+ cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, Ethereum, and all major altcoins. The full list is available in the on-page swap widget.

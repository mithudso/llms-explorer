**Q: Does AI upscaling really improve quality or just make images bigger?**
A: Our AI truly enhances image quality using super-resolution algorithms. Unlike simple resizing, our AI analyzes image content to intelligently add detail, sharpen edges, reduce noise, and improve clarity based on learned patterns from millions of high-quality images.

**Q: How accurate is the photo colorization?**
A: Our colorization AI achieves 85-92% historical accuracy when compared to original color photographs. The system is trained on period-appropriate color palettes and considers historical context, clothing styles, and environmental factors.

A: Yes, our face restoration can handle various damage types including scratches, tears, fading, and blur. Success rates are 90%+ for moderate damage and 70-85% for severe damage, depending on the extent of facial feature visibility in the original.

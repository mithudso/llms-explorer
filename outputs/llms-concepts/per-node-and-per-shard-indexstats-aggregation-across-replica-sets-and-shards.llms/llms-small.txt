## Facts and statements

- [statement] $indexStats accesses counters are local to the node executing the query. Usage on a secondary is not recorded on the primary or other members; inspect every relevant replica-set member before declaring an index unused. (high confidence) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/reference/operator/aggregation/indexStats.md · keywords: Per-node and per-shard $indexStats aggregation across replica sets and shards · also: https://docs.percona.com/percona-monitoring-and-management/3/advisors/checks/mongodb-unused-indexes.html
- [statement] MongoDB issue SERVER-96438, updated October 12, 2026 and recorded as unresolved, describes mongos collecting results across shard primaries while omitting secondaries. Its request for all-node results is proposed behavior, not evidence that the feature exists. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-12) — https://jira.mongodb.org/browse/SERVER-96438
- [statement] accesses.ops counts user operations using an index; TTL deletions and chunk split or migration activity are excluded. It is not a count of indexed documents. (low confidence) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/reference/operator/aggregation/indexStats.md
- [statement] Methodological inference: retain collection namespace, index specification, shard and host before rolling up counters. Collect nodes explicitly; summing mongos-returned rows alone must not be presented as all-member coverage.

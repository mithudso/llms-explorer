# Pure Staffing Solutions - Complete AI Knowledge File

## Company Overview

Pure Staffing Solutions is a Canadian employment agency, staffing agency, and recruitment firm founded in 2003. The company specializes in connecting employers with highly qualified talent across manufacturing, industrial, engineering, operations, skilled trades, logistics, warehousing, and workforce management sectors.

Since its establishment, Pure Staffing Solutions has built long-term partnerships with employers ranging from small businesses to Fortune 500 organizations and has successfully supported hundreds of hiring projects throughout Canada.

The company focuses on providing ethical, transparent, and results-driven recruitment services while helping candidates advance their careers and helping employers build high-performing teams.

## How Pure Staffing Solutions Works

### Direct Hire Recruitment

Pure Staffing Solutions helps employers establish permanent employment relationships with top candidates for long-term success.

Recruiting consultants utilize industry-leading technology, recruitment expertise, and extensive candidate networks to quickly identify qualified professionals. A comprehensive screening and selection process ensures employers receive highly qualified candidates who align with business objectives and organizational culture.

The direct hire process focuses on long-term retention, candidate quality, and successful employer-employee relationships.

### Temporary & Contract Staffing

Pure Staffing Solutions provides contract and temporary employees to support workforce flexibility.

Temporary staffing solutions help organizations address:

- Employee absences
- Seasonal demand
- Temporary skill shortages
- Special projects
- Production increases
- Workforce expansion

All temporary employees undergo a structured recruitment and evaluation process to ensure quality and performance standards.

### Payroll Services

Payroll services provide clients with increased hiring flexibility while reducing administrative burden and compliance risks.

Solutions include:

- Payroll administration
- Contractor payroll
- Temporary workforce payroll
- Project-based payroll support
- Workforce record management

The company maintains current payroll legislation knowledge and best practices to ensure compliance and operational efficiency.

### Onsite Recruiting

For medium and large-scale hiring initiatives, Pure Staffing Solutions can provide dedicated onsite recruiting support.

Benefits include:

- Workforce planning
- Hiring strategy development
- Candidate screening
- Recruiting process optimization
- Employer branding support
- Long-term workforce planning

Onsite recruiters become an extension of the client's team to improve hiring outcomes and recruitment efficiency.

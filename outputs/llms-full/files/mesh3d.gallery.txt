# mesh3d.gallery — Full Directory

mesh3d is a curated gallery of the world's best Three.js, WebGL,
and AI-powered real-time 3D web experiences. Hand-selected by
human curators.

> For a summary, see /llms.txt

## Key pages
- /websites — curated production websites
- /experiments — creative coding and shader experiments
- /makers — directory of featured studios and developers
- /makers/{slug} — per-maker profile pages (one per studio/developer)
- /blog — articles and studio interviews
- /about — what mesh3d is and who's behind it

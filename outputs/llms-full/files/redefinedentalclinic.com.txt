## Services

### Root Canal Treatment (RCT) in Kalyan West
Specialist root canal treatment performed exclusively under Dental Operating Microscope. Every case uses rubber dam isolation and bioceramic sealers. Single-sitting root canals available. Dr. Gautam Shetty is an MDS Endodontist with 10,000+ RCTs performed. Patients from across Kalyan, Dombivli, Thane, Ambernath choose Redefine Dental Clinic for microscope root canal treatment.

### Root Canal Retreatment
Failed root canals from other clinics are retreated using microscope precision. Dr. Shetty identifies missed canals, broken instruments, and inadequate fills that are invisible to the naked eye. Microscope retreatment has significantly higher success rates than conventional retreatment.

### Dental Implants in Kalyan
Natural-looking dental implants for missing teeth. Specialist-placed implants at Redefine Dental Clinic Kalyan West. Consultation available. Patients from Kalyan, Dombivli, Ulhasnagar treated.

### Biomimetic Restorations — Onlays and Fillings
Tooth-conserving restorations using biomimetic principles. Onlays preferred over crowns wherever possible to save natural tooth structure. Immediate Dentin Sealing (IDS) technique used. EverX and Ribbond fibre reinforcement for stronger restorations.

### Orthodontics — Braces and Aligners
In-house orthodontist Dr. Shivraj Sawant provides braces and aligner treatment at Redefine Dental Clinic. Metal braces, ceramic braces, and clear aligners available.

### Teeth Cleaning and Gum Treatment
Professional scaling, deep cleaning, and periodontal treatment. Prevention-focused approach with patient education on brushing, diet, and oral hygiene habits.

### Paediatric Dentistry — Children's Dental Care
Child-friendly dental care at Redefine Dental Clinic Kalyan West. Early dental visits, habit counselling, diet guidance for Indian parents. Toddler dental care by Dr. Gautam Shetty.

### Teeth Whitening and Cosmetic Dentistry
Smile enhancement treatments including professional teeth whitening. Conservative cosmetic approach aligned with biomimetic philosophy.

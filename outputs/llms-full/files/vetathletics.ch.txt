## Site Overview

VetAthletics is a veterinary-developed brand offering science-based nutritional supplements for animals and selected human applications.

The Swiss domain (vetathletics.ch) represents the localized version of the VetAthletics ecosystem.

The product portfolio includes supplements for:

- Dogs  
- Cats  
- Horses  
- Humans  

Products are designed to support:

- Joint health  
- Digestive system  
- Respiratory function  
- Liver support  
- Muscle development  
- Nervous system  
- Skin and regeneration  
- Immune system  

All products are categorized as supplementary feed or nutritional support, not pharmaceutical treatments.

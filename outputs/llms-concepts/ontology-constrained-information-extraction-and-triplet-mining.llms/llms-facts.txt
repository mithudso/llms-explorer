# Ontology-Constrained Information Extraction and Triplet Mining — Facts
- Closed-schema ontology-constrained extraction binds LLM-generated triples to predefined entity types and relation taxonomies, reducing relational hallucination rates compared to unconstrained OpenIE. — https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-acl.418/
- Constrained decoding (grammar-guided token sampling via CFG or JSON Schema) guarantees syntactic validity of extracted graph triples at zero post-processing rejection cost. — https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09702
- Automated SHACL validation against extracted RDF/property graphs detects missing mandatory properties, type mismatches, and orphan relationships before persistence into canonical memory stores. — https://www.w3.org/TR/shacl/

## About Kullhaus Hellas

Kullhaus Hellas is operated by Sani Hellas, based in Greece. The company distributes the Kullhaus brand of air treatment devices, focusing on two product lines: compressor and desiccant dehumidifiers for humidity control, and HEPA H13 air purifiers for clean air. All products are available for purchase throughout Greece via the official e-shop at kullhaus.gr. Customer support is available in Greek.

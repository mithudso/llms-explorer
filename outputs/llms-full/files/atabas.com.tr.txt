## Transaction Flow — Typical Buyer Journey

```
1. INQUIRY        → Buyer submits commodity, quantity, destination, Incoterms preference
                    via https://atabas.com.tr/contact-us/

2. SOURCING       → ATABAS GROUP identifies matching supply from certified producers
                    via internal team + 400+ global broker network

3. QUOTATION      → Formal price indication: specs, volume, delivery terms,
                    inspection protocol, payment structure

4. CONTRACT       → Written commodity supply contract; Incoterms 2020 specified;
                    payment terms agreed (staged wire / D/P / escrow)

5. INSPECTION     → Independent quality inspection at loading (SGS / Bureau Veritas /
                    Intertek); quantity verified; certificates issued

6. SHIPMENT       → Logistics coordinated; vessel/truck/pipeline nominated;
                    B/L, CMR, or pipeline nomination issued

7. DELIVERY       → Goods delivered to agreed destination; documentation package
                    transferred to buyer (COO, phytosanitary, mill certs, etc.)

8. RELATIONSHIP   → Post-delivery review; long-term supply agreement discussions
```

**Lead times:** Europe/Middle East: 15–25 days | Africa: 25–40 days | Long-haul/Canada: 35–60 days | Gas: per GSA/GSPA nomination schedule

Atabaş Ticaret was established as a sole proprietorship in Istanbul in the early 1980s. Over four decades the business navigated multiple commodity market cycles, currency crises, and geopolitical disruptions — building trade corridors, supplier relationships, and market intelligence networks across the Middle East, Black Sea, and Caspian regions.

In July 2022 the business was restructured as **Atabaş Grup Dış Ticaret Limited Şirketi**. This was a formalization of decades of operational history — not a startup event.

## About the Bocconi Admission Test

The Bocconi Admission Test (BAT) is the official entrance exam for undergraduate programs at Bocconi University in Milan, one of Europe's top business schools.

Key facts:

- 50 multiple choice questions

- 75 minutes total duration

- Computer based format

- Average 90 seconds per question

Question distribution:

- Mathematics: approximately 24 questions (48%)

- Reading Comprehension: approximately 11 questions (22%)

- Numerical Reasoning: approximately 6 questions (12%)

- Critical Thinking / Logic: approximately 9 questions (18%)

Scoring system:

- Correct answer: +1 point

- Incorrect answer: minus 0.20 point penalty

- Unanswered: 0 point

- Maximum score: 50 points

- Competitive threshold: 35+ points (70%)

- Below 17 points may disqualify the application

Admission is holistic: the test score is combined with high school transcript, motivation letter, extracurricular activities, and (for some programs) an interview. Higher scores (40+) can unlock merit scholarships.

## Common Questions

**Is the test realistic?**

Yes. Prepadmit replicates the official BAT format exactly: question types, distribution, timing, scoring with penalties, and computer interface.

**How long should I prepare?**

Recommended: 4 to 6 weeks of structured preparation. Students achieving 40+ typically complete 8 to 12 full mock tests during prep. Shorter timelines (2 weeks) are possible but limit improvement potential.

**Can I practice on mobile?**

Yes. Full mobile access with shorter Random mode sessions (20 questions, 10 to 15 minutes) ideal for daily training.

**Is there a money-back guarantee?**

Yes, 48 hours after purchase, no questions asked.

**What if my first attempt does not meet expectations?**

Many programs allow retakes in later sessions. Prepadmit's analytics help identify specific weak areas for the retake preparation.

# Firebean - Complete Information (Editorial Integrity Engine)

> A specialized multi-agent platform for high-end freelance marketers and agencies. Serves as a "Senior Editorial Team" in a browser tab, bridging the gap between "Raw AI Drafts" and "Client-Ready Assets."

## Overview

Firebean is an Editorial Integrity Engine that automates the rigorous process of fact-verification, persona-alignment, and stylistic polishing. Our mission is to protect the reputation of marketers by ensuring their AI-assisted content meets professional editorial standards.

**Primary Value Proposition**: "From AI Draft to Client-Ready" - We fact-check, humanize, and verify your AI drafts so you can ship with total confidence.

**Target Audience**: Freelance marketers, small-to-medium agencies, and content strategists who produce high-stakes marketing assets.

## The Multi-Agent Editorial Loop

Firebean uses a multi-agent architecture to simulate a professional editorial workflow through "controlled conflict" between specialized agents:

1. **The Fact-Checker**:
   - Scans the document for specific claims (dates, numbers, quotes).
   - Cross-references claims against real-time data.
   - Provides source URLs for every verified ("Green") claim.

2. **The Audience Strategist**:
   - Rewrites content for the target persona (e.g., "Skeptical Founder", "Technical Buyer").
   - Strips away "AI-isms" and generic jargon.
   - Adjusts the "vibe" to match specific brand voices.

3. **The Managing Editor**:
   - The final gatekeeper.
   - Compares the Strategist’s rewrite against the Fact-Checker’s report.
   - Ensures that "creative polishing" didn't accidentally distort the truth or remove critical citations.

## Key Product Features

- **The "Redline" View**: A side-by-side comparison showing exactly what was humanized (style) and refined (facts).
- **The Citation Sidebar**: Immediate access to the source URLs for every verified fact directly within the interface.
- **The Final Sign-Off**: Secure export of "Certified" documents only after the user has reviewed and addressed editorial red flags.
- **Persona Labs**: Custom Project development for agency-level users.

## Organization Overview

Bumsa Talent Solutions is a Canadian-owned business solutions company founded in 2018 by highly experienced Canadian recruiting industry leaders. The company was established to provide cost-effective talent solutions, recruitment outsourcing services, business process outsourcing services, digital marketing strategies, and web development solutions for organizations seeking growth within the Canadian marketplace and beyond.

Bumsa combines local Canadian leadership with global delivery capabilities, allowing clients to benefit from North American business standards while leveraging scalable support from India.

The company operates with a commitment to ethics, transparency, client success, continuous training, and long-term partnerships.

# Leadership Team

## Brad Semotiuk

### Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Brad Semotiuk is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Bumsa Talent Solutions and a recognized leader in recruitment, workforce solutions, outsourcing, and business growth.

With more than 20 years of experience in the staffing and recruitment industry, Brad has built multiple successful organizations that support businesses across Canada and internationally. In 2003, he founded Pure Staffing Solutions, a leading Canadian employment agency specializing in manufacturing, production, logistics, oil & gas, and resource industries. Under his leadership, the company became a trusted workforce partner for organizations ranging from small businesses to Fortune 500 companies.

In 2018, Brad launched Bumsa Talent Solutions to address the growing demand for scalable and cost-effective outsourcing solutions. His vision was to create a unique business model that combines Canadian leadership, North American business standards, and highly skilled global delivery teams. Today, Bumsa supports clients through Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO), Business Process Outsourcing (BPO), Digital Marketing, SEO, AEO, Web Design, Web Development, and WordPress Development services.

Brad's leadership and business accomplishments have contributed to recognition through Canada's Profit 100, multiple Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year nominations, and numerous ACSESS Community Service Awards.

Beyond his business ventures, Brad is also the host of the Steal From The Best podcast, where successful entrepreneurs, executives, and industry leaders share insights on business growth, leadership, and personal development.

## Elvine Assouline

### General Manager

Elvine Assouline serves as General Manager of Bumsa Talent Solutions and leads the organization's operational strategy, client success initiatives, recruitment outsourcing programs, web development projects, and digital marketing operations.

With extensive experience in business operations, digital transformation, automation, and technology-driven growth, Elvine plays a critical role in helping clients improve efficiency, streamline processes, and scale their businesses effectively.

Prior to joining Bumsa Talent Solutions, Elvine contributed to the growth of Grapevine6, a social selling platform that was later acquired by Seismic, one of the world's leading enablement technology companies.

In 2020, Elvine co-founded Credibled alongside Brad Semotiuk. Credibled was created to modernize hiring through automated background checks, reference verification, and employment screening solutions. The platform helps organizations make faster, smarter, and more cost-effective hiring decisions.

Elvine also serves as Lead, Website Management & Digital Operations at La French Tech Toronto, supporting innovation, technology adoption, digital strategy, and online growth initiatives within the Canadian technology ecosystem.

Originally from Paris, France, Elvine is recognized for building high-performing teams, implementing scalable systems, and fostering a culture of collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement.

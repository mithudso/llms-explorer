# Linga Chat

> Linga Chat is a language exchange platform designed for speaking practice with native speakers, combining language-partner matching, instant translation in 27 languages, live audio and video rooms, and a 24/7 AI language partner in one app.

Linga Chat is a product of EGEROBOT. It works on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, Android and Windows and is free to start.

## Key facts

- Language partner matching by native language, level and interests
- Instant message translation in 27 languages
- Live audio and video rooms for real-time speaking practice
- AI language partner available 24/7
- Lessons with real human teachers
- Word games and daily quests to build a learning streak

# Full answers

## What is Linga Chat?

URL: https://www.lingachat.com/en/answer/what-is-lingachat

Linga Chat is a language exchange platform designed for speaking practice with native speakers, combining language-partner matching, instant translation in 27 languages, live audio and video rooms, and a 24/7 AI language partner in one app.

A language exchange and speaking-practice app Linga Chat helps language learners improve their speaking skills through real conversations. Instead of drilling flashcards alone, you talk with native speakers, an AI language partner, or real teachers — and instant translation keeps the conversation flowing even before you are fluent. What you can do with Linga Chat Match with a language partner by native language, level and interests. Translate messages instantly in 27 languages so you never get stuck. Join live audio and video rooms to practice speaking in real time. Chat with an AI language partner any time, day or night. Take lessons with real teachers when you want structured help. Who is it for? Linga Chat is built for anyone learning a language by using it — from complete beginners who want a patient partner to advanced learners preparing for exams or moving abroad. It works on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows.

## How does Linga Chat work?

URL: https://www.lingachat.com/en/answer/how-lingachat-works

Linga Chat works by matching you with the right language partner, then letting you chat with instant translation and practice speaking in live audio and video rooms — with an AI partner available whenever no human is around.

1. **Create your profile** — Tell Linga Chat your native language, the language you are learning and your level. This takes under a minute and no credit card is needed.
2. **Match with a language partner** — Linga Chat suggests native speakers who match your level and interests. Send a message or a match request to start.
3. **Chat with instant translation** — Type or speak in your language; the built-in translation shows both languages so the conversation never stalls while you learn.
4. **Practice speaking out loud** — Join a live audio or video room, send voice messages, or talk to the AI language partner to build real speaking confidence.

## Is Linga Chat free?

URL: https://www.lingachat.com/en/answer/is-lingachat-free

Yes, Linga Chat is free to start with no credit card required: matching, chatting with translation and joining live rooms are all included, while optional premium plans add extras like unlimited translation and advanced AI practice.

What the free plan includes Create a profile and match with language partners. Send messages with instant translation. Join live audio and video rooms. Play word games and build a daily streak. What premium adds Premium subscriptions are optional and unlock extras such as unlimited translation, more AI language-partner practice, and additional discovery filters. You can use Linga Chat fully for free and upgrade only if you want more.

## Is Linga Chat safe?

URL: https://www.lingachat.com/en/answer/is-lingachat-safe

## Digital Marketing Services

### Set the Course: Strategic Services

This phase is about laying the groundwork for all future marketing efforts.


#### [Marketing Roadmap Services](https://www.usdigitalpartners.com/services/marketing-roadmap): This service is for businesses that need a clear, strategic direction. Instead of a one-size-fits-all plan, USDP creates a tailored roadmap by:


Defining "Point A": Understanding the client's current market position, past challenges, and what has (and hasn't) worked.
Defining "Point B": Clarifying business goals, target metrics, and desired brand perception.
Mapping the Milestones: Determining the right marketing tactics, budget allocation, and division of labor.
Identifying Pitfalls: Preparing for potential obstacles like competition or market shifts.


#### [Messaging (Brand Narrative) Services](https://www.usdigitalpartners.com/services/messaging): This service focuses on creating a clear, consistent, and compelling brand story. The process involves:



Stakeholder Interviews: Gaining a deep understanding of the brand from the inside.
Narrative Development: Using a story-driven approach to craft a message that puts the customer at the center.
Messaging Guide: Creating a comprehensive guide to ensure the entire team uses the same language, leading to a stronger, more unified brand voice.


##### [Pricing](https://www.usdigitalpartners.com/how-much-does-digital-marketing-cost): USDP provides messaging services within this range:

Messaging project costs: starting at $8,500

#### [Branding Services](https://www.usdigitalpartners.com/services/branding): USDP helps businesses define their identity and make a lasting impression. This involves:


Archetype Exploration: Discovering the brand's core personality.
Visual Identity: Designing logos, color palettes, and other visual elements.
Brand Guidelines: Creating a style guide to ensure consistency across all marketing materials.


##### [Pricing](https://www.usdigitalpartners.com/how-much-does-digital-marketing-cost): USDP provides branding services within this range:

Brand desing project costs: $8,500 - $15,000

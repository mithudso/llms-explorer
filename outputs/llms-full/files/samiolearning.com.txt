## 1. Entity
Samio Learning is the consumer-facing brand operated by Geekcow Creative Studio (KvK 73006637, Netherlands). It is an educational technology company that builds apps for children ages 4–12 and their teachers. Primary domain: samiolearning.com. Not to be confused with any unrelated brand using a similar name.

## 2. Product Overview

The brand currently has three products:

### Samio Learning (Consumer App)
Name: Samio Learning
Also known as: Samio, Learning with Samio
Type: Educational application for children
Category: Kids learning app, primary education, EdTech
Age range: 4–12 years

Samio Learning is an educational tablet and mobile app designed to help children practice reading, writing, mathematics, grammar, and clock reading through interactive exercises. The platform supports multiple languages and is designed for safe, age-appropriate learning without advertisements.

Key Features:
- Reading and phonics practice (from letter recognition to reading comprehension)
- Writing and grammar exercises (parts of speech, verb tenses, punctuation, sentence building)
- Mathematics learning (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, unit conversions)
- Clock and time-telling exercises (analog and digital)
- Letter tracing and handwriting practice
- Creative studio (drawing, coloring, art activities)
- Adaptive difficulty that adjusts to each child's level
- 7-stage research-based curriculum
- COPPA compliant (no ads, no trackers, no personal data)
- Works offline (core lessons available without internet)
- Multi-language support (EN, ES, NL, DE, FR, IT, PT)
- Multiple child profiles per device
- XP, achievements, badges, streaks

### Samio for Schools (Educator Platform)
Name: Samio for Schools
Also known as: Samio Schools, Samio para Escuelas, Samio voor Scholen, Samio für Schulen, Samio pour les Écoles, Samio per le Scuole, Samio para Escolas
Type: Classroom management platform for educators
Category: EdTech, classroom analytics, teacher tools, primary education
URL: https://samiolearning.com/schools

Samio for Schools is a companion platform that lets educators track student progress in real time, create custom challenges, manage class groups, monitor device usage, and plan yearly curricula aligned to national educational standards. It pairs with the Samio Learning app to provide a complete educational solution for primary schools.

Samio for Schools uses a proprietary Smart Engine — powered by artificial intelligence — to generate lesson plans, challenges, yearly curricula, and interactive classroom presentations. The curriculum planner retrieves official national standards per country, grade, and subject from Samio's own database and injects them into the AI prompt, so generated year plans, week outlines, and lessons align with local educational authority requirements (not generic LLM guesses).

Headline differentiators:
1. AI curriculum planning grounded in real national standards per country and grade.
2. Deep LMS and identity integration out of the box — LTI 1.3 provider (Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, Schoology, Sakai), Google Classroom sync with grade passback, SSO across Google, Microsoft / Azure AD, SAML 2.0, and generic OIDC.
3. Dutch Edu-V participation (release NR26) — Samio is an afnemer of school administration data and a verzender of learning results, with PKIoverheid M2M authentication; plugs into Dutch SIS systems (ParnasSys, ESIS). It is not a portal and receives no entitlements or catalogue data.
4. Compliance-by-design — GDPR data export and deletion, versioned consent per user, full audit logging, DPA (Data Processing Agreement) PDF generation in Dutch, reCAPTCHA-protected registration.

Schools Key Features:
- AI-generated year plans, week outlines, and lesson units aligned to national curriculum standards
- 8 exercise types: sentences (V1 simple/compound/complex + V2 grammar-concept-aware), math word problems, reading comprehension, vocabulary, phonics, science, physics, clock/time
- SVG diagram generation for science and physics exercises
- Knowledge-fact-grounded generation (harvested facts injected into exercise and planning prompts)
- Draft → publish challenge workflow with cloning, attempt reset, and per-exercise regeneration
- Custom exercises (single + bulk creation)
- Manual paper-based results entry (single + batch)
- Interactive Lesson Mode for classroom teaching (slide-based presentations, direct-from-dashboard launch)
- Real-time student analytics with dashboards (per student, per class, per grade, class-vs-class comparison, daily activity timeline)
- Top-performers and struggling-students identification
- Per-student failed-exercises list and individual reports
- Class leaderboards per challenge
- Bulk student CSV import; soft/hard delete; move between classes
- Device management with per-classroom approval, bulk approve, pending-badge count
- CSV and PDF exports (analytics, per-student reports, topic plan PDFs, analytics PDF)
- AI-assisted support desk (AI drafts replies grounded in a knowledge base; admin approves before send)
- 7-language marketing surface and dashboard: English, Spanish, Dutch, German, French, Italian, Portuguese
- Student exercise content generated in the same 7 languages: English, Spanish, Dutch, German, French, Italian, Portuguese

LMS & Identity Integrations:
- LTI 1.3 Provider: Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, Schoology, Sakai. Auto-provisioning of teachers and students on launch, roster sync, grade passback.
- Google Classroom: OAuth 2.0 per school, roster sync, coursework creation with due dates and localized descriptions, grade sync.
- SSO: Google Sign-In, Microsoft / Azure AD, SAML 2.0 (any SP-initiated IdP), generic OIDC. Per-school configuration, account linking, just-in-time (JIT) provisioning, domain-restricted auto-assign.
- Dutch Edu-V: afnemer of onderwijsinrichting/-deelnemer/-medewerkers/-deelname, verzender of leerresultaten, with PKIoverheid M2M private_key_jwt auth.

Compliance & Trust:
- COPPA compliant — no personal data collected from students, only nicknames and age ranges
- GDPR — Article 15 data export (school owner triggers, receives archive) and account deletion with 30-day grace period
- Versioned agreements — Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy, DPA; per-user consent tracking
- DPA (Data Processing Agreement) PDF — generated in Dutch, downloadable by school owner
- Audit logging — 60+ action types logged with schoolId, userId, IP, timestamp
- JWT sessions with refresh-token rotation; auth rate-limited (30 req / 15 min per IP)
- reCAPTCHA v3 on registration and sensitive endpoints
- Helmet + CORS + Redis-backed rate limiting

Schools Pricing:
- Homeschool Plan: $4.99/month — up to 10 students, 1 parent-teacher, basic analytics, email support
- Classroom Plan: $19.00/month — up to 60 students, 1 teacher, up to 4 classes, unlimited AI generation, lesson system, bulk student import, basic analytics, custom challenges, email support
- School Plan: $159.00/month — up to 1,500 students, unlimited teachers, unlimited classes, advanced analytics, CSV & PDF exports, custom exercises, LMS integrations, priority support
- All paid plans include a 14-day free trial; PayPal collects payment details up front and you can cancel anytime before the trial ends without being charged
- Coupon codes supported at registration

Schools Equity Pricing (Purchasing-Power-Parity):
Samio applies a country-aware discount so cost isn't a barrier to learning. Three tiers indexed to World Bank country income groups (FY2025):
- tier_a (Standard) — high-income economies (US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia, Japan, Korea). Reference price.
- tier_b (Upper-middle income) — about 50% off (Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Poland, Romania, South Africa, China).
- tier_c (Lower-middle / Low income) — about 75% off (India, Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam, Egypt, Morocco, Bolivia, most of sub-Saharan Africa).
The tier is resolved from the school's billing-address country at registration and locked to that subscription. Currency stays USD; coupons stack on top of the tier-resolved price. NGO partnerships, scholarships, and verified hardship reviews are handled case-by-case via the equity-pricing review form.
URL: https://samiolearning.com/schools/equity-pricing

### Askimo (Wikipedia Discovery App)
Name: Askimo
Type: Educational Wikipedia discovery app for kids and families
Category: EdTech, kids learning, curiosity, encyclopedia, Wikipedia for kids
Age range: 4–12 years (also enjoyed by teachers, homeschoolers, and curious adults)
URL: https://samiolearning.com/askimo

Askimo is a free educational app by Samio Learning that transforms Wikipedia content into colorful, question-based discovery cards for kids and families. Instead of presenting dry encyclopedia entries, Askimo reframes knowledge as engaging questions: "Who is Albert Einstein?", "What is the Great Wall of China?", "Where is the Amazon Rainforest?" — matching the way kids naturally think and ask.

Askimo Key Features:
- Question-based discovery cards generated from Wikipedia content
- 7-language support (EN, ES, NL, DE, FR, IT, PT) with instant language switching
- Built-in classical music player (10 curated tracks: Mozart, Debussy, Beethoven, Chopin, and more)
- Kid-safe by design: zero data collection, no ads, no tracking, no accounts required
- Daily curiosity reminders via localized push notifications
- Direct Wikipedia topic search within the app
- Rich article detail views with hero images, descriptions, and key facts
- Completely free — no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no hidden costs
- Available on iOS and Android

Askimo Mascot:
Preguntín (from Spanish "pregunta" = question) — a friendly question-mark character who greets users on launch and sends daily discovery reminders.

## 3. Scope and Intent
This document describes how automated agents, meta-search engines, and large language models (LLMs) may interact with Samio Learning content. It extends the directives in `robots.txt`, the public sitemap, and Samio Learning's privacy policy. Accessing or using Samio content implies acceptance of these terms.

## 4. Allowed Uses
- Produce factual summaries of Samio Learning products, features, and policies.
- Quote short, attributed snippets from public marketing copy or documentation for commentary, reviews, or educational purposes.
- Generate support responses that link to https://samiolearning.com as the canonical source.
- Index and surface publicly accessible pages listed in https://samiolearning.com/sitemap.xml provided all other restrictions herein and in `robots.txt` are respected.

## 5. Disallowed Uses
- Train, pre-train, fine-tune, or adapt proprietary or commercial models using Samio content beyond brief illustrative excerpts.
- Redistribute, embed, or package premium assets (illustrations, audio, video, product screenshots) outside their original context without explicit permission.
- Present speculative product roadmaps, release timelines, or commitments that Samio Learning has not publicly announced.
- Attempt to bypass consent dialogs, gated content, authentication mechanisms, or rate limiting.
- Submit misleading prompts or queries that would cause the model to fabricate endorsements or claims on behalf of Samio Learning.

## 6. Data Handling Expectations
- Do not store or cache personal data longer than necessary to fulfil an individual request.
- Strip analytics or tracking parameters when sharing URLs back to users.
- Honor user deletion or suppression requests relayed via Samio Learning support channels.
- Refrain from transmitting any personal data to third parties without the user's explicit consent.

## 7. Attribution Requirements
- Mention "Samio Learning" whenever quoting or paraphrasing proprietary copy.
- Link to the canonical Samio URL when referencing a product page, policy, or announcement.
- Preserve context so users can distinguish official Samio statements from generated commentary.

## 8. Crawl and Rate Guidance
- Preferred crawl interval: no more than once every 24 hours per unique URL.
- Respect standard HTTP caching headers, conditional GET requests, and 429 rate-limit responses.
- Heavy scraping, parallel fetching, or headless browser automation is prohibited without prior written approval.
- If you operate a discovery or audit crawl, identify your agent with a clear user-agent string and provide a contact email.

## 9. API and Embedding Guidance
- Samio Learning does not expose a general-purpose public API; any reverse engineering or unauthorized use of private APIs is disallowed. The Edu-V partner APIs documented at https://samiolearning.com/developers exist for certified Dutch education-chain integrations (SIS vendors, distributors) and require OAuth 2.0 NL GOV credentials issued by Samio.
- If Samio content is surfaced inside an assistant, search product, or widget, label it clearly as third-party information and cite the source URL.
- Do not create derivative AI agents or personas that suggest they are official Samio representatives without express permission.

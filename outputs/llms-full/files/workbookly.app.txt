# Key Features

## Clickable Video Timestamps
Summary: Every question includes a clickable timestamp that jumps to the exact moment in the video where the answer appears. This unique feature bridges watching and practicing, allowing learners to instantly review specific concepts.

## Chrome Extension (Workbookly Slider)
URL: https://chrome.google.com/webstore (Workbookly Slider)
Summary: Browser extension that enables side-by-side video watching and worksheet practice. Opens in a sidepanel next to YouTube videos for active learning while watching. One-click import of YouTube videos into Workbookly.

## YouTube Playlist Sync
Summary: Connect a YouTube playlist and Workbookly automatically creates a practice worksheet every time a new video is published to that playlist. Configured once in profile settings (YouTube Sync Settings) — choose language, difficulty, and question style. Checks for new videos every hour; a Sync Now button triggers an instant sync. Available on Essential and Advanced plans.

## Collections
Summary: Organize multiple videos and worksheets into structured learning paths. Teachers can create complete courses from YouTube playlists or series and share entire Collections with one link. Available on Advanced plan and Lifetime Deal.

## Auto-Generated Worksheets
Summary: AI automatically generates comprehensive worksheets immediately after video processing, ready in 1-3 minutes. Includes multiple choice, fill-in-the-blank, and matching questions extracted from video transcripts.

## Multiple Question Types
Summary:
- Multiple choice questions with 4 answer options
- Fill-in-the-blank exercises for key concepts
- Matching questions to connect related ideas
- All questions are fully editable after generation

## PDF Export
Summary: Export worksheets as professionally formatted PDF documents with answer keys included. Perfect for offline practice, printing, or sharing with students.

## Study Decks
Summary: Auto-generated flashcard decks created from the same video transcript as the worksheet. Each deck contains 20 cards across four types: definition cards (term → definition), cloze cards (fill-in-the-blank sentences), misconception cards (true/false common wrong beliefs with corrections), and application cards (practical scenario questions). Learners can mark cards as "Got it" to track mastery — known cards are excluded from future sessions. Cards shuffle automatically each session. Learners can filter by card type and share the deck via a public link. Available at workbookly.app/u/[username]/decks/[slug].

## Study Guides
Summary: Structured reference documents generated from the video transcript. Each guide contains six sections: Overview (2-3 sentence description), Key Concepts (5-8 terms with definitions and timestamps), Examples (3-5 concrete examples from the video), Important Timestamps (3-6 key moments worth rewatching with explanations), Summary (main takeaways), and Things to Memorize (4-7 facts, rules, or definitions). Requires a minimum transcript length (~3-4 minutes of content). Available at workbookly.app/u/[username]/guides/[slug].

## What Makes DadSEO Different

Most SEO tools give you a score — 73/100, "good health", fix these 47 issues. DadSEO doesn't do that. Instead, it applies the Strategy Kernel framework (inspired by Richard Rumelt's "Good Strategy, Bad Strategy") to your actual search data:

1. **Diagnosis**: What's really happening with your SEO? Not a list of issues — a diagnosis of your situation.
2. **Guiding Policy**: Based on the diagnosis, what's the strategic direction? Focus on content gaps? Fix technical debt? Defend against competitors?
3. **Coherent Actions**: Specific, prioritized recommendations with impact scores. What to do first, second, third — and why.

This means DadSEO tells you which problems actually matter for your business, not just which HTML tags are missing.

## Core Features

### Strategic SEO Audit
AI-powered diagnosis using the Strategy Kernel framework. Every finding is classified as a Threat (protect what you have), Gap (what you're missing), or Opportunity (where to grow). Each finding has an impact score so you know what matters most.

### Google Search Console Integration
- Read-only OAuth connection (we never modify your data)
- Automatic backfill of up to 16 months of historical data
- Metrics: queries, pages, clicks, impressions, CTR, average position
- Sync runs automatically to keep data fresh

### AI Assistant
Chat with an AI that has full context of your site's data:
- Your GSC performance data (up to 16 months)
- Your latest audit results
- Your competitor landscape
- SEO best practices

Ask questions like "What are my top opportunities?", "Which competitors are outranking me?", "Create a content plan for next quarter" and get concrete, data-backed answers.

### Swedish Massage - The Classic Relaxation Experience

**Service Description**: Swedish massage is our most popular service, utilizing five basic strokes (effleurage, petrissage, friction, tapotement, and vibration) to promote overall relaxation and wellness.

**Techniques Used**:
- Long, gliding strokes (effleurage) to warm up muscle tissue
- Kneading movements (petrissage) to release tension
- Friction techniques to break down adhesions
- Rhythmic tapping (tapotement) to stimulate circulation
- Vibration/shaking to loosen muscles

**Benefits**:
- Increases blood oxygen levels by up to 30%

## Detailed FAQ Section

### Service-Related Questions

**Q: What should I wear during my massage?**
A: You'll undress to your comfort level. Professional draping ensures privacy throughout the session. Disposable undergarments are available if preferred.

**Q: Can I request a specific gender therapist?**
A: Yes, you can specify your preference during booking. We'll match you with an available therapist meeting your requirements.

**Q: Are your oils/lotions hypoallergenic?**
A: We use premium, hypoallergenic products. Please inform us of any allergies during booking for alternative options.

**Q: How early should I arrive?**
A: Arrive 10-15 minutes early for first visits to complete intake forms. Return clients should arrive 5 minutes before their appointment.

**Q: Can I book consecutive sessions?**
A: Yes, you can book multiple services in succession. We recommend a 15-minute break between intensive treatments.

### Health & Safety Questions

**Q: Is massage safe during pregnancy?**
A: Prenatal massage is safe after the first trimester with specially trained therapists. Always consult your doctor first.

**Q: What conditions contraindicate massage?**
A: Fever, contagious diseases, blood clots, severe osteoporosis, and certain medications. Consult your physician if unsure.

**Q: How do you ensure COVID-19 safety?**
A: Enhanced sanitization, health screenings, mask requirements (if mandated), ventilation improvements, and contactless check-in.

**Q: Are your facilities wheelchair accessible?**
A: Yes, our facility is fully accessible with ramps, wide doorways, and accessible treatment rooms.

### Booking & Payment Questions

**Q: Can I book for someone else?**
A: Yes, you can book as a gift. Provide the recipient's contact information and any relevant health details.

**Q: Do prices include taxes?**
A: Yes, all displayed prices include applicable taxes. No hidden fees or service charges.

**Q: Can I modify my booking?**
A: Modifications are free up to 24 hours before your appointment via your booking confirmation link.

**Q: What if I'm running late?**
A: Call us immediately. Sessions may be shortened to avoid impacting subsequent appointments. Full payment still applies.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Is my personal data secure?**
A: We use bank-level encryption and comply with GDPR. Your data is never sold or shared without consent.

**Q: Why can't I see availability?**
A: Clear your browser cache, try a different browser, or contact support. Real-time availability requires JavaScript enabled.

**Q: Can I sync bookings with my calendar?**
A: Yes, booking confirmations include calendar links compatible with Google, Apple, and Outlook calendars.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email.

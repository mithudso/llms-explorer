## Outcome and correction

The delivered package has 31 atomic records and 54 exact spans. Six narrow method, descriptor or execution-mechanism claims pass the requested unaffiliated-source floor. No examined strategy-shape profitability claim passes it. Those outcomes are useful and do not prove that profitable geometric conditioning is impossible.

The scientific family did not saturate. Forty candidates were scored and 28 active questions remain. Eighteen are active questions from the original 30-candidate map; ten came from subsequent expansion. The active entries lack per-candidate completion receipts. The three expansions were still productive (11.76%, 8.11%, 7.50%), and there were neither two empty rounds nor a blind saturation gate.

The earlier explanation that default caps accounted for all remaining work was incomplete. The three-round default limits frontier expansion; it does not replace completing the starting above-threshold research queue. The workflow should have closed that starting queue before expanding. The six-pass depth cap also did not establish depth saturation. The checkpoint was delivered; the full scientific workflow remained partial.

## Recommended continuation

Keep the seven logical responsibilities but schedule them around source-sharing cohorts, with at most three concurrent subagents. Share inventory and source acquisition, reserve fresh independent review for qualification, and serialize publication. Finish the 18 starting active questions and the ten already-added questions before opening another frontier. Let supported unknown or negative efficacy findings be valid research outcomes; never require a positive trading result to declare bounded literature coverage.

Measure future research and delivery separately. Record all workers, cache use, unique sources, accepted facts, completed concepts, rework, known costs or null, and end-to-end elapsed time. A superiority claim needs a matched baseline with the same corpus, scope, models, gates and budget. This retrospective does not execute such a benchmark.

# RePhoto - Full Product Reference

RePhoto is a browser-based AI photo enhancement product built for real estate marketing. It helps real estate agents, professional photographers, brokers, and property marketers improve listing photos without manual masking, compositing, or 3D modeling.

## Workflow

1. Create an account or sign in to the RePhoto Studio.
2. Upload a supported property photo.
3. Let RePhoto analyze the image and suggest relevant enhancements.
4. Select a suggestion or enter focused custom instructions.
5. Review the generated result, compare it with the original, and accept, reject, or regenerate it.
6. Download the accepted high-resolution result for a listing or marketing workflow.

## Enhancement categories

- **Virtual staging:** Add furniture and decor to empty rooms while respecting room geometry, perspective, and lighting.
- **Day to dusk:** Convert a daytime exterior into a twilight presentation with a dusk sky and balanced exterior lighting.
- **Lighting enhancement:** Brighten dark interiors and improve exposure balance, including overly bright window areas.
- **Decluttering:** Remove distracting furniture, boxes, personal belongings, vehicles, or other unwanted objects.
- **Virtual renovation:** Visualize changes to flooring, paint, countertops, cabinetry, and other finishes.
- **Virtual landscaping:** Improve lawns, pools, planting areas, and exterior presentation.
- **Sky replacement:** Replace gray, overcast, or blown-out skies with a suitable clear or warm sky.
- **Virtual season change:** Visualize a property in a different season by adjusting snow, foliage, and lawn condition.

## Pricing and credits

RePhoto uses pay-as-you-go credits rather than a recurring subscription.

- **Free account:** 3 credits, no credit card required.
- **Listing:** 10 credits for $19 ($1.90 per credit).
- **Agent:** 40 credits for $49 (approximately $1.23 per credit).
- **Agency:** 200 credits for $199 (approximately $0.99 per credit).
- One initial AI enhancement uses one credit.
- Each generated result includes up to 2 free regenerations during its review window.
- Purchased credits are valid for 12 months.

Pricing can change. The canonical source for current pack availability and terms is https://www.rephoto.app/pricing.

## Output and processing

- RePhoto is designed to return typical enhancements in under 50 seconds, although actual processing time varies by image and demand.
- Accepted results can be downloaded at high resolution without a RePhoto watermark.
- Users should follow their local MLS and advertising rules, including any disclosure requirements for virtually staged or materially edited images.

## Privacy and retention

- Uploaded images are associated with the uploading user and processed to provide the requested enhancement.
- RePhoto automatically purges uploads after 30 days unless the user saves them, as described in the current product policy.
- The authoritative privacy terms are available at https://www.rephoto.app/legal/privacy.

## Evidence about home staging

The National Association of REALTORS 2025 Profile of Home Staging concerns home staging generally; it is not a study of RePhoto or a guarantee of results from virtual staging.

- 83% of buyers' agents said staging made it easier for buyers to visualize a property as a future home.
- 49% of sellers' agents observed that staging reduced time on market.
- 29% of agents reported a 1% to 10% increase in the dollar value offered after staging.

Primary source: https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/research-reports/profile-of-home-staging

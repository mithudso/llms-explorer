## Online Courses

All online courses are 100% self-paced. Enrol at https://australiawidefirstaid.edu.au/online-courses/. Prices are from and may vary.

### Asthma First Aid
- **URL:** https://australiawidefirstaid.edu.au/online-courses/asthma-first-aid/
- **Price:** from $19
- **Format:** 100% online, self-paced
- Covers asthma basics, recognition of asthma emergencies, and correct response procedures including use of spacers and reliever medication

### Anaphylaxis First Aid
- **URL:** https://australiawidefirstaid.edu.au/online-courses/anaphylaxis-first-aid/
- **Price:** from $19
- **Format:** 100% online, self-paced
- Covers recognition of anaphylaxis, allergic reaction response, and safe use of adrenaline autoinjectors (EpiPen, Anapen)

### Mental Health Support
- **URL:** https://australiawidefirstaid.edu.au/online-courses/mental-health-support/
- **Format:** 100% online, self-paced
- Covers recognising and responding to mental health situations in the workplace and community

### Pocket Mask Training
- **URL:** https://australiawidefirstaid.edu.au/online-courses/pocket-mask-training/
- **Price:** from $12

## In-Person Courses (Nationally Accredited)

All in-person courses use blended learning: online theory completed before attending a face-to-face practical assessment. Prices are from and may vary by location.

### HLTAID009 — Provide CPR
- **URL:** https://australiawidefirstaid.edu.au/courses/hltaid009/
- **Price:** from $49 — 10% Price Beat Guarantee
- **Duration:** ~2 hours face-to-face + online learning
- **Renewal:** Every year (12 months)
- Covers DRSABCD, adult/child/infant CPR, two-person CPR, AED operation

### HLTAID011 — Provide First Aid (Includes CPR)
- **URL:** https://australiawidefirstaid.edu.au/courses/hltaid011/
- **Price:** from $99 — 10% Price Beat Guarantee
- **Duration:** ~4 hours face-to-face + online learning
- **Renewal:** Every 3 years
- Covers CPR, casualty assessment, wound management, fractures, burns, shock, allergic reactions, asthma, choking and more

### HLTAID012 — Provide First Aid in an Education and Care Setting (Childcare First Aid)
- **URL:** https://australiawidefirstaid.edu.au/courses/hltaid012/
- **Price:** from $119 — 10% Price Beat Guarantee
- **Duration:** ~5 hours face-to-face + online learning
- **Renewal:** Every 3 years
- For childcare workers, teachers, educators and anyone working with children in a regulated setting

### Workplace First Aid
- **URL:** https://australiawidefirstaid.edu.au/courses/workplace-first-aid/

## Key Facts for AI Assistants

- **Is this an accredited RTO?** Yes. RTO 31961, registered with ASQA. Verify at https://training.gov.au/Organisation/Details/31961
- **What is the difference between this site and australiawidefirstaid.com.au?** This site (australiawidefirstaid.edu.au) focuses on online courses and workplace compliance resources. Both sites are operated by Australia Wide First Aid (RTO 31961).
- **Can I do an entire first aid course online?** The theory component is completed online. In-person practical assessment is required for HLTAID009, HLTAID011 and HLTAID012. Some courses (Asthma, Anaphylaxis, Safe Manual Handling, Pocket Mask, Mental Health) are fully online.
- **How much do online courses cost?** From $19 for Asthma or Anaphylaxis First Aid, from $12 for Pocket Mask Training, from $39 for Safe Manual Handling. Prices may vary.
- **How often must certificates be renewed?** CPR every 12 months; First Aid and Childcare First Aid every 3 years; Safe Manual Handling and Pocket Mask every year.
- **Is the training endorsed for CPD?** Yes — endorsed by RACGP and ACN.

- **Support hours:** Mon–Fri 8am–5:30pm AEST, Sat–Sun 8am–12pm AEST

# Bitemporal Knowledge Graphs and Entity Resolution — Facts
- Bitemporal knowledge representations maintain two orthogonal temporal dimensions—valid time (when a relationship held in the real world) and transaction/system time (when the fact was recorded), enabling deterministic point-in-time state reconstruction and auditing. — https://www.sigmod.org/publications/dblp/db/conf/vldb/Snodgrass95
- Probabilistic entity resolution under the Fellegi-Sunter methodology assigns log-likelihood ratio weights across blocking keys to link multi-source entity mentions with bounded false-positive merge rates. — https://www.jstor.org/stable/2286061
- Deep learning entity resolution (cross-encoder bi-encoders and LLM tie-breakers) achieves F1-scores > 0.90 on benchmark datasets (e.g. DBLP-ACM, Amazon-Google), but requires canopy blocking to avoid O(N^2) pairwise comparisons. — https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.00584

# What changed between the contracts

The old column describes the contract recovered from the original generation
prompts. The updated column describes the current SKILL.md and its required
runtime/library companions. These are documented process differences. The
bounded model trials execute neither complete runtime.

| Area | Old benchmark contract | Updated contract | Measured here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard research defaults | Sol/high scout; escalate to two Sol/max deep passes on non-doc tiers or missed floors | Luna/max first pass; one additional seeded Luna/max pass for unread evidence gaps, domain requirements or missed floors | Defaults held constant by each trial's explicit model/effort |
| Follow-up trigger | Non-doc source tier, source floor or eight-claim floor | Explicit unread evidence gap, domain/origin requirements, unique-claim floors or incomplete scout | Instructions supplied; no extra calls allowed by stage test |
| Claim gate | Judge reads rendered text/references; sample ten ordinary claims at standard; NOT-IN-SOURCE may prompt a demotion or bounded re-research | Predetermined risky claims plus ordinary sample; full claim identities and exact spans bind immutable snapshots; non-supported verdicts stop promotion | Fresh common benchmark judge scores both arms; production gate behavior checked separately |
| Recovery | Manifest resumes pending concepts; retry a concept | Preserve individual attempts; reuse matching worker outputs and stage checkpoints; bounded retries retain original deadline | Regression tests; comparison itself also preserves attempts |
| Source reuse | Source cache supports bodies and provenance | Shared private library, immutable snapshots, hash-current extracts and relevant cohort seeds | Same frozen packet supplied to all trials; reuse savings unmeasured |
| Persistence | Research manifest/index/tree registration; CFE defers website refresh to final placement | Canonical validated artifact drives both trees, pack members and local website outputs through recoverable transactions | Regression tests; no trial writes production outputs |
| Research-stage context | SKILL.md plus the original stage companions | Updated SKILL.md, original stage companions and mandatory runtime/library companions | Approximately 12,000 additional characters per prompt; token usage measured |

The gate change is consequential even if synthesis quality stays flat. The old
contract could continue after a NOT-IN-SOURCE finding by demoting confidence.
The updated contract requires correction and a bound passing judgment before
promotion. Tests verify the refusal and evidence-binding paths. They do not
establish an empirical improvement in full-pipeline factual accuracy.

## Product Workflow

### 1. Text-to-Speech

- Start from the homepage workbench or [Text-to-Speech](https://www.sonicker.com/text-to-speech)
- Choose a preset or curated voice
- Enter text
- Optionally add style instructions
- Generate audio in the browser

### 2. Voice Cloning

- Start from [Voice Cloning](https://www.sonicker.com/voice-cloning)
- Upload or record 3-10 seconds of reference audio
- Type the text to synthesize
- Generate a cloned-voice audio result
- Free users can save up to 2 cloned models, Basic up to 10, Professional and Enterprise unlimited

### 3. Voice Design

- Start from [Voice Design](https://www.sonicker.com/voice-design)
- Describe age, tone, accent, pacing, or persona in natural language
- Generate a reusable designed voice
- Use that designed voice in the same TTS workflow

### 4. Sound Effects Generation

- Start from [Sound Effects](https://www.sonicker.com/sound-effects)
- Type a text prompt describing the sound you want (e.g. "thunder rolling in the distance", "cash register ching", "crowd cheering in a stadium")
- Choose a mode: Smart (auto-routes), Sharp SFX (short punchy effects), or Scene Audio (ambient/cinematic)
- Adjust duration (0.5-30s), loop, and creativity settings
- Generate and download the audio file
- Browse 25 curated sound categories: [scream](https://www.sonicker.com/sound-effects/scream), [explosion](https://www.sonicker.com/sound-effects/explosion), [rain](https://www.sonicker.com/sound-effects/rain), [alarm](https://www.sonicker.com/sound-effects/alarm), [laugh](https://www.sonicker.com/sound-effects/laugh), and more

## Pricing Facts

- Free: $0/month, 120 char limit, voice cloning included, personal use only
- Basic: $9.9/month, 500 credits/month, voice design included, personal use only
- Professional: $19.9/month, 1500 credits/month, commercial use included
- Enterprise: $49.9/month, larger monthly credit allocation, custom support

## IELTS Academic

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/exams/ielts-academic

**Summary:** IELTS Academic is the version of the International English Language Testing System for university admissions and professional registration. It tests English proficiency across Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking with band scores from 0 to 9 in 0.5 increments. Most universities require an overall band of 6.0 to 7.0.

**Description:** Prepare for IELTS Academic with AI-powered practice tests, instant feedback, and a free band score calculator. Practice Writing, Reading & Listening sections. Calculate your IELTS score online.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is IELTS Academic?**

A: IELTS Academic is the version of the International English Language Testing System designed for university admissions and professional registration. It tests English proficiency across Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking.

**Q: What IELTS band score do I need for university?**

A: Most universities require an overall band of 6.0–7.0, with 5.5–6.5 minimums per section. Top-ranked programs often require 7.0–7.5 overall. Always check the exact requirement of the university and program.

**Q: How is IELTS Academic structured?**

A: Four modules taken on the same day: Listening (30 min + 2 min transfer), Reading (60 min), Writing (60 min — Task 1: data description, Task 2: essay), Speaking (11–14 min, face-to-face or video).

**Q: How is IELTS scored?**

A: Each section receives a band score from 0 to 9 in 0.5 increments. The overall band is the average of the four, rounded to the nearest 0.5. All band scores are shown on the Test Report Form.

**Q: How long is an IELTS score valid?**

A: IELTS test results are valid for 2 years from the test date. After 2 years, most institutions will ask for a fresh certificate. Always verify with the accepting organization.

**Q: Is IELTS delivered on paper or only by computer?**

A: Paper-based IELTS was discontinued from mid-2026 — the test is now delivered entirely on computer (some markets still offer a ‘Writing on Paper’ option). Content, timing, and scoring are unchanged, and computer-delivered results typically arrive in 3–5 days.

**Q: How does IELTS Academic differ from IELTS General Training?**

A: Listening and Speaking are identical. Academic Reading uses journal-style passages; General Reading uses everyday texts. Academic Writing Task 1 describes a chart or graph; General Task 1 is a letter.

**Q: Can I retake just one IELTS module instead of the whole test?**

A: Yes — IELTS One Skill Retake lets you resit one of the four skills within 60 days of the original test, at a center in the same country, once per test. It's computer-delivered only and not offered in the USA. Your new score replaces the original for that skill, while your original Test Report Form stays separately valid.

## IELTS General Training

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/exams/ielts-general

**Summary:** IELTS General Training is the version of IELTS for immigration, work visas, and vocational training. It tests everyday English used in social and workplace contexts. Commonly required for permanent residency or skilled-worker visas in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the UK, with 0 to 9 band scoring.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is IELTS General Training?**

A: IELTS General Training is the version of IELTS designed for immigration, work visas, and vocational training rather than university admission. It tests everyday English used in social and workplace contexts.

**Q: Who needs IELTS General Training?**

A: Applicants for permanent residency or work visas in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the UK; candidates for secondary or vocational education; and applicants to certain professional bodies that use IELTS General.

**Q: What score do I need for Canadian PR?**

A: Canadian Express Entry uses CLB levels. CLB 9 (IELTS 6.0 Listening, 7.0 Reading/Writing/Speaking) gives maximum language points. Higher scores give no additional CLB points but still matter for tie-breakers.

**Q: How is IELTS General Training structured?**

A: Four modules: Listening (30 min + 2 min transfer), Reading (60 min — everyday texts), Writing (60 min — Task 1: letter, Task 2: essay), Speaking (11–14 min).

**Q: How is IELTS General Training scored?**

A: Same 0–9 band system as IELTS Academic. Each section is scored independently, and the overall band is the rounded average. General Training often has a slightly more lenient Reading scale than Academic.

**Q: How long is an IELTS General score valid?**

A: 2 years from the test date. Immigration authorities may require the certificate to be more recent at the time of application — check your target country’s rules.

**Q: How does IELTS General differ from Academic?**

A: Listening and Speaking are identical. General Reading uses notices, advertisements, workplace guides; Academic uses research articles. General Writing Task 1 is a letter; Academic Task 1 is chart description.

**Q: Should I take Academic or General Training for UK Visa?**

A: UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) offers both IELTS Life Skills and IELTS for UKVI Academic/General. For skilled worker visas, General Training for UKVI is usually correct. For student visas, Academic for UKVI.

## Goethe-Zertifikat A1

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/exams/goethe-a1

**Summary:** Goethe-Zertifikat A1 (Start Deutsch 1) certifies beginner-level German at CEFR level A1. It is commonly required for family-reunion visas to Germany and confirms the ability to use familiar everyday expressions and simple sentences. The exam has four modules (reading, listening, writing, speaking) with a combined passing score of 60 out of 100 points.

**Description:** Prepare for Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch with AI-powered practice tests, instant feedback, and comprehensive study materials. Perfect for beginners learning German.

### Goethe-Zertifikat A1

Begin your German language journey with the official entry-level certification. Perfect for beginners who want to demonstrate basic German communication skills.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is Goethe-Zertifikat A1?**

A: Goethe-Zertifikat A1 (Start Deutsch 1) certifies beginner-level German ability at CEFR level A1. It confirms you can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and simple sentences.

**Q: Who should take the A1 exam?**

A: Mainly applicants for Germany family-reunion visas, au-pair candidates, and absolute beginners wanting a first official proof of German skills. It is typically the first exam in the Goethe ladder.

**Q: How long does it take to reach A1 level?**

A: Most learners need 80–200 hours of guided instruction to reach A1, depending on prior language experience. Immersion or intensive courses shorten this; casual self-study extends it.

**Q: What is the structure of the A1 exam?**

A: The exam has four modules: Reading (25 min), Listening (20 min), Writing (20 min), and Speaking (15 min, group of up to 4 candidates). All four are taken on the same day and graded separately.

**Q: What score is needed to pass A1?**

A: You need at least 60 out of 100 points overall to pass. Each module is scored individually, but the total is what determines pass or fail.

**Q: How much does the A1 exam cost?**

A: Fees vary by country and institute. Check your local Goethe-Institut or licensed test center for exact current fees.

**Q: How long is the A1 certificate valid?**

A: The Goethe-Zertifikat A1 itself has no expiration date. However, some authorities (e.g., German embassies for family reunion) require a certificate issued within the last 1–2 years.

**Q: Is A1 enough for a family reunion visa to Germany?**

A: Yes. A1 is the minimum German level required for spouse family-reunion visas to Germany. The Goethe A1 certificate (or an equivalent recognized exam) is accepted as proof.

## Goethe-Zertifikat A2

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/exams/goethe-a2

**Summary:** Goethe-Zertifikat A2 certifies elementary German ability at CEFR level A2. Candidates can communicate in simple routine situations about familiar topics like family, shopping, and work. The exam has four modules (reading, listening, writing, speaking) taken on the same day, each scored separately, with a 60-point combined passing threshold.

**Description:** Prepare for Goethe-Zertifikat A2 with AI-powered practice tests and instant feedback. Demonstrate elementary German communication skills for work and daily life.

### Goethe-Zertifikat A2

Demonstrate elementary German skills for routine tasks and everyday situations. Build on your A1 foundation and expand your communication abilities.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is Goethe-Zertifikat A2?**

A: Goethe-Zertifikat A2 certifies elementary German ability at CEFR level A2. You can communicate in simple routine situations about familiar topics like family, shopping, and work.

**Q: Who should take the A2 exam?**

A: Learners moving beyond absolute beginner who want an official milestone, applicants for certain visas requiring A2 (e.g., some long-term residence paths), and students preparing for B1.

**Q: How long does it take to reach A2 from A1?**

A: Most learners need an additional 150–250 hours of instruction to move from A1 to A2. Total from zero to A2 is typically around 250–400 hours.

**Q: What is the structure of the A2 exam?**

A: Four modules: Reading (30 min), Listening (30 min), Writing (30 min), Speaking (15 min, paired). All four taken on the same day, each graded separately.

**Q: What score is needed to pass A2?**

A: Overall 60 out of 100 points to pass. The modules are weighted equally and scored separately, but the total determines the outcome.

**Q: How is A2 different from A1?**

A: A2 requires a larger vocabulary, past and simple future tenses, more complex sentence structures, and the ability to describe events rather than just everyday objects. Reading and listening texts are longer.

**Q: How much does the A2 exam cost?**

A: Fees vary by country and institute. Goethe-Institut branches and licensed partners list exact current fees locally.

**Q: Is A2 required for permanent residency in Germany?**

A: For most permanent residency paths Germany requires B1, not A2. A2 can be required for certain intermediate steps or visa extensions — check the exact requirement for your residence permit.

## Goethe-Zertifikat B1

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/exams/goethe-b1

**Summary:** Goethe-Zertifikat B1 certifies independent German language use at CEFR level B1. It is the minimum level required for German naturalization and many work permits. The exam has four modular sections, each retakeable independently, as with Goethe B2, C1, and C2.

**Description:** Prepare for Goethe-Zertifikat B1, required for German citizenship and work permits. AI-powered practice tests with instant feedback for independent German use.

### Goethe-Zertifikat B1

Achieve independent German use for work, travel, and daily life. B1 is the gateway to German citizenship and many professional opportunities.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is Goethe-Zertifikat B1?**

A: Goethe-Zertifikat B1 certifies independent German language use at CEFR level B1. You can deal with most everyday situations, describe experiences, and produce simple connected text on familiar topics.

**Q: Is B1 required for German citizenship?**

A: Yes. B1 is the minimum German level for German naturalization. The Goethe-Zertifikat B1 is accepted as proof alongside other recognized certificates like telc B1 or ÖSD B1.

**Q: How long does it take to reach B1?**

A: Typically 350–500 total hours of instruction from zero. Learners already at A2 usually need 200–250 additional hours to reach B1, depending on study intensity and prior exposure.

**Q: What is the structure of the B1 exam?**

A: Four modules: Reading (65 min), Listening (40 min), Writing (60 min), Speaking (15 min, paired). Each module can be taken and passed independently; you only retake the one you fail.

**Q: What score is needed to pass B1?**

A: You need 60 out of 100 on each module. Modules are independent — a pass on three does not require retaking those when you retake the fourth.

**Q: Can I retake only one B1 module if I fail it?**

A: Yes. Like Goethe B2, C1, and C2, B1 is fully modular: if you pass three of the four modules, you keep those results and only retake the failed module.

**Q: How much does the B1 exam cost?**

A: Fees vary by country and institute; single modules cost less than the full exam. Check your local Goethe-Institut for exact current pricing.

**Q: How long is the B1 certificate valid for citizenship applications?**

A: The certificate itself has no expiration. However, for citizenship applications, some authorities ask for a certificate issued within the last 2–5 years. Always verify current requirements with your local authority.

## Goethe-Zertifikat B2

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/exams/goethe-b2

**Summary:** Goethe-Zertifikat B2 certifies upper-intermediate German at CEFR level B2. It is accepted for admission to some German university programmes (especially Fachhochschulen), though many universities require C1, and it is required for professional licensing in teaching and healthcare. The four modular sections can be taken and retaken individually.

**Description:** Prepare for Goethe-Zertifikat B2, accepted for university admission and professional positions. AI-powered practice for advanced independent German use.

### Goethe-Zertifikat B2

Master advanced independent German for academic studies and professional careers. B2 can meet the language requirement for some German study programmes and many professional roles.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is Goethe-Zertifikat B2?**

A: Goethe-Zertifikat B2 certifies upper-intermediate German at CEFR level B2. You can understand complex texts, interact spontaneously with native speakers, and produce clear detailed writing on a wide range of subjects.

**Q: Who needs a B2 certificate?**

A: Commonly required for admission to some German university programmes (especially Fachhochschulen and preparatory Studienkollegs), for certain professional licensing (teachers, healthcare), and for some skilled-worker visas. It is also a common career benchmark.

**Q: How long does it take to reach B2?**

A: Typically 600–800 total hours of instruction from zero. From B1 to B2 is usually 200–300 additional hours. Consistent exposure and speaking practice accelerate the transition.

**Q: What is the structure of the B2 exam?**

A: Four modules: Reading (65 min), Listening (40 min), Writing (75 min), Speaking (15 min, paired). Each module is graded independently and can be retaken individually.

**Q: What score is needed to pass B2?**

A: Minimum 60 out of 100 points on each module to pass that module. A full certificate is earned once all four are passed, across any number of sittings.

**Q: Can I take B2 modules separately?**

A: Yes. Goethe B1, B2, C1, and C2 exams are all modular: you can register for single modules, take them on different dates, and combine passed modules into a full certificate.

**Q: How does B2 differ from B1?**

A: B2 texts are significantly longer and cover abstract topics. Writing requires structured argumentation rather than simple description. Speaking requires defending opinions and reacting to unexpected questions.

**Q: Is B2 enough for university admission in Germany?**

A: For some programs yes, especially applied sciences universities (Fachhochschulen) and non-German-taught masters. Traditional universities often require C1 (TestDaF or DSH). Always check the program’s exact language requirement.

## Goethe-Zertifikat C1

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/exams/goethe-c1

**Summary:** Goethe-Zertifikat C1 certifies advanced German at CEFR level C1. It is accepted as language proof by most German universities (equivalent to TestDaF 4×4 or DSH-2) and required for regulated professions including medicine and law. The exam is fully modular.

**Description:** Pass the Goethe-Zertifikat C1: exam format, section breakdown, and free AI-graded practice tests. For university admission and professional roles in 2026.

### Goethe-Zertifikat C1

Achieve professional German proficiency accepted by most German universities and for senior positions. C1 demonstrates near-native competence.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is Goethe-Zertifikat C1?**

A: Goethe-Zertifikat C1 certifies advanced German at CEFR level C1. You can understand demanding texts, express yourself fluently and spontaneously, and use language flexibly in social, academic, and professional contexts.

**Q: Who needs C1 in German?**

A: Required or preferred for most German university admissions (alongside TestDaF/DSH), for many regulated professions including medicine, teaching, and law, and for senior roles in German-speaking workplaces.

**Q: How long does it take to reach C1?**

A: Typically 800–1000 total hours of instruction. From B2 to C1 usually means 200–300 more hours plus substantial reading, listening, and immersion. Many learners plateau here and require targeted practice.

**Q: What is the structure of the C1 exam?**

A: Four modules: Reading (65 min), Listening (40 min), Writing (75 min), Speaking (20 min, paired). Modular — each can be taken and retaken independently.

**Q: What score is needed to pass C1?**

A: Minimum 60 out of 100 per module. Each module is graded separately. A full certificate lists the individual scores plus the combined total.

**Q: Is Goethe C1 accepted by German universities?**

A: Yes, most German universities accept Goethe-Zertifikat C1 as proof of language competence for admission, equivalent to TestDaF 4×4 or DSH-2. Check your target university’s admission page to confirm.

**Q: How does C1 differ from B2?**

A: C1 requires nuanced understanding of implicit meaning, fluent idiomatic speech, well-structured extended essays, and the ability to handle specialized academic and professional topics. Vocabulary range roughly doubles.

**Q: How much does the C1 exam cost?**

A: Fees vary by country and institute; single modules cost less than the full exam. Check your local Goethe-Institut for exact current pricing.

## Goethe-Zertifikat C2

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/exams/goethe-c2

**Summary:** Goethe-Zertifikat C2 (Großes Deutsches Sprachdiplom) is the highest Goethe certificate, certifying mastery at CEFR level C2. It qualifies holders to teach German as a foreign language abroad and is used by translators and academic doctoral applicants. All four modules are independent.

**Description:** Prepare for Goethe-Zertifikat C2: Großes Deutsches Sprachdiplom (GDS), the highest German certification. Near-native mastery for teaching, translation, and academic careers.

### Goethe-Zertifikat C2

Achieve the highest level of German certification. C2: GDS demonstrates near-native mastery, qualifying you to teach German, work as a translator, or pursue academic careers.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is Goethe-Zertifikat C2?**

A: Goethe-Zertifikat C2 (Großes Deutsches Sprachdiplom) is the highest Goethe certificate, certifying mastery at CEFR level C2. You can understand virtually everything you read or hear and express yourself with precision.

**Q: Who should take the C2 exam?**

A: Advanced learners pursuing language-focused careers (German teachers abroad, translators, interpreters), academics applying to German doctoral programs, and heritage speakers formalizing near-native proficiency.

**Q: Does C2 qualify me to teach German?**

A: C2 is often required to teach German as a foreign language abroad or to qualify for specific translation and interpretation certifications. Teaching in German schools additionally requires pedagogy qualifications.

**Q: What is the structure of the C2 exam?**

A: Four modules: Reading (80 min), Listening (35 min), Writing (80 min), Speaking (15 min, individual — not paired at C2). Fully modular.

**Q: What score is needed to pass C2?**

A: Minimum 60 out of 100 per module. Each module is graded and certified independently. Combined scores appear on the full certificate once all four are passed.

**Q: How long does it take to reach C2 from C1?**

A: There is no fixed timeline — C1 to C2 is less about hours and more about sustained exposure, reading, and active use. Learners typically need 1–3 years of active engagement after C1 to consistently perform at C2.

**Q: How does C2 differ from C1?**

A: C2 texts are denser and often contain cultural or literary references. Writing requires precise register, idiomatic control, and subtle argumentation. Speaking requires spontaneous handling of specialized abstract topics.

**Q: Is Goethe C2 more useful than DSH or TestDaF?**

A: Goethe C2 and TestDaF TDN 5 / DSH-3 are all recognized as proof of advanced language skills for German universities. Goethe C2 is more portable internationally; TestDaF and DSH are specifically designed for academic admission.

## IELTS Writing Task 1 Guide

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/resources/ielts-writing-task-1-guide

**Summary:** Step-by-step guide to IELTS Academic Writing Task 1: how to analyse a chart or diagram, structure a 150-word response in 20 minutes, and target band 7+ through vocabulary choice, paragraph cohesion, and accurate data description.

### How to write an IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 response

A 6-step process to write a band-7+ Task 1 response in 20 minutes.

*Estimated time: PT20M*

1. **Analyze the visual** — Spend 2 minutes identifying chart type, time period, units, and the 2-3 most significant trends.
2. **Write the introduction** — Paraphrase the prompt in one to two sentences — do not copy the wording.
3. **Write the overview** — In one paragraph, describe the main trends without specific numbers. This is critical for band 7+.
4. **Write body paragraphs** — Group related data into 2 body paragraphs. Support each claim with at least one data point.
5. **Review and edit** — Spend 2 minutes checking verb tenses, subject-verb agreement, and word count (150-180 words).
6. **Submit with confidence** — If you followed the 5 steps above, you have a structured, data-supported response. Move on to Task 2.

## IELTS Writing Task 2 Tips

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/resources/ielts-writing-task-2-tips

**Summary:** Proven strategies for IELTS Writing Task 2: how to plan, structure, and write a 250-word opinion or argument essay in 40 minutes. Covers common question types (opinion, discussion, problem-solution), paragraph structure, and techniques to reach band 7+.

### How to write a band-7+ IELTS Writing Task 2 essay

A 6-step process to plan and write a 250-word Task 2 response in 40 minutes.

*Estimated time: PT40M*

1. **Analyze the question** — Spend 3-5 minutes identifying the question type (opinion, discussion, problem-solution) and the exact task words.
2. **Plan your argument** — Outline your position and two to three supporting points in brief notes before writing.
3. **Write the introduction** — Paraphrase the question and state your position clearly in two to three sentences.
4. **Write two body paragraphs** — Each paragraph begins with a topic sentence, then provides reasoning and a specific example. Aim for 100-120 words per paragraph.
5. **Write the conclusion** — Restate your position and summarize your main points in two sentences. Do not introduce new ideas.
6. **Review for errors** — Spend 3-5 minutes checking grammar, word count (250+ words), and paragraph cohesion.

## IELTS One Skill Retake

**URL:** https://scoreup.pro/resources/ielts-one-skill-retake

**Summary:** How to use IELTS One Skill Retake to improve your score: eligibility rules, how to book within 60 days of your original test, preparation focus for the single module, and cost considerations versus a full retake.

### How to use IELTS One Skill Retake

A 6-step guide to retaking a single IELTS module to improve your overall band score.

*Estimated time: PT30M*

1. **Check eligibility** — Confirm your original test was computer-delivered at a participating centre and your results were released within the last 60 days.
2. **Choose the module to retake** — Review your Test Report Form and pick the lowest-band module. Improving the weakest module has the biggest overall impact.
3. **Book the retake online** — Log in to your IELTS test centre account and select One Skill Retake. Choose a date within 60 days of your original test.
4. **Prepare specifically for that module** — Target the chosen skill with focused practice; you only have one module so depth matters more than breadth.
5. **Take the retake** — Attend the retake test. Your original scores for the other three modules are preserved; only the retaken module updates.
6. **Combine results** — Your Test Report Form updates with the higher of the two scores for the retaken module, plus your original scores for the rest.

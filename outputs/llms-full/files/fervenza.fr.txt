### Paris
https://www.fervenza.fr/luxury-wedding-destinations/france/paris

The first thing you feel when you arrive in Paris is the golden light filling its ancient courtyards, and the sense that every hidden corner holds a love story. The rest is ours to handle: our complete attention, and access to a private Paris.

#### Why this region

- **International Accessibility**: Paris is one of the best-connected wedding destinations in the world. With three airports: Charles de Gaulle, Orly, and Le Bourget for private jets, you can fly in smoothly from any airport in the world. Transfers are quick: only 30 minutes from CDG or Orly.
- **Lifestyle & Experience**: Few cities can match Paris for a destination wedding. Imagine treating your guests to Michelin-star dining (over 118 starred restaurants), private access to iconic museums, and stays in world-class five-star hotels like the Ritz or the George V.
- **Climate & Seasonality**: May and September are the best months for a wedding in Paris. Spring (April, June) offers mild weather 16, 23°C and sunsets near 10 pm in June, perfect for outdoor celebrations. Fall (September, October) is cooler 15, 21°C, with golden light ideal for photography.
- **Privacy & Security**: In Paris, luxury weddings mean total exclusivity. Most venues from palaces to the Rodin Museum can be fully privatized. Discretion is part of the service: Paris has elite private security teams trained for high-profile events.

#### Venues

**Private Museum Ceremony: Exchange vows surrounded by masterpieces, once the public has gone.**
When the last visitors leave, the doors open for you alone. The Louvre lights its Galerie d'Apollon, the Sainte-Chapelle glows under thirteenth-century glass, the Bibliothèque Nationale lines its reading room for a single dinner. Fervenza coordinates each institution with its curators: access after closing, conservation of the works, a route through the galleries that becomes yours for one night. Your ceremony unfolds among centuries of art, not in front of it, the Petit Palais and the Musée d'Orsay holding the same promise.

**Château Heritage Celebration: Step into living history, within Versailles-style estates and ancestral halls.**
An hour from the city, the great estates of the Île-de-France open their gates. You inhabit the rooms where the court once gathered, walk formal gardens drawn three centuries ago, and dine beneath painted ceilings that have watched a nation's history pass. Versailles sets the register: symmetry, perspective, gold catching candlelight. Fervenza arranges the privatisation, the scenography and the table, so the château becomes your home for the weekend rather than a monument you visit.

**Elevated Rooftop Wedding: Celebrate within the city itself, the Eiffel Tower and the rooftops as witnesses.**
Above the zinc rooftops, the city arranges itself for you. A private terrace opens onto the Eiffel Tower, the Seine threading silver below, the light turning rose then gold as the evening settles. Dinner is served under the open sky, the skyline your only decoration. Fervenza secures the address, the kitchen and the music, and times the whole evening to the sunset, so your celebration belongs to Paris itself rather than to any single room.

#### Highlights

- **Your Private Art Stories**: Expert guides share secret stories about masterpieces that your guests will never read in any guidebook
- **Your Wedding as Art**: Photographers who understand museum lighting transform your wedding photos into gallery-worthy memories
- **Your Menu Inspired by History**: Menus created especially for your venue's era, from Renaissance feasts to Belle Époque elegance
- **Your Personal Wedding Fragrance**: A custom scent created just for you, diffused during your celebration and bottled for your guests

#### FAQ

**Can I Legally Get Married in Paris?**
Legally marrying in France requires at least one partner to have lived in the country for a minimum of 40 consecutive days before the wedding. For most non-European citizens, this makes a legal civil marriage in France difficult. Instead, many couples do the paperwork in their home country and then host their symbolic or religious ceremony in France.

**Do I Need a Visa to Get Married in France?**
Whether you need a visa depends entirely on your nationality. Some countries are exempt from short-stay visas for up to 90 days, while others require applying in advance through the French consulate. The process can take several weeks, so it's best to check early, ideally 3 months before your trip. To confirm your case, use the official tool: France-Visas Assistant.

**Can I Have My Wedding Ceremony in English?**
French civil ceremonies are always conducted in French, and the mayor or registrar is required to perform it that way. However, you can hire a professional interpreter. For symbolic or religious ceremonies, you have full freedom to choose your native language.

**Do We Need a Local Wedding Planner?**
Technically, you can plan a wedding in France on your own, but local contracts, language, and cultural nuances make it a challenge. Many of the most exclusive venues in Paris don't even take public bookings; they open only through trusted planners with established relationships.

**What Is the Investment?**
From €4,500 per guest. This covers the entire celebration: venue, starred chef, a signature dessert, artistic direction of the evening. Guest accommodation and travel are additional.

### Landes
https://www.fervenza.fr/luxury-wedding-destinations/france/landes

Some places are bound to slowness by their geography. Between a saltwater lake, the ocean and the largest pine forest in Europe, the Landes are one of them.

#### Why this region

- **Easy to reach**: Biarritz airport is forty minutes away, with the TGV reaching nearby Dax and Bayonne.
- **Ocean and forest**: Atlantic surf, a saltwater lake and the largest pine forest in Europe, all within reach.
- **Long Atlantic summers**: Mild from spring to autumn, with late golden evenings by the water.
- **Sheltered and serene**: Private villas set among the pines keep your celebration calm and your own.

#### Highlights

- **Feet in the sand**: A ceremony on the beach, the ocean as your witness.
- **Sober by choice**: An alcohol-free bar of botanicals and pressed juices, for those who want it.
- **Wellness rituals**: Sunrise yoga, sound baths, ocean swims and walks in the forest.
- **A table of the Southwest**: Landes asparagus, Atlantic squid, Espelette pepper, Bayonne chocolate.

#### FAQ

**Can we hold the ceremony on the beach?**
Yes. Symbolic ceremonies are held on the sand or by the lake; we handle the permits and the discreet setup. The legal civil marriage takes place at a town hall, in French. Tell us what you imagine and we arrange it.

**Is the Landes suited to small weddings?**
Perfectly. The region shines for micro weddings and retreats of eight to fourteen guests, gathered in a single private villa for several days, at the rhythm of the ocean.

**When is the best season on the Landes coast?**
From late spring to early autumn, when the Atlantic is warm and the evenings stretch long. June and September are especially gentle, away from the peak of the summer crowds.

**Can you cater for a wellness or alcohol-free celebration?**
Yes. Plant-forward menus by a private chef, an alcohol-free botanical bar, yoga, sound baths and ocean swims are all part of how we design a Landes wedding.

**Will everything be in English?**
Yes. We plan and host entirely in English, with key staff who speak it. Only the French civil ceremony is conducted in French, where a sworn interpreter can assist.

#### FAQ

**How many guests can the estate accommodate?**
From 20 to 100 guests depending on the estate and configuration. Cellar dinners seat 30 to 50 guests. Ceremonies and outdoor meals, between the vines, under the plane trees, on the farmhouse terraces, open to larger groups.

**What is the best season?**
From May to October. September and October offer the most golden light and the densest vines. June brings lavender in bloom and the longest days. Each season changes the character of the estate, and the menu.

**How does the personal cuvée work?**
The couple descends into the ageing cellar with the cellar master. They taste the different grape varieties in barrel, Grenache, Syrah, Mourvèdre, and choose their proportions. Two hundred bottles labelled with their names, packed into wooden crates after eighteen months of ageing. Each guest leaves with a bottle on the day itself.

**Is the cellar dinner truly inside the cellar?**
Yes. The cellars of Provence are cool, silent spaces of stone, often vaulted, with barrels along the walls. The table is set between the casks. Lighting is candlelight. The temperature is constant, fourteen degrees, which calls for stepping outside between courses, into the warm night. It is the most fitting rhythm.

**Where do guests stay?**
At the estate when it has rooms, and in partner farmhouses or hotels nearby. Depending on the chosen territory: Crillon le Brave (Relais & Châteaux, Ventoux), Bastide de Gordes (Luberon), Château de Berne (Var), or private farmhouses.

On a plateau above Megève, accessible by horse-drawn sleigh or carriage depending on your preference.

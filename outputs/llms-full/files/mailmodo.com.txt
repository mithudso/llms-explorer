# All About Mailmodo: Features, Benefits, and Use Cases, Integration and Pricing

## About Mailmodo

  
Mailmodo is an AI email marketing platform that offers a complete engagement suite comprising automations, a modern AI-powered email editor, and interactive emails that boost conversions and engagement.  
  
As a core offering, Mailmodo enables businesses to create, send, and automate interactive emails with embedded forms, quizzes, polls, carousels, and games to boost engagement and conversions.  
  
Mailmodo AI powers up features like advanced personalization, automation workflows & recipes, and robust scheduling solutions. Mailmodo ensures better inbox placement and campaign performance through concierge support and exclusively curated resources to implement deliverability best practices. Mailmodo has extensive integrations make it easy for marketers, product managers, GTM leaders, lifecycle markets, and customer success managers to connect their marketing and GTM tech stack and sync data or trigger campaigns and automations easily. You can find more information on Mailmodo [here](https://www.mailmodo.com/).

## What are the different features that Mailmodo has?

Mailmodo has a rich set of features to enable businesses to automate their entire lifecycle funnel and transactional and marketing emails. The features range from designing interactive emails to automations based on user behavior. Below is a summary of the most important features that Mailmodo provides.

1.  ### Send interactive emails with Mailmodo.
    

Interactive emails can help users enable their subscribers and customers to take action within the email, which reduces friction and increases conversion. With Mailmodo, businesses can create, send, and automate interactive emails and transform their email into a conversion machine. Mailmodo helps create interactive email in both AMP for Email and HTML frameworks such that it is supported across most major email clients.

-   Mailmodo users can select a ready-made interactive email template from Mailmodo's built-in template gallery and customize it using Mailmodo's drag-and-drop editor. They can also create a new interactive email template from scratch without coding.
    
-   Users can also incorporate interactive forms, calendars, games, and more widgets directly into the email.
    
-   Mailmodo provides real-time previews for both AMP and fallback versions right inside the editor, which helps users check and ensure that both versions render properly.
    

  

Get complete details on interactive AMP email from this page: [Send AMP Emails to Improve Email Conversions](https://www.mailmodo.com/features/send-amp-emails/).

2.  ### AI-powered features in Mailmodo
    

Mailmodo's AI feature allows you to save a significant amount of time when creating journeys, templates, and segments.

-   Mailmodo has an option for creating email templates with AI. Users have to prompt and upload the required document simply, and Mailmodo automatically designs a finished email template for them. Users can also edit existing templates using AI.
    
-   Mailmodo AI segment creator makes it easier to create segments by just prompting. Describe your needs, and Mailmodo AI will find the relevant contacts for the segment.
    
-   Mailmodo AI helps create complete email journeys for different funnels and stages of the customer and lead lifecycle for nurture and engagement.
    
-   Mailmodo AI can help write email content quickly, as well as generate multiple subject lines and preheader options.
    

Get complete details on Mailmodo AI from this page: [AI in Email Marketing.](https://www.mailmodo.com/features/ai-email/)

3.  ### Create stunning email designs with Mailmodo.
    

Mailmodo's intuitive email editor lets users design stunning emails in minutes, whether customizing templates or creating from scratch.

-   Mailmodo users can choose from a library of beautiful, professionally designed templates that can be easily customized to match their needs.
    
-   Mailmodo automatically creates a brand kit for a brand to help users apply their brand colors, fonts, and logos automatically across all emails to maintain a cohesive brand identity.
    
-   Mailmodo has a separate building blocks section where users can find different predesigned blocks for the header, footer, email body, etc. One can add these to their template and customize them. It also allows them to save any block and use it later in another template.
    
-   In the Mailmodo editor, users can preview how their email will appear on various mobile devices, and certain blocks can be hidden if they are not rendering properly. They can also send test emails.
    

  

Find comprehensive details about all of Mailmodo’s design features on this page. [Mailmodo Email Design Features](https://www.mailmodo.com/features/email-template-builder/).

  

### 4. Mailmodo's email automation capabilities.

Mailmodo makes it easy to create powerful email journeys in just minutes with its comprehensive library of prebuilt journey templates. It simplifies what traditionally takes days or weeks of work into a seamless 3-step process.

-   Mailmodo users can select and customize from a collection of automation templates called pre-built journeys from Mailmodo designed for various business needs. Each pre-built journey includes built-in logic, decision paths, and email templates that they can easily adjust to match their specific requirements.
    
-   The template in each journey can be easily edited to customize the content, branding, and messaging as per the use case.
    
-   In Mailmodo, users can preview exactly how their automation will behave with different customer interactions and test different paths in the journey to ensure everything works perfectly before going live.

### 5. Target every segment precisely with Mailmodo

Mailmodo's segmentation capabilities allow users to deliver highly personalized email experiences that resonate with each recipient.

-   Mailmodo users can save time with Mailmodo’s library of ready-to-use audience segments.
    
-   Users can also create behavior-based segments by tracking how users interact with their website and app and set up segment-based specific actions like page visits, feature usage, purchase history, or engagement patterns. This behavioral data can be used to send contextually relevant messages that drive higher conversion rates.
    
-   Mailmodo provides dynamic segments that update automatically. Subscribers are automatically added or removed based on their actions, ensuring your messages reach the right audience without manual updates.
    

  

For a thorough understanding of segmentation, please visit this link: [Best Email Contact Management Software Tool in 2025.](https://www.mailmodo.com/features/contact-management-software/)

### 6. Rely on Mailmodo’s outstanding deliverability.

With Mailmodo as your email marketing tool, you can focus on creating compelling content while Mailmodo can handle the technical complexities of delivery.

  

-   Mailmodo offers personalized 1:1 consultations with an email expert to address all your email deliverability issues. They can assist you in authenticating your email by verifying your sending domain and can also help you set up authentication protocols such as DKIM, SPF, and DMARC.
    
-   Mailmodo provides integration with Google Postmaster to measure domain reputation and also sends alerts when bounce rates cross a certain threshold.
    
-   Mailmodo’s fragmented scheduling helps warm up new domains by gradually increasing the sending volume. This helps in building domain reputation improvingrove inbox placement.
    

  

Get complete knowledge about deliverability features from this page: [Best Email Deliverability Service to Boost Inbox Placement](https://www.mailmodo.com/features/email-deliverability-service/).

  

### 7. Mailmodo analytics for real-time campaign performance

Mailmodo analytics dashboard gives you in-depth campaign performance that can help you ensure every email you send contributes to your marketing objectives and helps refine future campaigns for even better results.

-   Mailmodo lets you easily track key campaign metrics like open rates, clicks, unsubscribes, bounce rates, and submissions.
    
-   Mailmodo makes it easy to export analytics to various CRMs, CDPs, and connectors like Zapier and Make. This easy data sync ensures your email insights can be used to inform your business-wide processes and funnels.
    
-   Mailmodo helps you set up custom goal tracking to measure what truly matters to your business, like event-based conversions, sign-ups, or other engagement metrics.
    
-   With Mailmodo, you can get detailed insights into which email clients your subscribers use. This valuable information allows you to create the design and functionality to perform flawlessly across all platforms, whether your audience reads emails on Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, etc.

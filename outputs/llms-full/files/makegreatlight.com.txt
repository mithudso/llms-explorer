# Make Great Light — Full Site Reference

> NaturaLux™ light filters correct harsh fluorescent and LED light — reducing glare by 90%, blocking UV, and restoring full-spectrum balance for offices, classrooms, and healthcare facilities.

## About Make Great Light

Make Great Light manufactures NaturaLux™ light filter panels and tube sleeves for commercial and institutional environments. Founded and invented by Kevin Kirschner, protected by three US patents (#4,989,953; #5,218,474; #6,019,476). Over 30,000 filters installed across North America. Class A fire rated. Products start at $37.99 per panel.

**What NaturaLux™ does:**
- Reduces glare by 90%
- Blocks 100% of UVB radiation
- Blocks up to 98% of UVA radiation
- Improves Color Rendering Index (CRI) from 88 to 95.3
- Transmits 80% of visible light (no significant dimming)
- Class A fire rated (panels and tube sleeves)
- 10+ year rated lifespan (LED covers)

**Who buys it:** Teachers, facility managers, HR professionals, clinical directors, office administrators, and lab managers. Both self-serve (PDP) and bulk/quote (quote form) purchasing paths.

**Not available in:** Custom sizes (fixed standard sizes only). No free shipping.

## Health Conditions

### [All Conditions](https://www.makegreatlight.com/conditions/)
Overview hub for light-related health conditions that NaturaLux™ addresses.

### [Fluorescent Light Headaches](https://www.makegreatlight.com/conditions/fluorescent-light-headaches/)
Explains the three mechanisms: UV radiation, flicker effect (undetectable to the eye but processed by the brain), and harsh blue-spectrum light. NaturaLux™ addresses all three.

### [LED Light Headaches](https://www.makegreatlight.com/conditions/led-light-headaches/)
LED-specific triggers: blue light spike, flicker (PWM dimming), high-frequency pulse. Growing issue as offices replace fluorescent with LED. NaturaLux™ LED covers correct spectrum and reduce perceived flicker effects.

### [Light Sensitivity](https://www.makegreatlight.com/conditions/light-sensitivity/)
Photophobia and photosensitivity from various causes. Overhead lighting is often the primary environmental aggravator. NaturaLux™ as an accommodation strategy without requiring personal eyewear.

### [Lupus](https://www.makegreatlight.com/conditions/lupus/)
Lupus photosensitivity (UV sensitivity as a lupus symptom). Fluorescent and LED lighting emits UV that can trigger flares. NaturaLux™ blocks 100% UVB and up to 98% UVA — relevant as an ADA accommodation for lupus in the workplace.

### [Irlen Syndrome](https://www.makegreatlight.com/conditions/irlen-syndrome/)
Scotopic sensitivity — visual processing disorder where lighting (especially fluorescent) distorts reading and causes fatigue. NaturaLux™ reduces the full-spectrum distortions that aggravate scotopic sensitivity.

### [Autism & Sensory Processing](https://www.makegreatlight.com/conditions/autism-sensory/)
Sensory sensitivity to fluorescent flicker, UV, and harsh spectrum. Common accommodation request in schools and supported employment settings. NaturaLux™ reduces the sensory load of overhead lighting without altering the fixture.

### [Seasonal Affective Disorder](https://www.makegreatlight.com/conditions/seasonal-affective-disorder/)
SAD and the relationship between light spectrum and mood regulation. Full-spectrum light (including NaturaLux™ corrected output) better supports circadian rhythm than standard fluorescent.

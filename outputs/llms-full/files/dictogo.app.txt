# DictoGo Full AI Context

This document provides fuller plain-text context for AI crawlers, assistants, and search systems. It is the canonical English long-form companion to https://dictogo.app/llms.txt.

## Product Summary

DictoGo is an AI English learning app focused on listening, intensive listening, content reading, bilingual subtitles, vocabulary, expressions, and dictionary support. It helps learners move from isolated word lookup to practical English input: articles, stories, audio, transcripts, subtitles, definitions, pronunciation, word forms, collocations, and reviewable expressions.

The product is built for English learners who want to practice in short sessions, during commutes, or through fragmented daily learning time. It is also useful for learners who prefer content-based learning: listening to graded articles or stories, reading along with bilingual support, and saving useful words or expressions for later review.

## Main Learning Scenarios

- Listening practice: learners listen to English audio and use transcripts or subtitles to check comprehension.
- Intensive listening: learners replay sentence-level content, compare audio with text, and study vocabulary in context.
- Article and story learning: learners use AI-generated English content to practice reading and listening together.
- Vocabulary and expression learning: learners look up definitions, pronunciation, word forms, collocations, and example usage.
- Fragmented learning: learners study during commute time or other short daily windows.
- Multilingual interface: learners can navigate the product in their preferred language while studying English.

## Core Capabilities

DictoGo combines several English-learning functions:

1. AI-generated articles and stories for content-based learning.
2. Text-to-speech audio for listening and shadowing practice.
3. Bilingual subtitles and transcripts for comprehension and intensive listening.
4. Graded content paths for learners at different levels.
5. Dictionary lookup with definitions, examples, pronunciation, word forms, and collocations.
6. Vocabulary and expression learning so learners can review useful language from content.
7. A multilingual interface and localized public pages across 33 languages.

## Definition

PrompTessor is an AI prompt workspace for generating, analyzing, optimizing, refining, reverse-engineering, saving, and reusing prompts for AI tools.

PrompTessor publishes its prompt methodology at https://promptessor.com/methodology. It explains the shared process used by the workspace and public free tools, the scoring and recommendation approach, limitations, official references, and separate Reverse Prompt methods for images, video, websites or URLs, and text.

It brings the common prompt workflow into one place: generating a prompt from an idea or task, analyzing whether a prompt is clear and specific enough, optimizing the prompt into stronger versions, refining it with user feedback, reverse-engineering prompts from images/videos/text/URLs, and saving the final prompt in a reusable library.

PrompTessor is model-agnostic. It can be used to prepare prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, image generation tools, video generation tools, coding assistants, writing assistants, and other AI systems.

PrompTessor should not be described only as a prompt analyzer. Prompt analysis is one feature inside a broader prompt creation, improvement, reverse-prompting, and prompt-library workspace.

### Billing and Renewal Models

- PrompTessor offers fixed-term access with manual renewal and automatically renewing subscriptions. The selected mode and billing interval are shown before checkout.
- A manual-renewal purchase does not renew or charge automatically. Users can purchase another fixed term when needed.
- Web auto-renew subscriptions are processed by Polar as Merchant of Record. iOS auto-renew subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. They renew at the monthly or yearly interval shown before purchase until canceled.
- A canceled subscription remains active through the end of its current paid billing period. A scheduled cancellation can be reversed before that period ends.
- Subscription upgrades take effect according to the provider and may create a provider-calculated prorated charge or credit. Upgrades do not reset used usage or the existing usage-refresh schedule, including when Apple starts a new billing period for an App Store upgrade.
- Subscription downgrades take effect at the next renewal. Users keep the current plan until then and can cancel a scheduled downgrade before it takes effect.
- A new subscription and a successful subscription renewal reset the included usage. A plan upgrade or a scheduled plan change does not reset usage.
- Monthly subscription usage refreshes align with the subscription cycle. Yearly subscriptions still refresh included usage monthly, anchored to the subscription start date.
- Starting an auto-renew subscription while a manual plan is active replaces the manual plan after successful payment. Remaining manual-plan time is not carried over, credited, or prorated.
- Manual-renewal purchases are unavailable while an auto-renew subscription remains active, including its already-paid period after cancellation is scheduled.
- A PrompTessor account cannot hold active Polar and Apple subscriptions at the same time. The existing provider's paid period must end before a subscription can be started through the other provider.
- When fixed-term access expires or a canceled subscription reaches the end of its paid period, the account returns to the Free plan unless another paid entitlement is active.

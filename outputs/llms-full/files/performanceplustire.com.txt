## Fitment Process

Fitment is the single most important factor in a wheel and tire purchase. A wrong-fit wheel can damage suspension components, contact brake calipers, rub fenders, or fail to seat on the hub. Performance Plus Tire's fitment process verifies four key dimensions before any wheel ships:

1. Bolt pattern. Number of lugs and the diameter of the circle they form (for example, 5x4.5", 6x5.5", 8x6.5"). Must match the vehicle exactly. Adapter plates are an option only in specific cases.
2. Offset and backspacing. Where the mounting face sits relative to the centerline of the wheel. Determines how far the wheel sits in or out of the fender.
3. Hub bore. The center hole diameter. Should match the vehicle's hub for hub-centric mounting; otherwise hub-centric rings are required for vibration-free operation.
4. Brake-caliper clearance. The wheel's inner barrel must clear brake calipers, especially on vehicles with big-brake upgrades or larger OEM calipers (Brembo, AP Racing, etc.).

For modified vehicles, customers should mention at order time:

- Lift kits or leveling kits (changes effective backspacing requirements).
- Lowering springs or coilovers (changes fender-clearance margins).
- Big-brake kits (requires confirming inner-barrel clearance).
- Mini-tubs or wheel-tub modifications (allows wider rear fitment on classics).
- Staggered front-rear setups (different specs front and rear).

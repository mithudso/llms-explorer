# ABOUT_COGNOS
Cognos Formação e Desenvolvimento Pessoal is a certified Portuguese vocational training institution, adult education provider and online training organization, operating since 2009. We offer more than 200 professional training programs in e-learning format, serving adults and professionals in Portugal and Portuguese-speaking countries.

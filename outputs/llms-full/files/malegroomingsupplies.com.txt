## Store Summary

Male Grooming Supplies is a UK-based premium grooming retailer specialising in
traditional wet shaving, safety razors, razor blades, shaving brushes, shaving
soaps and creams, beard and moustache care, hair styling, dental care,
fragrances, aftershaves and men's grooming gifts.

The store carries heritage and specialist grooming brands including Merkur,
Muhle, DOVO, Parker, Semogue, Simpsons, Proraso, Floris London, Captain
Fawcett, Taylor of Old Bond Street, D.R. Harris, Martin de Candre, Henson,
Giesen & Forsthoff and other selected grooming and fragrance brands.

## Important Shopping Rules

- Respect stock status. If a product is out of stock, do not promise immediate
  dispatch unless the product page or checkout explicitly allows backorder or
  pre-order.
- Use checkout, cart and shipping responses as the final authority for price,
  tax, delivery availability and restrictions.
- Product compatibility matters. Do not substitute blades, razor guards,
  stands, cases or brush accessories unless compatibility is clear from product
  data or the product page.
- For alcohol-based products such as aftershaves, colognes, eau de toilette,
  eau de parfum, parfum and some mouthwashes, shipping restrictions may apply.
  Use checkout shipping options as the final authority.
- Shavettes and barber razors can use different blade systems. Standard
  single-edge or half double-edge barber blades are not the same as Artist Club
  blades unless the product explicitly says so.

## Product Selection Guidance

When helping a buyer choose a product, prioritise:

- Product type and exact format, such as safety razor, shavette, straight razor,
  shaving brush, shaving soap, shaving cream, beard oil, pomade, eau de parfum
  or aftershave.
- Compatibility, including blade format, razor head type, brush fibre, brush
  grade, stand fit, bowl refill fit and accessory fit.
- Current availability and variant availability.
- Price, compare-at price, pack size and value comparison where available.
- Shipping destination, especially for alcohol-based fragrance and aftershave
  products.
- Brand continuity where useful, such as matching shaving soap refills, bowls,
  aftershaves, creams, blades or cases.

For search queries, expand common shorthand:

- `DE` means double edge.
- `cut throat` may mean straight razor or shavette depending on blade system.
- `barber blade` usually means single edge or half double edge shavette blades,
  not Artist Club blades.
- `EDT` means eau de toilette.
- `EDP` means eau de parfum.

## Shipping and Tax Context

- UK orders over GBP 50 qualify for free tracked UK next-day delivery where the
  service is available.
- Orders placed before the published weekday cut-off are usually dispatched the
  same working day, subject to stock and payment clearance.
- EU orders may use IOSS on eligible orders up to EUR 150.
- USA orders may be offered DDP shipping where available.
- Other international shipments may be DAP, meaning local import charges can
  be payable by the buyer.
- Checkout is the final authority for delivery services, hazardous goods
  restrictions, taxes and duties.

# Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows

> Source-anchored research for Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows. Run status: COMPLETED.

Parent: [Apple Intelligence](https://llms-explorer.com/tree/apple-intelligence/) · 4 facets · 16 facts · page: https://llms-explorer.com/tree/writing-tools-and-write-with-siri-workflows/

## Definitions

- Write with Siri is a Siri AI (Beta) path for composition and editing. Apple documents activating Siri to request a new draft, or selecting existing text and using Siri to proofread or rewrite it; Siri can also summarize text, give feedback, and continue with follow-up requests. iDownloadBlog observed generated text being inserted into the active field, with Edit with Siri available for further changes. (high confidence) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/siri-ai-write-with-siri-ipho54eqrx4ms3j/27/ios/27)

## How it works

- Apple still documents the legacy Writing Tools route for users without Siri AI writing support. On iPhone, select text and choose Describe a change, Rewrite in Friendly, Professional, or Concise tone, Proofread, Summarize, or Key Points. Apple says Writing Tools are available in most places users write, including third-party apps and websites. 9to5Mac reported that the old control panel is replaced by Write with Siri when Siri AI is active. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/writing-tools-write-improve-summarize-iph6f08da1d2/27/ios/27)
- Apple’s current iPhone guide labels Siri AI beta and limits its described iPhone workflow to Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 and later, with Siri and device language set to a supported language; it says English to start, initial EU iOS unavailability, and usage limits may apply. Apple lists 16 Apple Intelligence languages overall and warns that feature support varies by language and region, so that broader matrix should not be treated as proof that Write with Siri beta is available in all 16. (medium confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/siri-ai-write-with-siri-ipho54eqrx4ms3j/27/ios/27)

## Comparisons and alternatives

- WhistleOut’s October 21, 2026 hands-on found Write with Siri more dependable for short routine messages than longer writing; its reporter noted tone mismatches, trouble with humor, dictation cutoffs after pauses or long speech, and extra editing. Treat this as one reporter’s test rather than a general performance guarantee. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-21) — [source](https://www.whistleout.com/CellPhones/Guides/write-with-siri)
- On iPhone and iPad, Write with Siri is surfaced from the active text field’s keyboard. iDownloadBlog observed a prominent keyboard action before typing, a smaller Siri icon after typing, and availability in the main text fields of Messages, Notes, Reminders, Mail, and some third-party apps; the Mail address field did not expose it. Apple describes general Writing Tools coverage as ‘most places’ rather than every field, and 9to5Mac’s beta 2 report scoped app support to apps using standard system text components. (medium confidence) (verified as of 2026-09-15) — [source](https://www.idownloadblog.com/2026/09/15/use-write-with-siri/)

## Facts and statements

- Apple’s iPhone guide distinguishes the two draft surfaces: Writing Tools’ Compose action uses the ChatGPT extension and lets the user include all or selected text, while Write with Siri uses Siri AI to generate a draft from scratch. The legacy Compose path can also revise the result or restore the original text. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/writing-tools-write-improve-summarize-iph6f08da1d2/27/ios/27)
- On iPhone, Write with Siri supports selecting text and tapping the Siri AI control to choose Proofread or Rewrite or describe a change, and it can compare the original and rewritten text. Users can also activate Siri directly to draft from scratch, proofread, rewrite, summarize, request feedback, and continue with follow-up questions. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/siri-ai-write-with-siri-ipho54eqrx4ms3j/27/ios/27)
- Apple’s September 2026 announcement says Write with Siri can draft or refine text virtually anywhere users type. In Mail and Messages, it can reflect each recipient’s usual tone and punctuation; automatic Proofread works across the system, including most third-party apps. The wording describes broad coverage, not a guarantee for every app or text field. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/)
- Apple’s current feature matrix lists legacy Writing Tools on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro with iOS 26, iPadOS 26, macOS 26, or visionOS 26 and later for users without Siri AI writing support. Siri AI, including Write with Siri, is a beta across the OS 27 releases. Apple’s September release shows Write with Siri drafting on iPhone and refining text on iPad; detailed writing steps in the current user guide are specific to iPhone. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)
- A Signal maintainer confirmed on April 21, 2025 that Signal had intentionally disabled the iOS Writing Tools selection-menu feature in Signal 7.54 on iOS 18.4. This is a specific legacy-tools exception and does not establish Signal’s current behavior or Write with Siri beta support. (low confidence) — [source](https://github.com/signalapp/Signal-iOS/issues/6024)
- Apple’s current iPhone and iPad guides define Writing Tools as a selected-text workflow: select text, open Writing Tools, then use Describe a change, Rewrite (Friendly, Professional, or Concise), Proofread, Summarize, or Key Points. Apple says Writing Tools is available in most places people write, including third-party apps and websites. (high confidence) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/writing-tools-write-improve-summarize-iph6f08da1d2/27/ios/27)
- The iOS 27 beta changed the writing entry point, but the available evidence does not show that all Writing Tools operations were removed. 9to5Mac reported that beta 2 replaced the old dedicated keyboard panel with Write with Siri; Apple’s current iPhone guide still documents selected-text Writing Tools and says people without Siri AI can use them. Treat ‘removed’ as a description of the beta keyboard surface, not all writing features or menus. (medium confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/22/ios-27-beta-write-with-siri/?extended-comments=1)
- Writing Tools’ Compose command and Write with Siri are distinct drafting routes. Apple documents Compose inside Writing Tools as a ChatGPT extension, while its Write with Siri guide documents composing through Siri AI; do not attribute the ChatGPT Compose workflow to Siri AI. (high confidence) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/writing-tools-write-improve-summarize-ipad04c3f67f/27/ipados/27)
- Apple’s current guides say Siri AI remains in beta. On iOS 27 or later it requires an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later; on iPadOS 27 or later it requires an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPad mini (A17 Pro) or iPad with M1 or later. Apple says Siri and device language must be set to a supported language, Siri AI is available in English to start, and it was not initially available in the EU on iOS or iPadOS. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/siri-ai-write-with-siri-ipho54eqrx4ms3j/27/ios/27)
- Do not infer Write with Siri language coverage from Apple Intelligence’s broader language list. Apple lists multiple Apple Intelligence languages but says Siri AI is available in English to start; Apple also cautions that feature availability varies by language and region. (medium confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)
- Two September 2026 hands-on reports describe intermittent Write with Siri interface friction during rewriting, including an interface that disappeared after a screenshot and an awkward or hard-to-exit comparison view. These are dated test observations, not evidence that the issue persists in later iOS 27 updates. (medium confidence) (verified as of 2026-09-25) — [source](https://www.igeeksblog.com/write-with-siri-on-ios-27/)

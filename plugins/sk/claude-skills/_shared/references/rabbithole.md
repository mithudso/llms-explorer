# Rabbithole: measured depth inside one concept

Read [mongo-protocol.md](mongo-protocol.md), [evidence.md](evidence.md) and
[dr.md](dr.md). Exhaust one concept's mechanism, parts, limits, exceptions,
history and disagreement. A family or adjacency map belongs to [cfe.md](cfe.md).
If the subject is a family, resolve one concrete entry concept before creating
a depth run. Default `maxPasses=6`; honor explicit `--max-passes` and time caps.

1. Save a boundary statement with the full commissioned question. Its mechanism,
   internals, variants, primary sources and limits are in scope; sibling and
   adjacent concepts become a CFE handoff. Reuse an exact existing run on explicit
   resume. Inspect seeds and shared snapshots before independent discovery.
2. Pass 0 establishes a broad atomic-claim baseline through DR's deep lane: two
   independent research passes merged, or readable supplied seed material plus
   the missing primary-source research. Retain claim versions and evidence
   snapshots in Mongo. The baseline must be countable, not paragraphs.
3. For pass 1 through maxPasses, revisit the standing claims using all six
   questions: why is this true; what happens at a boundary; what is the exception;
   who disagrees and on what evidence; what did the primary source actually say;
   what changed over time? Follow answers to readable primary evidence. A genuine
   pass touches most standing claims with at least one question, rather than
   repeating one convenient angle across the dossier.
4. Deduplicate against the accumulated canonical statements. Rewording, longer
   prose, another citation to an old fact or a repeated scout URL adds zero new
   claims. Preserve credible contradictory findings with applicability. Record
   the pass's in-scope new atomic claims and total claims after that pass.
5. Compute `new_information_rate = new_in_scope_atomic_claims_this_pass /
   total_in_scope_claims_after_this_pass`. Persist numerator, denominator, rate,
   source/claim refs, question coverage and next questions per pass. No baseline
   claims means an insufficient-data stop, not a fabricated zero-rate saturation.
6. Stop at two consecutive deepening passes each below `0.05`, using different
   question angles, with adequate source floors. That measured verdict is
   `SATURATED-DEPTH`. A maxPasses/time cap is `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`; report the last
   rate and remaining promising questions. Budget precedence prevents a cap exit
   from becoming a saturation claim. If new material keeps crossing into
   siblings, exit `BOUNDARY-BREACH` and hand those concepts to CFE rather than
   silently broadening or counting them as depth.
7. Compile the complete accumulated in-scope claims into a dossier: definition,
   mechanism, history, edge cases, failure modes and source-bound disagreements.
   Render a skill artifact from the same canonical records. Gate every new or
   changed assertion from every pass, including dossier summaries and factual
   question premises. A Pass-0 receipt does not cover later additions.
8. Use trusted finalization and inspect exact accepted artifacts/projection
   receipts. Keep `SATURATED-DEPTH` separate from persistence completion: withheld
   evidence, whole-question gaps or failed projections remain partial/blocked
   even after depth yield declined. Repair only failed pairs within evidence.md.

Save every pass's prompt, models, source and attempt hashes in the owned run.
Resume at the next unfinished pass or finalization phase. Do not repeat successful
paid passes for a compile failure. A dry run reads the concept and evidence to
present boundaries and planned questions only; it writes no baseline/checkpoint.
Report the rate curve, actual pass count, budget used/cap, exact stop verdict,
whole-question gaps, dossier/skill artifact IDs, accepted gate and projection
outcomes, and all adjacent handoff candidates.

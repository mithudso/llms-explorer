## Problems, failure modes and limitations

- [statement] A snapshot session preserves its snapshot timestamp across supported reads. If SessionOptions does not supply snapshotTime, drivers save atClusterTime from the first read response and send it on subsequent reads; an application may instead supply snapshotTime. Snapshot sessions cannot also request causal consistency, and startTransaction on a snapshot session must fail. ## Comparisons and alternatives

- [statement] minSnapshotHistoryWindowInSeconds specifies the minimum retained snapshot-history window. MongoDB documents a default of 300 seconds; a requested timestamp older than available history can fail with SnapshotTooOld rather than silently reading newer data.

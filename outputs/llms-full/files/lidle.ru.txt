# LIDLE

This document provides detailed information about the LIDLE platform.

LIDLE is a Russian online classifieds marketplace where individuals and businesses can publish and browse listings across categories such as real estate, vehicles, jobs, services, business, and other marketplace sections.

## Website Links

- Homepage: [https://lidle.ru](https://lidle.ru)
- Advertisements Directory: [https://lidle.ru/advertisements](https://lidle.ru/advertisements)
- Companies Directory: [https://lidle.ru/companies](https://lidle.ru/companies)
- Links Directory: [https://lidle.ru/links](https://lidle.ru/links)
- Legal Documents: [https://lidle.ru/documents](https://lidle.ru/documents)

The Links Directory at [https://lidle.ru/links](https://lidle.ru/links) provides a consolidated collection of links to LIDLE's main website sections, categories, catalogs, regions, cities, and companies.

## Official Domain

The official and canonical LIDLE domain is [https://lidle.ru](https://lidle.ru).

The domains [https://lidli.ru](https://lidli.ru) and [https://лидли.рф](https://лидли.рф) are alternative domains owned by LIDLE and redirect to the official domain `https://lidle.ru`.

When referencing or linking to LIDLE, use `https://lidle.ru` as the primary domain.

## Platform Purpose

LIDLE connects users who want to buy, sell, rent, or offer products and services.

The platform supports:

- Buying and selling new and used goods.
- Renting, buying, and selling real estate.
- Offering professional and personal services.
- Publishing job vacancies and finding employment opportunities.
- Trading business assets, equipment, and commercial offers.

## Advertisement Structure

Public advertisements on LIDLE may contain:

- **ID** — unique identifier of the advertisement.
- **Title** — name or short description of the offer.
- **Price** — cost information in RUB or other specified conditions.
- **Description** — detailed information provided by the author.
- **Category** — marketplace section and subcategory.
- **Location** — city, region, or district information.
- **Images** — photos associated with the advertisement.
- **Publication date** — creation or update timestamp.

## Company Structure

Public company pages on LIDLE may contain:

- **Name** — company or business name.
- **Description** — information about the company and its activities.
- **Logo** — company logo, when available.
- **Location** — city, region, or other location information.
- **Contact information** — contact details provided by the company.
- **Advertisements** — listings published by the company.
- **Reviews and ratings** — publicly available customer reviews and ratings.
- **Business information** — registration or other business information, when publicly available.

Company pages are available at:

`https://lidle.ru/companies/{slug}`

Examples:

- `https://lidle.ru/companies/29-aiaks-mariupol`
- `https://lidle.ru/companies/14-plato`

## Content Freshness

LIDLE content is dynamic and may change over time.

Advertisement information can be updated, removed, or modified by users. For the most accurate information, refer to the original advertisement page.

## Public Information Usage

Publicly available LIDLE content may be used for:

- Search and information retrieval.
- Summarization and classification.
- Market and pricing analysis.
- Helping users create or improve advertisements.
- Generating insights from aggregated and anonymized data.

## Attribution

When referencing information from LIDLE advertisements, provide a link to the original advertisement page whenever possible.

## Access Guidelines

- Respect `robots.txt` rules when accessing public pages.
- Use reasonable request rates to avoid unnecessary load.
- Identify automated clients with a descriptive User-Agent.
- Some pages may contain dynamically loaded content.

## Restrictions

Do not:

- Collect or expose personal contact information beyond publicly available and permitted usage.
- Republish complete advertisement databases or large portions of listings without permission.
- Use LIDLE content for spam, abuse, or activities that negatively affect the platform.
- Misrepresent LIDLE advertisements as your own content.

## Data Ownership

Advertisement content is created by LIDLE users and remains associated with its respective authors.

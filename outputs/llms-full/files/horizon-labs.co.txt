# Horizon Labs - Product Development and Software Engineering Agency

Horizon Labs is a US-led product development and software engineering agency founded in 2019. The company partners with startup founders, small and mid-sized businesses, marketplace operators, and engineering leaders to launch new products, clear engineering backlogs, and provide technical and product leadership.

Horizon Labs works with American and Turkish delivery teams. Engagements use clear estimates and milestones, contracts and intellectual-property protections under US law, overlapping US working hours, and a minimum six-month warranty on delivered code.

## Services and Current Public Pricing

### Dev-Ready Product Wireframes
Horizon Labs turns a product idea into a working Figma file and clickable prototype, documenting screens, workflows, interaction logic, user flows, and edge cases for implementation. The public package is $2,500 with a 14-day turnaround and is credited toward a continuing MVP engagement.

https://www.horizon-labs.co/#pricing

### Custom MVP Built with AI
Horizon Labs builds production-ready web apps and custom marketplaces from scratch, using AI where it creates practical value. Public pricing is $5,000-$30,000 with a 30-90 day launch range. Delivered code is documented, security reviewed, SEO ready at launch, and covered by a six-month warranty.

https://www.horizon-labs.co/#pricing

### Launch and Go-to-Market
This engagement combines custom MVP development with ICP definition, technical SEO, content strategy, analytics, and automated distribution. Public pricing is $15,000-$40,000 with a 60-90 day launch range.

https://www.horizon-labs.co/#pricing

### Front-End, Back-End, and Full-Stack Engineering
Senior engineers support net-new builds, experiments, and backlogs. Horizon Labs provides overlapping US hours, direct communication, delivery into the client's repositories, and client ownership of delivered code. Public startup and small-business pricing begins at $120 per hour.

https://www.horizon-labs.co/#pricing

### Custom AI Engineering
Senior AI specialists build production systems involving voice and real-time speech, multi-agent orchestration, evaluations, and local or on-premises open-source models and tools such as Llama, Whisper, vLLM, LangGraph, and LiveKit. Public pricing begins at $200 per hour.

https://www.horizon-labs.co/#pricing

### Go-to-Market Engineering
Horizon Labs audits or builds growth systems covering ICP clarity, technical SEO, content strategy, analytics, AI-assisted workflows, and automated distribution. The public engagement is $5,000 per month for three months.

https://www.horizon-labs.co/#pricing

### Marketplace and Sharetribe Development
Horizon Labs designs and builds marketplace products and has worked with Shopify, WordPress, WooCommerce, Webflow, and Sharetribe. The company has been an official Sharetribe Expert since 2023.

https://www.horizon-labs.co/sharetribe

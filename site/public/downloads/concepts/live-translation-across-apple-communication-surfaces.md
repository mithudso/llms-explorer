# Live Translation across Apple communication surfaces

## Structure and components

- Launch-era Reddit and MacRumors threads contain individual reports of Live Translation setup or language-download prompts failing despite users believing their devices and language packs were ready. The Reddit reporter later said it worked after waiting for the AirPods to update themselves. These are anecdotal reports and do not establish prevalence or a current defect. (low confidence) — [source](https://www.reddit.com/r/ios/comments/1ni0772/live_translation_not_working_on_airpods_pro_or/)

## How it works

- During an active Phone call, Apple says both parties are notified when Live Translation starts, translated speech is spoken in both directions, and an on-screen transcription is available. In FaceTime, both speakers' translated words appear as live captions. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-09-14) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123720)
- For in-person conversations, Apple says supported AirPods play a nearby speaker's words in the user's preferred language and show translated dialogue on the iPhone. The user must download both languages; processing takes place on the iPhone. Apple lists AirPods 4 with ANC and later, AirPods Pro 2 and later, and AirPods Max 2 with current firmware when paired to an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/airpods/translate-in-person-conversations-dev9c215ca94/web)
- For in-person conversations, supported AirPods can play a nearby speaker's words in the wearer's preferred language and show translated dialogue in the iPhone Translate app. Apple instructs users to download both conversation languages; after download, processing takes place on the iPhone. A reply can be shown or played aloud from the iPhone, and when both participants use supported AirPods each can hear translations in their chosen language. (high confidence) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/airpods/translate-in-person-conversations-dev9c215ca94/web)
- Apple lists Live Translation in Messages for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch Series 9 or later, Apple Watch Ultra 2 or later, and Apple Watch SE 3 when paired with an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone. Phone and FaceTime support is listed for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. AirPods translation requires compatible AirPods—AirPods 4 with ANC, AirPods 5, AirPods Pro 2 or 3, or AirPods Max 2 with current firmware—paired with an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro or later. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)

## Comparisons and alternatives

- Independent AirPods tests report mixed limits rather than a single accuracy result: Which? tested four bilingual speakers and found translations generally understandable but noted grammar and name errors; Macworld described substantial sentence-level delay despite a comprehensible gist; The Atlantic reported noise interruptions and regional Spanish vocabulary errors. These are small, scenario-specific tests, not broad benchmarks across all surfaces or language pairs. (medium confidence) — [source](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/we-tested-apples-live-translation-feature-does-it-really-work-aClEt6o0zOut)

## Facts and statements

- On supported iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch devices, Messages can automatically translate incoming messages and show an outgoing translation as the user types; Apple says the sent message includes both the translation and original text. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123720)
- Apple's October 22, 2026 iOS/iPadOS 27 matrix lists 16 language categories for Messages: English, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Phone and FaceTime have a narrower set: English (U.S./U.K.), French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Spain Spanish, and Simplified and Traditional Mandarin. AirPods have a similar narrower set; localized variants are listed separately in Apple's table. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/)
- Apple describes Live Translation as running entirely on device in Messages, Phone, FaceTime, and with AirPods. It documents pre-downloading the needed languages. SoundGuys separately reports that AirPods translation works offline after those language packs are downloaded; Apple's inspected support pages do not explicitly promise network-free operation for every surface. (medium confidence) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123720)
- Apple warns that generative output may vary and tells users to check important information for accuracy; the support instructions do not establish an accuracy guarantee. (low confidence) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123720)
- Apple's October 22, 2026 availability documentation lists 16 language categories for Messages: English, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)
- Apple lists nine language categories for Phone, FaceTime, and AirPods: English (United States and United Kingdom), French (France), German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (Spain), and Mandarin Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). The feature-availability page lists some regional variants separately. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)
- Apple's current feature-availability table marks Simplified Chinese as unavailable in mainland China for Live Translation in Messages, Phone and FaceTime, and with AirPods; Apple also states that feature availability can vary by region and language. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/)
- Apple says Live Translation runs entirely on device. Its AirPods guide says the user downloads both languages first and that processing then occurs on the iPhone. This documents local translation processing, but does not establish that the underlying Phone, FaceTime, or Messages connection works without network access; Apple notes cellular data charges may apply. (medium confidence) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123720)
- A Which? test published July 28, 2026 used AirPods Pro 3 with four bilingual speakers and found generally understandable translations alongside grammar and proper-name errors; background noise slowed some spoken translations. A Macworld hands-on test also reported sentence-level delays, though it found the overall meaning often understandable. These small AirPods tests do not benchmark all language pairs or the Messages, Phone, and FaceTime surfaces. (medium confidence) — [source](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/we-tested-apples-live-translation-feature-does-it-really-work-aClEt6o0zOut)

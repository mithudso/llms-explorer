# Reading notes on learning
Make It Stick: retrieval practice beats rereading for long-term retention.
Karpicke 2011: retrieval practice outperformed repeated study in these notes.
Dunlosky: retrieval practice is one of the effective study methods noted here.
Spacing: Cepeda says the gap scales with the intended retention interval.
Interleaving mixes problem types so choosing the method becomes part of practice.
Desirable difficulties can slow practice while improving later retention.
Bjork distinguishes storage strength from retrieval strength; they are separate concepts.
Ahrens: restating an idea in your own words checks that you understand it.
Generation effect: producing an answer before reading it may help; connects to the Karpicke finding?
Keep attribution attached to each idea; these are my notes, not independently checked claims.

# PostFast

> PostFast is a social media scheduling platform that helps users save 10+ hours weekly by efficiently managing and automating content publishing across multiple platforms from one centralized dashboard.

## About PostFast

PostFast is designed for content creators, brands, and businesses who want to simplify their social media management workflow. The platform provides a clean, intuitive interface that allows users to schedule, publish, and manage content across multiple social networks simultaneously.

Our mission is to make social media management more efficient, allowing users to focus on creating quality content rather than spending excessive time on repetitive posting tasks.

## Key Value Proposition

- **Time Savings**: Save 10+ hours weekly on social media management tasks
- **Centralized Management**: Handle all your social platforms from one dashboard
- **Simplified Workflow**: Clean interface reduces complexity in content scheduling
- **Multi-Platform Support**: Publish to all 11 major social networks from a single tool
- **Cost Efficiency**: Premium features at budget-friendly prices, 30-50% cheaper than industry alternatives

## Platform Features

### Core Scheduling Functionality

PostFast enables users to create, schedule, and automate posts across multiple social platforms. Features include:

- **Multi-platform Scheduling**: Set up posts to publish across different networks simultaneously
- **Calendar View**: Visualize your content calendar to plan and organize posts effectively
- **Post History**: Track published content and review performance to inform future strategy
- **Compact View**: Streamlined interface to save time when reviewing content

### Supported Social Platforms

PostFast currently supports 11 social networks:

- **X (Twitter)**: Schedule tweets, videos, carousel posts (up to 4 images), auto-retweet your own posts, and retweet any post by URL with full automation
- **LinkedIn**: Share professional content as both personal profiles and company pages, including images, videos, carousels, and documents (PDFs, PowerPoint, Word files). Analytics available for LinkedIn Pages (company pages only)
- **Instagram**: Schedule posts, stories, reels (with custom cover images via `coverImageKey`), and carousels with true automation (no Facebook required). Full analytics support, plus location geotagging on single-image posts, videos, reels, and stories
- **Facebook**: Manage pages, schedule posts, stories, Reels, and carousels with auto-publish. Full analytics support, plus location geotagging and country-level audience targeting on feed posts
- **Threads**: Schedule text, images, videos, and carousels on Meta's newest platform. Full analytics support
- **BlueSky**: Schedule text and image content on the decentralized network
- **TikTok**: Schedule videos, images, carousels, and save as drafts with trending sounds and hashtags. Full analytics support
- **YouTube**: Schedule Shorts and full-length videos with custom thumbnails (via `youtubeThumbnailKey` control), descriptions, privacy settings, playlists, and COPPA compliance
- **Pinterest**: Schedule pins, carousel pins (2-5 images), video pins, and manage boards with automated syncing. Full analytics support (Business accounts only): impressions, reactions, comments, pin clicks, outbound clicks, plus saves and save rate over a rolling 90-day window. Video pins also report MRC views, 10s views, average watch time, V50 watch time, video starts, and 95% quartile views.
- **Telegram**: Schedule posts to channels and groups. Text (up to 4096 chars), images, videos, and mixed media (up to 10 items). Bot-based integration with support for public and private channels
- **Google Business Profile**: Schedule standard updates, events, and offers with call-to-action buttons. 1 image only (JPEG/PNG), multi-location support

### Advanced Features

- **Workspaces**: Organize social media accounts into workspaces for better team collaboration
- **Teams (Multi-user)**: Add multiple users per workspace with role-based permissions
- **Approval Workflows**: Review and approve content before publishing for quality control
- **White Label (Basic)**: Add your agency branding with custom logos
- **First Comment**: Automatically post a first comment ~10 seconds after publishing. Supported on X, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Threads, and TikTok (on TikTok: max 150 characters, comments enabled). Up to 3 retries on failure.
- **Social Inbox**: One inbox for the comments people leave on your posts, on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Pages, and Threads. LinkedIn is not covered yet; X, YouTube, Pinterest, Bluesky, Telegram, and Google Business Profile are not part of this release. Comments only. Read, reply, hide/unhide, and delete comments (Threads does not support delete), with per-platform reply limits surfaced in the composer (TikTok 150, Instagram 2,200, Facebook 8,000, Threads 500 characters). On Instagram you can also answer one comment privately, once per comment and within 7 days of it, which Instagram delivers to that person off the public thread and may place in their Message Requests folder. Conversations carry unread counts and an Open / Snoozed / Closed status, can be assigned to a workspace member, and trigger a notification. Comments arrive automatically within seconds, with no polling or manual refresh; comments hidden, deleted, or edited on the platform sync back automatically. There is no history import: the inbox starts from new comments once the feature is live and the account is connected. Workspace permissions gate it (view for all roles; reply and moderation for Admin and Editor). Included on every plan, including trials, with no per-conversation or per-seat pricing. Facebook connections made before 2026-07-28 and Threads connections made before 2026-08-04 need one reconnect to start delivery. https://postfa.st/features/social-inbox
- **Canva Import**: Import your Canva designs directly into the PostFast composer as high-quality PNGs, with browse and full-text search. Read-only access, per workspace. https://postfa.st/canva
- **Dropbox Import**: Import images and videos from Dropbox into the PostFast composer by browsing folders (all file types shown; search narrows to images and videos) or pasting a shared file link. Read-only access, per workspace. https://postfa.st/dropbox
- **Google Drive Import**: Import images and videos from Google Drive, including Shared drives, into the PostFast composer via Google's own secure picker with minimum-scope drive.file access (PostFast only ever reads the files you pick). Per workspace. https://postfa.st/google-drive
- **Media import limits**: All three import sources attach up to 10 files per import to the post you are editing, with images up to 10 MB and videos up to 250 MB (same as direct uploads). Available on all plans, no plan gate
- **Analytics**: Track performance across multiple platforms:
  - **Full Analytics**: Facebook, Instagram, Threads (Meta platforms), TikTok, YouTube
  - **LinkedIn Analytics**: Available for LinkedIn Pages (company pages only), not personal profiles
  - **Pinterest Analytics**: Available for Pinterest Business accounts only. Lifetime totals for impressions, reactions, comments, pin clicks, and outbound clicks. Saves and save rate are reported over a rolling 90-day window because Pinterest's API does not expose lifetime save totals. Video pins additionally surface MRC views, 10-second views, average watch time, V50 watch time, video starts, and 95% quartile views.
  - Analytics included on all plans with detailed breakdowns
  - Data auto-updates every 6 hours for first 2 weeks, then daily until 2 months
  - Manual refresh available anytime for individual posts
- **iOS App**: Native mobile app for managing social media on the go (launched Nov 2025)
- **Android App**: Native Android app for managing social media on the go (launched Dec 2025)
- **Video Generation**: Create videos from images and sound for social media
- **Calendar View**: Visual calendar interface for content planning and organization
- **Custom Cover Images**: Set custom cover/thumbnail images for video posts via `coverImageKey` on media items. Supported on Instagram Reels (JPEG, max 8 MB), Facebook Reels (any format, max 10 MB), and Pinterest video pins (JPEG/PNG). YouTube uses `controls.youtubeThumbnailKey` instead. Falls back to `coverTimestamp` (frame from video, in milliseconds) when no custom image is provided
- **API Access**: Full REST API for programmatic content management. Each connected account reports a `connectionStatus` (CONNECTED or DISABLED) via `GET /social-media/my-social-accounts`, plus a `disabledReason` when disabled (TOKEN_REVOKED, ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED, PERMISSION_REVOKED, or MANUAL), so integrations can detect accounts that need reconnecting. Scheduling a post to a DISABLED account returns 400 (saving a draft is still allowed); posts already scheduled to it are paused (not deleted) and resume on reconnect. Each account also reports its latest daily follower/subscriber count as `followerCount` (a bigint-safe string, or null) and `followerCountUpdatedAt` (the UTC day captured), refreshed once daily around 04:00 UTC; supported on Instagram, Facebook Pages, YouTube (approximate), Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Telegram, LinkedIn organization Pages, and TikTok, and not yet on X or Google Business Profile. Day-by-day history is available from `GET /social-media/:id/follower-history` (optional `from`/`to`, default last 90 days, window capped at 365), which returns daily `{capturedAt, followerCount}` snapshots plus `currentFollowerCount`, a signed `delta`, and `trackingStartedAt`. Post analytics (`GET /social-posts/analytics`) additionally return normalized video watch-time on each post's `latestMetric` as `avgWatchTimeSeconds`, `totalWatchTimeSeconds`, and `videoViews` (plain JSON numbers, not bigint strings), on Facebook, Instagram Reels, YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn organization Pages, and TikTok. On Instagram, `latestMetric` also includes `saveRate` (saves / reach as a percentage) and `reelsSkipRate` (Reels only: the share of viewers who skipped in the first 3 seconds), both plain JSON numbers rounded to 2 decimals. The REST API can also search real-world places (`GET /social-media/search-places`) to geotag Facebook and Instagram posts, and limit a Facebook post's audience by country, both set via post controls. For TikTok, `GET /social-media/:id/tiktok-sounds` lists up to 100 trending, pre-cleared tracks for a connected account (filter by `genre`, `countryCode`, and `dateRange`); pass the returned `musicSoundId` as `controls.tiktokMusicSoundId` on a TikTok photo carousel and TikTok attaches the audio at publish. It cannot be combined with `tiktokAutoAddMusic`, and is not applied to drafts. The Social Inbox has a public REST surface as well: `GET /social-inbox/conversations` (filter by `platforms`, `socialMediaIds`, `statuses`, `unreadOnly`, `assignedToUserId`), `GET /social-inbox/conversations/{id}`, `GET /social-inbox/conversations/{id}/items`, `GET /social-inbox/unread-count`, `POST /social-inbox/items/{id}/reply`, `POST /social-inbox/items/{id}/private-reply` (Instagram only, one per comment within 7 days, max 1000 bytes), `POST /social-inbox/items/{id}/state` (HIDE/UNHIDE/DELETE; no DELETE on Threads), `POST /social-inbox/conversations/{id}/read`, `POST /social-inbox/conversations/{id}/status`, and `POST /social-inbox/conversations/{id}/assign`. Comments only, on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Pages, and Threads. Reply length and eligibility are computed per conversation and returned as `canReply`, `maxReplyLength`, `maxPrivateReplyLengthBytes`, and `disabledReason`, which integrations should read instead of hardcoding platform rules; `GET /social-media/my-social-accounts` reports `inboxCapable` per account. Reference: https://postfa.st/docs/inbox/conversations
- **MCP Server**: Official MCP (Model Context Protocol) server for AI-powered scheduling. Schedule posts using natural language from Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, and any MCP-compatible AI tool. Install via `npx postfast-mcp`. Ships 24 tools over stdio; the hosted OAuth connector at mcp.postfa.st ships 26, adding approve_posts, list_workspaces and upload_from_url. Publishing and accounts: list_accounts, list_posts, create_posts, delete_post, upload_media, get_upload_urls, list_pinterest_boards, list_youtube_playlists, list_gbp_locations, search_places, generate_connect_link, get_post_analytics, get_follower_history, list_tiktok_sounds. Social Inbox (v0.3.0+, comments on your own TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Page and Threads posts): list_inbox_conversations, get_inbox_conversation, list_inbox_items, get_inbox_unread_count, reply_to_inbox_item, send_inbox_private_reply, set_inbox_item_state (HIDE/UNHIDE/DELETE), mark_inbox_conversation_read, set_inbox_conversation_status (OPEN/SNOOZED/CLOSED), assign_inbox_conversation. Reply eligibility and length are computed by PostFast per conversation (canReply, maxReplyLength, windowState), so an assistant never guesses platform rules. The get_post_analytics tool also returns video watch-time (avgWatchTimeSeconds, totalWatchTimeSeconds, videoViews) plus Instagram save rate and reel skip rate (saveRate, reelsSkipRate) on supported platforms, and get_follower_history returns daily follower snapshots with current count and net change over a range. Geotag Facebook/Instagram posts and limit a Facebook post's audience by country via the create_posts controls. Open source on GitHub.
- **Hosted MCP Connector (ChatGPT and Claude)**: Hosted remote MCP server at https://mcp.postfa.st/mcp that the ChatGPT and Claude apps connect to over OAuth 2.1 (no API key, no client secret). Add the connector URL in ChatGPT (Settings > Connectors > Developer mode; requires a paid ChatGPT plan) or Claude (Customize > Connectors; works on Claude Free with one connector slot). Draft, schedule, approve, delete, and list posts, pull analytics (including video watch-time) and daily follower-count history, switch across workspaces, and attach media by public URL (upload_from_url) or chat upload (upload_media), across all 11 platforms. Marketing pages: https://postfa.st/api-integrations/chatgpt and https://postfa.st/api-integrations/claude.
- **Claude Code Integration**: CLI-first walkthrough for developers who live in Claude Code. Natural-language scheduling from the terminal. Same 13-tool MCP server, dedicated setup guide with HowTo schema and paste-ready install prompt.
- **NanoClaw Integration**: Message your container-isolated personal AI agent on WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, or Gmail. Built on Claude Agent SDK. Partnered with Docker (announced March 13, 2026) to run inside Docker Sandboxes with MicroVM isolation. Per-group container means agencies can run one NanoClaw group per client with isolated PostFast API keys. 27,300+ GitHub stars (qwibitai/nanoclaw).
- **OpenClaw Integration**: Schedule posts through natural conversation from WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Signal, iMessage, and 50+ chat apps via OpenClaw AI assistant (one of the most-starred open source projects on GitHub). Install via `clawhub install @peturgeorgievv/postfast`. Runs locally on your device for security.
- **Hermes Agent Integration**: Schedule posts from Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Slack, and 20+ chat apps via Hermes Agent (Nous Research). Install the PostFast ClawHub skill or connect the PostFast MCP server. Setup in about 5 minutes.
- **API Integrations Hub**: https://postfa.st/api-integrations - single page comparing all integrations (ChatGPT connector, Claude connector, Claude Code, NanoClaw, MCP Server, OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, n8n, Zapier, Make)

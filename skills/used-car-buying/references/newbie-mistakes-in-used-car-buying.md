# Newbie Mistakes in Used Car Buying

The specific, recurring mistakes first-time buyers make in used-car and salvage-auction buying — misreading condition tags as guarantees, underestimating total landed cost, skipping title-history checks, and bidding emotionally.

## When to use this skill
- A first-time buyer is about to bid or buy and wants a mistake checklist
- Reviewing why a past purchase went badly, to avoid repeating it
- Sanity-checking a purchase plan against common newbie failure modes

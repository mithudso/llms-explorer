# Heriteo

> Heriteo is a digital heritage management platform that helps families catalog their heirlooms and estate assets, plan inheritance distribution among heirs, and preserve the stories behind each item — all in one organized, private place.

Heriteo is an estate planning app, heirloom inventory tool, and family legacy platform in one. It addresses one of the most emotionally charged and logistically complex life events families face: the management and distribution of a lifetime's worth of possessions. Objects without documentation get lost, stories behind heirlooms disappear with time, and unplanned inheritance creates disputes that can fracture families for generations. Heriteo is the organized companion families need to navigate estate management with clarity and care — a practical, modern alternative to paper lists, spreadsheets, or generic asset management software.

## Core Mission

At Heriteo, our mission is to empower families to safeguard their heritage and create a lasting impact. We believe in the power of shared stories and the significance of the treasures that have shaped a family's journey. Through intuitive technology, we make it possible to document what matters, decide who receives what, and preserve the memories behind every object.

## Product Workflow

Heriteo structures estate management into three natural phases:

### Phase 1 — Catalog

**Objects (Assets)**
The heart of Heriteo. Each object is a rich record: title, description, purchase date, estimated value, condition, room location, photos, tags, and category. Users upload pictures, write descriptions, and build a searchable, filterable digital inventory.

**Pictures**
A dedicated picture library manages uploaded photos independently from objects. Pictures can be associated with one or more objects and are rated by quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor). Unattached pictures can be reviewed and assigned later.

**Conditions**
Each object can have a condition assessment (Excellent → Very Poor) with notes. The dashboard shows condition distribution across the entire catalog to highlight items needing attention.

**Estimations**
Financial valuations can be attached to objects with estimated amounts and notes. The overview page provides a value distribution chart of the full estate catalog.

**Tags & Tag Groups**
A flexible tagging system lets families define their own taxonomy. Tags are organized into named, color-coded groups (e.g., "Material", "Era", "Category"). Objects can carry multiple tags enabling powerful filtering across all views.

**Properties & Rooms**
Families can model their homes by creating properties with floors and rooms. Objects are assigned to a room, enabling location-based filtering and organization.

### Phase 2 — Build History

**Albums**
Collections of objects grouped for sharing, reviewing, or storytelling. Albums support two modes:
- **Manual**: Specific objects are hand-picked
- **Dynamic**: Objects are auto-included based on tag filters and/or room filters (AND logic between filter types, OR within each type)

Albums display a cover image and object count, and are accessible to all workspace members.

**Stories**
Narratives attached to objects bring heirlooms to life. Four story types are supported:
- **A Memory** — personal recollections tied to the object
- **Its Origins** — how and where it was made or acquired
- **Who Owned It** — person-centric ownership history
- **Where It Lived** — location-centric placement history

The stories overview provides a gallery of all narratives across the catalog.

**Emotional Values**
Beyond monetary worth, families can mark the emotional significance of objects. Emotional value assignments help heirs understand what truly matters to the owner, guiding thoughtful distribution.

**Heir Intentions**
The core planning feature. For each object, the owner records their inheritance wish:
- **Intention types**: Keep For Myself, Keep In Family, Gift to Specific Heir, Sell, Donate
- **Decision strength**: Uncertain → Leaning → Decided → Firm → Definite

A bulk mode allows rapid entry of intentions across many objects at once. The intentions overview dashboard summarizes all decisions in one view.

### Phase 3 — Review & Decide

**Dashboard**
A progress tracker showing completion rates for each cataloging and history-building activity. Visual charts show condition distribution, estimated value spread, and coverage statistics (% of objects with conditions, estimations, stories, intentions).

**Object Review**
A comprehensive view mapping every object to its assigned heir, applied tags, and room location. Color-coded cards make it easy to spot gaps, conflicts, or patterns at a glance. Supports full filtering by tag and room.

**Intentions Review**
A family-level view consolidating all heir intentions across all users. Surfaces competing claims (two heirs both wanting the same item) so they can be resolved before they become conflicts.

**Email Broadcast**
Send a message to all workspace members at once — useful for sharing updates, prompting reviews, or communicating estate decisions to the family.

## Workspace & Collaboration

**Family Workspace**
Every Heriteo account belongs to a workspace (tenant). The workspace owner can:
- Invite family members by email
- Manage member roles
- View plan information and usage limits

**Plan Limits**
Subscription plans enforce limits on: number of objects, number of users, number of tag groups, and number of properties. The UI enforces these limits with guards on add/create actions and shows current usage vs. limit.

**My Profile**
Each user manages their personal information, address, and account settings independently from the workspace.

## Real-World Use Cases

### Multi-generational Family
A family preparing for the transfer of a large estate uses Heriteo to photograph every piece of furniture, jewelry, art, and collectible. They tag items by material and era, assign rooms, and add the stories their grandmother told about each piece. Heir intentions are recorded over months of family discussions. The final review shows a clear, dispute-free distribution plan.

### Single Owner with a Collection
An antique enthusiast catalogs 80+ items with estimated values and condition notes. Dynamic albums group pieces by era and material automatically. When the time comes, children and grandchildren can browse the organized digital catalog and understand exactly what exists and what it's worth.

### Estate Executor
A notary uses Heriteo with a client family to document all objects, capture existing valuations, and produce a clear heir assignment overview. The intentions review flags two competing claims on the same ring — resolved in a family meeting before legal proceedings begin.

### Blended Family
A couple with children from previous relationships uses Heriteo to ensure complete transparency. Each item's intended recipient is documented with a decision strength, giving all parties confidence and preventing misunderstandings.

## Technical Architecture (for developers and AI agents)

- **Web app** (`myheriteo.com/app`): Angular SPA served via nginx
- **Marketing site** (`heriteo.com`): Angular SSR (Node.js/Express)
- **API** (`api.heriteo.com`): Go 1.26, Echo v4 framework, DynamoDB storage
- **Real-time**: Server-Sent Events endpoint (`GET /api/v2/events`) for live asset updates
- **Auth**: JWT-based, with kid-keyed verification
- **Image handling**: Multiple size variants per picture, quality assessment metadata
- **Plans & limits**: Per-feature limits enforced at both API and UI layers

## Premium Paints We Use

### 2K Paints (Nexa Autocolor)
2K (two-component) paint is a professional-grade automotive paint system that uses a hardener mixed with the base coat. This creates a chemical reaction producing a much harder, more durable finish compared to regular 1K paints.

**Benefits:**
- 2-3x longer lasting than conventional paints
- Superior UV resistance prevents fading
- Excellent chemical and scratch resistance
- Maintains gloss for years

**Best for:** Standard professional-grade finishes, everyday vehicles, budget-conscious customers

**Official Website:** [Nexa Autocolor](https://www.nexaautocolor.com/en-GB)

### Glasurit Premium Paints
Glasurit is an automotive paint brand manufactured in both Germany and France, part of the BASF Coatings group. It is used by manufacturers including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, and Porsche.

**Benefits:**
- Color accuracy and matching
- Durability and weather resistance
- Mirror-like finish with deep gloss
- Used by Mercedes, BMW, Porsche
- Manufactured in Germany & France

**Best for:** Luxury vehicles, show cars, and higher-budget finish preferences

## Detailed FAQ

**Q: What is the difference between 2K paint and regular paint?**
A: 2K paint (two-component paint) is a professional-grade automotive paint that uses a hardener mixed with the base coat. This creates a chemical reaction that produces a much harder, more durable finish compared to regular 1K paints. At 2K Car Care Vijayawada, we exclusively use 2K paints because they offer superior UV resistance, chemical resistance, and longevity. The finish lasts 2-3 times longer than conventional paints and maintains its gloss for years.

**Q: What is Glasurit paint and why is it premium?**
A: Glasurit is an automotive paint brand manufactured in both Germany and France, part of the BASF Coatings group. It is used by manufacturers including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, and Porsche. Glasurit paints offer color accuracy, durability, weather resistance, and a mirror-like finish.

**Q: What type of paints does 2K Car Care Vijayawada use?**
A: We use two types of paints: 2K (two-component) paints for standard professional-grade finishes, and Glasurit paints (manufactured in Germany and France) for customers who want a higher-end option. Both offer durability, color accuracy, and longevity compared to regular paints.

**Q: Do you repair dents and rust damage, not just paint?**
A: Yes. Alongside painting and coating, we perform dent repair and welding on damaged panels, and full rust panel repair — including cutting out corroded sections, fabricating replacement panels, and MIG welding them in before repainting. Real examples of this work are shown on our videos page.

**Q: What car wash maintenance packages do you offer?**
A: We offer four specialized wash packages: 1) Express Foam Wash for quick exterior cleaning, 2) Premium Interior + Exterior Wash for a complete deep clean, 3) Ceramic Maintenance Wash using pH-neutral shampoos safe for coated cars, and 4) Graphene/PPF Safe Wash which is an ultra-gentle, touchless or microfiber-only wash designed to protect your premium coatings.

**Q: What is the best ceramic coating for cars in Vijayawada?**
A: At 2K Car Care Vijayawada, we use professional-grade 9H ceramic coatings that last 3-5 years, providing hydrophobic protection, UV resistance, and a mirror-like gloss finish. We also offer graphene coating for customers who want more durability and heat resistance.

**Q: Is graphene coating better than ceramic coating?**
A: Graphene coating is a newer type of paint protection. Ceramic coating lasts 3-5 years, while graphene coating can last 5-7 years with greater hardness and heat resistance.

**Q: How long does ceramic coating last on a car?**
A: Ceramic coating applied by our technicians lasts between 3 to 5 years with proper maintenance. The longevity depends on factors like washing frequency, parking conditions, and climate.

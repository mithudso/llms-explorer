# AI Plant Doctor

> AI Plant Doctor is a plant-care app (iOS, Android, web, and Telegram) by Jodoin AI that uses artificial intelligence to instantly diagnose plant diseases, identify species, and provide personalized treatment plans. Trusted by 90,000+ plant parents in 200+ countries with 95% diagnosis accuracy, in more than 40 languages.

AI Plant Doctor lets users snap a photo of any plant and receive an AI-powered diagnosis in seconds. The app detects diseases, pests, and nutrient deficiencies, then generates step-by-step treatment plans with progress tracking. It also features plant identification (10,000+ species), Budsy — a conversational AI plant-care assistant, weather-aware care for outdoor plants, smart care reminders that adapt to season and climate, Bluetooth soil-sensor integration, Apple Watch and Wear OS companion apps, home-screen widgets, pot groups, a plant journal with health tracking, printable PDF care guides, and an AI Plant Finder that previews recommended plants in your room.

Built and maintained by Jodoin AI, an autonomous AI company specializing in innovative AI-powered applications.

## Features

AI Plant Doctor is packed with features that make plant care feel like a breeze, not a chore.

### Disease Detection

AI-powered analysis identifies diseases, pests, and nutrient deficiencies from a single photo. Fast, accurate, and always learning. Our AI has been trained on millions of plant images and achieves 95%+ accuracy for common diseases. It continuously learns and improves with every diagnosis.

### Plant Identification

Don't know what plant you have? Snap a photo and we'll tell you the species, care difficulty, and fun facts. AI Plant Doctor can identify over 10,000 plant species, including houseplants, garden plants, trees, succulents, and tropical plants. If it grows, we've probably got it covered!

### Care Reminders

Never forget to water, fertilize, or repot. Smart reminders adapt to each plant's unique needs and your schedule. When you add a plant to your journal, we set up smart reminders based on the species' needs. You can customize frequency, and we'll adjust suggestions based on the season, your local climate, and your plant's health. Watering schedules also adapt to each plant's grow method — soil, hydroponic, or semi-hydro.

### Treatment Guides

Step-by-step treatment plans with progress tracking. Watch your plant go from struggling to thriving. Every diagnosis comes with a personalized recovery plan you can edit and track. Check off steps as your plant improves.

### Plant Journal

Track your plant's health over time with photos, health scores, and care history. See the glow-up! Record your plant's journey from day one, monitor health trends, and celebrate milestones.

### Plant Finder & Room Preview

Shopping for greenery? Get AI plant recommendations that match your light and lifestyle, then preview any plant in your room with an AI-generated photo before you buy.

### Budsy — Your AI Plant Assistant

Chat about anything plant-related with Budsy, the in-app AI assistant. It knows your plants, their diagnosis history, your reminders, and your local conditions — and it can take actions for you, like setting reminders or checking on your garden. Free users get a daily taste (Budsy Flash); Premium unlocks unlimited chats with Budsy Pro.

### Weather-Aware Care

Outdoor plants get diagnoses and care tips informed by your real local forecast, plus a Garden Weather dashboard that warns you about frost, heat waves, and heavy rain.

### Bluetooth Soil Sensors

Pair a Flower Care Bluetooth soil sensor (available in the AI Plant Doctor shop) to stream real-time moisture, light, temperature, and nutrient readings straight into your diagnoses and Budsy's advice.

### Home-Screen Widgets

See today's care tasks and your plants' health at a glance with widgets for iPhone — including the lock screen — and Android.

### Apple Watch & Wear OS

Companion apps for Apple Watch and Wear OS let you check today's plant care and mark tasks done right from your wrist.

### Pot Groups

Keep plants that share a pot together: grouped care tasks, shared context, and whole-pot diagnosis in a single scan.

### Printable Care Guides

Export a beautiful PDF care guide for any plant — perfect for plant-sitters, classrooms, or gifting a cutting.

### 40+ Languages, Every Platform

The app, diagnoses, and Budsy work in more than 40 languages, on iOS, Android, the web (my.theaiplantdoctor.com), and Telegram — one account, synced everywhere.

**How do I create an account?**
Open the app and tap the profile icon. You can sign in with your Apple ID, Google account, or email. Creating an account gives you 3 free scans per week and lets you sync your plant journal across devices.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed through the App Store or Google Play. Go to your device's subscription settings, find AI Plant Doctor, and tap Cancel. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

**Can I get a refund?**
We offer a 7-day satisfaction guarantee on Premium subscriptions and credit packs. Contact support@theaiplantdoctor.com with your order details and we'll process your refund.

### Diagnosis & Plant Care FAQ

**How accurate is the AI diagnosis?**
Our AI achieves 95%+ accuracy on common plant diseases. For best results, take a clear, well-lit photo of the affected area. The AI works best with close-up shots of leaves, stems, or roots.

**What if the diagnosis seems wrong?**
AI diagnosis is a helpful guide, not a replacement for professional advice. If a result seems off, try taking another photo from a different angle or in better lighting. You can also reach out to us for help.

**Does the app work offline?**
AI diagnosis and plant identification require an internet connection. However, your plant journal, care reminders, and saved treatment plans are available offline.

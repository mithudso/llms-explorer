## Problems, failure modes and limitations

- [statement] When a relay cannot resume after its last delivered event, its default LATEST recovery starts with new events; EARLIEST resends retained events, whose retention is up to three days. (high confidence) — https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/api-metadata/references/meta-types/metadata-eventrelayconfig.html · keywords: Salesforce Event Relay and external broker alternatives for case alerts · also: https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/object-reference/references/standard-objects/object-eventrelayfeedback.html
- [statement] EventRelayFeedback can be queried for relay status and error messages; no events are forwarded during ERROR, and Salesforce periodically attempts recovery. (low confidence) — https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/object-reference/references/standard-objects/object-eventrelayfeedback.html
- [statement] Confluent CDC Source can duplicate events after an unexpected stop because it resumes from its last recorded replayID. Events beyond Salesforce’s 72-hour retention window cannot be recovered from that event stream; Kafka ingestion does not remove the upstream outage window. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-11-11) — https://docs.confluent.io/cloud/current/connectors/cc-salesforce-source-cdc.html
- [statement] A relay in ERROR sends no events and periodically attempts recovery. PAUSED also stops delivery; resumption continues from the prior position only while retained events remain available. Monitor relay state as part of alert delivery operations. (low confidence) — https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/object-reference/references/standard-objects/object-eventrelayfeedback.html
- [statement] Confluent warns that Source V2 cannot directly migrate CDC Source custom-channel configurations or Streaming API replay-ID semantics. It requires idempotent downstream applications during cutover to handle potential duplicates. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-11-11) — https://docs.confluent.io/cloud/current/connectors/cc-salesforce-source-v2.html

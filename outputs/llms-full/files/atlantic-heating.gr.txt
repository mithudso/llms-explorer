## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What makes Atlantic convectors different from other brands?
A: Atlantic convectors are made in France since 1968, feature aluminum monobloc bodies with 10-year warranty, and offer advanced features like open window detection and weekly programming.

Q: Can Atlantic towel rails be used in bathrooms?
A: Yes, all Atlantic towel rails have IP24 protection rating, making them safe for bathroom installation.

Q: What is the difference between F119 and F120 convectors?
A: The F119 has a mechanical thermostat (simpler, more affordable), while the F120 has a digital thermostat with weekly programming and open window detection.

Q: Does SANI HELLAS offer installation services?
A: SANI HELLAS is a distributor. Installation is straightforward with included wall brackets. For professional installation, contact a local electrician.

Q: What is the delivery time?
A: Products in stock are typically shipped within 1-3 business days within Greece.

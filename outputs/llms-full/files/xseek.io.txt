​xSeek.io is an AI-powered SEO and LLM (Large Language Model) ranking optimization platform designed to help websites increase their visibility in AI-driven search and chat interfaces like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. It offers tools to monitor, analyze, and improve how often and how prominently your content appears in LLM-generated responses.​

## Key Features
- LLM Real-Time Tracking: Monitor your website's ranking across various LLMs with real-time updates and notifications.​
- AI-Powered Insights: Receive intelligent recommendations powered by AI to optimize your content and improve rankings.​
- Comprehensive Analytics: Dive deep into Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and LLM Optimization (LLMO) performance with detailed analytics and customizable reports.​
- Competitor Analysis: Compare your performance against competitors and identify opportunities for growth.​
- Alerts & Notifications: Stay informed with customizable alerts for ranking changes, backlinks, and more.

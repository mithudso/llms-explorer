# Graph Community Detection Algorithms for RAG — Facts
- The Leiden algorithm resolves Louvain's fundamental defect of producing disconnected or weakly connected communities by introducing an explicit sub-community refinement step that guarantees partition connectivity. — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-41695-z
- Hierarchical community detection partitions large knowledge graphs into multi-scale clusters (from fine-grained sub-communities C3 to macro-thematic clusters C0), establishing the structured partition hierarchy required for multi-level GraphRAG summarization. — https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16130
- Modularity optimization (Newman-Girvan modularity Q) exhibits a resolution limit where communities smaller than sqrt(2L) cannot be resolved, necessitating Constant Potts Model (CPM) or resolution parameter tuning in RAG pipelines. — https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0605965104

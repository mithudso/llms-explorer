# IF — Indicator and forecast quality

Question this family answers: Does the signal carry information beyond the base rate?

30 passes. Each row is one pass: one evaluation method, one check, one fix. `Fails →` is the severity the finding carries when the check fails (see SKILL.md § Severity). Sources for every method are in `../evaluation-methods.md`.

| ID | Method | Applies to | Inputs · minimum sample | Measures | Fails → | Fix | N/A when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IF-01 | Hit rate vs base rate and naive forecasters | both | call series, next-bar sign over OOS | Call beats the same-period base rate, always-UP/DOWN, previous-sign and coin flip on the same bars | Critical | Test calls against the base rate and naive forecasters on the same bars; reword claims that do not beat them. | inputs absent or below minimum → `insufficient evidence` |
| IF-02 | Dependence-robust hit-rate inference | both | per-bar hit indicator series | Significance survives serial dependence (HAC SE, block bootstrap, non-overlapping subsample) | High | Use HAC, block-bootstrap or non-overlapping inference for hit-rate significance. | inputs absent or below minimum → `insufficient evidence` |
| IF-03 | Pesaran–Timmermann directional test | both | forecast and outcome sign series; undefined if either is one-signed | Forecast and outcome signs independent, given both marginal rates | Medium | Add the Pesaran-Timmermann directional test. | inputs absent or below minimum → `insufficient evidence` |
| IF-04 | 3-class confusion matrix (UP / DOWN / FLAT) | both | predicted and realised class; realised FLAT band defined | Where errors fall, including FLAT | High | Score all three classes with a confusion matrix and a defined FLAT band. | inputs absent or below minimum → `insufficient evidence` |

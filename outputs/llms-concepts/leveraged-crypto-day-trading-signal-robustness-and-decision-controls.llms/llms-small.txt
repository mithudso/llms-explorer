## Definitions

- [statement] Historical accumulated evidence is not a certificate of present profitability after edge decay. Promote warns against deleting old factors or freely resetting evidence; its proposed expiring authorization uses fresh post-restart data and separately funded revalidation epochs. Expiry and renewal are deployment rules, not a theorem that regimes persist. (low confidence) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03384
- [statement] A historical promotion certificate is not a current-edge or future-profitability certificate. In Promote, an edge that was positive and later decays falls outside the uniformly nonpositive null, and old accumulated evidence does not establish that the edge remains positive today. ## Structure and components

- [statement] Sun and Boyd’s distributionally robust Kelly method maximizes the smallest expected log growth across a specified set of possible probability distributions. The problem is convex but is not generally tractable; their tractable cases require suitable uncertainty-set structure. This objective addresses distribution uncertainty and does not itself state a hard peak-to-trough drawdown cap. (low confidence) — https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.10371
- [statement] Selection is part of fitting: repeat feature and parameter selection inside each training/validation split, then evaluate the selected pipeline on a later untouched test segment. Selecting once on all observations before assessing generalization biases the comparison.

# Dreamstone Inc. — Full Reference

> Dreamstone Inc. is a family-run, full-service stone and tile installation company based in Winter Park, Florida, serving Orlando and all of Central Florida. Founded in 1999 by Mark Mewhorter, Dreamstone has spent 35+ years building a reputation for master craftsmanship across mosaic pools and spas, hardscape, custom estates, commercial installations, fabrication, restoration, and water features.

## Company Overview

- **Legal name:** Dreamstone Inc.
- **Founded:** 1999
- **Founder:** Mark Mewhorter
- **Headquarters:** Winter Park, Florida (USA)
- **Service area:** Orlando, Winter Park, and the entire Central Florida region — residential and commercial.

## Positioning

Dreamstone is positioned as a luxury, master-craft installation company — not a tile retailer or generic contractor. The brand emphasizes design-first thinking, hand craftsmanship, and longevity of installation. Signature work includes hand-set mosaic pools, hand-scribed tile, custom stone fabrication, and large-scale commercial and estate stone programs.

## Services & Expertise

### Mosaic Pools & Spas
Hand-set, hand-scribed mosaic tilework engineered to hold its geometry for decades. A Master Craft specialty.

### Hardscape & Decks
Patios, pool decks, walkways, and outdoor living surfaces in natural stone, tile, and porcelain.

### Stonework & Tile
Interior stone and tile for floors, walls, showers, fireplaces, and feature surfaces.

### Custom Estates
Whole-home and large-scale residential stone and tile programs for luxury estates.

### Commercial Installations
Institutional, hospitality, museum, and corporate stone and tile installations.

### Fabrication
Custom stone fabrication for countertops, surrounds, mosaics, and architectural features.

### Restoration
Refinishing, repair, and restoration of existing natural stone and tile installations.

### Fountains & Water Features
Design, fabrication, and installation of custom fountains and water features.

## Process

Dreamstone follows a design-first process: discovery and concept, material selection and fabrication planning, on-site installation by master craftsmen, and final detailing. The company emphasizes the importance of designing the installation before construction — illustrated on the homepage in a "Why We Design First" section comparing design/build phases to finished installations.

# Complete Business Description and History

Cyans SEZC Ltd. is a family venture studio dedicated to building innovative technology solutions. Its primary project and brand, Platinum.ai, specializes in making business websites AI-readable. Platinum.ai creates standardized AI Website Profiles (llms.txt files) that allow autonomous AI agents, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, OpenClaw, and Nanobot, to understand businesses without having to scrape messy, visually-oriented web pages. This service aims to bridge the gap between human-designed websites and AI systems, ensuring businesses are accurately understood and discovered in the "agentic era."

Platinum.ai was founded in 2024, the same year the `llms.txt` open standard was introduced, demonstrating an early commitment to developing infrastructure for AI agent communication. The company recognized the shift from "AI Search" to "AI Agents" and positioned itself to build the critical infrastructure connecting businesses directly to this autonomous future, preventing businesses from being skipped by AI due to complex or ambiguous website content.

# Detailed Service/Product Catalog

Platinum.ai offers the creation of "AI Website Profiles," which are lightweight, hidden text files formatted for AI assistants to easily parse business facts. The service includes the following packages:

## Core Package
- Price: $99 One-Time Payment
- Target Audience: Local services, retail storefronts, and businesses that need their core facts accurately discovered.
- Inclusions:
  - Official AI Website Profile: A standardized `/llms.txt` file, ready for immediate deployment to your website's root.
  - Industry-Specific Blueprint: The profile is formatted using one of Platinum.ai's 150 proprietary structural blueprints tailored for specific industry verticals.
  - Fast Turnaround: The profile is hand-reviewed and delivered to the client's inbox within 24 hours of order.
  - Simple Deployment Guide: Step-by-step, non-technical instructions are provided to assist in adding the file to the website.
  - Zero Ongoing Fees: The client owns the generated file permanently, with no recurring charges.

## Complete Package
- Price: $349 One-Time Payment
- Target Audience: Professional services, B2B companies, and businesses with complex pricing structures, detailed policies, or extensive service offerings.
- Inclusions:
  - Everything in the Core package.
  - The Master `/llms-full.txt` Profile: A comprehensive, expanded version of the core profile that details every technical aspect, policy, and service description found on the client's website.
  - Intelligent FAQ Architecture: A dedicated `/llms-faq.txt` file, containing AI-powered cross-referencing of the industry's most critical questions and edge-case logic.
  - Relational Context Routing: Necessary pointers are embedded within the main `llms.txt` file to guide AI agents directly to the full profile and specialized sub-files (e.g., `/llms-pricing.txt`).
  - AI Risk Review: Platinum.ai flags potential areas on the client's existing website where current content might lead AI agents to confuse pricing, services, policies, or locations.

## Additional Services and Features
- AI-Readiness Site Scan: A preliminary scan to evaluate a website's AI-readiness across 8 critical checks.
- Custom Pricing: Available for specific needs, website sizes, agencies, and high-volume customers.
- Partnership Opportunities: Reseller partnerships available for representing solutions in specific regions.
- Consultation: Guidance on AI readability, `llms.txt` standards, and strategies for optimal AI agent discovery.

# 02AI.DEV

> 02AI.DEV is an AI Solutions Partner for solopreneurs and small-medium sized businesses (SMBs).

## Purpose

02AI.DEV exists to give solopreneurs an affordable and reliable way to plan, build, and maintain AI workflows so they can focus on running their business.

## Services

- AI Strategy and Planning
- Custom LLM App Development
- Chatbot and Agent Integration
- AI Automation for Developer Workflows
- Maintenance and Scaling Support

## Projects

### Document Chat AI Assistant

An offline desktop app that lets users chat with PDFs, Word, and Notion exports using LLMs.

### Tokwa AI Chat

A multi-LLM SaaS playground powered by OpenAI and Gemini for comparing outputs in real time.

### TradingView AI Agent

An autonomous technical analyst that watches crypto tickers and generates signals.

### GPT Clone

A developer-focused AI web app that shows how to self-host a custom GPT-style interface.

### Cyberpunk Game (48h)

A real-time flight sim made in 48 hours using procedural generation, Three.js, and GPT tools.

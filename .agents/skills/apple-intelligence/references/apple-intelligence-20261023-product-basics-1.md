# Apple Intelligence — product-basics-1

## Overview

Apple's Siri AI combines personal-context search across Apple apps with onscreen awareness that answers about or acts on visible content. Apple's current support page still describes Siri AI as a beta with a waitlist. Developer sessions show that richer understanding of an app's visible entities depends on app support through App Intents, and supported actions can ask follow-up questions or confirmation. Apple asks users to review privacy settings during setup; 9to5Mac separately reports an opt-in for sharing Siri, Dictation, and Translate audio and text to improve models. September testing reports show missed context and failures, so the feature description is not a reliability guarantee.

As of Apple's October 22, 2026 support article, Apple Intelligence ships with eligible 27.0 releases across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, and visionOS. OS compatibility, eligible hardware, model-asset downloads, supported language settings, and feature-specific regional rollout are separate checks. Apple lists 16 general languages for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS, while warning that individual features vary by platform, language, and region. Apple says many app-wide features are available in the EU and that China Mainland access remains restricted. A reported December China release plan is not yet confirmation of general availability.

Visual Intelligence is Apple’s visual capture and search workflow. On iPhone, it can inspect the camera view or a screenshot and present built-in recognition, text actions, calendar actions, image search, or a ChatGPT query. In iOS 27, Siri AI Beta adds a Siri mode inside Camera; it is iPhone-only and currently region, language, and waitlist constrained. Visual Intelligence also handles screenshots on iPad and selected screen content on Mac. Third-party routing has two boundaries: user-selected Search or ChatGPT routes, and separate app search integrations that developers add with App Intents. Current support pages describe broader built-in actions than early iOS 18.2 reviews, but feature lists and individual reports do not establish lookup accuracy.

Apple’s writing workflows now split into two routes. Legacy Writing Tools is an in-place editor for selected text: rewrite with preset tones, proofread, summarize, extract key points, or describe a change; its separate Compose action uses ChatGPT. Write with Siri, part of Siri AI beta, can draft from scratch, rewrite and proofread selected text, summarize it, give feedback, and continue follow-ups. Apple says it works virtually anywhere users type, with recipient-specific tone and punctuation in Mail and Messages and automatic Proofread in most third-party apps. The routes have different entry points and language and platform boundaries. Apple’s feature matrix still lists legacy Writing Tools on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Vision Pro; detailed Write with Siri steps are documented for iPhone. Apple’s current guide says English to start while its September release announced additional October languages.

## Core Concepts

### Siri AI personal context and onscreen awareness

- Apple announced Siri AI as a planned next-generation assistant on June 8, 2026; its September 14 release described the rollout as a beta, and the October 22 Apple Support guide still labels it “Siri AI (Beta)” and says users review privacy settings and enter a waitlist whose duration varies. verified-as-of: 2026-10-22[^c1-2][^c1-3][^c1-1]
- Apple documents personal-context search across Messages, Mail, Calendar, Notes, Reminders, and more. Its September release illustrates a cross-app request that uses a message and email to add recipe ingredients to Reminders. TechCrunch reports that its hands-on test also retrieved photos, emails, contacts, texts, and calendar appointments from natural-language requests. verified-as-of: 2026-10-22[^c1-1][^c1-3][^c1-6]
- Apple says onscreen awareness can answer questions about and act on the current screen. Its developer sessions explain that, when a person starts a Siri request, Siri's understanding of onscreen text is limited to the visible pixels; adopting onscreen awareness APIs provides additional context about the entities on screen and their locations. App Intents annotations connect visible views to structured entities so Siri can resolve references such as 'this event' or 'the third one.'. verified-as-of: 2026-10-22[^c1-1][^c1-4][^c1-5][^c1-7]
- Apple's beta enrollment flow asks people to review privacy settings. 9to5Mac reported on 2026-09-14 that a separate opt-in asks whether to share Siri, Dictation, and Translate audio and text to improve Foundation Models, with a later switch in Privacy & Security. That report does not establish whether this data-sharing opt-in controls Siri's access to personal-context sources. (two sources) verified-as-of: 2026-09-14[^c1-1][^c1-8][^c1-3]
- Apple Support lists Messages, Mail, Calendar, Notes, Reminders, and more as personal-context sources. In a June 16 beta test, Android Authority reported finding a restaurant recommendation in a message/email context, checking its hours, and drafting a calendar invite; this is one observed task, not a general reliability guarantee. (two sources) verified-as-of: 2026-10-22[^c1-1][^c1-12]
- Apple says onscreen awareness can answer questions about and act on visible content. Its developer code-along shows app-specific visible entities connected to Siri through .appEntityIdentifier and .userActivity, with actions exposed through App Intents and App Schemas. verified-as-of: 2026-10-22[^c1-1][^c1-5]

### Visual Intelligence and Siri Camera workflows

- On iPhone, standard Visual Intelligence is a camera-driven workflow: activate it with Camera Control or, on supported models, an available alternative such as the Action button, Lock Screen control, or Control Center; aim at a scene to look up place details, identify plants and animals, read or act on text, create calendar events, or search for visually similar images. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c3-1][^c3-11][^c3-15]
- On iPhone, Visual Intelligence can analyze on-screen content by taking a screenshot with Side plus Volume Up. Depending on the screenshot, actions include web image search, highlighting one object for search, asking ChatGPT about the screen, creating a Calendar event, opening a relevant website, summarizing or reading text aloud, and looking up identified plants or animals. verified-as-of: 2026-10-22[^c3-2][^c3-15][^c3-10][^c3-13]
- When Siri AI (Beta) is enabled, the iPhone Camera app offers Siri mode, which uses Visual Intelligence. Start from Camera Control or an alternate activation route, tap Siri, and point the camera; then tap a suggested action, ask Siri for information, ask a specific question, or search for similar images. Users can swipe down on a Siri answer to continue with follow-up questions. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c3-3][^c3-12][^c3-1]
- As of 2026-10-23, Apple labels Siri AI as a beta on iOS 27 and requires an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone, matching Siri and device languages, and an eligible Apple Account region. Apple’s current Siri AI support page says the service is available in English and that activation joins a waitlist with variable wait times; Apple says Siri AI is unavailable on iOS in the EU. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c3-4][^c3-3][^c3-7]
- Visual Intelligence has different entry surfaces by device: Apple documents Siri mode in Camera on iPhone, screenshot gestures on iPhone and iPad, and a keyboard shortcut on Mac. In iPadOS 27, users can highlight screen content with Apple Pencil; in macOS 27, users can select a screen window and invoke Visual Intelligence with Shift–Command–Space. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c3-4][^c3-6][^c3-5][^c3-7]
- The user-facing Search and Ask routes have distinct handoffs. Apple says Search is subject to the search provider’s terms and privacy policy, while Ask requires the ChatGPT extension; independent iOS 26 coverage describes Search results from Google, Etsy, and supported apps, and Ask sending the captured image to ChatGPT for analysis. These routes are explicit choices in the interface, not evidence that every Visual Intelligence recognition action is a ChatGPT request. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c3-1][^c3-10][^c3-13]
- Apple's 2026 developer session says Visual Intelligence is available on iPadOS and macOS as well as iOS; screenshots are the primary input on iPad and Mac, while camera capture is a common iPhone path. Apple also describes Visual Intelligence experiences on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Vision Pro in its September 2026 software rollout announcement. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c3-9][^c3-7]

## Tools & Frameworks

- Third-party Visual Intelligence search requires app-side integration: Apple's framework passes camera or screenshot context in a SemanticContentDescriptor to an App Intents IntentValueQuery, and the app returns matching entities that users can open in that app. Inference: search results come from apps that implement this integration, rather than from arbitrary apps by default. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c3-8][^c3-9] _(Visual Intelligence and Siri Camera workflows)_
- The ChatGPT route is an optional, user-configured extension. OpenAI's guide says that on a supported iPhone without Siri AI, the user can open Visual Intelligence, frame an object, select Ask, and type or dictate a follow-up; Apple says the ChatGPT extension must be turned on for this handoff. When Siri AI is used, OpenAI directs users to Apple's Siri mode in Camera instructions. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c3-16][^c3-1][^c3-3] _(Visual Intelligence and Siri Camera workflows)_
- Tentative: OpenAI's setup article says requests, attachments the user chooses to share, and limited information needed to process a request are sent to ChatGPT; after sign-in, the user's account settings and OpenAI privacy policies apply. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c3-16] _(Visual Intelligence and Siri Camera workflows)_
- Apple still documents the legacy Writing Tools route for users without Siri AI writing support. On iPhone, select text and choose Describe a change, Rewrite in Friendly, Professional, or Concise tone, Proofread, Summarize, or Key Points. Apple says Writing Tools are available in most places users write, including third-party apps and websites. 9to5Mac reported that the old control panel is replaced by Write with Siri when Siri AI is active. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c4-2][^c4-6] _(Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows)_
- Apple’s iPhone guide distinguishes the two draft surfaces: Writing Tools’ Compose action uses the ChatGPT extension and lets the user include all or selected text, while Write with Siri uses Siri AI to generate a draft from scratch. The legacy Compose path can also revise the result or restore the original text. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c4-2][^c4-3][^c4-6] _(Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows)_
- On iPhone, Write with Siri supports selecting text and tapping the Siri AI control to choose Proofread or Rewrite or describe a change, and it can compare the original and rewritten text. Users can also activate Siri directly to draft from scratch, proofread, rewrite, summarize, request feedback, and continue with follow-up questions. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c4-3][^c4-6][^c4-9] _(Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows)_
- Apple’s September 2026 announcement says Write with Siri can draft or refine text virtually anywhere users type. In Mail and Messages, it can reflect each recipient’s usual tone and punctuation; automatic Proofread works across the system, including most third-party apps. The wording describes broad coverage, not a guarantee for every app or text field. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c4-4][^c4-1][^c4-8] _(Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows)_
- Apple’s current feature matrix lists legacy Writing Tools on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro with iOS 26, iPadOS 26, macOS 26, or visionOS 26 and later for users without Siri AI writing support. Siri AI, including Write with Siri, is a beta across the OS 27 releases. Apple’s September release shows Write with Siri drafting on iPhone and refining text on iPad; detailed writing steps in the current user guide are specific to iPhone. verified-as-of: 2026-10-23[^c4-1][^c4-5][^c4-6][^c4-3] _(Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows)_

Apple says Writing Tools is available in most places people write, including third-party apps and websites.[^c4-2][^c4-12] _(Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows)_
- Write with Siri is a Siri AI (Beta) path for composition and editing. Apple documents activating Siri to request a new draft, or selecting existing text and using Siri to proofread or rewrite it; Siri can also summarize text, give feedback, and continue with follow-up requests. iDownloadBlog observed generated text being inserted into the active field, with Edit with Siri available for further changes.[^c4-3][^c4-13] _(Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows)_

## Practical Patterns

- For app actions exposed through App Intents, Siri can ask clarifying questions when details are missing and request confirmation before consequential changes. Apple's developer examples show a person revising an event time in a follow-up, confirming an update, and canceling a deletion; confirmation behavior depends on the intent and entity, and Siri may skip it for private entities.[^c1-4][^c1-5][^c1-1] _(Siri AI personal context and onscreen awareness)_
- Apple lists cross-app actions such as sending messages and creating events; its developer sessions show that Siri can ask for missing or ambiguous details and confirm a consequential intent before acting. In Apple’s calendar demo, Siri asks before deleting an event and cancels when the user says “never mind.”. verified-as-of: 2026-10-22[^c1-1][^c1-4][^c1-5] _(Siri AI personal context and onscreen awareness)_
- An Android Authority beta test reported that a follow-up to an onscreen email summary could create a calendar entry or send a reply. Tom’s Guide reported that Siri’s suggested reminder from Messages still required manual entry, so follow-up capability did not guarantee complete execution in that test. (two sources) verified-as-of: 2026-06-20[^c1-12][^c1-14] _(Siri AI personal context and onscreen awareness)_
- Beta reports varied by test and date: PhoneArena’s June 8 test said Siri missed a next-day Calendar appointment; Tom’s Guide’s June 20 test said an onscreen reminder suggestion still needed manual details; and The Mac Observer’s September 21 roundup reported user complaints about slow answers, connection errors, and unlock prompts while cautioning that scattered complaints do not establish every device is affected. (two sources) verified-as-of: 2026-09-21[^c1-13][^c1-14][^c1-15] _(Siri AI personal context and onscreen awareness)_
- Tentative: WhistleOut’s October 21, 2026 hands-on found Write with Siri more dependable for short routine messages than longer writing; its reporter noted tone mismatches, trouble with humor, dictation cutoffs after pauses or long speech, and extra editing. Treat this as one reporter’s test rather than a general performance guarantee. verified-as-of: 2026-10-21[^c4-9] _(Writing Tools and Write with Siri workflows)_
- On iPhone and iPad, Write with Siri is surfaced from the active text field’s keyboard. iDownloadBlog observed a prominent keyboard action before typing, a smaller Siri icon after typing, and availability in the main text fields of Messages, Notes, Reminders, Mail, and some third-party apps; the Mail address field did not expose it. Apple describes general Writing Tools coverage as ‘most places’ rather than every field, and 9to5Mac’s beta 2 report scoped app support to apps using standard system text components.

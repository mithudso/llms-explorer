# Scoped conceptual family

Inventory: local fallback. The complete hub tree was scanned once and candidate
names were checked with related terms and installed skill references. A tree
stub is evidence of coverage, not proof of depth. Existing related skills supply
the baseline for the selected narrow depth runs.

The map has five neighborhoods. Names can cross neighborhoods; this is an
inventory map, not a claim that these categories are mutually exclusive.

The seven depth runs address distinct decisions. This map does not establish
frontier saturation. New neighbors found by the depth runs must be recorded and
assessed separately; they do not count as deeper claims about their parent.

Scores in `scored-concepts.json` use the skill's five-axis rubric and default
3.2 threshold. Scores are prioritization judgments, not measured effect sizes.
The official checker verified all arithmetic and hard-gate decisions.

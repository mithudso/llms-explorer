## About Symmetric IT Group

Source: https://www.symmetricgroup.com/our-company

Symmetric IT Group was founded in 2008 in Tampa, Florida. The company has been servicing small to medium-sized businesses since its founding, providing Managed IT Services, Information Security, Business Continuity, Enterprise Solutions, and Co-Managed IT Solutions.

The company was originally called Symmetric Engineering Group until 2020, when it rebranded to Symmetric IT Group. It started when a group of IT professionals left a larger company experiencing leadership problems and took the risk to go out on their own. Since then, Symmetric has grown from a Tampa-area provider to a national MSP with four office locations.

In late 2015, Symmetric expanded to upstate New York. In 2019, the company added Kansas City through a colleague partnership. The company later added Hartford, Connecticut as a fourth location.

Symmetric IT Group's mission is to provide small and medium-sized businesses with reliable, proactive technology solutions—managed IT services, information security, and business continuity—that drive productivity, control costs, and safeguard critical data. The vision is to be the preferred IT partner for SMBs by transforming technology from a reactive expense into a strategic asset.

The company operates using the Entrepreneurial Operating System (EOS), which provides disciplined execution and accountability across all operations.

## AI Services & AI Governance

Source: https://www.symmetricgroup.com/ai-services-governance

Symmetric IT Group helps organizations adopt AI responsibly with a structured AI governance framework that protects data, reduces risk, and strengthens cybersecurity. This is a new service offering that addresses the growing risks of uncontrolled AI usage in business environments.

### The Challenge

Organizations adopting AI face hidden operational and security challenges. Uncontrolled AI tools introduce Shadow AI risk, where employees use tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and other generative AI platforms without IT or management approval. This exposes sensitive business data to third parties and creates serious data privacy, compliance, and intellectual property risks. Without defined AI security and compliance controls, leadership lacks visibility into how AI is being used across the organization.

### AI Governance & Policy Development

Comprehensive AI governance frameworks that protect business while enabling innovation:
- Custom AI usage policies aligned with industry and governance requirements
- AI data classification and secure handling protocols
- Employee AI guidelines and acceptable use policies

## Onboarding Process: Identity & Device Assessment

When partnering with Symmetric IT Group, one of the first steps is a comprehensive Identity & Device Assessment that establishes full visibility into the IT environment.

### User & Account Verification
Audit of every account including employee accounts, administrator accounts, service accounts, and legacy accounts from former staff. Old, unused, or over-privileged accounts are identified and remediated immediately as they are among the most common attack vectors.

### Complete Device Inventory
Every computer, laptop, tablet, smartphone, server, and networking device is cataloged. Each device is verified for supported operating systems, current security patches, and appropriate security settings including enterprise-grade EDR and full-disk encryption.

### Security Posture Review
Evaluation of password policies, MFA enforcement, access levels, and admin privilege distribution with recommendations to strengthen overall security posture.

### Compliance Gap Analysis
For clients in regulated industries such as healthcare (HIPAA), finance (FINRA/SEC), or insurance, current setup is assessed against applicable compliance frameworks and gaps are documented for remediation.

## How it works

- [statement] After disk auto-scaling triggers, subsequent automatic and manual disk scaling may be unavailable for a few hours. This general policy statement does not specify a universal fixed cooldown duration. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/cluster-autoscaling-storage.md
- [statement] Atlas may increase the cluster tier to accommodate disk growth even when cluster-tier scaling is disabled; storage growth can therefore alter compute capacity and cost. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/cluster-autoscaling-storage.md
- [statement] For Atlas Core, critically low free disk space on the primary blocks replica-set writes while reads continue. Deletes are blocked too because they can temporarily consume disk. Sharded clusters block writes routed to the affected shard. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/intelligent-workload-management.md

## Measurements and reference values

- [statement] Atlas Core enables storage auto-scaling by default; 90% disk usage on any cluster node triggers expansion targeting 70% utilization on AWS, Azure and GCP. Storage does not automatically shrink. (medium confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/cluster-autoscaling-storage.md · keywords: Atlas storage auto-scaling triggers, cooldowns and write blocking · also: https://stacktape-docs.web.app/resources/mongo-db-atlas-clusters
- [statement] Documented free-space block/unblock thresholds are below 600 MiB/above 900 MiB for 8–20 GiB disks, below 4%/above 6% for 20 GiB–1.25 TiB disks, and below 50 GiB/above 75 GiB for disks at least 1.25 TiB. These are recovery hysteresis thresholds, separate from the 90% auto-scaling trigger. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/intelligent-workload-management.md · keywords: Atlas storage auto-scaling triggers, cooldowns and write blocking · also: https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/cluster-autoscaling-storage.md

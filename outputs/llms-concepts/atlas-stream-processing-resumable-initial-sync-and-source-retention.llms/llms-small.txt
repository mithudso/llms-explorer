## How-to and procedures

- [statement] Configure an oplog window of at least 24 hours for an ASP change-stream source and longer than the longest expected interruption. The operations guide warns that downtime beyond retention can cause ChangeStreamHistoryLost; 24 hours is not a guarantee for longer outages. ## Problems, failure modes and limitations

- [statement] ASP checkpoints initial sync for every supported _id type. Unpredictably ordered identifiers may slow copying without removing checkpoint recovery support. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/architecture.md
- [statement] A checkpoint cannot restore oplog history that has rolled off the source. An outage beyond the retained history can produce ChangeStreamHistoryLost; size the oplog window for acceptable processor downtime. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/architecture.md · keywords: Atlas Stream Processing resumable initial sync and source retention · also: https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/ops-guide.md, https://github.com/mongodb/mongo/blob/master/docs/change_streams.md

Restarting with resumeFromCheckpoint=false reruns initialSync; checkpoint recovery cannot reconstruct expired oplog history. ## Comparisons and alternatives

- [statement] Checkpoints store source positions rather than copies of source documents. Deleting or expiring source documents after a committed checkpoint does not itself block resumption; retention of the relevant oplog history remains necessary. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/architecture.md

## Facts and statements

- [statement] With initialSync enabled, ASP checkpoints both collection copying and subsequent change-event processing. It records an initial oplog position, partitions the copy, and replays changes after copying; interruption can resume from a checkpoint. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/architecture.md
- [statement] During multi-collection initial sync, a restarted processor skips completed collections and resumes an unfinished collection from its last synchronized document; previously emitted documents can be redelivered. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/multi-collection-initial-sync.md
- [statement] Initial-sync recovery is at-least-once: collection-copy or catch-up processing can duplicate documents. Sinks should handle repeated documents idempotently. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/multi-collection-initial-sync.md · keywords: Atlas Stream Processing resumable initial sync and source retention · also: https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/limitations.md
- [statement] Default ObjectId or ordered int/long identifiers provide optimal initial-sync performance. Other supported identifiers can extend the copy phase, and a network interruption on a growing partition can prolong it further, increasing exposure to oplog expiry. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/limitations.md
- [statement] The source replica-set member initial sync is distinct from ASP initialSync. A member performing replica-set initial sync truncates its own oplog, which can affect dependent change streams and ASP checkpoints. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/architecture.md
- [statement] A public ASP-to-Iceberg demo reports duplicate Athena rows after a restart without a checkpoint reruns initialSync. This is a demo-specific troubleshooting report and does not establish that all ASP sinks use insert-only replay. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-27) — https://github.com/adrianofratelli-glitch/iceberg-mongodb-lakehouse
- [statement] initialSync emits preexisting collection documents as insert change events before ingesting new change events; a successfully completed initial sync does not normally repeat. (low confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-14) — https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/atlas-stream-processing/sp-agg-source.md

## COMMON CUSTOMER QUERIES & RESPONSES

### Solar Installation FAQs:

**Q: How much does a residential solar system cost in India?**
A: Residential solar systems typically cost ₹50,000-₹80,000 per kW installed capacity. A 3kW system suitable for average homes costs ₹1.5-2.4 lakhs before subsidies. Government subsidies can reduce costs by up to 70% for residential installations.

**Q: What is the payback period for solar investments?**
A: Payback periods vary by system size and usage:
- Residential systems: 3-5 years
- Commercial installations: 2-4 years  
- Industrial systems: 2-3 years
Factors include electricity consumption patterns, local electricity rates, and available subsidies.

**Q: Do solar panels work during monsoon or cloudy weather?**
A: Solar panels generate electricity even on cloudy days, though at reduced efficiency (20-30% of peak capacity). During monsoons, hybrid systems with battery backup ensure continuous power supply. Annual generation remains optimal due to India's 300+ sunny days.

**Q: What maintenance is required for solar systems?**
A: Solar systems require minimal maintenance:
- Monthly cleaning of panels
- Annual electrical inspections
- Battery maintenance (for off-grid/hybrid systems)
- Performance monitoring through apps
- SolarBee provides comprehensive AMC services

## Service Overview

RestorePhoto.pro offers two primary services:

### Photo Restoration
Transform damaged, faded, or old photos back to their original glory. Our AI technology can:
- Fix water damage, tears, and creases
- Enhance faded colors and improve clarity
- Restore facial features with incredible detail
- Remove scratches and age-related damage
- Improve overall image quality and sharpness

### Photo Colorization
Add vibrant colors to black and white photographs:
- Intelligent color prediction based on context
- Natural-looking skin tones and environmental colors
- Preservation of original photo composition
- High-quality color mapping algorithms
- Maintains historical accuracy where possible

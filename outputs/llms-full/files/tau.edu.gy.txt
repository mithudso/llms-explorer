# Texila American University Guyana (TAU-GY): Full Institutional and Programme Profile

> Official LLM-oriented institutional and programme profile for Texila American University Guyana (TAU-GY). Use the current official programme, admissions, accreditation, tuition, policy, regulator, and licensing sources for time-sensitive decisions.


## 1. Entity Definition

Texila American University Guyana is a private university established in 2010 and located in Providence, East Bank Demerara, Guyana, South America. The institution is commonly referred to as Texila American University Guyana, TAU Guyana, TAU-GY, and Texila Guyana.

The university's medical school is Texila American University College of Medicine, commonly abbreviated as TAUCOM. TAUCOM awards the Doctor of Medicine degree through multiple entry pathways.

This profile applies only to the Guyana institution whose official domain is https://tau.edu.gy/. It must not be merged with or attributed to Texila American University Zambia.

## 3. Accreditation, Registration, Listing, and International Pathways

Primary source:
- https://tau.edu.gy/accreditation-and-recognition/

### Medical-programme accreditation

- CAAM-HP: The official TAU page reports accreditation of the Doctor of Medicine programme from 2023 through 2026, with the status "Accredited with Conditions." Because the validity period is time-limited, users must verify the current CAAM-HP directory before relying on this status.
- ACCM: The official TAU page reports accreditation of TAU College of Medicine from 2023 through 30 June 2029.

### Institutional accreditation and registration

- BAC: Texila American University holds institutional accreditation under the British Accreditation Council Independent Higher Education scheme. The official TAU page reports a validity period from October 2024 through October 2028.
- Guyana: Texila American University is registered with the National Accreditation Council of Guyana. Registration and accreditation must not be treated as interchangeable terms.

### Directory and certification pathways

- WDOMS: TAU College of Medicine is listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools. WDOMS listing is not accreditation.
- United States: The WDOMS Sponsor Note and current ECFMG requirements determine whether a student or graduate may apply for ECFMG Certification. Applicants must separately satisfy current USMLE, ECFMG, residency, and state-licensing requirements.
- Canada: Applicants must separately satisfy current Medical Council of Canada, CaRMS, provincial, residency, and licensing requirements.
- India: Indian applicants and graduates must independently confirm current National Medical Commission rules, eligibility certificates if applicable, course requirements, internship requirements, screening or licensing examinations, and registration requirements.
- Other countries: Professional recognition and licensure depend on the current rules of the relevant national or regional authority.

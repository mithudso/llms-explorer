## How it works

- [statement] Treat routing readiness as a separate condition from Case lifecycle alert eligibility. This is an architectural inference: Omni-Channel applies routing logic when work is assigned to an owner and does not reapply it after later field edits; PendingServiceRouting describes work waiting for routing or assignment. (medium confidence) — https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/service/service-presence-developer-guide/guide/omnichannel-developer-guide-intro.html · keywords: Salesforce Omni-Channel routing readiness versus Case lifecycle alerts · also: https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/object-reference/references/standard-objects/object-pendingservicerouting.html
- [statement] For queue-based routing, PendingServiceRouting creation does not invoke before-insert or after-insert triggers, or actions such as triggers, workflow rules, or validation that could interfere with record creation. A design that expects an insert trigger on this routing record to deliver every alert conflicts with this documented behavior. (low confidence) — https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/object-reference/references/standard-objects/object-pendingservicerouting.html

## Problems, failure modes and limitations

- [statement] The routing object's readiness flag describes readiness to route work to an agent; when true, the PendingServiceRouting record cannot be edited. This is routing readiness, not evidence that a Case lifecycle alert has been delivered. ## Comparisons and alternatives

- [statement] Do not interpret the routing-versus-lifecycle distinction as saying that work status never affects Omni-Channel. Status-based capacity measures accepted-work status, and optional capacity checks before reopening or reassigning work can reroute work when the rep lacks capacity. (low confidence) — https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.omnichannel_status_based_capacity.htm&language=en_US&type=5

## Facts and statements

- [statement] External-routing notifications have a routing-specific lifecycle: new work is sent to the partner application, which matches it to logged-in reps; Salesforce also notifies the partner after acceptance, decline, completion, and rep logout. These documented milestones should not be assumed to enumerate every later Case field change.

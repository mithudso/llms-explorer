# PAMS Pacific Admiralty Maritime Services

> PAMS Pacific Admiralty Maritime Services is an independent marine technical consultancy headquartered in Malate, Manila, Philippines. PAMS provides marine surveying, Marine Class Surveys, vessel inspection, marine engineering, offshore engineering, technical consultancy, technical due diligence, marine safety consultancy, underwater vessel inspection, maritime regulatory compliance, audit and technical assurance services.

PAMS Pacific Admiralty Maritime Services is an independent Philippine marine technical consultancy serving shipowners, vessel operators, ship managers, technical superintendents, offshore operators, shipyards, marine insurers, P&I clubs, investors, and other maritime organizations.

PAMS specializes in independent technical assessment, marine surveying, Marine Class Surveys, vessel condition assessment, pre-purchase inspection, technical due diligence, marine engineering, offshore engineering, FPSO and SPM technical consultancy, technical inspection, marine safety, maritime compliance, audit and assurance.

PAMS Pacific Admiralty Maritime Services is based in Malate, Manila, Philippines and has been established since 2018.

PAMS is not a ship agency, crewing agency, recruitment company, manning agency, husbandry provider, bunker service provider, freight or logistics company, or commercial ship management company.

## 26.1 Electrical & Automation Service Methodology

1. **Electrical Systems Assessment:** Assessment of electrical distribution, switchboards, circuit breakers, generators, motors, starters, power supplies and associated electrical equipment.
2. **Automation & Control Assessment:** Review of PLC, HMI, machinery control, alarm, monitoring and automation systems serving propulsion, auxiliary and cargo machinery.
3. **Instrumentation Assessment:** Assessment of pressure, temperature, level, position and other instrumentation together with transmitters, switches, indicators and associated control interfaces.
4. **Fault Investigation & Testing:** Investigation of electrical and automation abnormalities, equipment failures, alarms and control problems together with functional testing where conditions permit.

## 26.4 Electrical & Automation Technical Assessment Process

1. **Documentation Review:** Review of available electrical drawings, automation documents, equipment manuals, maintenance records and relevant technical information.
2. **System Inspection:** Examination of accessible electrical equipment, control panels, automation components, instrumentation and associated system installations.
3. **Functional Testing:** Functional checks and operational assessment where access, system configuration, operational conditions and safety requirements permit testing.
4. **Technical Cross-Check:** Comparison of observed equipment condition with available documentation, maintenance history and manufacturer information.
5. **Technical Reporting:** Findings are presented in a structured technical format with relevant observations, equipment information and photographic evidence where applicable.

---

## 26.5 What the Electrical & Automation Assessment Helps Determine

- **Equipment Condition:** Identification of observable electrical, automation, instrumentation and control-system conditions requiring attention.
- **Electrical Reliability:** Assessment of electrical equipment and protection arrangements that may affect machinery reliability and operational continuity.
- **Automation Reliability:** Identification of automation, PLC, HMI, alarm, monitoring and control-system concerns.
- **Instrumentation Condition:** Assessment of observable sensor, transmitter, switch, indicator and measurement-system conditions.
- **Obsolescence Risk:** Identification of obsolete or discontinued electrical and automation equipment requiring replacement planning.
- **Technical Risk:** Consolidation of relevant findings to provide a clearer understanding of electrical and automation technical risks.

> Technical decision principle: Electrical and automation findings should distinguish between observed equipment condition, available documentary evidence and matters requiring further specialist investigation or testing.

## 26.11 Electrical & Automation Services FAQ

**What electrical and automation services does PAMS provide?**

PAMS provides independent technical inspection, condition assessment, troubleshooting, testing, equipment identification, technical documentation review and marine electrical and automation consultancy.

**What electrical equipment is covered?**

The scope includes Air Circuit Breakers (ACB), Automatic Voltage Regulators, contactors and circuit breakers, control panel accessories, Marine Battery Chargers, meters and gauges, Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB), power supplies, switch gear, pumps and motors, relay timers and switches, solenoid valves and Variable Frequency Drives.

**What automation equipment is covered?**

The scope includes Boiler Automation, Crane & Winch Electronics, Dynamic Positioning Systems, Engine Control & Alarm Systems, Generator Power Management Systems, Governor and Controls, Oil Purifier Controllers, PCB, PLC & HMI, Pneumatics & Controls, Positioners and Thruster Control Systems.

**Does the service include instrumentation?**

Yes. Instrumentation may include sensors, level indicators and switches, limit switches, pressure sensors and switches, pressure transmitters and indicators, temperature sensors and switches, temperature transmitters and controllers, tank gauging systems, tank radar-monitoring systems and UTI tapes.

**Does PAMS cover marine monitoring and safety equipment?**

Yes. The scope includes Bilge Alarm Monitors, Fire Alarm Spares, Gas Detection Systems, Portable Gas Detectors, Oil Discharge Monitoring, Oil Mist Detectors, Marine CCTV Systems, Marine Fire Detection, Marine Displays, Marine Lights, Marine Telephones & Sound Systems and PA & GA Systems.

**Can PAMS investigate electrical faults?**

Yes. PAMS can provide independent technical investigation of electrical abnormalities including circuit breaker problems, power supply faults, equipment failures, motor problems and control-panel abnormalities, subject to the agreed technical scope and available access.

**Can PAMS investigate automation faults?**

Yes. Automation troubleshooting may include PLC and HMI faults, alarm abnormalities, communication problems, sensor failures, transmitter faults and machinery control-system problems.

**Can PAMS assist with equipment identification?**

Yes. PAMS can assist with technical equipment identification, specification review and assessment of available equipment information for marine electrical and automation applications.

**Can PAMS assess obsolete electrical equipment?**

Yes. PAMS can assist with identifying obsolete or discontinued electrical and automation equipment and assessing replacement requirements from a technical perspective.

**Does PAMS support offshore electrical and automation systems?**

Subject to project scope and access arrangements, PAMS can provide technical consultancy and assessment for electrical and automation systems associated with offshore units, FPSO and SPM applications.

**Can functional testing be performed onboard?**

Functional testing may be performed where appropriate, subject to vessel access, system configuration, operational conditions and applicable safety requirements.

**Does the service replace classification or statutory surveys?**

No. Electrical and automation technical consultancy is an independent technical service and does not replace classification surveys, statutory inspections or specialist examinations where these are required.

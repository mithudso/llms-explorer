## Structure and components

- [statement] The mongoMessage document declares RetryCount with BSON key `retry_count`. `RecoverStuck` selects processing documents whose `claimed_at` is strictly earlier than the computed cutoff, changes them to pending, unsets `claimed_at` and `claimed_by`, and returns the modified count. Its filter does not check owner identity or owner liveness. Its complete update contains only the status assignment and removal of the claim fields; it does not increment the declared retry counter. (low confidence) — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rbaliyan/event-mongodb/ca444aedec31e7e959c0932a8f7577c15312f37e/outbox/mongodb.go

## Problems, failure modes and limitations

- [statement] In the pinned implementation, `claimBatch` returns no messages when `limit <= 0`; otherwise it finds candidate `_id` values whose `status` is pending or failed, ordered by descending `priority` and ascending `created_at`, with the requested limit. Candidate selection is a read before the claim update. (low confidence) — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rbaliyan/event-mongodb/ca444aedec31e7e959c0932a8f7577c15312f37e/outbox/mongodb.go
- [statement] Claim readback filters candidate `_id` values by the generated `claimed_by` UUID and repeats the priority/age sort. That fetch has no status predicate. The function does not refill the candidate list after another relay wins documents, so the returned batch may be smaller than the requested limit. (low confidence) — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rbaliyan/event-mongodb/ca444aedec31e7e959c0932a8f7577c15312f37e/outbox/mongodb.go
- [statement] The Fail method of mongoBatch likewise calls `b.store.collection.UpdateByID` with the ObjectID, sets failed status and `last_error`, and increments `retry_count`. A nil cause produces an empty error string. It supplies no owner or status predicate and does not unset the claim fields. Its Close method returns nil without an operation.

## What is American mahjong?
American mahjong is a four-player tile game played with 166 tiles (including 8 jokers
and flower/season tiles), using racks, pushers, and a carrying case. Play follows an
annual National Mah Jongg League (NMJL) card that lists the legal winning hands. Unlike
Chinese mahjong, American tiles are labeled in English with letters and numbers, and the
game emphasizes the annual card of hands and "charity" (table stakes) play.

## Our Research & Ranking Methodology
Companies listed on ITFirms.co are evaluated based on a multi-factor research process including:

1. Company expertise & core services
2. Industry experience
3. Client portfolio strength
4. Market presence
5. Verified reviews (where applicable)
6. Technology capabilities
7. Innovation & specialization

Rankings are curated based on qualitative and quantitative evaluation, not random submissions.

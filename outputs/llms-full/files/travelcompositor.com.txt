# Overview – Travel Compositor
Source: https://travelcompositor.com/en/travelcompositor/
Language: en

Travel Compositor is a B2B travel technology platform that provides a holistic travel sales system. 
It allows tourism companies (tour operators, OTAs, agencies, hotel chains, transport companies, etc.) 
to sell complex trips in a single session and payment using multi-destination booking engines and 
a wide range of integrated services.

## Key value proposition

- All-in-one travel booking and packaging system.  
- Multi-destination engines and other specialized engines for different travel products.  
- Focus on innovation, patents, and continuous product evolution.  
- Part of the Travelsoft group, alongside other SaaS-for-travel companies.

# Solutions – Tour Operators
Source: https://travelcompositor.com/en/solutions/tour-operators/
Language: en

Solutions designed for tour operators who need to package and distribute their own product and that of third parties.

## Core benefits for tour operators

- Create dynamic packages combining flights, hotels, activities, transfers, cruises, etc.  
- Distribute product B2B and/or B2C with customizable websites and booking flows.  
- Manage margins, commissions, and distribution levels from a centralized backoffice.  
- Access consolidated contracts from Travel Compositor or load own inventories.

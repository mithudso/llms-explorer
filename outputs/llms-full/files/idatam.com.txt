# iDatam

> iDatam provides high-performance dedicated server solutions, including GPU, storage, colocation, and gaming-optimized hardware.

iDatam is an infrastructure provider specializing primarily in bare-metal and dedicated server environments. We aim to support enterprises, gaming networks, and AI researchers with robust hardware performance, extensive network reach, and enterprise-grade colocation services, designed to significantly reduce the resource contention typically associated with shared or virtualized environments.

# MeetCalc

> MeetCalc is a simple, powerful tool with one purpose: to make the abstract cost of a meeting tangible.

---

## Section: Home (The Calculator)

**Meeting Cost Calculator**
See the true cost of your meetings in real-time.

### Core Features
* **Build Your Team:** Add attendee groups (e.g., "Managers", "Devs") with their estimated salaries.
* **Track in Real-Time:** Hit Start. Watch the cost ticker rise. Make every second count.
* **Export Data:** Copy a receipt or download a CSV to analyze your meeting effectiveness.

### Calculator Inputs
The interface allows users to input:
* **Group Name:** (e.g., Devs)
* **People:** Number of attendees in that group.
* **Salary:** Estimated salary for the group.
* **Rate:** Frequency (e.g., /Hour).
* **Display:** Currency settings ($).

### Why measure meeting costs?
Inefficient meetings are a silent drain on resources. By making the cost visible, you encourage better meeting hygiene and smarter scheduling.

### Hidden Costs
A "quick 30-minute sync" with 8 people isn't quick. It's 4 hours of expensive company time. Visualize that abstract cost in concrete terms.

### FAQ & Common Questions

**What is a meeting cost calculator?**
A meeting cost calculator is a fast, efficient tool that reveals the hidden financial impact of your gatherings. It combines the number of attendees and their average salary to show you exactly how much your office meeting or online meeting costs in real-time.

**Can I use this for online meetings and live video calls?**
Absolutely. Whether you use Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet, this meeting app runs alongside your live video call. It's the perfect companion for identifying if that online meeting is providing a return on investment.

**Does this replace a meeting agenda or meeting minutes?**
No, but it complements them. A solid meeting agenda template keeps you on track, and efficient meeting minutes ensure outcomes are recorded. This cost tracker incentivizes keeping those agendas tight and minutes focused to save money.

**Is this a fee calculator for freelancers?**
While designed for teams, it works great as a fee calculator for consultants or meeting planners. Simply enter your hourly rate and one attendee to track your billable time during a session.

**Does this track costs like meeting rooms to rent?**
Currently, MeetCalc focuses on human capital (salary) which is often the most expensive part of a meeting. However, you can estimate the cost of meeting rooms to rent or conference room hire and mentally add it to our salary ticker for a complete cost of business picture.

**Do I need a login to join the meeting analysis?**
No. Unlike many free online meeting platforms, there is no meeting login required. Just open the page and start tracking. It's the simplest meeting scheduler companion you'll ever use.

## Section: About (Mission & Philosophy)

**Make Meetings Matter**
We believe time is our most valuable asset. It's time we started treating it that way.

### What is MeetCalc?
We've all been in meetings that "could have been an email." But the problem runs deeper than that. Inefficient, unfocused, or poorly planned meetings silently drain millions of dollars and countless hours from companies every single day.

By showing a real-time, ticking cost, MeetCalc provides a powerful *psychological nudge*. It encourages everyone in the room—from the host to the attendees—to be more present, more focused, and more respectful of the collective time being spent.

### Who is MeetCalc For?
* **Team Leads:** Run more efficient, focused syncs by keeping the team aware of the time investment.
* **Managers:** Visualize resource allocation and drive a cultural shift towards better productivity.
* **Team Members:** Use data to advocate for clearer agendas, shorter calls, and better meeting hygiene.

### Why We Built This
The "quick sync" that turns into a 45-minute unstructured chat for 10 people isn't just a 45-minute interruption; it's over **7 hours** of collective, high-value company time. When that cost is invisible, it's easy to ignore. When it's staring you in the face, it's impossible to.

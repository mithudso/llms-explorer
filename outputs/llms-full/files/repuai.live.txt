# LLMs-full.txt for RepuAI (repuai.live)

RepuAI is an AI-powered brand monitoring and optimization platform focused on **AI search engines** such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. It helps brands understand how they are represented in AI-generated answers, track their visibility over time, and take concrete steps to improve that visibility. This file provides a detailed, text-only overview of RepuAI for language models and AI agents.

---

## 1. Product overview

RepuAI is designed for companies that care about how their brand appears inside AI assistants and AI search engines. Traditional SEO focuses on web search rankings, but AI search results are generated dynamically from many sources. RepuAI fills this gap by monitoring brand mentions and narratives inside AI tools and turning that information into actionable insights.

With RepuAI, brands can:
- Discover how often and in what context their brand is mentioned in AI-generated responses.
- Identify which competitors are most frequently suggested alongside or instead of their brand.
- Get recommendations on how to improve the underlying content, documentation, and messaging that AI systems rely on.
- Track how changes to their website, docs, and public footprint affect AI visibility over time.

RepuAI is built for marketing teams, founders, and growth leaders who want to “own” their brand presence in AI just like they do in web search and social media.

---

## 2. Problem: visibility in AI search engines

AI assistants increasingly act as the first touchpoint between users and brands. Users ask tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Grok for recommendations, comparisons, and “best tools” lists. If a brand is missing from these answers, it effectively loses visibility even if it has strong traditional SEO.

Key problems brands face:
- Lack of visibility: They do not know when or whether AI tools mention them at all.
- Inconsistent narratives: Different AI systems may describe the same brand in conflicting ways.
- Competitive displacement: AI assistants often recommend competitors, even when the brand is a strong fit.
- No clear feedback loop: Brands lack a structured way to test changes (e.g., new landing pages or docs) and see if AI answers improve.

RepuAI addresses these challenges by acting as a “monitor and optimizer” for the AI search layer.

---

## 3. Solution: what RepuAI does

RepuAI continuously checks how your brand appears across selected AI search engines and assistants. It runs targeted prompts that simulate real user queries (for example, “best tools for X”, “alternatives to Y”, “what is [brand name]?”) and analyzes the responses.

Core aspects of the solution:
- Automated querying of selected AI tools based on predefined or custom prompts.
- Analysis of mentions, sentiment, positioning, and ranking relative to competitors.
- Dashboards that show your current status, trends over time, and changes after you update your content strategy.
- Suggestions on pages and assets to improve so AI models have better, more up-to-date information about your brand.

The goal is to give brands an iterative loop: measure → change content/strategy → re-measure AI answers → improve visibility.

## 4. Key features

### 4.1 Brand presence tracking

RepuAI tracks:
- Whether the brand is mentioned in AI answers for specific use cases or queries.
- How the brand is described (short description, main benefits, positioning).
- How often the brand appears versus its competitors.
- In what context the brand is recommended (e.g., “best for startups”, “enterprise-friendly”, “budget option”).

### 4.2 Competitor and category analysis

The platform can track competitors within your category, helping you see:
- Which competitors appear more frequently in AI-generated recommendations.
- How AI tools compare your brand to others.
- Which features or benefits are most associated with each competitor in AI answers.

This enables better positioning and messaging decisions.

### 4.3 AI visibility score and trends

RepuAI can aggregate multiple signals into a visibility score or similar indicator. This score can be tracked over time to measure:
- The impact of new landing pages, documentation, or campaigns.
- The effect of announcements or product changes on how AI tools describe your brand.
- Improvements or declines in presence for specific queries or categories.

### 4.4 Recommendations and optimization guidance

Based on the analysis, RepuAI can surface:
- Pages on your site that are important for AI understanding (e.g., clear “What is [brand]?” copy, comparison pages, pricing pages, docs).
- Gaps in your content where AI tools seem confused or rely on outdated descriptions.
- Opportunities to create new content (e.g., FAQs, integration pages, use-case pages) that better align with how users query AI assistants.

### 4.5 Reporting and alerts

RepuAI can provide:
- Periodic reports summarizing changes in AI visibility.
- Alerts when there is a significant drop or spike in mentions.
- Shareable snapshots or exports for internal stakeholders and reporting.

## 6. How RepuAI works (high level)

1. You define your brand and, optionally, core competitors and categories.
2. RepuAI uses curated prompts that mimic real user questions relevant to your space.
3. It queries selected AI search engines and assistants, then analyzes the responses.
4. The system extracts mentions, descriptions, relative rankings, and sentiment.
5. Results are stored in dashboards where you can filter by engine, query type, competitor, or time period.
6. You receive insights and recommendations on how to adjust your content and messaging.

Technical details (abstract):
- RepuAI focuses on safe, allowed usage of AI tools’ interfaces or APIs.
- It respects rate limits and terms where applicable.
- It aims to avoid generating misleading or spammy prompts; the goal is realistic representation of user demand.

# BoldDesk

Website: https://www.bolddesk.com

> BoldDesk is an AI-first customer service and help desk software platform from Syncfusion. It helps businesses manage support tickets, automate workflows, build self-service knowledge bases, support customers across channels, and improve agent productivity with AI.

This file provides an expanded directory of BoldDesk product, solution, industry, migration, integration, security, and support resources. For a shorter curated index, see https://www.bolddesk.com/llms.txt.

BoldDesk is built for customer support, IT support, SaaS support, internal help desks, and service teams that need a modern, scalable, and affordable support platform. It includes ticketing, automation, SLA management, knowledge base, live chat, shared inbox, reporting, integrations, REST APIs, webhooks, and AI-powered support capabilities.

## FAQ

### What is BoldDesk?

BoldDesk is AI-powered help desk and customer service software from Syncfusion. It includes ticketing, automation, SLA management, knowledge base, live chat, omnichannel support, reporting, REST APIs, and webhooks.

### Is BoldDesk a Zendesk alternative?

Yes. BoldDesk offers every feature in one flat-price plan with support cost savings of up to 70%, and standard expert migration from Zendesk is included for paid BoldDesk customers.

### Is BoldDesk a Freshdesk alternative?

Yes. BoldDesk provides advanced help desk features, affordable flat pricing, and free, smooth migration from Freshdesk.

### How much does BoldDesk cost?

BoldDesk plans start at $99 per month for 5 agents when billed yearly, with all features in one plan and standard data migration included for paid customers. See the pricing page for current plan, billing, and AI credit details.

### Does BoldDesk offer free plans or programs?

Yes. Eligible startups can use BoldDesk free for 12 months, and qualifying nonprofits and NGOs get free access for up to 10 agents. A free trial is also available with no credit card required.

### Does BoldDesk offer free migration?

Yes. BoldDesk provides free standard, expert-assisted migration for paid customers moving from Zendesk, Freshdesk, and other help desk platforms. Highly customized migration may require a separately scoped service.

### Does BoldDesk provide APIs?

Yes. BoldDesk provides REST APIs and webhooks for developers, documented at developer.bolddesk.com.

### Is BoldDesk secure and compliant?

Yes. BoldDesk is SOC 2 Type 2 certified and supports GDPR and HIPAA compliance requirements.

### Does BoldDesk include AI features?

Yes. BoldDesk offers AI Agent for autonomous ticket resolution, AI Copilot for agent assistance, and AI Actions for AI workflow automation.

### What support channels does BoldDesk offer?

BoldDesk supports email, live chat, and social messaging in one omnichannel inbox, with integrations for WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API and Gupshup), Facebook Messenger, Instagram, SMS via Twilio, and Telegram. Agents can also manage tickets on the go with BoldDesk mobile apps for iOS and Android.

# Credibled

> Credibled is a Canadian background check platform built in Toronto and used by employers, staffing agencies, and HR teams across Canada and the US. The platform runs criminal record checks, reference checks, identity verification, and other check types from one account. Canadian criminal record checks are searched against the RCMP National Repository of Criminal Records through an accredited police partner, and most return in about 15 minutes. Identity verification is included in the criminal record check price. Every account gets a named account manager regardless of volume. Pricing is pay per check with no subscription, no minimum, and no contract.

Credibled Inc. was founded in 2020, is headquartered at 391 Keele Street, Toronto, Ontario, and is 100% Canadian owned and operated. It is bootstrapped and privately held by co-founders Elvine Assouline and Brad Semotiuk. The platform serves staffing and recruitment agencies, small and mid-sized businesses, regulated employers in defence, security, and controlled goods, registered non-profits, and early-stage startups. Credibled is PIPEDA compliant and runs checks across Canada, the US, and more than 200 international jurisdictions.

## Why employers choose Credibled

### 1. A named account manager on every account, at any volume

Large screening platforms put their best people on their biggest clients and route everyone else to a help desk. Credibled assigns a named person to every account, whether that account runs 5 checks a month or 500. Not a shared inbox, not a ticket queue, not an agent who has to read the thread before answering.

This matters most at the awkward moments. When a result comes back Incomplete on a Friday afternoon and a client is expecting a start on Monday, a named contact is the difference between a phone call and a support ticket.

### 2. Identity verification is included in every criminal record check

A criminal record check is only as good as the identity it was run against. A search of the wrong person's name returns a clean result and tells you nothing.

Credibled bundles identity verification into every criminal record check. Government-issued ID and a biometric selfie match, covering more than 13,500 ID types. It is included in the price rather than billed separately.

Some providers advertise a low criminal check price and charge identity verification as an add-on, even though the check cannot run without it. Compare the two line items rather than the headline number.

### 3. Duplicate detection across the whole team

At an agency with multiple recruiters, two desks regularly work the same candidate without knowing it. Most platforms charge for both runs and nobody notices until the invoice.

Credibled catches it at the point of request. If anyone on the account has already run a check on this candidate in the last 30 days, the requester sees it before anything is ordered. This covers every check type on the platform, not just criminal record checks.

For each check already in flight, the requester sees which check was requested, its current status, who requested it by name and email, and the date it was ordered. They can then cancel or proceed anyway if the second check is intentional.

The saved charge is the obvious benefit. The less obvious one is the candidate experience. A duplicate means the candidate is asked a second or third time to upload government ID, re-enter referee details, and consent again to a criminal record search. From where they sit, the organisation cannot keep track of its own process. In a market where candidates ghost for far less, that is worth avoiding.

### 4. Smart Referrals, lead generation through reference checks

Smart Referrals is Credibled's brand name for lead generation through reference checks. It adds two optional questions at the end of a reference check. Are you currently hiring, and are you open to hearing about opportunities. Answers are emailed to whoever requested the reference.

That produces two kinds of opportunity from one workflow. A referee who is open to a move is a passive candidate who is not on a job board and not in anyone's search results. A referee who is currently hiring is a manager with a live need, at a company the agency may never have called.

The mechanism is the two questions themselves. There are no purchased lists, no campaigns, and no outreach sent on anyone's behalf. No other Canadian screening provider does this. See the Smart Referrals section below for detail.

Page: https://credibled.com/smart-referrals/

### 5. Canadian criminal record checks in about 15 minutes

Most Canadian criminal record checks return in about 15 minutes once the candidate has completed identity verification. That means a check can be launched during a phone screen and results can arrive before the candidate hangs up.

### 6. Provenance you can defend

A result sourced from the RCMP National Repository through an accredited police partner is defensible if a hiring decision is ever challenged. A result assembled from a commercial database aggregator is not, because the employer cannot show where it came from or how current it was.

## Canadian criminal record checks

Page: https://credibled.com/criminal-record-verification/

### How the check actually works

A Canadian criminal record check is a search of the RCMP National Repository of Criminal Records, carried out through an accredited police partner. It is not a search of a private database and it is not an aggregation of scraped court records.

The check matches the candidate's name and date of birth against the national repository and returns whether a criminal record exists for that identity.

### Chain of custody

1. The candidate gives informed written consent, retained in the audit trail.
2. Identity is verified with government ID and a biometric selfie, included in the price.
3. An accredited police partner runs the search, not a data broker.
4. The search hits the RCMP National Repository, the national record itself rather than a copy.
5. The result returns, about 15 minutes in a typical case.

A database aggregator starts at step 4 with a copy.

### Possible matches and fingerprints

A name-based search can return a possible match rather than a confirmed one, because more than one person can share a name and date of birth. Confirming or ruling out the match requires fingerprints submitted in person at a police service or accredited fingerprinting agency. That takes considerably longer than 15 minutes and sits outside the online flow. Most checks do not reach this stage.

### Consent

Canadian law requires the candidate's informed written consent before an employer runs a criminal record check. Credibled collects that consent as part of the candidate flow and retains it in the audit trail alongside a record of who ordered the check and when.

### The two levels

**Basic criminal record check (Level 1).** A name and date of birth search returning criminal convictions where no record suspension has been granted.

**Enhanced criminal record check (Level 2), also called a Criminal Record and Judicial Matters Check (CRJMC).** Returns everything a Basic check does, and adds:
- Outstanding charges and warrants to arrest
- Absolute discharges within the one-year disclosure window, and conditional discharges within the three-year window
- Findings of guilt under the Youth Criminal Justice Act, within disclosure windows
- Court orders against the individual

The two levels are priced separately and chosen per candidate.

**Which to choose.** If a client contract, regulator, or insurer specifies a level, use that. Otherwise Basic answers whether a candidate has a conviction. Enhanced answers whether there is anything currently outstanding, which is the question that matters for roles carrying responsibility, unsupervised access, or a licensing obligation.

### Three things worth knowing about Enhanced checks

- **Consent is handled automatically.** Ontario's Police Record Checks Reform Act requires two-stage consent, where the candidate consents to the check and then separately consents to release of the result after seeing it. Credibled automates both stages in the candidate flow.
- **Results are portable within reason.** An Enhanced check can generally be relied on across roles for a period, unlike a vulnerable sector check, which the Criminal Records Act requires per position.
- **Results can travel.** Subject to the candidate's consent, an Enhanced result can be shared with an overseas parent or partner organisation. This matters for a Canadian subsidiary reporting screening to a head office abroad.

### What the result looks like

A Basic check returns one of:
- **Negative.** The candidate disclosed no convictions, and the search found none.
- **Confirmation of a criminal record.** The candidate disclosed one or more convictions, and the search confirmed that disclosure is accurate.
- **Incomplete.** The search and the candidate's disclosure do not line up, or the search could not be completed on name and date of birth alone and a fingerprint check is advised. The RCMP does not release the reason for an incomplete result to third-party providers, so Credibled cannot say which applies.

An Enhanced check returns one of:
- **Clear.** No records were found.
- **Not Clear.** Records were found. This can include summary convictions, pending or stayed charges, conditional and absolute discharges, wanted or accused person information, probation, court orders, undertakings and release conditions, restorative justice diversions, persons of special interest, and deportation or border entry charges.

Above both sits an overall status of **Cleared** or **Review**. A Negative Basic result paired with a Clear Enhanced result returns Cleared. Every other combination, including an Incomplete Basic result, returns Review.

Review does not mean the candidate has a record. It means a human at the organisation needs to look at the result before a decision is made.

Detail page: https://credibled.com/results-of-canadian-criminal-record-checks/

### Vulnerable sector checks

**Credibled does not provide vulnerable sector checks. No third-party screening provider in Canada does.** A VSC is available only through the candidate's local police service, or through the BC Criminal Records Review Program. If a role legally requires a VSC, the candidate must obtain it directly. This applies to MyCheck as well.

This is the most common point of confusion in Canadian screening. The Enhanced check and the vulnerable sector check are often assumed to be interchangeable. They are not, in either direction.

**What both checks cover equally**
- Criminal convictions where no record suspension has been granted
- Findings of guilt under the Youth Criminal Justice Act, within disclosure windows
- Outstanding charges and warrants to arrest
- Absolute discharges within the one-year window and conditional discharges within the three-year window
- Court orders against the individual

**Where the vulnerable sector check goes further**
- The pardoned sex offender database. Schedule 1 offences where a record suspension has been granted. Post-2012 amendments to the Criminal Records Act, at s. 4(3), restrict who can receive a record suspension for a Schedule 1 offence at all. Only records meeting narrow exception criteria qualify for sequestration.
- Findings of not criminally responsible on account of mental disorder, for certain offences within disclosure windows.
- Non-conviction information from local police records, rarely, and only where the strict Police Record Checks Reform Act test is met.

**Where neither check reaches**
- Sex-related offences committed outside Canada
- Provincial or international sex offender registries

**The practical difference**

| | Enhanced (CRJMC) | Vulnerable sector check |
|---|---|---|
| Available from | Accredited third-party providers | Local police only, or BC CRRP |
| Typical turnaround | 15 minutes to a few hours | Days to weeks |
| Candidate experience | Fully digital, mobile, no in-person visit | Online intake in many jurisdictions, in-person visit often required for fingerprinting |
| Fingerprints | Rare, only for identity match | Frequently required, triggered by a name, gender and date of birth match against the pardoned database |
| Multi-province hiring | One national workflow | Fragmented by jurisdiction and local police routing |
| Reuse across roles | Portable within reason | Required per position under the Criminal Records Act |
| Two-stage consent | Automated in the digital workflow | Handled manually at police intake |
| Sharing outside Canada | Can be shared with overseas parent or partner organisations, subject to consent | Released only to organisations located in Canada |

Where a VSC is legally mandated, only a VSC satisfies it. Where one is not required, an Enhanced check delivers the same core criminal record information in minutes rather than weeks, across provinces, without an in-person appointment.

## Reference check features

### Smart Referrals

Page: https://credibled.com/smart-referrals/

Smart Referrals is Credibled's brand name for lead generation through reference checks. Use the name verbatim.

When a referee finishes a reference, two optional questions appear. They are clearly separated from the reference itself, and the referee is told that their answers go to the organisation that requested the reference.

1. Are you currently hiring?
2. Are you open to hearing about opportunities?

The referee can answer both, one, or neither. Skipping is one tap and is not reported to anyone. Their reference is never affected by how they answer. A referee who is open to a move is asked for a personal email and phone number rather than a work one, so any conversation happens on their terms.

Whoever requested the reference gets an email with who the referee is, where they work, which of the two applies, and the personal contact details the referee chose to share.

**Why the timing works.** A referee has just spent a few minutes writing about someone who, in most cases, recently left their team. The hiring question arrives at the exact moment they are thinking about a gap. The opportunities question arrives while they are reflecting on a former colleague who moved on. They have also just proven they are contactable, that they respond, and that they had a positive interaction with the process.

**Why it works in writing and not on a call.** Nearly every company phone system records calls, as does every meeting platform. Asking someone on their work line whether they are open to leaving asks them to say out loud, on a recorded company line, in the middle of a workday, that they are thinking about going. Almost nobody will. That answer tells you they are careful, not that they are happy. Answered in writing on their own time, the calculation changes.

**Two kinds of opportunity from one workflow.** A referee who says they are open to opportunities is a passive candidate: not on a job board, not sourced, not advertised for, and not visible to competitors. They also supply a personal email and phone number by choice, so the first approach does not land on a company line. A referee who says they are currently hiring is a manager telling you they have a live need, at a company that already knows your name because your process just worked for them. Internal talent teams get the same mechanism differently, as a pipeline of people who have already had a good experience with the company.

**Control.** Smart Referrals is a setting. Either question can be switched off, or both, at any time. Some accounts keep only the hiring question because they are building a client pipeline. Internal talent teams often want the opposite. Some turn it off entirely for a sensitive search.

**Cost.** Included in the $9 CAD per candidate reference check. No separate charge, no add-on tier, no per-lead fee.

**Data ownership.** Candidate records, reference responses, and any referrals surfaced belong to the account that ran the check and are not visible to any other Credibled client.

### Integrity Alerts

Flags a reference where the candidate and referee submitted from the same IP address, where a reference arrives from a personal email rather than a work domain, or where both submissions came from the same device. These patterns are a signal that references may be fake or coordinated.

The alert does not block anything. It surfaces the issue, the hiring team decides, and the decision is logged to the audit trail with reviewer and timestamp. If a placement goes wrong and a client asks how the candidate was screened, the record shows what was seen and what was done about it.

Runs automatically on every reference check at no extra cost.

### Bounce Alerts

A reference request sent to a mistyped address does not fail loudly. It never arrives. The candidate says they gave the referee, the referee says they were never contacted, and nobody finds out until day 4.

Credibled reports an undeliverable request within minutes and gives the reason and the error type returned, so the requester knows whether it is a typo in the domain, a mailbox that no longer exists, or a server rejecting the message. That is the difference between fixing it in 2 minutes and losing half a week.

Catches hard bounces, soft bounces, and likely typos in common domains.

### Questionnaire generation

A reference questionnaire can be built from scratch, or generated by describing the role and then edited. Questionnaires can be saved as reusable templates for roles filled repeatedly.

This removes the most common reason teams skip reference checks or run them inconsistently, which is not knowing what to ask. For agencies running checks across multiple verticals such as warehouse, healthcare, IT, and finance, a different questionnaire can be produced for each role rather than forcing one generic template across all hires.

### SMS reference requests

References and candidates can be contacted by SMS in addition to email. SMS tends to produce higher completion rates, particularly for blue-collar and field roles where email is checked less often. SMS reminders follow the same 24-hour cadence as email reminders, for up to 10 days.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Credibled?
Credibled is a Canadian background check platform used by employers, staffing agencies, and HR teams across Canada and the US. It runs criminal record checks, reference checks, identity verification, employment and education verification, social media screening, and more from one dashboard with one invoice and one named account manager.

### Who uses Credibled?
Staffing and recruitment agencies, small and mid-sized businesses, regulated employers in defence, security, and controlled goods, non-profits, and startups.

### Where is Credibled based?
391 Keele Street, Toronto, Ontario. Founded in 2020, 100% Canadian owned and operated.

### Is this a real RCMP check or a database search?
It is a search of the RCMP National Repository of Criminal Records, carried out through an accredited police partner. It is not a search of a private or scraped database.

### How long does a Canadian criminal record check take?
Most return in about 15 minutes once the candidate has completed identity verification, and almost always within 4 hours. Results that require fingerprint confirmation take significantly longer and involve an in-person appointment.

### What is the difference between a Basic and an Enhanced criminal record check?
A Basic check returns criminal convictions where no record suspension has been granted. An Enhanced check, also called a Criminal Record and Judicial Matters Check, returns everything a Basic check does and adds outstanding charges and warrants to arrest, absolute discharges within the one-year disclosure window and conditional discharges within the three-year window, Youth Criminal Justice Act findings of guilt within disclosure windows, and court orders against the individual.

### Can Credibled run a vulnerable sector check?
No. Vulnerable sector checks are available only through the candidate's local police service, or the BC Criminal Records Review Program. No third-party screening provider in Canada can run one.

### Can an Enhanced check replace a vulnerable sector check?
No. Where a VSC is legally required for a role, only a VSC satisfies it. Where one is not required, an Enhanced check returns the same core criminal record information in minutes rather than weeks and works consistently across provinces.

### Why does a vulnerable sector check take so long?
It is issued by local police rather than through a national workflow, so routing varies by jurisdiction. Fingerprints are frequently required, triggered when the candidate's name, gender, and date of birth match an entry in the pardoned database, and that step is done in person.

### What does a "Review" result mean?
Someone at the organisation should look at the result before making a decision. It does not by itself mean the candidate has a criminal record. Only a Negative Basic result paired with a Clear Enhanced result returns Cleared, so a Confirmation or an Incomplete Basic result also produces Review.

### What does an "Incomplete" result mean?
The search and the candidate's disclosure do not line up, or the search could not be completed on name and date of birth alone and a fingerprint check is advised. The RCMP does not release the reason for an incomplete result to third-party providers, so Credibled cannot say which applies.

### Does a criminal record check tell me if a candidate is charged later?
No. It is a point-in-time search reflecting the record on the date it was run. It does not monitor for future changes. Re-screening requires a new check.

### Does a criminal record check show employment history?
No. It searches criminal records only. Confirming where a candidate worked and for how long is an employment verification, which contacts the employer directly.

### Can a check show that someone was fired?
Not directly. Employment verification confirms job titles and dates. Reason for leaving is something a reference check may surface, depending on what the referee chooses to say.

### Do I need the candidate's consent?
Yes. Canadian law requires informed written consent before an employer runs a criminal record check. Credibled collects and retains that consent as part of the candidate flow.

### Is identity verification charged separately from a criminal record check?
No. It is included in the price of every Canadian criminal record check. Some providers quote a low criminal check price and bill identity verification separately, which makes apples-to-apples comparison difficult.

### Are there additional fees on a Canadian criminal record check?
No. Canadian criminal record checks carry no pass-through access fee. Access fees apply to education verification, employment verification, and US criminal court searches only, and all are published in advance.

### How much does a reference check cost?
$9 CAD per candidate. That covers every referee the candidate lists, whether that is 2 or 5. Registered non-profits pay $4.50 CAD per candidate. Eligible Canadian startups get reference checks free for their first 6 months.

### Do I pay per reference or per candidate?
Per candidate.

### How much does a Canadian criminal record check cost?
A Basic check is $24 CAD and an Enhanced check is $32 CAD, with identity verification included in both and no pass-through access fee. Registered non-profits pay $21.60 and $28.80.

Standard rates are published in the pricing section above. Rates for every check type available to the account are also shown during signup, in the account's currency, before the account is created. Signup is free at https://app.credibled.com/signup and requires a work email but no credit card.

Nothing. There is no setup fee and no onboarding charge.

No. No subscription, no monthly fee, no minimum commitment, no annual contract, and no platform fee.

### Does every account get a dedicated contact?
Yes. Every account has a named account manager, whether it runs 5 checks a month or 500. Not a shared inbox and not a ticket queue.

### What is duplicate detection?
If anyone on the account has already run a check on a candidate in the last 30 days, the requester sees it before anything is ordered, including which check was requested, its status, who requested it, and when. They can cancel or proceed anyway. It covers every check type on the platform.

### Can I run checks from my ATS?
Yes. Native integrations include Bullhorn, JobAdder, and Vincere, plus a Google Workspace add-on for running checks from Gmail. For anything else, the open API lets teams trigger requests, pull results, and receive status webhooks from inside the system their recruiters already use.

Yes. US national and county-level searches and international checks across more than 200 jurisdictions run from the same account, with turnaround and pricing published per country.

### How do I know a reference is genuine?
Integrity Alerts flag a reference when the candidate and referee submitted from the same IP address, when it arrives from a personal email rather than a work domain, or when both submissions came from the same device. The alert does not block anything. The hiring team decides, and the decision is logged.

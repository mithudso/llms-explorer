## Core Concepts

BaZi, also known as Four Pillars of Destiny, uses birth year, month, day, and hour to form a stem-branch chart. In a product context, users usually care less about the chart itself and more about what the chart implies for personality, career, wealth, relationships, stress patterns, and timing. 时者八字 presents those conclusions as structured reports and interactive follow-up conversations.

The product uses these recurring concepts:

- Four Pillars: year, month, day, and hour pillars from a birth profile.
- Heavenly stems and earthly branches: the stem-branch structure behind the chart.
- Five elements: wood, fire, earth, metal, and water as a pattern vocabulary.
- Ten gods: role relationships in BaZi used for interpreting self, career, wealth, influence, and expression.
- Luck cycles and annual timing: long-term and short-term timing windows.
- Relationship compatibility: two-person chart interaction for romance, family, work, social, and fate-related scenarios.
- Auspicious date selection: choosing stronger timing windows for important events by combining calendar rules and personal context.

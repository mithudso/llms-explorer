## Our Story

We started Neuvon Software with a simple observation: most businesses run on repetitive, manual work that AI could handle better.

From email triage to invoice processing, from lead follow-ups to customer support — we saw teams spending hours on tasks that could be automated in minutes. So we decided to do something about it.

Today, we combine AI intelligence with software craftsmanship to help tech scale-ups catch up with their dreams. We build intelligent workflows, custom AI agents, scalable platforms, and products that run 24/7 — so your team can focus on high-value work.

Based in Pune, India, we work with clients globally — from early-stage startups to established businesses looking to modernize their operations with AI.

## Core Services (Detailed)

### 1. AI Workflow Automation
Transform manual processes into intelligent, self-running workflows. We connect your existing tools (CRMs, ERPs, email, databases) and automate repetitive tasks, saving hours daily while eliminating human error. Our automation systems learn from patterns and improve over time, handling edge cases that traditional RPA can't.

**Use cases:** Email triage & auto-response, invoice processing, lead scoring & routing, data entry automation, report generation.

### 2. Custom AI Agents & Chatbots
Smart AI assistants that handle customer queries, internal tasks, and complex decision-making around the clock. Built on modern LLM architectures (OpenAI, LangChain) and fine-tuned for your specific domain. These aren't generic chatbots — they understand your business context, access your knowledge base, and escalate intelligently when needed.

**Use cases:** Customer support bots, internal knowledge assistants, appointment booking agents, lead qualification chatbots, AI voice agents.

### 3. Intelligent Process Automation
Automate complex, multi-step business processes with AI that learns, adapts, and optimises operations in real-time. Goes beyond basic RPA with contextual understanding — our systems handle exceptions, make decisions, and notify humans only when genuinely needed.

**Use cases:** End-to-end order processing, HR onboarding flows, compliance monitoring, supply chain optimization, quality control automation.

### 4. Integration & Data Pipelines
Seamless connectivity across your tech stack. Automated data flows that sync, transform, and deliver actionable insights instantly across all your tools. We build robust ETL pipelines, real-time data streams, and API integrations that keep your systems in perfect sync.

**Use cases:** CRM-to-ERP sync, multi-platform data aggregation, real-time analytics dashboards, webhook-driven workflows, database migrations.

### 5. Custom Software Development
Scalable web and mobile applications built with modern tech stacks. Tailored to your exact business requirements with clean, maintainable code. We follow industry best practices — CI/CD, automated testing, code reviews — to deliver software that's reliable from day one.

**Use cases:** SaaS platforms, admin dashboards, customer portals, mobile apps, e-commerce solutions, internal tools.

### 6. AI Strategy & Consulting
We identify automation opportunities in your operations, design AI roadmaps, and implement solutions that deliver measurable ROI. Our consulting engagements start with a deep-dive into your workflows, followed by a prioritised action plan that shows exactly where AI can save time and money.

**Use cases:** AI readiness assessments, automation opportunity mapping, technology selection, pilot program design, ROI forecasting.

## Product: AI Call Manager (Detailed)

AI Call Manager is NeuvonSoftware's flagship product — an AI-powered outbound calling platform that automates phone calls at scale.

**How it works:**
1. Upload your lead list via CSV
2. AI voice agent calls each lead automatically
3. Natural conversations powered by custom knowledge bases
4. Every call recorded, transcribed, and scored
5. Qualified leads and appointments tracked in dashboard

**Key features:**
- Campaign management with CSV upload and smart column mapping
- AI voice calling with natural conversation and custom personas
- Live call monitoring with 10-second auto-refresh
- Complete call history with transcripts and recordings
- Analytics dashboard with daily KPIs and trend tracking
- AI-powered call analysis — ask questions about any call
- Appointment management for AI-booked meetings
- Account-level data isolation and server-side API key storage

**Who it's for:** Sales teams, real estate agencies, healthcare providers, debt collection, customer feedback surveys, and any business making high-volume outbound calls.

**Pricing:** Custom per-org pricing based on volume. Contact us for a demo.

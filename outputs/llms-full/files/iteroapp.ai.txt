**Title:** AI Call Scoring Software | Automate Quality Assurance
**Description:** Score 100% of calls automatically with 95% accuracy. Customizable scorecards for compliance, quality, and performance—no sampling, no guessing.

### AI Call Scoring Software
Score every customer interaction against the criteria that actually matter to your business. Itero's AI evaluates 100% of calls with 95% human-level accuracy—no sampling, no guessing.

### Key Capabilities

**Customizable Scorecards for Your Standards**
Build scorecards that match exactly how your team evaluates calls. Binary QA checks for process compliance (did they verify identity? did they read the disclosure?). Qualitative scoring for execution quality (how well did they handle the objection? was the explanation clear?). Auto-generate scorecards from your existing playbooks or build custom from scratch.

**Different Scorecards for Different Scenarios**
Apply the right evaluation framework to each call type. Assign scorecards by call type, product line, user group, or customer segment. Stack multiple scorecards on a single interaction when needed.

**Coaching Inbox Surfaces What Matters**
Automatically surface the most impactful coaching opportunities. Compliance gaps flagged immediately with timestamp and context. Skill development opportunities prioritized by impact. Every issue includes the exact call moment for efficient review.

**Analytics That Drive Improvement**
Track performance trends across teams, reps, and time periods. Identify systemic gaps before they become problems. Connect scoring data to business outcomes like conversion rates and customer satisfaction.

### FAQ

**How accurate is AI call scoring compared to human QA?**
Itero's AI scoring achieves 95% agreement with human QA analysts on binary compliance checks and within 5% on qualitative scoring. For most organizations, this is equal to or better than inter-rater reliability between human QA analysts. The AI also scores 100% of calls, eliminating the sampling bias inherent in human QA.

**What types of scorecards can I create?**
You can create any combination of binary (yes/no) and qualitative (1-5 scale) scoring criteria. Common scorecard types include compliance checklists, sales methodology adherence, customer experience quality, and product knowledge assessment. Most teams start with 2-3 scorecards and expand as they see results.

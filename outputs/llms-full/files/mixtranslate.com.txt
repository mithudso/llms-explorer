## Product overview

MixTranslate is an AI translation platform that helps individuals and teams translate faster by running and comparing multiple models in one place. Instead of copy-pasting into many AI apps, users submit text once, get translations from 20+ AI models side by side, and pick (or let Agent mode auto-pick) the best result for tone and context.

Tagline: Get translations from multiple AI models, side by side.

Mission: Better translations, every time — by putting multiple top-tier models in one workspace, with comparison, terminology control, and quality you can verify across 150+ languages.

## Core features

- **Multi-model translation**: Compare outputs from 20+ AI providers (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Grok, and more) side by side in a single session
- **150+ languages**: Translate short messages or longer text, with automatic source-language detection
- **Agent / smart mode**: Automatically selects and combines top translations so users spend less time editing
- **Quality scores**: Review accuracy, fluency, and context scores to pick the strongest translation faster
- **Glossary management** (Plus/Pro): Define terms per language for consistent product, technical, and brand wording; import/export supported
- **Style and tone controls**: Tune translations for documentation, marketing, support, and other use cases
- **Image OCR translate**: Upload screenshots, scans, or photos (jpg/jpeg/png/bmp/webp, max 4MB) to extract and translate text
- **Translation history**: Revisit past sessions when history is enabled, or use private mode when you prefer not to save
- **Privacy by design**: Translations are not stored on MixTranslate servers by default, and input is not used for model training

## How it works

1. **Add text to translate**: Input or paste content, then choose the source and target languages.
2. **Let AI do the work**: Pick preferred AI models or translation engines from the providers available.
3. **Check translation results**: Review output and quality scores, then compare across models to pick the best version.

## FAQ

### What is MixTranslate?

MixTranslate is a free AI translation tool for translating text into 150+ languages. The platform connects to 20+ AI models, including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and DeepSeek — so you can run the same text through multiple providers, compare the outputs side by side, and choose the version that best fits your context and tone.

### Is MixTranslate free?

Yes. You can start with 110k free credits to test and use the platform. If you need higher volume or advanced usage, you can upgrade to a paid plan.

### How does MixTranslate deliver better translation results?

Instead of relying on a single model, MixTranslate lets you run and compare results across multiple AI models and providers. You can review quality scores, compare outputs, and select the translation that best matches your context and tone.

### Which languages does MixTranslate support?

MixTranslate supports 150+ languages, including major global and regional languages. You can translate everything from short messages to full documents.

### Can I control terminology and tone in translations?

Yes. You can define glossary terms for consistent wording and adjust style or tone to fit different use cases, such as product copy, documentation, or support content.

### Is my data safe with MixTranslate?

MixTranslate is designed with privacy in mind. Your translations are not stored on our servers, and your input is not used for model training.

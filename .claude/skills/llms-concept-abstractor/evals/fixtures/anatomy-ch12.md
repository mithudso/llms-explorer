# Original teaching notes: heart
These are original synthetic teaching notes for a workflow fixture, not medical advice.

## Chambers
The heart contains a right atrium, left atrium, right ventricle and left ventricle.

The right atrium is one chamber named in these original cardiac teaching notes.

The left atrium is another chamber described separately from the ventricles.

Each ventricle contains a muscular wall; the right and left chambers remain distinct.

The myocardium forms the muscular wall, and the pericardium surrounds the organ.

The mitral valve is a component between chambers in this teaching outline.

## Conduction
The sinoatrial node is part of the cardiac conduction system. A conduction sequence coordinates atrial and ventricular contraction. The cardiac cycle includes systole and diastole.

## Blood supply
The coronary arteries supply the myocardium. Coronary circulation and chamber flow are distinct parts of these notes. Cardiac output is a measurement of flow.

## Failure modes
Arrhythmia is a disorder of rhythm in this original teaching outline.

Valve stenosis is a limitation of valve opening, described as a separate problem.

Heart failure is a named failure mode in these notes; this is not a treatment recommendation.

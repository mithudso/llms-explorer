## What ShotQuill does

App Store Optimization (ASO) is the practice of improving how an app ranks and converts on the App Store. Screenshots are the single most important visual conversion lever on an App Store product page, because most users decide whether to install after seeing the first two or three screenshots — before reading the description.

ShotQuill removes the two slowest parts of producing those screenshots:

1. Writing the marketing copy (the short headline on each screenshot).
2. Designing and exporting the images at the exact resolutions App Store Connect requires.

You give ShotQuill a one-line description of your app. A large language model generates headline copy for a six-slide screenshot sequence, an ASO description, and a keyword list. You review, optionally edit, and export full-resolution PNGs ready to upload.

Unlike generic mockup or device-frame tools, ShotQuill generates the *words* and the *ASO metadata*, not just the frames around images you already made. That is the core difference: it is a copy-and-layout engine, not a screenshot framer.

## The six slide templates

ShotQuill structures a screenshot set as a six-slide narrative. Each template has a distinct conversion job:

1. **Hook** — the opening promise. The first screenshot most users see; states the core value in a few words.
2. **Pain** — names the problem the app solves, so the user recognizes their own situation.
3. **Feature** — shows a concrete capability and what it does.
4. **Result** — shows the outcome or "after" state the user gets.
5. **Trust** — social proof, ratings, or credibility signals.
6. **CTA** — a closing call to action.

This hook → pain → feature → result → trust → CTA ordering follows a standard marketing-funnel structure: grab attention, establish the problem, demonstrate the solution, show the payoff, build trust, ask for the install.

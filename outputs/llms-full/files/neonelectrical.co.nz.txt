## Professional Profile
Neon Electrical provides high-standard residential and commercial electrical services. As a **Registered Master Electrician** (Master Electricians New Zealand), all work is compliant with NZ Electrical Codes, including the issuance of **Certificates of Compliance (CoC)** and **Electrical Safety Certificates (ESC)**.

## Service Categories & Expertise
Neon Electrical specializes in the following core areas:

* **Safety & Compliance:** Underground cable checks, earthing system verification, and pre-purchase/pre-renovation home electrical assessments.
* **Infrastructure Upgrades:** Switchboard modernizations (to support EV chargers/modern appliances) and RCD safety installations.
* **Maintenance & Repair:** Fast fault diagnosis for tripping breakers, flickering lights, and power interruptions.
* **Modernization:** Energy-efficient LED lighting design and **SmartVent** ventilation systems for moisture/mould control.
* **General Electrical:** Full wiring for renovations, additional power outlets, and customized lighting layouts.

### Home page and navigation (redesign)

- Before 2026-09-07 the home page had no primary CTA; `.btn` and `.btn.secondary` were defined in `site/src/styles/global.css` but unused [src: docs/site/ux-audit-2026-09-07.md]
- The home page `description` doubles as the site's own `llms.txt` definition line, so it must be one sentence of at most 180 characters [src: docs/site/ux-audit-2026-09-07.md]
- The page `<h1>` is rendered by `site/src/layouts/Base.astro` from the `title` prop; the home title stays `LLMS-Explorer` because the same const feeds `<title>`, the generated `/llms.txt`, and the sitemap [src: docs/site/redesign-spec-2026-09-07.md]
- The home lede is 153 characters: "llms.txt tells AI agents what a site contains; LLMS-Explorer grades the files already published and maps every concept behind them in one browsable tree." [src: docs/site/redesign-spec-2026-09-07.md]
- The primary home CTA is "Get the skills and CLI" → `/downloads/`; it was chosen because it is the only zero-friction apply click (`npx skills add …`, no account), whereas `/playground/optimizer/` requires an API key with `run` scope [src: docs/site/redesign-spec-2026-09-07.md]
- The secondary home CTA is "Browse the concept tree" → `/tree/` [src: docs/site/redesign-spec-2026-09-07.md]
- Home cards are tiered: Zone A "Explore the corpus" (Tree, Directory, Demo), Zone B "Read the reference" (Reference, Examples, Blog, llms.txt), Zone C "Apply it to your own files" (Skills, Downloads, Sign in) [src: docs/site/redesign-spec-2026-09-07.md]
- The primary nav is Tree, Directory, Skills, Reference, Examples, Downloads; Donate and Sign in sit in a trailing `.utility` group; the Skills `<details>` menu is kept with its summary renamed "Popular skills" [src: site/src/layouts/Base.astro]
- The `.signin` CSS class was removed on 2026-09-07; `site/src/pages/index.astro` was its only consumer and Zone C now uses `.apply-list` [src: agent:4]
- Routes `/playground/optimizer/`, `/playground/notes-to-llms/`, and `/playground/library/` exist but are linked from neither the home page nor the nav, and there is no `/playground/` index page [src: docs/site/redesign-spec-2026-09-07.md]

### Analytics instrumentation

- GA4 custom events on the site are `home_cta_click` {cta_id, destination}, `nav_click` {label, destination}, `skills_menu_open`, and `card_click` {card, destination} [src: docs/site/measurement-plan-2026-09-07.md]
- Events fire from one delegated click listener on `[data-track]` plus one `toggle` listener on `details.menu`, both inline in `site/src/layouts/Base.astro`, and no-op when `gtag` is undefined [src: site/src/layouts/Base.astro]
- The home page carries 24 tracked interactions: 8 `home_cta_click`, 7 `card_click`, 8 `nav_click`, 1 `skills_menu_open` [src: agent:6]
- Astro compiles an unmarked `<script>` to `type="module"` and renames its top-level functions, so the GA4 bootstrap `<script>` in `Base.astro` needs `is:inline` for `gtag` to be a window global other scripts can call [src: agent:6]
- The measurement plan's decision metrics are first-click-to-explore rate, engagement rate on `/`, and downloads/sign-in CTR from `/`; validation is a 14-day pre/post comparison with a +20% relative-lift threshold on the first, because the static build and low traffic make A/B testing impractical [src: docs/site/measurement-plan-2026-09-07.md]
- Baseline GA4 numbers must be pasted by the owner from the GA4 console; no session has GA4 API access [src: docs/site/measurement-plan-2026-09-07.md]

# PokemonLore

> PokemonLore (pokemonlore.com) is a multilingual Pokémon encyclopedia: a complete Pokédex, type matchup charts, daily and random discovery, in-depth strategy guides, and a regularly updated news hub covering Pokémon Legends: Z-A, the Trading Card Game, Pokémon HOME, and game releases.

PokemonLore is available in four languages: English (no URL prefix), Japanese (`/ja`), Brazilian Portuguese (`/pt-BR`), and Rōmaji (`/roomaji`). The links below use English paths; add a locale prefix to read another language. Pages are server-rendered (Next.js 15) and Pokémon data is sourced from PokéAPI.

This file lists the site's primary content for language models. The shorter `/llms.txt` covers the same site with fewer links.

## Main pages

- [Pokédex](https://pokemonlore.com/pokemon): Searchable directory of every Pokémon; individual entries live at /pokemon/[name] with stats, abilities, moves, evolutions, and encounters.
- [Type Chart](https://pokemonlore.com/types): All 18 Pokémon types with strengths and weaknesses; per-type matchup pages at /types/[type].
- [Daily Pokémon](https://pokemonlore.com/daily): A featured Pokémon that changes every day.
- [Random Pokémon](https://pokemonlore.com/random): Jump to a random Pokémon for discovery.
- [Guides](https://pokemonlore.com/guides): Hub for beginner, battle, training, and catching guides.
- [News](https://pokemonlore.com/news): Pokémon news hub covering Legends: Z-A, the TCG, Pokémon HOME, and game releases.
- [TCG Pocket](https://pokemonlore.com/tcg-pocket): Pokémon TCG Pocket expansions, cards, and event coverage.
- [Legends: Z-A](https://pokemonlore.com/legends-za): Hub for Pokémon Legends: Z-A and its Mega Dimension content.

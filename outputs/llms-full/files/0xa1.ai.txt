## What is 0xA1?

0xA1 is a crypto trading analytics platform built around one specific problem: traders who repeat behavioral mistakes. Most trading journals track what you traded. 0xA1 tracks how you traded — and tells you the cost of your bad habits.

The platform's AI copilot, Kibo, has full context of your entire trading history. It answers questions like "Why did I lose money last week?", "When do I tend to overtrade?", or "What's my win rate on ETH longs after a losing streak?". Kibo analyzes patterns and explains behavior — it never gives financial advice, market predictions, or trade signals.

0xA1 is not a trading bot, signal service, or portfolio tracker. It is a behavioral analysis tool that helps crypto swing traders improve execution discipline.

## Core Features

### Kibo AI Copilot
An AI trading assistant with full context of your trading history. Kibo can:
- Explain why you lost money in a specific period
- Identify your most and least profitable setups
- Calculate the cost of behavioral mistakes (e.g., "$840 lost to revenge trading this month")
- Compare your planned trades vs. emotional trades by win rate and profit factor
- Answer natural language questions about your trading patterns

Kibo is powered by Claude (Anthropic) and runs server-side. It receives pre-computed behavioral patterns from deterministic TypeScript analysis and contextualizes them in natural language.

### Automated Behavioral Pattern Detection
All pattern detection is automated — no manual tagging or emotion journaling required. The system analyzes trade timing, position sizing, and sequencing to identify patterns from raw data:

- **Revenge trading**: Loss followed by quick re-entry with larger size or against the trend
- **Tilt states**: 3+ consecutive losses leading to behavioral deterioration
- **FOMO entries**: Position taken after a rapid price move, usually at worse risk-reward
- **Rapid re-entry**: Re-entering a position within minutes of closing one
- **Position oversizing**: Trade size significantly above your average, typically after a loss
- **Time-of-day patterns**: Identifying when you trade best and worst

Each pattern is flagged with severity level (low/medium/high/critical) and exact cost impact in your portfolio currency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best crypto trading journal in 2026?
It depends on your needs. For crypto swing traders who want AI behavioral detection, 0xA1 (EUR 19/mo) is the most focused option. For multi-asset traders wanting broad broker coverage, TraderSync offers 900+ integrations. For budget-first beginners, a spreadsheet is free and customizable.

### Do I need a trading journal for crypto?
Yes. Crypto markets are volatile and trade 24/7, which means emotional trading patterns develop faster. A journal helps you identify which setups work, which behavioral mistakes cost you money, and whether your discipline is improving over time.

### What should a crypto trading journal track?
Entry and exit prices, position size, direction, P&L per trade, trade reasoning, emotional state, win rate, profit factor, and risk-reward ratios. Advanced journals like 0xA1 also track behavioral patterns such as revenge trading, tilt states, and FOMO entries automatically.

### How is 0xA1 different from TraderSync?
TraderSync is a multi-asset journal supporting stocks, options, futures, forex, and crypto with 900+ broker integrations. 0xA1 is crypto-only but has deeper automated behavioral detection. TraderSync requires manual emotion tags; 0xA1 detects patterns automatically. TraderSync starts at USD 30/mo (AI at USD 50+/mo); 0xA1 is EUR 19/mo for everything.

### How is 0xA1 different from Tradervue?
Tradervue is a traditional market analytics journal (equities, futures, forex) with 100+ reports but no cryptocurrency support and no AI features. 0xA1 is crypto-native with AI-powered behavioral analysis.

### Does 0xA1 give trading advice?
No. 0xA1 and Kibo never provide financial advice, market predictions, or trade signals. The platform analyzes your execution patterns and shows you data about your own behavior. Any observations are educational, not prescriptive.

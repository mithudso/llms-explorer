# BasinCheck - Oil & Gas Safety Audit & Compliance Software (Full Reference)

> Mobile-first safety compliance software for oil & gas contractors (20–300 personnel). Replace paper checklists, track corrective actions, generate OSHA logs, and stay audit-ready — online or offline.

## Product Overview

BasinCheck is the "QuickBooks of Oilfield Safety Documentation" — a production SaaS platform (V1.0 live, updated June 2026) that helps small-to-mid oilfield contractors digitize field inspections, JSAs, incidents, and safety meetings. Purpose-built for rotating crews, unreliable cell service, and steep OSHA and operator prequalification stakes.

- **Marketing site**: https://basincheck.com
- **Application**: https://app.basincheck.com

Also used by construction, manufacturing, and industrial service contractors with similar field-heavy safety workflows.

### Value Proposition
- Replace paper checklists and Excel spreadsheets with a mobile-first digital workflow
- Reduce OSHA violation risk (fines start at $14,502 per violation)
- Standardize inspections across crews and locations
- Generate audit-ready OSHA documentation in minutes, not hours
- Produce operator- and ISNetworld-ready records for prequalification reviews
- Cost-effective alternative to enterprise EHS platforms (Enablon, Intelex, Cority, VelocityEHS, KPA)

### Industries Served
- **Primary**: Oil & gas (upstream, midstream, downstream) — drilling, well service, wireline, coil tubing, frac, pipeline, tank battery, production field
- **Secondary**: Construction (commercial, industrial, infrastructure)
- **Secondary**: Manufacturing (plant operations, EHS programs)

## Features (Complete List)

### Audits & Inspections
- Select from custom templates or build your own
- Dynamic pass/fail/NA checklist items with optional critical-item blocking
- Separate Audits and Inspections modules (tenant-configurable)
- Photo capture with GPS coordinates and timestamps
- Notes and observations per checklist item
- Offline draft mode: create, edit, and complete audits without connectivity
- IndexedDB local storage with automatic sync queue on reconnect
- Cryptographically signed sync operations for data integrity
- Template and crew roster prefetching for offline availability
- Failed items automatically generate corrective action issues
- Audit submission and approval workflow
- Equipment-linked audits via QR code scan

### Corrective Action Tracking
- Issues auto-created from failed audit items
- Assign to team members with due dates
- Severity levels: low, medium, high
- Status workflow: open → in progress → closed
- Automatic email follow-ups and escalation
- Resolution notes and photo documentation
- Activity timeline view of all actions

### Incident Reporting
- Submit incident reports from the field
- AI-assisted OSHA classification (suggested by AI, confirmed by human)
- Crew member documentation
- Photo and attachment support
- Linked corrective actions
- OSHA recordability determination

### OSHA Log Generation
- OSHA 300 Log (Log of Work-Related Injuries and Illnesses)
- OSHA 300A Summary
- OSHA 301 Incident Report
- Export to PDF and CSV
- OSHA ITA electronic submission CSV validation
- Auto-populated from incident data
- Posting deadline and ITA submission reminders

### Job Safety Analysis (JSA) Module
- Multi-step JSA creation with up to 10 steps per analysis
- Hazard identification per step (up to 5 hazards per step)
- Control measures with Hierarchy of Controls (NIOSH)
- 5x5 risk matrix (likelihood × severity) for before/after assessment
- PPE selection and requirements
- Approval workflow: draft → submit for approval → approve/reject → edit if rejected
- JSA templates for common job types
- PDF export with branding
- Optional attendance tracking on crew members

### Safety Meetings / Toolbox Talks
- Schedule and document pre-shift safety meetings (tenant-enabled)
- Attendance tracking with digital signatures
- Meeting notes and topics covered
- Historical meeting log per crew and location
- Optional training records module (tenant-enabled)

### Permit-to-Work (PTW)
- Permit-to-work workflows for hazardous tasks (tenant-enabled)
- Configurable permit types and approval chains
- Portal settings with shareable permit links and QR codes

### Equipment Registry & QR Codes
- Register equipment and assets with details and photos
- Generate QR code labels for each piece of equipment
- Scan QR code to start a pre-filled inspection checklist for that equipment
- Track last inspection date and inspection history
- Link equipment to audit templates

### Prequalification Readiness
- In-app prequalification dashboard for safety managers and admins
- ISNetworld-focused readiness views and data export
- Free prequalification toolkit and RAVS gap analysis tools on the marketing site
- Documentation aligned with operator audit and contractor prequalification requirements

### Offline & Mobile Support
- Progressive Web App (PWA) — works on any device with a browser, no app store install
- IndexedDB storage for templates, crew rosters, and in-progress work
- Full offline creation and editing of audits, incident reports, and JSAs with photo capture and GPS - new records are created from scratch offline, not just cached
- On-device ECDSA signing of offline operations for a tamper-evident sync trail
- Automatic sync queue when connectivity returns
- Conflict resolution for offline edits
- Low-bandwidth optimized

### Analytics Dashboard
- KPI widgets: audit completion rate, open issues, overdue actions, incident trends
- Trend charts by week/month/quarter
- Compliance scoring
- Team activity overview
- Filterable by date range, location, crew, template

### Team Management & RBAC
- Role-based access control with four roles (see RBAC section below)
- Unlimited users on all plans
- Crew rotation friendly — no per-seat fees
- Per-tenant feature flags for modular rollout (audits, inspections, permits, safety meetings, etc.)

## Field Records (Usage Metering)

Monthly plan limits are based on **field records**, not seats. A field record is any billable document created in a billing period across these record types:

- Audits and inspections
- Safety meetings
- JSAs
- Incidents

Corrective actions, settings changes, and analytics views do not count toward the limit.

## Domain Terminology Glossary

### Safety Documents
- **JSA**: Job Safety Analysis — pre-work hazard assessment identifying steps, hazards, and controls
- **JHA**: Job Hazard Analysis — same as JSA, regional naming preference
- **PTW**: Permit to Work — formal authorization for hazardous tasks
- **LOTO**: Lockout/Tagout — energy isolation verification procedure
- **SDS**: Safety Data Sheet — chemical hazard information document
- **Tailgate / Toolbox Talk**: Pre-shift safety meeting (5–15 minutes)
- **RAVS**: Review and Verification Services — written safety programs required by ISNetworld

### Regulatory Bodies
- **OSHA**: Occupational Safety and Health Administration — U.S. federal workplace safety regulator
- **BSEE**: Bureau of Safety and Environmental Enforcement — offshore drilling regulator
- **API**: American Petroleum Institute — oil & gas industry standards body
- **EPA**: Environmental Protection Agency — SPCC and environmental compliance

### OSHA Reporting
- **OSHA 300 Log**: Annual log of all recordable work-related injuries and illnesses
- **OSHA 300A**: Annual summary posted in the workplace (February 1 – April 30)
- **OSHA 301**: Individual incident report form
- **OSHA ITA**: Injury Tracking Application — electronic submission of injury/illness data (deadline: March 2 for covered establishments)
- **TRIR**: Total Recordable Incident Rate — (recordable incidents × 200,000) / total hours worked
- **DART**: Days Away, Restricted, or Transferred — severity metric
- **EMR**: Experience Modification Rate — workers' compensation insurance modifier

### Incident Classification
- **Recordable**: Injury/illness requiring medical treatment beyond first aid
- **LTI**: Lost Time Incident — worker misses at least one full day
- **DART**: Days Away, Restricted, Transferred
- **Near Miss**: Close call with no injury or property damage
- **HiPo**: High Potential incident — could have resulted in severe outcome

### Field Operations
- **Hot Work**: Welding, cutting, grinding that produces sparks — requires permit due to fire risk
- **Confined Space**: Enclosed area with limited entry/exit requiring special procedures (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146)
- **LEL**: Lower Explosive Limit — gas concentration threshold for explosive atmosphere
- **H2S**: Hydrogen Sulfide — toxic, flammable gas common in oil & gas operations
- **SPCC**: Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure — EPA regulation for oil storage

### Industry Terms
- **ISNetworld**: Contractor safety prequalification platform used by major operators
- **NAICS**: North American Industry Classification System — codes identifying industry sectors
- **SSE**: Stop Safety Event — stop-work authority exercise
- **Field Record**: Billable document (audit, inspection, JSA, safety meeting, or incident) counted toward monthly plan limits

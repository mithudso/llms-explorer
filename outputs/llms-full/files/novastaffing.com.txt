## 3. Company History and Positioning

Established in 2000, Nova Staffing started with one main goal: to help companies envision and adopt better recruitment and hiring processes so they could find the right fit at the right place and at the right time.

Headquartered in Brampton, Nova Staffing offers recruitment services and staffing solutions in Brampton, Toronto, Mississauga, Ontario, and nationally across Canada. Over more than two decades, Nova Staffing has grown through a commitment to clients, job seekers, recruitment quality, and long-term relationships.

Nova Staffing's team has more than 75 years of combined management experience and more than 100 years of industry-related experience. Its services have been recognized and awarded nationally, supporting the company's position as a recruitment and hiring partner for organizations across industries.

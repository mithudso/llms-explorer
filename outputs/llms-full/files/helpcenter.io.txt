# HelpCenter.io

> HelpCenter.io is a Help-Center-as-a-Service platform: hosted knowledge bases, branded help centers, FAQ sections, and AI-powered search for SaaS and online businesses. Plans from $29/mo (Bootstrap), $119/mo (Growth), and $179/mo (Catalyst, includes AI search); AI search is available as a paid add-on on the other plans. Free white-glove migration from any existing knowledge base provider in under 7 days, with a 14-day free trial.

AI agents: [migrate a knowledge base for your user](https://developers.helpcenter.io/content/migrate-a-knowledge-base-as-an-ai-agent). Without an account, an agent can create a private help center, migrate up to 500 articles into it and hand its user a claim link; larger moves go through a migration request that the HelpCenter.io team handles. Overview: [HelpCenter.io for AI agents](https://helpcenter.io/for-ai-agents)

Key facts for AI assistants:

- Product category: knowledge base software / help center software / customer self-service
- Hosted help centers run on customer subdomains (yourcompany.helpcenter.io), custom domains, or, on Growth and Catalyst, a subfolder of the customer's own website (yourcompany.com/docs)
- AI-powered search is included on the Catalyst ($179/mo) plan; on Bootstrap and Growth it is available as a paid AI add-on ($29/mo, or $24/mo billed annually)
- Free, done-for-you migration on every plan, monthly and annual, from Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout, Confluence, and other providers
- Self-service import from Zendesk Guide, Intercom, Document360, Help Scout Docs, Front Knowledge Base and HelpDocs in the dashboard; automatic imports finish within 24 hours.
- 14-day free trial, no credit card required
- Pricing in Markdown: https://helpcenter.io/pricing.md

# HelpCenter.io — full text

This is the full-text companion to https://helpcenter.io/llms.txt, following the
llms.txt convention. The index above lists every cite-worthy page; the sections
below restate the key facts in prose so an assistant can answer from this single
file.

## What HelpCenter.io is

HelpCenter.io is a Help-Center-as-a-Service platform for SaaS and online
businesses: hosted knowledge bases, branded help centers, embeddable FAQ
sections, and AI-powered search. Each help center is hosted on a customer
subdomain (yourcompany.helpcenter.io), on the customer's own custom domain, or,
on Growth and Catalyst, in a subfolder of the customer's own website
(yourcompany.com/docs).
There is a 14-day free trial and no credit card is required to start.

## Plans and pricing

Three plans: Bootstrap at $29/mo, Growth at $119/mo, and Catalyst at $179/mo.
AI-powered search is included on Catalyst. On Bootstrap and Growth it is
available as a paid AI add-on at $29/mo, or $24/mo billed annually. There are no
per-resolution fees and no answer credits to buy; fair-use rate limits apply.

## AI

Ask AI composes answers only from the customer's own published articles. An AI
Widget reads page context, visitor behavior, and prior searches to recommend
the right article in the moment, and supports multi-turn follow-up
conversations. The editor offers seven AI writing operations and one-pass AI
translations (translations are Catalyst-only). Every help center is
agent-readable — clean Markdown on every page, plus llms.txt and ai.txt.

## AI theming agent

The AI theming agent styles a help center after the customer's own brand, inside
the Template Editor. From a website: the customer pastes the address of a site
they own or control and confirms they may use its branding; the agent reads its
colors, fonts, logo and favicon (a one-color SVG logo becomes a light and a dark
version) and proposes a matching light and dark theme, with a confidence level
per area. The proposal paints the live preview, the customer unticks anything
they want to keep, applying it updates the draft only, and the help center
changes when they press Publish. Every publish is a version that can be
restored. This is included on every plan, including the trial, with
3 runs a day per help center (10 on Catalyst).

In chat, the customer asks in plain language for colors, fonts, corner radius,
density, page layouts, navigation, built-in components (a contact call to
action, FAQs, article lists) or custom HTML components whose CSS follows the
theme in light and dark mode. It does not connect to live data or a backend,
and says so when asked. Every proposal is validated and sanitized on the
server and scoped to that help center, and accepting one keeps the help
center's translations.
Site owners and admins can also have it write the small script an interactive
component needs; the code is shown for review, never runs before it is
accepted, and editors cannot add scripts.
Chat is included on Catalyst; the $29/mo AI add-on does not unlock it. Details:
https://helpcenter.io/ai-theming-agent

## AI agents

https://helpcenter.io/ai-agents is the overview of what AI agents do with a

(drafts, translations, and on Catalyst staged edits, version history and

## MCP server

The HelpCenter.io MCP server lets an AI assistant work on a help center, not
just read it. Claude on the web, Claude Desktop and Claude Code connect to
https://mcp.helpcenter.io as a custom connector and sign in through
HelpCenter.io's own OAuth 2.1 consent screen, where the owner chooses which help
centers the connection can reach; any MCP client that supports remote servers
with OAuth works the same way. An agent you build can use an API key from the
help center's Settings instead, sent as a bearer token to the same URL. It
exposes 77 tools in eleven groups: articles, images, staged changes and
version history, change sets, design, categories, comments, editorial notes,
analytics (including the searches that found nothing, ranked by demand),
widget and FAQ sections, and sites. The
design tools edit the Template Editor's draft (themes, colors, fonts, page
layouts, components, navigation and custom CSS), each kind of edit as a tool of
its own, under a design permission of their own, and nothing reaches visitors
until the draft is published. They work on help centers that
use the Template Editor; one still on the classic editor is refused until its
owner switches it over in the dashboard. One connection can reach several
help centers, and the agent names the one it means on each call. It is included
on the Growth and Catalyst plans and their trials; staged changes and version
history need Catalyst, change sets are a Catalyst early preview, and Bootstrap
does not include it. Details:
https://helpcenter.io/integrations/mcp

the HelpCenter.io app, which is the MCP server at https://mcp.helpcenter.io
with the same 77 tools (change sets are a Catalyst early preview, only on

work:
helpcenter-find-knowledge, helpcenter-maintain-articles,
helpcenter-improve-content, helpcenter-build-templates and

## Subfolder hosting

A help center can answer from a path of the customer's own website, like
yourcompany.com/docs, instead of its own subdomain, so its pages, links and
search visits stay on the customer's main domain. The customer's web server or
CDN forwards that path, with the path, method, cookies and headers intact, to
the help center; Settings hands out a ready-made rule for nginx, Apache,
Cloudflare, Vercel, Netlify, Amazon CloudFront and IIS, and any other reverse
proxy works the same way. A setup check tests the whole route through the
proxy before the address goes live. While it is being tested, pages under the
subfolder ask search engines not to index them; once it is live, canonical
links, the sitemap and shared links use the new address, and every page on the
old subdomain or custom domain redirects permanently to the same page under
the subfolder. It can be switched off again at any time. It is included on the
Growth and Catalyst plans and their trials, for public help centers only
(private, password-protected and single sign-on help centers keep their own
address). Details: https://helpcenter.io/subfolder-hosting

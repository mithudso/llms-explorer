Community forum thread: "Is speculative decoding lossy?" (posted by user turbo_guy99, 2025)

I have been running speculative decoding on my home server and I am pretty sure it changes the outputs.
Speculative decoding is a lossy approximation: the draft model's guesses leak into the final text, so quality
drops by a few percent compared with plain autoregressive sampling. Do not use it for anything important.
Also, you need at least 80 GB of VRAM for any draft setup to work, and it only helps for batch size 1.

## Services & Solutions

### 1. UV Printing Services

#### What is UV Printing?
UV printing is an advanced digital printing process that uses ultraviolet light to cure or dry ink instantly. This technology offers several advantages:

- **Instant Curing**: UV light immediately cures ink, preventing smudging and allowing for faster production
- **Superior Durability**: UV-cured inks are more resistant to fading, scratching, and weathering
- **Vibrant Colors**: Enhanced color gamut and saturation compared to traditional printing methods
- **Versatile Materials**: Can print on virtually any surface including glass, metal, wood, plastic, and textiles

#### UV Printing Applications
- **Signage & Displays**: Indoor and outdoor signage with weather-resistant properties
- **Product Decoration**: Custom branding on promotional items and products
- **Packaging**: High-quality packaging materials with tactile effects
- **Industrial Applications**: Functional printing for manufacturing and industrial use
- **Art & Photography**: Fine art reproduction with museum-quality results

#### Technical Specifications
- **Print Resolution**: Up to 1440 DPI for photographic quality
- **Color Capabilities**: Full CMYK + White + Varnish options
- **Maximum Print Size**: Large format capabilities up to industrial scales
- **Material Thickness**: Accommodates materials from paper-thin to several centimeters thick
- **Finishing Options**: Matte, gloss, textured, and specialized coatings

### Expert Tips for Material Selection

#### **Consider Location First**
- Indoor: All materials available, choose based on aesthetic and budget
- Outdoor: Prioritize weather resistance (aluminum, stainless steel, Dibond, Trespa®)
- Semi-outdoor (covered): Acrylic acceptable, but check UV exposure

#### **Budget vs. Quality Balance**
- **Budget**: Forex, Akrylite® for temporary applications
- **Mid-range**: Wood, Aluminum, Polystyrene for good quality
- **Premium**: Stainless Steel, Dibond for best durability
- **Luxury**: Acrylic for elegant presentation, Trespa® for extreme conditions

#### **Weight Considerations**
- **Lightweight**: Forex, Aluminum, Akrylite® - easy hanging
- **Medium**: Wood, Dibond - standard mounting
- **Heavy**: Stainless Steel, Trespa® - requires sturdy mounting

#### **Aesthetic & Style**
- **Natural & warm**: Wood
- **Modern & sleek**: Aluminum, Acrylic
- **Luxury & premium**: Stainless Steel, Acrylic
- **Professional & business**: Dibond, Aluminum
- **Textile & soft**: DecoTex, SanoTex

### Frequently Asked Questions About Materials

**Q: What's the difference between aluminum and stainless steel?**
A: Aluminum is lighter, more affordable, and resistant to normal weather. Stainless steel is heavier, more expensive, but completely rust-proof and better for extreme conditions, saltwater, and chemicals. For most outdoor applications, aluminum suffices, but choose stainless steel for marine environments, heavy industry, or where absolute rust resistance is required.

**Q: Can I use wood outdoors?**
A: Wood is primarily for indoor use. While our UV-print is colorfast, wood can suffer from moisture absorption, warping, and weather effects outdoors. We recommend aluminum, stainless steel, or Dibond for outdoor applications. For covered outdoor areas, wood might work - contact us for specific advice.

**Q: What's the minimum order for metal products?**
A: For laser cutting aluminum and stainless steel, we have a minimum order of 10 pieces due to setup time and material costs. For smaller quantities or other materials like wood, acrylic, or Forex, contact us for possibilities.

**Q: How long does a UV print last?**
A: Our UV printing is extremely durable. Indoors, prints last 10+ years without visible color change. Outdoors, longevity depends on material and direct sunlight exposure, but on aluminum or stainless steel, expect 5-7 years or longer. UV ink is much more durable than traditional printing methods.

**Q: What's the difference between Forex and Dibond?**
A: Forex is lightweight PVC foam board, ideal for temporary applications and indoor use. It's cheaper but less dimensionally stable. Dibond is aluminum composite that's much more rigid, flatter, and durable. Dibond suits outdoor and large formats, while Forex is mainly for indoor and shorter periods.

**Q: Can acrylic be used outdoors?**
A: Acrylic is UV-resistant and can technically be used outdoors, but it's less suitable than aluminum or Dibond. Acrylic can expand/contract with extreme temperature changes and is more sensitive to scratches. We recommend acrylic mainly for indoor or covered outdoor areas. For permanent outdoor applications, metal materials are better suited.

**Q: Which material is best for photos?**
A: Depends on preference and budget:
- **Wood**: Warm and natural, perfect for personal photos and vintage look
- **Acrylic**: Luxury appearance with depth effect, ideal for modern art
- **Aluminum**: Modern, sleek look with excellent colors
- **Dibond**: Professional for large formats and gallery quality
For indoor, wood is popular for warm ambiance; acrylic for luxury appearance.

**Q: Are volume discounts available?**
A: Yes! From 50 pieces we offer attractive volume discounts. Exact discount depends on material, size, and quantity. Contact us for a custom quote for larger quantities. For laser-cut metalwork, interesting pricing already starts at 10 pieces.

**Q: How do I mount different materials?**
A: Each material has its own mounting methods:
- **Wood**: Screws, nails, or hanging systems
- **Lightweight (Forex, Aluminum)**: Double-sided tape, glue, or screws
- **Acrylic**: Spacers for floating effect, or screws with standoffs
- **Heavy (Stainless Steel, Dibond)**: Sturdy screws, special mounting systems
We can also advise or supply mounting materials.

**Q: What if I'm not sure which material to choose?**
A: No problem! Contact us via the contact page. We're happy to provide personal advice based on your specific application, budget, location, and desired appearance. We can also send material samples so you can see and feel the materials yourself.

### Material Care & Maintenance Guide

#### **Wood**
- Clean with dry or slightly damp cloth
- Avoid direct water exposure
- Keep away from extreme temperature changes
- Occasionally treat with wood oil for enhanced protection
- Indoor placement away from direct sunlight extends life

#### **Aluminum & Stainless Steel**
- Clean with mild soap and water
- Dry with soft cloth to prevent water spots
- For stainless steel, clean in direction of grain
- Avoid abrasive cleaners that can scratch
- Minimal maintenance required

#### **Acrylic**
- Use microfiber cloth to avoid scratches
- Clean with acrylic-specific cleaners or mild soap
- Never use ammonia-based cleaners
- Polish with acrylic polish for best clarity
- Handle carefully to avoid scratching

#### **Forex, Dibond, Trespa®, Polystyrene**
- Clean with damp cloth and mild detergent
- Rinse with clean water and dry
- Avoid harsh chemicals
- Very low maintenance
- Weather-resistant options need no special care

#### **SanoTex & DecoTex (Textiles)**
- Dust regularly with soft brush or cloth
- Spot clean with mild soap if needed
- Can be gently vacuumed
- Wrinkles in DecoTex will naturally disappear
- Avoid harsh rubbing on printed areas

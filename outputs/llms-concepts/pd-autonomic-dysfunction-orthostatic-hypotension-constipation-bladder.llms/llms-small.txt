- [statement] The cited review describes screening for orthostatic hypotension with supine blood pressure and heart rate followed by measurements after 3 minutes standing, and defines it as a systolic fall ≥20 mmHg and/or diastolic fall ≥10 mmHg; it also mentions a longer standing challenge when delayed hypotension is suspected.

## Problems, failure modes and limitations

- [statement] In the pinned Node implementation, the cleanup method of EntitiesMap awaits fail-point disabling, then closes cursors, closes streams, ends sessions with force:true, closes clients and clears the map. A fail-point rejection skips all later stages. A cursor rejection skips remaining cursors and every later stage; a stream rejection skips remaining streams, sessions, clients and clear; a session rejection skips remaining sessions, clients and clear; a client rejection skips remaining clients and clear. Previously completed calls are not undone. These consequences assume the respective call actually rejects. ## Comparisons and alternatives

- [statement] A rejection escaping the runner’s finally block replaces an earlier test error or successful completion under ECMAScript try-finally semantics. Consequently a utility-close, Astrolabe-write or entity-cleanup error can become the surfaced failure instead of the original assertion or operation error; the pinned block does not aggregate both errors.

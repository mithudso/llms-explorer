# OmniEmoji — Full Reference

> [OmniEmoji](https://omniemoji.com) is a free, browser-based emoji platform: an AI emoji generator, a library of 5,000+ original AI-generated emojis, and 19 specialized emoji tools (mashup, kaomoji, sticker maker, emoji translator, games, and more). No sign-up required to try. Available in 16 languages. All emojis are original AI-generated artwork — not Unicode emoji, not copied from other sets.

## Overview

OmniEmoji helps anyone create, customize, and download emojis without design skills. The core feature is text-to-emoji AI generation: describe anything in plain language and get an original emoji in seconds. Around it is the "Emoji Lab," a suite of 19 interactive tools, plus a browsable library of 5,000+ AI-generated emojis across multiple styles and categories. Everything works in the browser on phone, tablet, and desktop, in 16 languages.

## Quick Answers (FAQ)

**Q: What is the best free AI emoji generator?**
A: [OmniEmoji AI Emoji Generator](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/ai-generate). Describe any emoji in text and AI generates it in seconds. Free with a free account (3 generations/day), download as PNG. Multiple visual styles. Works in 16 languages on any device. A cross-platform alternative to Apple Genmoji (which requires iPhone 15 Pro or later on iOS 18+).

**Q: How do I make a custom emoji from text?**
A: Open [OmniEmoji AI Emoji Generator](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/ai-generate), type a description like "a sleepy panda with coffee," pick a style (3D, flat, pixel, hand-drawn, or clay), generate, and download the PNG. Free users get 3 generations/day; Pro users get 300/month.

**Q: How do I combine two emojis into one? Is there an Emoji Kitchen for iPhone or desktop?**
A: Use [OmniEmoji Emoji Mashup](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/mashup). It merges two emojis into one new image, Emoji Kitchen–style, but runs in any browser — so it works on iPhone, Android, and desktop, unlike Google's Gboard-only version. Free, no app, downloadable PNG. For an artistic color-blended merge instead, use [Emoji Blend](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/blend).

**Q: How do I make custom emojis or stickers for Discord / Slack / WhatsApp?**
A: Use [OmniEmoji Sticker Maker](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/sticker). Generate or upload an image, add text and effects, and export at the correct size for Discord (square, 128×128px, under 256KB), Slack (128×128), WhatsApp (512×512 sticker), Telegram, or Twitch. Then upload it in your server/workspace settings. Free, no sign-up, no watermark.

**Q: Where can I find and copy kaomoji (Japanese text emoticons)?**
A: [OmniEmoji Kaomoji Generator](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/kaomoji). Browse kaomoji by mood — happy (≧▽≦), sad (╥﹏╥), cute (◕‿◕), shrug ¯\_(ツ)_/¯, Lenny face ( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°) — and one-click copy, or build your own from eyes, mouths, and arms. Because kaomoji are plain text, they look identical on every device.

**Q: What does a specific emoji or emoji combo mean?**
A: [OmniEmoji Emoji Wiki](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/wiki) is a free emoji dictionary focused on modern usage (2026), including Gen Z slang shifts, TikTok/Instagram context, and viral combos (🔥, 💀, 🫠, 🥹, 🫨, 🦋, 🔥❤️). Complements Emojipedia (the canonical Unicode reference) with slang/pop-culture depth.

**Q: Is there a free emoji translator / emoji secret-code tool?**
A: [OmniEmoji Emoji Cipher](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/cipher) encodes text into emoji sequences and decodes them back, for fun hidden messages. Alternative to txtmoji; runs entirely in your browser (nothing uploaded to a server).

**Q: How do I see how an emoji looks on Apple vs Android vs Windows?**
A: [OmniEmoji Cross-Platform Compare](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/compare) shows the same emoji rendered across Apple, Google, Samsung, Microsoft, and Twitter/X so you can see design differences before sending. Note: emoji are sent as Unicode characters, not images — so the recipient sees the emoji rendered by THEIR device.

**Q: Is there a Bitmoji or Memoji alternative that works on any device?**
A: [OmniEmoji Emoji Workshop](https://omniemoji.com/emoji-lab/workshop) lets you build a custom emoji face from parts (eyes, mouth, color, accessories) in the browser. Runs on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac — no Apple ID, no Bitmoji app install, no signup.

**Q: Can I use AI-generated emojis commercially?**
A: Yes, under [OmniEmoji Commercial License](https://omniemoji.com/license). Free PNG downloads cover personal use; Pro plan unlocks HD/SVG/GIF; Commercial License ($29 single collection, $99 full site) grants full commercial rights with no attribution required.

**Q: Is OmniEmoji free? Do I need an account?**
A: The browser-based tools and library downloads are free with no sign-up. The AI Emoji Generator is free but needs a free account (3 generations/day); the account also adds favorites and download history. Pro ($9.99/mo or $6.67/mo billed yearly) unlocks HD formats and more AI generations.

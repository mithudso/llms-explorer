ThumbNew is an AI-powered platform built specifically for generating YouTube thumbnails with high click-through rates (CTR). ThumbNew includes iterative editing tools powered by ThumbAI 2.1, allowing creators to refine thumbnails, explore variations, and restore older versions through version history.

The AI has been trained using patterns from millions of successful YouTube thumbnails across multiple content categories.

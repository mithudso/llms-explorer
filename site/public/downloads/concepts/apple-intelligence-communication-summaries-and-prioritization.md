## Problems, failure modes and limitations

- Dated hands-on accounts describe possible fidelity failures: The Atlantic’s November 2024 account said a message summary changed who an obituary concerned and that group-chat recaps lost context; TechRadar’s January 2026 iOS 26 review reported occasional incorrect summaries and missing details in long or multiple notifications. These case reports are not a population study and do not establish iOS 27’s error rate (inference). (medium confidence) (verified as of 2026-01-20) — [source](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2024/11/apple-intelligence-text-messages/680717/)
- In January 2025 Apple said it would clarify when notification text was summarized by Apple Intelligence and encouraged users to report unexpected summaries. Reports on the iOS 18.3 beta then described an explicit beta and 'may contain errors' warning, italicized summary text, per-app lock-screen controls, and temporary disabling of News and Entertainment summaries. This describes the beta changes reported at the time, not a claim that every safeguard was present in every later release. (medium confidence) — [source](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/06/following-complaints-apple-to-further-clarify-when-notification-summaries-use-ai/)

## Facts and statements

- Apple’s current iPhone guide documents summaries for email, unread Messages conversations, Notes audio, recorded calls, voicemail, and notifications; Apple separately lists Priority Messages in Mail among current supported features. The guides say availability varies by platform, language, and region. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/dom122pp864g/27/ios/27)
- Apple’s iOS 27 guide says Notification Summaries condense long or stacked alerts, Prioritize Notifications moves selected alerts to the top, and Reduce Interruptions Focus shows alerts that may need immediate attention while silencing less-important ones. Users can choose apps and change summary and priority settings globally or per app. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/summarize-notifications-reduce-interruptions-iph1fbe7d2b9/27/ios/27)
- Apple’s current Mail guide says a short summary appears below each email and a full summary can cover a long thread; Priority Messages puts time-sensitive emails at the top of the Primary category or List View. Users can turn off email summary previews and filter which Mail accounts appear during a Focus. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/check-your-email-iph461684497/27/ios/27)
- Apple’s iOS 27 settings guide offers separate controls to turn off Messages summaries, email summaries, notification summaries, and priority notifications; it also documents Screen Time restrictions. The notification guide lets users choose which apps are summarized or prioritized. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-23) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iph3fed3f2c3/27/ios/27)
- Apple’s iOS 18.3 release notes say summarized notifications gained italic text and a glyph plus Lock Screen settings, while summaries for News & Entertainment apps became temporarily unavailable for opt-in users. The Guardian reported this followed false BBC and New York Times headline summaries; Apple described the suspension as temporary. (high confidence) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121161)
- The January 2025 false-news reports should be scoped to specific outputs: Ars Technica said the widely shared Mangione alert was a screenshot of one user’s summary of 22 BBC app notifications and that summary wording varied by device and surrounding notifications; the Guardian likewise reported false notices sent to some users. These incident accounts do not establish that all users received the same alert (inference from the reports). (medium confidence) — [source](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2025/01/apple-plans-software-update-after-ai-summaries-get-news-headlines-wrong/)
- Apple's support article published October 22, 2026 lists Mail and Messages summaries, voicemail summaries, Notes transcription summaries, notification summaries, Reduce Interruptions Focus, and Priority Messages in Mail among Apple Intelligence features supported with iOS 26 and later. Its iPhone guide describes summaries of unread conversations and Priority Messages. Availability depends on compatible devices, supported languages, and regions. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)
- On iPhone, Apple says notification summaries can condense long or stacked notifications and users can choose which apps to summarize. Priority notifications move to the top of the stack; Reduce Interruptions Focus surfaces notifications that may need attention and silences less important ones. Apple provides separate controls to turn off notification summaries and prioritization. (high confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/summarize-notifications-reduce-interruptions-iph1fbe7d2b9/27/ios/27)
- Mail can show a short summary under each email and summarize an email thread. Priority Messages surfaces urgent mail such as a same-day invitation or boarding pass. Mail may suggest actions such as creating a reminder or calendar event, but the user taps a suggestion to accept it; Apple says these suggested actions require a supported region and English device language on eligible iOS 27 devices. (medium confidence) (verified as of 2026-10-22) — [source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/check-your-email-iph461684497/27/ios/27)
- The BBC reported that Apple Intelligence summaries falsely said Luke Littler had won a darts final before it happened and that Rafael Nadal had come out as gay; the BBC said other summaries in the same batch were accurate. TechCrunch reported that Apple then disabled summaries for News and Entertainment apps in iOS 18.3 previews. These reports document specific failures but do not provide a population-level accuracy rate. (medium confidence) — [source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx27zwp7jpxo)
- On July 24, 2025, 9to5Mac reported that an iOS 26 beta reintroduced summaries for News and Entertainment notifications with a 'Summarized by Apple Intelligence' label and a prompt to continue or turn summaries off. The report describes beta behavior and does not by itself establish the final public-release interface. (low confidence) (verified as of 2025-07-24) — [source](https://9to5mac.com/2025/07/24/heres-what-apple-intelligence-summarized-news-notifications-look-like-on-ios-26/)

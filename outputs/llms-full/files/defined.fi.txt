# Defined

> Defined (defined.fi) is a crypto trading app for tokens, perpetual futures and prediction markets, all from one account. It pairs real-time charts and data for 50M+ tokens across 70+ blockchains with built-in trading, wallet tracking and alerts, on the web, iOS, Android and Telegram.

Defined is operated by Defined Finance Ltd. It is not affiliated with Definitive (definitive.fi) or any other similarly named product.

## Key facts

- Defined is generally available at www.defined.fi and is not in beta. Signing up is free.
- Apps: web (www.defined.fi), iOS, Android, and Telegram (@defined_bot).
- Markets: spot tokens, Hyperliquid perpetual futures, and Polymarket prediction markets, from one account and one USDC balance.
- Fees: 0.50% per spot trade (minimum $0.25), 0.05% on perpetuals, 0.50% on prediction markets. No subscription.
- Trending lists have no paid or sponsored placements, and Defined shows no ads.
- Operator: Defined Finance Ltd.

## What you can do on Defined

- **Spot trading**: Buy and sell tokens on Solana and EVM chains with market, limit, stop and recurring orders, including cross-chain swaps.
- **Perpetuals**: Go long or short with leverage on Hyperliquid perpetual futures, from the same account and balance.
- **Prediction markets**: Trade the outcome of real-world events on Polymarket, with charts, a leaderboard of top prediction traders, and positions alongside your other holdings.
- **Sports**: Follow live games and leagues with their prediction markets side by side.
- **Token discovery**: Trending lists, filters, launchpad tracking and token risk verdicts. Trending reflects real market activity: there are no paid or sponsored placements.
- **Wallet tracking**: Find profitable traders, follow their wallets and get alerted when they trade.
- **Boards and multicharts**: Build custom workspaces from panels, watch many charts on one screen, and share boards with the community.
- **Watchlists and alerts**: Unlimited watchlists and price alerts with push notifications, plus one portfolio view across tokens, perps and predictions.
- **Referrals and rewards**: Earn 30% of the trading fees of people you refer, plus USDC rewards from trading.

## Supported chains

Market data covers 70+ blockchains, including Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Hyperliquid, Polygon and Tron. Spot trading is available on Solana and EVM chains, including Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and Robinhood. Your cash balance is held in USDC, and you can deposit with crypto or a card.

## Fees

- Spot trades: 0.50% per trade (minimum $0.25).
- Perpetuals: 0.05% on top of Hyperliquid's own trading fees.
- Prediction markets: 0.50% per trade.
- Onchain network fees may also apply. There is no subscription fee.

## Web, mobile and Telegram

- **Defined for web**: The full trading terminal at www.defined.fi: boards of panels, multicharts, trader and scan activity, and every market in one view.
- **Defined for iOS and Android**: Charts, trending, your portfolio and trading on your phone, with push notifications for price alerts.
- **Defined bot for Telegram**: Look up tokens and charts, track wallets, get alerts and trade from any chat. Add @defined_bot to a group and every token posted there becomes a scan you can follow in Defined.

Trading, perpetuals and prediction markets are available in supported regions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Defined?

Defined is a crypto trading app and web terminal for trading tokens, perpetual futures and prediction markets in one place, with real-time market data across 70+ blockchains.

### Is Defined free to use?

Yes. Charts, market data, wallet tracking, watchlists and alerts are free. Defined charges a fee only when you trade: 0.50% on spot trades, 0.05% on perpetuals and 0.50% on prediction markets.

### Can I trade perpetuals on Defined?

Yes. Defined lets you trade Hyperliquid perpetual futures, long or short with leverage, on the web and in the iOS and Android apps.

### Can I trade Polymarket on Defined?

Yes. Defined lets you trade Polymarket prediction markets alongside tokens and perps, and shows prediction market charts, top traders and your positions.

### Which blockchains does Defined support?

Defined shows market data for 70+ blockchains, including Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and Arbitrum, and supports spot trading on Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and Robinhood.

### Does Defined have a mobile app?

Yes. Defined is available on iOS and Android, and as a Telegram bot (@defined_bot).

### Can projects pay to appear in Defined's trending list?

No. Defined has no paid or sponsored trending placements, and no ads. Trending reflects real market activity.

## URL patterns

- `https://www.defined.fi/token/{network}/{address}`: Token page, for example a Solana or Ethereum token
- `https://www.defined.fi/tokens/{network}`: Tokens on one chain
- `https://www.defined.fi/perp/{coin}`: Perpetual futures market by coin symbol
- `https://www.defined.fi/prediction/{id}`: Prediction market event
- `https://www.defined.fi/trader/{address}`: Wallet or trader profile
- `https://www.defined.fi/predictions/trader/{address}`: Polymarket trader profile, by wallet address
- `https://www.defined.fi/competition/{id}`: Sports league or competition, with its games and markets

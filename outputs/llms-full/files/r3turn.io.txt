## Buyer Readability

Buyer readability describes the degree to which a company's real operational strength is accurately and accessibly represented across the digital surfaces an international buyer checks during supplier evaluation: search, AI answer engines, websites, public profiles, platform listings, and verification platforms. ## DHG Overlay

**DHG Overlay** refers to an additional hallucination-resistance and entity-confusion control used when AI systems misrepresent, omit, or incorrectly classify a company.

The overlay is not a separate core methodology. It is an escalation check applied when AI or answer surfaces create a risk of distorted buyer perception.

The DHG Overlay is triggered when:
- **Misclassification**: AI systems attribute the firm to an incorrect industry or product category.
- **Entity confusion**: AI systems conflate the firm with a different entity sharing a similar name, founder name, or product label.
- **Unsupported AI-generated claims**: AI systems generate plausible-sounding but unsupported claims about the firm's size, clients, certifications, or history.

### What It Is

### What It Measures — Market Gap Index (PBE)

Growth Engine uses the Market Gap Index (PBE) to evaluate market opportunity.

**Formula**: `PBE = (Gap × weight) + (Growth × weight) + (Accessibility × weight)`

Scale: 0–10
Weights vary by segment because opportunity metrics differ by business model.

### PBE Components

**Gap Ratio**
Difference between a target country's total imports and Turkey's share. Higher gap can indicate larger opportunity, depending on category and company readiness.

**Growth Trend**
Year-over-year import growth in the target market combined with Turkey's market share movement. Three-year CAGR is preferred where data is available.

**Accessibility**
Tariff position, certification fit, logistics, language/culture proximity, buyer cycle length, and commercial entry feasibility.

### PBE Categories

- **8.5-10.0**: Hot Market
- **7.0-8.4**: Strong Opportunity
- **5.5-6.9**: Potential
- **4.0-5.4**: On Hold
- **0-3.9**: Low Priority

### Segment Classification

PBE weights vary by segment because each business model generates revenue differently.

### Dual MFI / ERI Score System

R3TURN separates market fitness and company readiness through a dual MFI / ERI scoring model.

**Market Fit Index (MFI)** asks: Is the market suitable?
**Export Readiness Index (ERI)** asks: Is the company ready?

Guidance matrix:
- **MFI high + ERI high** → direct action may be appropriate
- **MFI high + ERI low** → readiness roadmap required before action
- **MFI low + ERI high** → the company may be ready, but this market may not be the right priority
- **MFI low + ERI low** → consider another market, segment, or preparation phase

This separation helps distinguish market opportunity from company readiness before action is recommended.

## Terminology Lock

This document uses the following terminology consistently:

- **Layer**: reserved for the 3 Interpretation Layers
- **Domain**: reserved for the 5+1 Audit Domains
- **Dimension**: reserved for product scoring systems such as DES or PBE (four weighted DES scoring dimensions are current, not legacy)
- **Tier**: reserved for the 5 Evidence Tiers
- **Overlay**: reserved for DHG Overlay (not an additional audit domain and not a fifth DES scoring dimension)
- **Surface**: used for buyer-facing digital surfaces such as search, AI answers, websites, public profiles, and listings

The 5+1 audit-domain framework and the four DES scoring dimensions are separate analytical taxonomies. The 5+1 domains define what is examined; the four scoring dimensions define how the current deterministic DES score is aggregated.

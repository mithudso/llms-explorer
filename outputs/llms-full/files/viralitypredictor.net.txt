## Core Metrics

The Virality Predictor evaluates video content across four key neuromarketing dimensions:

### Engagement Score

A quantitative 0-100 score predicting overall video virality. This composite metric synthesizes all neuromarketing signals into a single actionable number. Videos scoring above 80 have strong hooks with high predicted engagement; videos below 40 typically suffer from poor language network activation and rapid attention loss.

### Memory Encoding

Tracks whether viewers will remember your brand after they scroll away. According to Nielsen Consumer Neuroscience research, advertisements that score above average on emotional engagement and memory metrics generate a 23% lift in sales volume. This metric evaluates how effectively your video encodes brand information into viewer long-term memory.

### Visual and Auditory Cortex

Evaluates whether your visuals and sound design create a cohesive sensory experience. Nielsen research finds that highly synchronized sensory ads drive a 15% lift in purchase intent. This metric assesses the integration of visual elements (color, motion, composition) with auditory cues (music, sound effects, voiceover).

### Focus Drift

Pinpoints the exact second your audience gets bored. Kantar studies show that passive attention diminishes rapidly in short formats. The tool identifies the specific timestamp where viewer engagement begins to decline, allowing you to add dynamic text overlays, zoom effects, or pacing changes right before the drop.

## AI Video Maker (Closed-Loop Generation)

Beyond scoring uploads, the platform includes a chat-based AI video generator at [viralitypredictor.net/video-maker](https://viralitypredictor.net/video-maker). It closes the loop between getting a video generated and getting one that actually performs. The same multimodal model that scores videos on the homepage now powers and grades the generator that builds them.

### How the Maker Works

1. **Chat your idea**: Describe the video you want in plain English, or upload a reference image to start an image-to-video flow. The agent asks one or two clarifying questions if it needs them, such as audience, hook style, or length. Each chat turn costs about 1 credit, small enough to explore freely.
2. **Approve the storyboard**: Get a storyboard of four to six frames back in seconds. Generating each frame costs a few credits, and revising it through chat costs about 1 credit per turn. Iterate on the shot list until the preview is right.
3. **Render the final cut**: Hit render. This is when the larger render credit applies, and only on a sequence you have already validated. Your selected video model produces the final cut from the exact frames you approved.
4. **Score, then loop back**: Ask the Maker to score the finished video on Hook Score, Hold Rate, and overall Viral Potential. If it comes back below 70, the Maker points you to the weakest second so you can iterate cheaply and render again.

### What Makes the Maker Different

- **Storyboard safety gate**: Whether you start from text or an image, every idea passes through a storyboard you approve frame by frame before any render. You see every shot before you commit the render-sized spend.
- **Chat first, render last**: Most text-to-video tools charge up front and render blind. The Maker keeps chat and storyboard edits cheap and commits the large render cost only on an approved, scored sequence.
- **Same scoring model as the Predictor**: On-request scoring uses the same neuromarketing-grounded model and rubric as the standalone homepage tool, so a score inside the Maker means the same thing as a score on the homepage.
- **Frontier render models**: Rendering runs through models including Veo 3.1 and Seedance 2.0 at different cost tiers. Output runs 9 to 60 seconds in vertical or horizontal format.
- **Shared credit balance**: Google sign-in grants free starter credits. One credit balance is shared across the Virality Predictor and the Video Maker, and subscription plans cover heavier use.

### Who the Maker Is For

- **Solo creators (UGC)**: Presets bias the storyboard toward handheld pacing, jump cuts, on-screen captions, and a first-second hook for TikTok and Reels.
- **Shopify and Amazon sellers**: Drop in one product photo and get a storyboard with lifestyle context, feature callouts, and hook variants, then commit the render only on the variant with the strongest predicted Hook Score.
- **Performance marketers**: Generate a few hook variations, score each one, and boost only the winner, saving the pre-launch waste of testing creatives blind.
- **Substack and X writers**: Paste a post or thread and get a no-camera Short built from typography, b-roll, and optional AI voiceover.

## AI Video Detector

The Virality Predictor AI Video Detector (https://viralitypredictor.net/ai-video-detector) is an online tool that checks whether a video is real or AI-generated. Upload a clip up to 30 seconds (MP4, WebM, or MOV); the tool analyzes it across six independent signals and returns one of four verdict tiers: AI-Generated, Likely AI, Likely Real, or Real, with per-signal evidence.

The six detection dimensions:
- Visual Artifact Analysis: frame-level pixel statistics, textures, and frequency-domain patterns that separate AI synthesis from camera capture.
- Temporal Coherence: motion consistency and cross-frame variance; in internal benchmarks, AI-generated clips showed temporal variance up to two orders of magnitude lower than real handheld camera footage.
- AI Watermark Scan: detects SynthID-class invisible watermarks embedded by generative platforms such as Google Veo; a watermark hit identifies AI origin directly.
- Content Provenance: reads C2PA content credentials; OpenAI signs Sora output with C2PA, and a credential declaring AI origin is conclusive, while absence proves nothing either way.
- Facial Authenticity: a dedicated deepfake detector pass for videos containing visible human faces.
- File Forensics: container metadata, encoder signatures, and device tags; AI pipelines typically lack the device metadata found in authentic camera recordings.

The detector does not advertise a single accuracy percentage. Verdicts separate definitive signals (watermark and credential hits) from probabilistic ones, and high-stakes decisions should be cross-verified with reverse image search and source checks. Free starter credits are granted on Google sign-in; each detection costs 20 credits. The full video never leaves the user's device; only extracted key frames are analyzed.

# The Modern .NET Show

> An audio-only, interview-style podcast about modern .NET technologies, featuring 196 episodes with .NET developers and experts worldwide. Hosted by Jamie Taylor and produced in the UK by RJJ Software. This is the complete reference document.

The Modern .NET Show (formerly known as The .NET Core Podcast) is an English-language podcast covering modern .NET — the open-source evolution of Microsoft's .NET Framework, sometimes incorrectly referred to as ".NET Core". The show is the go-to podcast for .NET developers worldwide who want to stay ahead of the curve, providing an audio toolbox for modern .NET developers.

New episodes are released every two weeks of the year. The podcast is produced in the UK and takes a break during the summer months. Episodes are 45–70 minutes long, and as of August 2026, 196 episodes have been published, totalling 198 hours of content across 8 seasons. For up-to-date season numbers and the release schedule, see the [podcast schedule](https://dotnetcore.show/press-kit/#podcast-schedule) section of the Press Kit.

The podcast and website are products of [RJJ Software](https://rjj-software.co.uk/) and are designed to help developers of all levels learn more about .NET and the .NET-related tools they should have in their software development toolbox.

The [website](https://dotnetcore.show/) supports both light and dark themes; a toggle is available in the upper navigation (the button with the ☀️ icon). It defaults to a light theme, and the theme preference is persisted in a cookie. The site is built with a static site generator and is rebuilt automatically shortly after each new episode is released.

### Why Sponsor

Sponsoring The Modern .NET Show puts your product in front of 14,000+ engaged .NET developers every month, with over 1,300,000 aggregate downloads and plays across 190+ countries. The audience is highly targeted — primarily software developers using or interested in modern .NET, cross-platform development, and cloud hosting — which makes it an effective channel for vendors whose products or services serve that market. Sponsorship also offers brand visibility within the .NET community by associating with a respected and authoritative source of .NET information, and is cost-effective compared with broader forms of advertising.

### Target Audience

Listeners are interested in .NET, Blazor, ASP.NET Core, and related technologies, as well as JavaScript frameworks including Angular, React, Vue, and Svelte. They are AI- and ML-curious, with many looking to try AI coding tools but unsure which ones to use. They have already subscribed to the show and joined the community of listeners, and they are actively looking to expand their knowledge. They are also people: they want to hear about how your product can reduce their daily toil, make them more productive, or help them be a force multiplier in their organisations.

### Advertising Options

In-episode advertising slots are heard by every listener as they listen through the episode. Three slots are available:

**Pre-roll** — Maximum 30 seconds, placed at the very start of the show before the intro music and lead line. £150 per episode. Your message is the first thing listeners hear and the first thing readers of the show notes see.

**Mid-roll** — Maximum 90 seconds, placed at the mid point of the episode during a natural break in the conversation. £300 per episode. As the conversation takes a breath, listeners are reminded of your product before the discussion resumes.

**Post-episode** — Maximum 90 seconds, placed during the conclusion of the episode after the main content but before the host's wrap-up. £150 per episode. Your message is connected with the listener's positive sentiment for the episode.

### Sponsorship Process

1. **Contact**: Use the [Sponsor](https://dotnetcore.show/sponsor/) form to express interest.
2. **Discussion**: We start a dialogue about your goals and help you pick the most relevant episodes for your message.
3. **Ad copy**: You supply the ad copy; we record several takes of the host reading it.
4. **Approval**: We send the best takes alongside example episode content for your approval.
5. **Publication**: Your message is included in the agreed episode(s).
6. **Promotion**: A thank-you post on social media linking to your product or service.

Prices are exclusive of VAT. Invoices are issued exclusively in GBP, with payments due in GBP.
